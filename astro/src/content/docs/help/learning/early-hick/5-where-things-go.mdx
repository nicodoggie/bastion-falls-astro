## What You'll Learn

This lesson teaches five place endings:

- `-las`, into;
- `-imris`, out of or from;
- `-itar`, up or onto;
- `-asam`, down or onto;
- `-esp`, under or below.

The technical name is spatial case. In this book, call them place endings.

## The "Into" Ending `-las`

The ending `-las` means movement inward or into something. The formal grammar calls it the illative.

<InterlinearGloss>
  <GlossToken source={"branum"} gloss={"PAST"} />
  <GlossToken source={"gal"} gloss={"man"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-es"} gloss={"MAIN"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossToken source={"rismater"} gloss={"reed.boat"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-las"} gloss={"INTO"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossTranslation>{"The man entered the reed boat."}</GlossTranslation>
</InterlinearGloss>

The boat is marked with `-las` because the motion goes into the boat.

## The Five Place Endings

| Ending   | Plain meaning | Example           | Beginner reading |
| -------- | ------------- | ----------------- | ---------------- |
| `-las`   | into          | `rismater-las`    | into the reed boat |
| `-imris` | from, out of  | `rismater-imris`  | from the reed boat |
| `-itar`  | up, onto      | `rismater-itar`   | onto the reed boat |
| `-asam`  | down, onto    | `rismater-asam`   | down from the reed boat |
| `-esp`   | under, below  | `rismater-esp`    | under the reed boat |

## Place Endings Can Carry the Action

Early Hick can sometimes use a place ending where English would use a separate action word.

<InterlinearGloss>
  <GlossToken source={"materok"} gloss={"handler"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-es"} gloss={"MAIN"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossToken source={"rismater"} gloss={"reed.boat"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-imris"} gloss={"FROM"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossTranslation>{"The handler exits the reed boat."}</GlossTranslation>
</InterlinearGloss>

There is no separate word for "exit" in the sentence. The `-imris` ending does the work.

Do not add the action ending `-'er` to a place ending in this pattern. The place ending is still a
noun ending, not an action word. Some older fixed expressions later become action words, but this
lesson uses the ordinary place-ending pattern.

## Into a New State

The "into" idea can also mean changing into a new state.

<InterlinearGloss>
  <GlossToken source={"kamar"} gloss={"red"} />

Think of the sky moving into blueness.

## Try It

Choose the best English meaning for each word.

1. `rismater-las`
1. `rismater-imris`
1. `rismater-itar`
1. `rismater-esp`

Meanings: under the reed boat, into the reed boat, onto the reed boat, from the reed boat.
