## What You'll Learn

This lesson teaches four common time and action markers:

- `branum`, past;
- `branrum`, far past;
- `mulsum`, future;
- `tel`, finished action.

## Sentences Without a Time Word

If a sentence has no time word, read it as present time unless the story around it says otherwise.

<InterlinearGloss>
  <GlossToken source={"'al"} gloss={"person"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-es"} gloss={"MAIN"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossToken source={"barak"} gloss={"walk"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-'er"} gloss={"ACTION"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossTranslation>{"\"The person walks\" or \"The person is walking.\""}</GlossTranslation>
</InterlinearGloss>

## Past and Future Words

Early Hick can put a time word near the beginning of the sentence.

<InterlinearGloss>
  <GlossToken source={"branum"} gloss={"PAST"} />
  <GlossToken source={"materok"} gloss={"handler"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-el"} gloss={"DOER"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossToken source={"ward"} gloss={"guide"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-'er"} gloss={"ACTION"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossTranslation>{"The handler guided."}</GlossTranslation>
</InterlinearGloss>

<InterlinearGloss>
  <GlossToken source={"mulsum"} gloss={"FUTURE"} />
  <GlossToken source={"materok"} gloss={"handler"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-el"} gloss={"DOER"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossToken source={"ward"} gloss={"guide"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-'er"} gloss={"ACTION"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossTranslation>{"The handler will guide."}</GlossTranslation>
</InterlinearGloss>

The far-past word `branrum` is used for events long ago, especially in poetry, ritual, or old
stories.

<InterlinearGloss>
  <GlossToken source={"branrum"} gloss={"FAR.PAST"} />
  <GlossToken source={"'al"} gloss={"person"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-es"} gloss={"MAIN"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossToken source={"veni"} gloss={"burden"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-tar"} gloss={"UP"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossTranslation>{"The person lifted long ago."}</GlossTranslation>
</InterlinearGloss>

## Finished Action with `tel`

The word `tel` marks an action as finished.

<InterlinearGloss>
  <GlossToken source={"'al"} gloss={"person"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-es"} gloss={"MAIN"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossToken source={"barak"} gloss={"walk"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-'er"} gloss={"ACTION"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossToken source={"tel"} gloss={"FINISHED"} />
  <GlossTranslation>{"The person has walked."}</GlossTranslation>
</InterlinearGloss>

You can combine a time word with `tel`.

<InterlinearGloss>
  <GlossToken source={"branum"} gloss={"PAST"} />
  <GlossToken source={"'al"} gloss={"person"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-es"} gloss={"MAIN"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossToken source={"barak"} gloss={"walk"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-'er"} gloss={"ACTION"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossToken source={"tel"} gloss={"FINISHED"} />
  <GlossTranslation>{"The person had walked."}</GlossTranslation>
</InterlinearGloss>

## Try It

Translate each sentence.

1. `mulsum 'ales barak'er`
1. `branum 'ales barak'er tel`
1. `branrum 'ales venitar`

<PopQuiz title="Answers">

1. "The person will walk."
1. "The person had walked."
1. "The person lifted long ago."

</PopQuiz>
