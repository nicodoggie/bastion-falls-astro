## What You'll Learn

This lesson reviews the whole beginner path:

- read word endings before guessing from word order;
- spot action words with `-'er`;
- read place endings as part of the meaning;
- use time words to place a sentence in past, far past, or future.

## Practice 1: A Simple Action

<InterlinearGloss>
  <GlossToken source={"'al"} gloss={"person"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-es"} gloss={"MAIN"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossToken source={"barak"} gloss={"walk"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-'er"} gloss={"ACTION"} type="suffix" />
</InterlinearGloss>

<PopQuiz title="Translation">

"The person walks" or "The person is walking."

</PopQuiz>

## Practice 2: Doer and Receiver

<InterlinearGloss>
  <GlossToken source={"materok"} gloss={"handler"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-el"} gloss={"DOER"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossToken source={"rismater"} gloss={"reed.boat"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-es"} gloss={"RECEIVER"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossToken source={"ward"} gloss={"guide"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-'er"} gloss={"ACTION"} type="suffix" />
</InterlinearGloss>

<PopQuiz title="Translation">

"The handler guides the reed boat."

The ending `-el` marks the handler as the doer. The ending `-es` marks the reed
boat as the receiver.

</PopQuiz>

## Practice 3: Into the Boat

<InterlinearGloss>
  <GlossToken source={"branum"} gloss={"PAST"} />
  <GlossToken source={"gal"} gloss={"man"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-es"} gloss={"MAIN"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossToken source={"rismater"} gloss={"reed.boat"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-las"} gloss={"INTO"} type="suffix" />
</InterlinearGloss>

<PopQuiz title="Translation">

"The man entered the reed boat."

The time word `branum` puts the sentence in the past. The place ending `-las`
means "into."

</PopQuiz>

<InterlinearGloss>

## Practice 5: Color Change

<InterlinearGloss>
  <GlossToken source={"kamar"} gloss={"red"} />
  <GlossToken source={"kel"} gloss={"sky"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-es"} gloss={"MAIN"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossToken source={"kakel"} gloss={"blue"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-las"} gloss={"INTO"} type="suffix" />
</InterlinearGloss>

<PopQuiz title="Translation">

"The red sky turns blue."

The ending `-las` means "into," so the sky moves into blueness.

</PopQuiz>

## From In-World Texts

These examples come from longer in-world Early Hick pieces. At this stage, do not try to explain
every word. Practice spotting the pieces you already know.

### Distant Past

From [The Giant of Inespell][giant]:

<InterlinearGloss
  source={"branrum 'aterbran mator'alul branes"}
  gloss={"FAR.PAST before greater.tribe dawn"}
/>

<PopQuiz title="Reading">

This line begins with `branrum`, the far-past word. A beginner translation is:

```txt
"In the distant past, before the dawn of the Greater Tribe."
```

</PopQuiz>

### Teaching and Learning

Also from [The Giant of Inespell][giant]:

<InterlinearGloss>
  <GlossToken source={"'ilisimris"} gloss={"teach"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-'er"} gloss={"ACTION"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossToken source={"tamin"} gloss={"forge"} />
  <GlossToken source={"-es"} gloss={"MAIN"} type="suffix" />
  <GlossToken source={"'isel"} gloss={"us"} />
</InterlinearGloss>

<PopQuiz title="Reading">

The action word is `'ilisimris'er`, "teaches" or "taught." In the poem, this
line means:

```txt
"They taught us the forge."
```

</PopQuiz>

### Phrase Spotting

From ['ir'erlas 'ibrigralel eldegrales][poem]:

```txt
'ir'erlas 'ibrigralel eldegrales
```

<PopQuiz title="What to spot">

Even without a full translation, you can spot familiar endings:

1. `-las`, the "into" place ending;
1. `-el`, the doer ending;
1. `-es`, the main/receiver ending.

</PopQuiz>

## Try It

Translate these without opening the answers first.

1. `mulsum 'ales barak'er`
1. `materok-es rismater-imris`
1. `kel-es kakel'er`

<PopQuiz title="Answers">

1. "The person will walk."
1. "The handler exits the reed boat" or "The handler moves from the reed
   boat."
1. "The sky is blue."

</PopQuiz>
