**House Sobriquet** holds the county of [Bede](../locations/bede.mdx) in the
[Rannek Marches](../locations/rannek-marches.mdx).

The current Count of Bede is **Nicolas Sobriquet**, familiarly known as **Nick Bede**. Sobriquet is
the family name; Bede is the name of the county. The house's seat is not recorded here.
