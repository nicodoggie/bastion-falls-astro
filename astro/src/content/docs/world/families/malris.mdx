**House Malris** is an old family of the [Rannek Delta](../locations/rannek-delta.mdx), holding
[Reedpool](../locations/reedpool.mdx).

Malris and [House Risen](risen.mdx) are rivals. Each claims that the other began as its cadet
branch; neither account of their seniority and genealogy is established.

The current head is **Count Malris**, who insisted on his title during the council at
[Maidenfeld Keep](../locations/maidens-field.mdx). His personal name and the house's seat are not
recorded here.
