**House Surrata** is a Seneran noble dynasty of Kalassarian extraction whose lords have governed the
Inespell Gulf coast since the Fall of Western Kalassar. The family was closely associated with the
rise of House Maidenfeld, received a royal march along the gulf, and was later elevated to a
marquessate after the War of Thyme and Fire as part of House Dragonheart's effort to absorb former
marcher autonomy into the central peerage. In the present era the head of the house combines the
marquessate of the Inespell Marches with the ancient office of Lord High Admiral of the Realm and
command of the Eastern Fleet.

## History

### Origins and the Maidenfeld alliance

From about 500 AI onward, Surrata lordship over the Inespell Gulf littoral predates or coincides
with the consolidation of Kalassarian successor polities in western Senera. Surrata captains and
castellans are recorded as key supporters of the Maidenfeld dynasty during its ascent; once
Maidenfeld kings were secure, the monarch is said to have confirmed the whole gulf as a **march**
under the first **Marcher Lord of Surrey**, formalizing military and fiscal authority over a
strategic coastline rather than a compact county.

### War of Thyme and Fire and the marquessate

After the War of Thyme and Fire, the Dragonheart crown treated Surrata power as a political risk.
The former **Inespell Marches** were reorganized as a **marquessate**: Surrata gained higher
precedence among the titled nobility but surrendered the exceptional autonomy that marcher lordship
had implied— closer supervision from Thrantorbury in exchange for rank and ceremonial honor.

### Modern role

Today the Surrata head bears the title of **Marquess of the Inespell Marches** and, by long-standing
grant, serves as **Lord High Admiral of the Realm**, linking the family indivisibly to Senera's
eastern sea defense and to the administrative center at Surrey-on-the-Brams.

## Titles and offices

- **Marquess of the Inespell Marches** — territorial title over the reorganized eastern march.
- **Lord High Admiral of the Realm** — crown office entwined with the house; confers command of the
  **Eastern Fleet** and a voice in naval policy out of proportion to land income alone.

## Seat and domain

The principal seat is **Surrey-on-the-Brams**, capital of the Inespell Marches in eastern Senera.
The toponym reflects deliberate **Senerization** of older Kalassarian forms (for example
_Surrata-in-Bramaes_ in imperial registers), pairing a Kalassarian-derived "Surrey" element with the
Hick hydronym _Brams_ (from _bram-aes_), a pattern later extended to other strategic harbors.

## Holdings and infrastructure

Beyond the urban seat, the house's influence rests on coastal fortification and fleet support: an
island fortress complex, the kingdom's principal naval headquarters, eastern commercial harbors, and
purpose-built port works suited to marshaling squadrons in the Inespell Gulf.
