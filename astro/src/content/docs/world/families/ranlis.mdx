**Ranlis** is a Seneran noble family tied to [Faltir](../locations/faltir.mdx), the local saltworks,
and the political order surrounding [Castle Malthrek](../locations/the-maltreks.mdx).
The family is represented during the Castle Malthrek crisis by
[Count Godwin Ranlis](../characters/godwin-ranlis.mdx) and
[Comtesse Aveline Ranlis](../characters/aveline-ranlis.mdx).

## Overview

The Ranlis family appears in Faltir's salt-founding story. In that account, a prospector found
salt, mining and merchant allies followed, and the Maltreks eventually recognized or rewarded the
operation. Faltir is the capital of [Ranlis County](../locations/ranlis-county.mdx), and local
accounts place the house close to the wealth and suffering created by the mines.

During the Castle Malthrek crisis, the family's authority was visible in both public display and
private hardship. An ornate Ranlis carriage procession passed through Faltir while poor locals
collected trampled salt from the road to wash, filter, dry, and reuse. At the same time, miners
faced increased quotas, long shifts, and exhaustion under a system associated with the Count's
household and with Aveline's authority in Godwin's absence.
