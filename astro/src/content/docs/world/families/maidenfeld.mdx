The _Maidenfeld_ family was the founding dynasty of the
[Kingdom of Senera](../locations/senera/index.mdx), which took control of the administration of the
island while the [Kalassarian Empire](../organizations/kalassarian-empire.mdx) was deteriorating.
Their patriarch, [Antonius I Maidenfeld](../characters/antonius-i-maidenfeld.mdx), was a
high-ranking administrator in the Kalassarian-held Senerian government, who took advantage of the
attempt to quell the various Hapgarian rebellions to establish himself as the de facto ruler of the
island.

They were eventually supplanted by the currently-ruling [Dragonheart Dynasty](dragonheart.mdx). As a
compromise following their defeat in the
[War of Thyme and Fire](../events/war-of-thyme-and-fire.mdx), the Maidenfelds were allowed to retain
a portion of their agricultural business ventures. Today, the Maidenfelds supply for approximately
70% of all rare herbs and spices in [Western Betera](../locations/betera/index.mdx).

## Submarines and the Spice Source

At the [September 12 inquiry](../notes/the-vengeful/2026-09-12.mdx), the family acknowledged owning
advanced submarines, including the vessel aboard the MS Paprika. MS means Maidenfeld Ship.
The Baron said the submarines were developed and employed primarily to keep the spice source
secret. He also argued that the family's silence about Reformist activity did not mean it had
remained idle against the movement. The truth and completeness of these explanations remain open;
acknowledged ownership does not verify the claimed purpose or the family's allegiance.

## History

### Investment in Delta Flood Control

The Maidenfelds already held Maiden's Field when the Ranneks coordinated the large
[delta flood-control project](../locations/rannek-marches.mdx#the-delta-flood-control-project),
well after the War of Thyme and Fire. Unlike the incoming cadet branches that financed works in
exchange for new titles and lands, the Maidenfelds invested heavily to protect their existing
holdings and maritime interests. Access to Maiden's Bay was a condition of their participation.

### Bargains and Hag Exclusion

Bargains restrict what the Maidenfelds can disclose about protected matters. These restrictions
do not establish the truth of the explanations the family is able to give.

Seneran hags cannot enter any Maidenfeld demesne, including those who call themselves the
Children of the Crone. The exact contractual wording remains unspecified; the exclusion is
not a general prohibition on magic.

## Heads of House

### Monarchical Period (466-790 AI) \{#monarchical-period}

The Maidenfeld dynasty ruled Senera from 466 AI to 790 AI:

- **Anthony I** (r. 466-491) - Founder of the dynasty and first King of Senera, formerly a
  Kalassarian administrator
- **Mark I** (r. 491-517) - Consolidated royal power and established early institutions
- **Flavius I** (r. 517-542) - Known as "the Golden-haired"
- **Lucy** (r. 542-559) - First queen regnant during a period of internal strife
- **Thralwen I** (r. 559-580) - Introduced more Hick cultural elements to the royal court
- **Claude I** (r. 580-605) - First ruler from the Eastern cadet branch
- **Julius II** (r. 605-612) - Ascended at age 7, ruled under a regency council
- **Valentine I** (r. 612-652) - Known for agricultural reforms and long reign
- **'Irekgral I** (r. 652-670) - Called "the Steadfast", embraced Hick heritage
- **Mark II** (r. 677-699) - From the Northern cadet branch
- **Bramgal I** (r. 699-725) - "The Tide-strength"
- **Anthony II** (r. 725-790) - Last Maidenfeld ruler, lost throne in the War of Thyme and Fire

### Post-Monarchial Period (790-1235 AI)

After losing the throne in 790 AI, the Maidenfeld family was granted a Viscounty under the Rannek
Marquessate as an overture for peace by the Dragonhearts.

The following were heads of house during this period. Note that the Dragonhearts instituted Equal
Primogeniture after the War of Thyme and Fire, so the titles may pass down to the eldest child
regardless of gender:

- **Lucia II** (r. 790-812) - Daughter of Anthony II, negotiated family's survival
- **Bramgal II** (r. 812-840) - Expanded agricultural holdings in western Senera
- **'Irekbres I** (r. 840-859) - Known as "the Shrewd", usurped from her brother
- **Valentine II** (r. 859-891) - Established the spice monopoly
- **Thralwen II** (r. 891-920) - First head under formal equal primogeniture
- **Mark III** (r. 920-952) - Expanded trade routes to Poasah
- **Bramgal III** (r. 952-989) - "The Spicemaster"
- **Lucy III** (r. 989-1018) - Survived the Apgarian Revolution period
- **Flavius II** (r. 1018-1052) - Reformed family business structure
- **'Irekgral II** (r. 1052-1089) - Established Betera trade networks
- **Thralwen III** (r. 1089-1120) - Built the new family estate
- **Valentine III** (r. 1120-1159) - Weathered the Ahurian expansions
- **Bramwen I** (r. 1159-1198) - First to use hybrid naming style
- **Toranthus I** (r. 1198-1235) - Supported the Great Emancipation
- **[Malvius II](../characters/malvius-ii-maidenfeld.mdx)** (r. 1235-present) -
  Current head of house
