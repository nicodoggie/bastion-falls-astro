**House Marcellan** is a noble family associated with the
[Rannek Marches](../locations/rannek-marches.mdx). Its head holds the rank of count and its
demesne is [Mercer County](../locations/mercer-county.mdx), south of
[Reedfield](../locations/reedfield.mdx).

Count Marcellan was reported to have served as second in command during the fighting around
[Maiden's Field](../locations/maidens-field.mdx). The count's personal name and the house's seat
are not recorded here.
