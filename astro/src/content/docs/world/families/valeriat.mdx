**House Valeriat** is a noble family of the [Rannek Marches](../locations/rannek-marches.mdx).
Its current head holds the rank of viscount and governs lands neighboring
[Maiden's Field](../locations/maidens-field.mdx).

Viscount Valeriat supplied troops during the revolt and attended the council at Maidenfeld Keep.
The name of the house's demesne, its seat, and the viscount's personal name are not recorded here.
