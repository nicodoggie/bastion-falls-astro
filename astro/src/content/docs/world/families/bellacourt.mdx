**House Bellacourt** is a baronial family of the [Rannek Marches](../locations/rannek-marches.mdx).
Its lands are known as [Verdiflora](../locations/verdiflora.mdx).

The current head, Baron Bellacourt, represented the house at the council in
[Maidenfeld Keep](../locations/maidens-field.mdx) during the revolt. His personal name and the
house's seat and ancestry are not recorded here.
