The _Bentayga_ clan is a close-knit family of ethnically\
[Parpelissian](../../ethnicities/parpelissian.mdx) sailors who have come to spread across the
islands on the [Sarmatian Sea](../../locations/sarmatian-sea.mdx). They are renowned to be natural
seafarers and navigators that they are highly sought after by shipping companies, smugglers,
privateers and navies around the known world.

A disproportionate number of them have moved into Raibon Island and intermarried with some of the
prominent families on the island.
