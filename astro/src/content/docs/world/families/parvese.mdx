The **Parvèse** are a powerful criminal family based in the East Side of Port Rainoso. Under
[Rosemunda Parvèse](../characters/rosemunda-parvese.mdx), their holdings include the
[Parvèse Houses of Pleasures](../organizations/parvese-houses-of-pleasures.mdx), casinos, and other
establishments throughout the district. Their operations also include the procurement and
trafficking of people.

Parvèse operations supplied much of the human and organ trafficking facilitated by the
[Mirian Trading Company](../organizations/mirian-trading-company.mdx) Liaison Office in Raibon. The
harvested organs were primarily livers taken from people sensitive to magic and were trafficked as
part of the office's cooperation with the Reformist Church.
