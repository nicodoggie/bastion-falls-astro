The McFarland family has roots in the northern island kingdom of
[Iutland](../../locations/iutland.mdx). A branch of the family had migrated southwards, and settled
in [Raibon Island](../../locations/raibon-island.mdx).

Some of them have intermarried with the [Savoy](../savoy/index.mdx) of
[Port Rainoso](../../locations/port-rainoso.mdx). The branch of the family is well-known for being
competent lawyers or accountants.
