The Strandiz family is an [Anasaian](../../locations/anasai.mdx) family well-known for their long
military tradition. Their progenitor, [Matthew I Strandiz](../../characters/matthew-i-strandiz.mdx),
came to prominence by saving the life of then crown prince Hudor of Anasai. Since then, the Strandiz
men have served in various militaries throughout Apgarian history.
