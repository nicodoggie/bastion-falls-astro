The **Fruit Merchants Guild**, colloquially called the **Fructarians**, is a trade organization
associated with fruit merchants and fruit distribution networks.

## Malthrek Lead

During the operations around Castle Malthrek, a magical
[Fruit Merchants Guild Signet Ring](../items/fruit-merchants-guild-signet-ring.mdx) bearing the
guild's symbol was recovered from the ashes of a burned grove and lodge compound used by Reformists.
The ring suggested that someone connected to the Fruit Merchants Guild had dealings with the site or
with the supply route that passed through it.

The lead was especially concerning because nearby intelligence raised the possibility that fruit
shipments could be used to carry or conceal parasites. Whether the guild as an institution was
compromised, whether individual members were involved, or whether its signet had been misused
remained unresolved.
