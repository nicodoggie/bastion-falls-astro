_The Children of the Coven_ is a secret society of Hags of the
[Feywild's](../locations/feywild.mdx)'s Murkendraw. They are led by the Archfey
[Baba Yaga](../characters/baba-yaga.mdx), who is treated as their goddess.

## Theran Diaspora

During the [Draco-Giant War](../events/draco-giant-war.mdx), some members of the Children of the
Coven fled the Feywild and made their way to [Thera](../locations/thera.mdx) via
[The Realm Bridge](../novelty/the-realm-bridge.mdx), hidden among the refugees from the war. Their
express purpose was to explore this other plane of existence and see if they could find a benefit
for themselves and the Baba Yaga. However, they did not foresee that they would be trapped in this
other plane. They would be unable to make contact with other Hags in the Feywild, not even Baba Yaga
herself. Among the Hags that made it to Thera was the [False Crone](../characters/false-crone.mdx)
and her followers, who called themselves the Children of the Crone.
