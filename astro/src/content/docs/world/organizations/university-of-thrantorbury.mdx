The **University of Thrantorbury** is a prestigious institution in
[Thrantorbury](../locations/thrantorbury.mdx), the mountain capital of the
[Kingdom of Senera](../locations/senera/index.mdx). Its colleges teach magical disciplines alongside
natural sciences, public administration, and maritime studies. Cartography is a particular
strength; its maritime program is more modest, limited by its inland setting and the waters
available for practical instruction.

## Main Campus and Academic Buildings

The **Main Campus** occupies the northern academic core. The **Library** stands to its west, with
**Natural Sciences** farther southwest. The university also has a **geography department**;
dormitories near it housed [Matthew Malthrek](../characters/matthew-malthrek.mdx) during his
studies. The plan does not assign the department or his room to a particular building.

The **Public Administration Campus** lies northeast of the central Quad. The university's own
**school administration offices** stand behind it, to the north, distinct from the teaching
campus.

The specialist colleges are distributed around the central campus:

- **College of Divination:** on the western side, south of Natural Sciences.
- **College of Theology:** southwest of the Church of the Angel of Civilization.
- **College of Transmutation:** farther southwest, near the southwestern residential enclave.
- **College of Illusion:** east of Public Administration and northeast of the Artefactory.
- **College of Enchantment:** on the eastern side, south of Illusion.
- **College of Abjuration:** southeast of the Quad, above the Grounds.
- **College of Evocation:** on the southeastern edge, beside the lower Grounds and lagoon.

The **Artefactory**, the university's **Artificer College**, stands east of the Quad. It is separate
from the neighboring colleges of Illusion, Enchantment, and Abjuration.

## The Quad and Student Life

The **Quad** is a central gathering place, with a fountain at its heart. Gardens, benches, and
outdoor study areas are interspersed around it and along the surrounding walks. A **cafeteria**
stands just southwest of the Quad, near the routes to the academic buildings and **Gymnasium**.

The **Church of the Angel of Civilization** stands west of the Quad and south of the Main Campus.
The Gymnasium lies farther south. Garden seating, shaded study spots, and leisure spaces break up
the routes between the colleges and the Grounds.

Student accommodation is divided among three enclaves: the **Northwest Dormitories**,
**Northeast Dormitories**, and **Southwest Dormitories**. **Faculty residences** occupy a separate
area north of the Main Campus.

## The Grounds and Lagoon

The **Grounds** descend toward a lagoon in the lowest part of the campus. This is the only nearby
body of water large enough for the university's maritime practical studies.

The **College of Maritime Studies** occupies a separate building near the College of Evocation
and the lagoon. Its courses include **Maritime Cartography**, the subject studied by Matthew
Malthrek. The lagoon allows for practical instruction, though it does not make the mountain
university a major center of seafaring training.

Evocationists also practice in this lower area to limit potential damage to the rest of the
campus. The plan separates their practice terraces from the maritime boat landing.

## Beneath the University

A **teleportation circle** lies beneath the university. It was reported compromised during the
[Vengeful affair](../notes/the-vengeful/2026-06-14.mdx#the-mistress-and-the-decision-to-rescue-matthew),
preventing its use as a route into or out of Thrantorbury. Its chamber and access route are not
shown on the campus plan.
