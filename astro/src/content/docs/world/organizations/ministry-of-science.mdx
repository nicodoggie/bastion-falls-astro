## Publishing and Distribution

[Luana Victorique Dubreuil](../characters/luana-victorique-dubreuil.mdx) and
[Mystine Nightingale](../characters/mystine-nightingale.mdx) call the working group responsible for
publishing and distributing their magazines **the Management**. It probably operates as a subgroup
within the Ministry of Science overseeing its publication arms, though its exact formal status and
authority have not been established.
