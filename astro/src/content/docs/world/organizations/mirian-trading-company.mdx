The **Mirian Trading Company** formerly maintained a liaison office in Raibon as part of a trade
route carrying Herb of the Harem from Ahuria into Apgar and Senera. The office cooperated with the
Reformist Church, illegally smuggled its members into Raibon, and facilitated human and organ
trafficking. Hedwig Rosenkrantz shut it down after this activity was uncovered, ending the route's
role in the regional supply chain.

Parvèse operations supplied much of the trafficked population and harvested organs moving through
this arrangement. The organs were primarily livers taken from people sensitive to magic.

After the office closed, the Maidenfelds consolidated control over the Herb of the Harem trade in
both Senera and Apgar. Later proposals to cultivate the herb with druids and orb-rich growing
conditions were intended to create a black-market alternative to this monopoly rather than restore
the old Ahurian import route.
