The Ministry of the Occult uses tarot-based titles for its members. These titles are distinct from
their personal names.

### Known Holders

- **La Papessa:** the woman who referred the Maerwen twins to *Legally Bare* and gifted the
  *Banish Blight* artifact embedded in the Green Lord's shrine in
  [Queilei](../locations/queilei.mdx).
- **La Morte** (Death): [Minfilia Diaz](../characters/minfilia-diaz.mdx), whom the Ministry has
  given this title without her knowledge.

[Mystine Nightingale](../characters/mystine-nightingale.mdx) may also be a member; her membership
and title remain unconfirmed.
