_Hermanos de la Libertad_ was a [Mehicanian](../locations/mehicania.mdx) revolutionary organization
that was founded in 979 AI in the context of the
[First Apgarian Revolution](../events/first-apgarian-revolution.mdx) and the
[Paisa Massacre](../events/paisa-massacre.mdx). Though the initial incarnation of the organization
was ultimately a failure, the name would be revived a number of times in the centuries to follow.
The most famous of these was formed during the 1207 AI
[Mehicanian Revolt](../events/mehicanian-revolt.mdx).
