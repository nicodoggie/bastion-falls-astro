_Ramboys_ is an adult magazine approved by the
[Ministry of Science](ministry-of-science.mdx), alongside
[Nightshift Nurses](nightshift-nurses.mdx) and [Legally Bare](legally-bare.mdx). It remains in
development and is not yet ready for publication. Its features center on male models and are
marketed principally toward the gay community and middle-aged housewives.
The working group that publishes and distributes these magazines is called **the Management** by
[Luana Victorique Dubreuil](../characters/luana-victorique-dubreuil.mdx) and
[Mystine Nightingale](../characters/mystine-nightingale.mdx) and is probably a subgroup within the
Ministry.

## Models and Bottoms

Where its sister publications may pair a featured model with a **Dick Model**, commonly abbreviated
as **DM**, _Ramboys_ calls the corresponding partner a **Bottom**.

[Hellion Blanchimontt](../characters/hellion-blanchimontt.mdx) is the publication's first
established model. He conditioned his participation on [Trajan](../characters/trajan.mdx) serving as
his Bottom.

## Proposed Coded Trade

Hellion and Captain Illegal E. Bear have discussed concealing coded pickup-port information within
_Ramboys_ to support a black-market Herb of the Harem trade outside the Maidenfeld monopoly. Hellion
must submit the idea for the Management's risk-management review before it can be approved or
adopted as part of the magazine's distribution practices.
