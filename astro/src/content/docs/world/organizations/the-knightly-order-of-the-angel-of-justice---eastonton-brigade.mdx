The Eastonton Brigade of the
[Knightly Order of the Angel of Justice](/world/organizations/the-knightly-order-of-the-angel-of-justice/)
is a branch of the Holy Paladinate based in
[Eastonton](/world/locations/eastonton/). of [Senera](/world/locations/senera/).

The brigade is notable for its part in the
[Poasan-Apgarian Conflict](/world/events/poasan-apgarian-conflict/), where they
held firm against Apgarian forces as the Senerans were forced to withdraw from
[Sanery](/world/locations/sanery).
