The **Realm Bridge** was a stable fey crossing that carried refugees from the
[Feywild](../locations/feywild.mdx) into [Senera](../locations/senera/index.mdx) during the
[Draco-Giant War](../events/draco-giant-war.mdx). The crossing became inseparable from later Western
Beteran traditions that interpret the Aurorae as a bridge between worlds.

## The Crossing

Someone whose identity and purpose remain unknown foresaw or studied the approaching crossing, but
its destination was not known before it opened. [Auraxx](../characters/auraxx.mdx) persuaded the
Archfey to send the fey communities displaced by the war through it. An unnamed superior within the
draconic forces ordered him not to accompany them, but he entered in humanoid form so that the
refugees would not arrive undefended.

The Realm Bridge opened over Senera. The refugees who crossed it became the ancestors of the fey
communities now living on the island. Auraxx's decision to accompany them had been anticipated by a
curse that imprisoned him in a gold-canary figurine, although the unknown observer of the crossing
and the curse-maker have not been established as the same person.

## Cultural Memory

Western Beteran languages commonly associate the Aurorae with travel between worlds. The realization
of the idea differs across regional traditions, but the bridge metaphor remains widespread.

Northern Apgarian folklore mentions that the colors of the Aurorae show which entities are traveling
between the worlds during the celestial events.

Hickic folklore calls the Lights the Elven Bridge, *'eldegralen asadimris*. The tradition remembers
the elves and other fey creatures who traveled the bridge to escape the Draco-Giant War.
