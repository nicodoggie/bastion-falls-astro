**The Book of the Archfey** is a book of stories about the Archfey of the
[Feywild](../locations/feywild.mdx). They contain descriptions, illustrations,
stories and anecdotes about them, divided into sections by season.

The book focuses only on the Archfey of the
[Seasonal Courts](../locations/feywild.mdx#seasonal-courts), and have
descriptions relating them to colors and gemstones, as well as some description
of their [Elven Boons](./the-elven-boons.mdx).

Each of the Archfey are associated with a season, a color, and a gemstone:

- King Oberon
  - Season: Spring
  - Color: Green
  - Gemstone: Emerald
- Queen Titania
  - Season: Summer
  - Color: Orange
  - Gemstone: Topaz
- Queen Arcadia
  - Season: Fall
  - Color: Red
  - Gemstone: Ruby
- Prince of Frost
  - Season: Winter
  - Color: Blue
  - Gemstone: Sapphire
