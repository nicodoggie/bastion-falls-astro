The Kolhan Conquest is a collective name for a series of campaigns that began when the Ahurians
landed on the southern shores of Poasah in 433 AI and ended a year after the
[Razing of the City of Kalassus](razing-of-the-city-of-kalassus.mdx) in 466 AI. At the end of the
Kolhan Conquest, the Principality of Kolha Ahuria is officially founded.
