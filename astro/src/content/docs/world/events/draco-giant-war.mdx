**The Draco-Giant War** is a major historical event that engulfed the
[Feywild](../locations/feywild.mdx) into a massive conflict between the Giants and the Dragons. The
conflict led to the deaths of large numbers of fey folk, in no small part due to the physical size
of the combatants, who often overlooked the fair-sized.

## The Fey Refugees

As the war displaced fey communities, someone foresaw or studied the impending arrival of a stable
[_fey crossing_](../novelty/the-realm-bridge.mdx) to the world
[Thera](../locations/thera.mdx). Its destination was unknown to the refugees gathering around it.

[Auraxx](../characters/auraxx.mdx), a gold dragon involved in the war, persuaded the Archfey to send
the displaced communities through the crossing. Although an unnamed superior within the draconic
forces ordered him not to accompany them, he assumed his humanoid form and entered the crossing so
that he could protect them wherever they arrived.

The crossing carried them into [Senera](../locations/senera/index.mdx), then a region of Thera that
later became an island. The refugees became the ancestors of Senera's fey
communities, although Auraxx's defiance exposed him to a curse prepared in anticipation of his
choice.
