_The Arrival of the Elves_ was a historical event in what is now
[Senera](../locations/senera/index.mdx) that marked the arrival of the Feyfolk that escaped the
[Feywild](../locations/feywild.mdx) during the onslaught of the
[Draco-Giant War](./draco-giant-war.mdx).

Affected communities were gathered close to an impending _fey crossing_ called by the
[Ma'al](../ethnicities/hick.mdx) as the ['eldegralen asadimris](../novelty/the-realm-bridge.mdx).
The event was celebrated annually by both the _Seneran elven communities_ and the Ma'al up until the
[First Elven Conflict](first-elven-conflict.mdx) started in 650 PF.

Despite the name of the event, elves were not the only refugees from the Feywild. Many kinds of fey
crossed the Realm Bridge together and became the ancestral fey population of Senera.

[Auraxx](../characters/auraxx.mdx) had persuaded the Archfey to send the refugees through the
crossing. Although an unnamed superior within the draconic forces ordered him not to accompany them,
he entered in humanoid form so he could protect them wherever they arrived.
