Since [The Arrival of the Elves](./tallas-eldales.mdx), the
[local population](../ethnicities/hick.mdx) of what is now Senera have lived side-by-side with these
fey visitors. Prophetic traditions of the various ma'al tribes had forseen their arrival in some
way, which had given them time to prepare to receive them.

All of the ma'al tribes were hesitant to welcome the elves, however, it was agreed duing the moot
that a greater disaster would befall them if they refused.

## The Fey Deal

It is often misunderstood that the [Elven Boons](../novelty/the-elven-boons.mdx) were given in
exchange for succor, but the original deal did not include them. Instead the provisions included the
following:

- The Fey will be given a parcel of land in the [Footbowl Basin](../locations/footbowl-basin.mdx)
- The Fey will be given leave to allow only the elven hosts to reside in the lands given to them
