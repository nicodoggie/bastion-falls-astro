Also known as _'Irursibris'eres Eldalul Ma'iruretil_ in the language of the Hicks, the date was once
celebrated annually as the day that the Elves and the Hickic tribes of Senera made their compact. It
was on this day that the the feyfolk that had sook refuge from the
[Draco-Giant War](./draco-giant-war.mdx) were officially given lands to call their homes in specific
parts of Senera, in exchange, the _ma'al_ were given the
[Elven Boons](../novelty/the-elven-boons.mdx).

It was feared that some of the Chromatic Dragons may have taken humanoid form in an attempt to take
over the new lands, beyond the reach of the Archfey.
