The **Seneran Wars of Succession** was a series of interconnected civil wars
that lasted from 675 to 690 AI caused by the death of
['Irekgral the Steadfast](/world/families/maidenfeld/#monarchical-period).

## Background

'Irekgral the Steadfast, born Julius Maidenfeld, continued the controversial
attempt at excising the portions of Seneran culture that were influenced by the
[Kalassarian Empire](/world/organizations/kalassarian-empire/), and return to
what was recently rediscovered as the "true" Seneran culture. Like his paternal
great grandmother, [Thralwen I](/world/families/maidenfeld/#monarchical-period)
who began this campaign of cultural purism, he took a regnal name reconstructed
from words from the [Early Hickic](/world/languages/hickic/seneran/early-hick/)
language. Unlike Thralwen I, however, 'Irekgral was heavy-handed in his efforts.
This made his new policies very unpopular with the nobility, and the
[Orthodox Church of the Divine Masochist](/world/organizations/orthodox-church-of-the-divine-masochist/).
