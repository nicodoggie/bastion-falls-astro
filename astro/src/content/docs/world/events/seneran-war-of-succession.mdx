The **Seneran Wars of Succession** was a series of interconnected civil wars
that lasted from 675 to 690 AI caused by the death of
['Irekgral the Steadfast](../families/maidenfeld.mdx#monarchical-period).

## Background

'Irekgral the Steadfast, born Julius Maidenfeld, continued the controversial
attempt at excising the portions of Seneran culture that were influenced by the
[Kalassarian Empire](../organizations/kalassarian-empire.mdx), and return to
what was recently rediscovered as the "true" Seneran culture. Like his paternal
great grandmother, [Thralwen I](../families/maidenfeld.mdx#monarchical-period)
who began this campaign of cultural purism, he took a regnal name reconstructed
from words from the
[Early Hickic](../languages/hickic/seneran/early-hick/index.mdx) language.
Unlike Thralwen I, however, 'Irekgral was heavy-handed in his efforts. This made
his new policies very unpopular with the nobility, and the
[Orthodox Church of the Divine Masochist](../organizations/orthodox-church-of-the-divine-masochist.mdx).
