The second in a number of attempts to retake the lands of
[Burtz](/world/locations/burtz/) from the warlords ant the
[Reformists](/world/religions/divine-masochism/reformist/) that have taken over
since the [Fall of Bastion](/world/events/fall-of-bastion/).

The name derives from being _Followers of the Whip_,
which was a turn of phrase to mean one who follows in the teachings of the
[Divine Masochist](/world/characters/divine-masochist/).
