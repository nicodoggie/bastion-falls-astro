The _Parade of the Spirits_ is a traditional event held by the
[Hags of Hama](../organizations/hags-of-hama.mdx) after the death and selection
of a new [Moon Witch](../organizations/hags-of-hama.mdx#priestess-of-the-moon).

Prior to the event, vessels of the Spirits of the Sea, the Spider, the Rain, and
the Snake are gathered, along with the candidates for the new Moon Witch are
brought back the the Heart of the Coven of the Hags of Hama.
