**Laudine Maidenfeld nee Rannek** is a Seneran noblewoman born into
[Rannek](../families/rannek.mdx) and married into the [Maidenfeld](../families/maidenfeld.mdx)
family. She is the daughter of [Uther Rannek](uther-rannek.mdx) and
[Maria Rannek](maria-rannek.mdx), and a sister of [Angel Rannek](angel-rannek.mdx).

## Family Position

Laudine belongs to the generation of Rannek children that includes Lance, Merlin, Gwyniviere, and
Angel. Her marriage connects her to one of Senera's older noble houses, whose post-monarchy lands
and interests were historically placed under the Rannek Marquessate.

## Reputation

Laudine owns a prominent line of makeup, beauty boutiques, and haute couture fashion houses famous
throughout Senera. Her businesses make her one of the more visible public figures among the Rannek
children, especially in aristocratic and fashionable circles.

Laudine also trades in favors and possesses unusual influence.[^laudine-favors] Her commercial
reach offers one likely explanation for that reputation, especially among noble households,
fashionable clients, and the circles that depend on her businesses.
