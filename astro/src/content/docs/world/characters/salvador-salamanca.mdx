_Salvador del Latigo_ or more famously _Salvador Salamanca_, was a magical researcher and the
founder of a number of magical institutions in [Mehicania](../locations/mehicania.mdx). Noted as one
of the founders of the anti-royalist movement
[Hermanos de la Libertad](../organizations/hermanos-de-la-libertad.mdx), he authored the
controversial manifesto _El Govierno Magocratico de la Tierra Mehicania_, which advocated for a
magocratic government in Mehicania.
