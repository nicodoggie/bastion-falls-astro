**Melki** was a student of Gerta Moon's Boarding School during the Gerta's Home affair. The
surviving records describe her less through magical office than through domestic tastes, especially
cooking, salty food, and a collection of soy sauces brought to her through Sanerian merchants.

Melki was one of the older girls at the school. She hoped to travel widely, eat the foods of many
regions, and eventually attend culinary school in [Sanery](../locations/sanery.mdx). Gerta Moon
appears to have encouraged this interest by obtaining unusual seasonings for her through commercial
contacts.

Her family situation had become uncertain before the school fled Sanery. Melki's parents stopped
answering letters, and she was expected to stay with an aunt in Rainoso while the matter was
investigated. Gerta suspected Reformist involvement and advised the aunt to contact authorities
rather than accept the silence as ordinary neglect.

Melki was part of the group forced from Sanery after Reformists attempted to seize both the girls
and the magical resources guarded by Gerta Moon and Atomina Russell. The planned relocation to
[Sentimental Island](../locations/sentimental-island.mdx) failed when the students' vessel capsized
in a storm, leading to their rescue by the [Rozenmaiden](../vehicles/rozenmaiden.mdx). Her known
schoolmates included [Lime Schulze](lime-schulze.mdx),
[Luana Victorique Dubreuil](luana-victorique-dubreuil.mdx), [Snow Skalla](snow-skalla.mdx),
[Sigrid the Netweaver](sigrid-the-netweaver.mdx), [Bianca Bentayga](bianca-bentayga.mdx), and
[Fawn Plaisir](fawn-plaisir.mdx).

After the Sentimental Island undead attacks, Melki was among the Gerta Moon students brought to the
bog for the Parade of Spirits. She was considered as a possible Moon Witch candidate, but did not
accept the role because she wanted to travel the world and become a chef. Later accounts place her
with Andy Anderson during the same period and connect these events to the group's displacement into
the Feydark.
