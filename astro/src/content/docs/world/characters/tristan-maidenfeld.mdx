**Tristan Maidenfeld** is a member of the [Maidenfeld family](../families/maidenfeld.mdx) who
works directly with [Baron Malvius II Maidenfeld](malvius-ii-maidenfeld.mdx).

## The Hicklander Crisis

During the unrest in Maiden's Field, Tristan was cited within the family as a source of information
about the treatment of Hicklanders. His account was invoked in support of the claim that excessive
punishments by the family's personnel had been isolated and corrected. That assurance did not settle
the wider dispute over workers' treatment or the causes of the revolt.

Tristan was absent from a subsequent gathering with the Baron, and his whereabouts became a matter
of concern among the allies responding to Crispin Mallow's attacks.
