_Stacy Cartwright_ is one of the Malthrek Court Wizards mentioned in the Vengeful notes.

As of the Castle Malthrek funeral operation, her exact return timing and morning duties remain an
open question. She was one of the court wizards sent to fetch nobles for the funeral, and three
other Malthrek Court Wizards remain unaccounted for.
