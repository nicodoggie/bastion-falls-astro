**Stacy Cartwright** is a transmuter and one of the Malthrek Court Wizards. She was among the court
wizards sent to fetch nobles for the Castle Malthrek funeral, while three of her colleagues remained
unaccounted for.

## The Gilded Punishment

Stacy attempted to expose [Crispin Mallow](crispin-mallow.mdx). In punishment, Crispin used her as a
testbed for incorporating [Auraxx's](auraxx.mdx) anatomy before attempting the greater
transformation upon himself. He selected the least magically powerful portions of the dragon for the
experiment: scale-bearing skin; distal forelimb bones and tendons with a claw root; terminal wing
digits and membrane; and pieces of rib, sternum, cartilage, and marrow. These grafts compel her
magic and preserve her life through an excruciating transformation, leaving Stacy conscious inside a
body being continually rewritten against her will. The skin grafts are the foundation of the
counterfeit scale covering that spreads across her transformed body.

The punishment is recoverable by design, although not mercifully so. When Stacy suffers mortal
injury, the dragon remnants unfold into a counterfeit gilded body rather than allowing her to die.
Restorative magic causes the incompatible tissue to reject its host, and careful magical or medical
intervention can suppress the control graft. Destroying the transformed grafts leaves Stacy
unconscious but alive. Removing Auraxx's remnants can therefore save Stacy, but it cannot restore
the dragon's dismantled body.

## Recovery

Sister Pira and Gwyniviere removed the known grafts while healing Stacy between procedures, and
she survived and awakened. Their efforts may have removed the control itself. Any remaining
difficulties could involve trauma responses rather than continued magical control; neither a
surviving controller nor a purely psychological explanation is established. Her recovery remains
open, separately from [Auraxx's later release](auraxx.mdx#release-and-remaining-questions).
