**Fawn Plaisir** was the nickname and school name of **Adeline Plaisir**, a young student
associated with Gerta Moon's Boarding School and the
[Hags of Hama](../organizations/hags-of-hama.mdx). She is recorded as the holder, or intended
holder, of the Spirit of the Snake. Her disappearance during the Reformist attack on the school
became one of the more damaging failures in the early Gerta Moon accounts.

School records describe Fawn as a cheerful orphan with brown braided pigtails and red ribbons. She
liked traps, bows, and the idea of hunting, and she showed a strong childish attachment to Galahad,
the school's guard. Her exact age is uncertain in surviving accounts, which place her either in
late childhood or at the beginning of adolescence.

Fawn was part of the group forced from [Sanery](../locations/sanery.mdx) after Reformists attempted
to seize both the girls and the magical resources guarded by Gerta Moon and Atomina Russell. The
planned relocation to [Sentimental Island](../locations/sentimental-island.mdx) failed when the
students' vessel capsized in a storm, leading to their rescue by the
[Rozenmaiden](../vehicles/rozenmaiden.mdx). Her known schoolmates included
[Lime Schulze](lime-schulze.mdx), [Luana Victorique Dubreuil](luana-victorique-dubreuil.mdx),
[Snow Skalla](snow-skalla.mdx), [Sigrid the Netweaver](sigrid-the-netweaver.mdx),
[Bianca Bentayga](bianca-bentayga.mdx), and [Melki](melki.mdx).

## Identity Complication

During the Reformist attack on Gerta Moon's school, Fawn was replaced by a Face Stealer who assumed
her identity among the students. The true Fawn was not kept as a major prize; the Reformists
apparently failed to recognize her importance and sold her into slavery. Later references to Fawn in
the Gerta's Home records must therefore be read carefully, since some describe the real child and
others describe the infiltrator occupying her identity.

Fawn was later rescued in Shagnery by members of the Rozenmaiden's crew. At the time, the ship's
field command had temporarily fallen to [Dominguez Tailleur](dominguez-tailleur.mdx), because both
the captain and the first mate were occupied by other obligations. The Shagnery rescue confirmed
that the real child had survived the replacement and sale.

After the Shagnery rescue and the defense of Sentimental Island from undead attacks, Fawn was among
the Gerta Moon girls brought to the bog for the Parade of Spirits. The same sequence of events led
into the group's displacement into the Feydark.
