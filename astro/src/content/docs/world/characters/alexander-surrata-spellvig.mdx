**Alexander Surrata-Spellvig** is a member of the Surrata-Spellvig cadet branch of
[House Surrata](../families/surrata.mdx). Records identify him as a sibling of
[Henry Surrata-Spellvig](henry-surrata-spellvig.mdx) and [Lilya Bkorneblume](lilya-bkorneblume.mdx).

Accounts of his abilities describe him as a human barbarian with AC 16, 80 maximum hit points, 40
feet walking speed, and command of Common, Common Sign Language, and Orc.
