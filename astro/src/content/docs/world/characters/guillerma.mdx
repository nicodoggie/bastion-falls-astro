**Guillerma** is a human adventurer associated with
[The Hunting Lodge](../organizations/the-hunting-lodge.mdx) and
[La Camaraderie](../organizations/la-camaraderie.mdx). Some records also link
her to the [Hags of Hama](../organizations/hags-of-hama.mdx) as allies and to
the
[Reformed Church of the Divine Masochist](../organizations/reformed-church-of-the-divine-masochist.mdx)
as an enemy.

Accounts of her abilities describe her as a chaotic, elusive performer with
monastic and roguish training. She has AC 16, 72 maximum hit points, 30 feet
walking speed, and unusually sharp insight. Her known languages are Apgarian,
Common, Elvish, and Thieves' Cant.

## Biography

Guillerma travels under the cover identity **Gianna Campbell** during parts of
the Vengeful affair. Records pair her with
[Dorothy Marie Campbell](dorothy-campbell.mdx) through sending stones, and later
describe an established connection between them.

During the investigation into reavers and reformist activity, Guillerma worked
with [Aigral Eastonton](aigral-eastonton.mdx). Reports place the two of them on
their way to the docks of [Eastonton](../locations/eastonton.mdx), and also
record Guillerma arranging dance parties at the plaza as a way to draw public
notice.

Guillerma contacted [Minfilia Diaz](minfilia-diaz.mdx) from
[Senera](../locations/senera/index.mdx), warning that the bishop of
[Port Surrey-on-the-Brams](../locations/port-surrey-on-the-brams.mdx) might be
connected to reformist activity and asking for one of the hags to be sent over.

## Description and Affiliations

Guillerma is described as an entertainer with brown hair, hazelnut eyes, and
caramel skin. She is associated with The Hunting Lodge and La Camaraderie, and
some accounts identify the Hags of Hama as allies and the Reformist's Church as
enemies.
