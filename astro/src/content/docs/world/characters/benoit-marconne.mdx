**Benoit Marconne**, also known as **Camden Dubreuil**, is a young human heir connected to the
Dubreuil family and the
[Parvese Houses of Pleasures](../organizations/parvese-houses-of-pleasures.mdx) of
[Port Rainoso](../locations/port-rainoso.mdx). He was raised under the patronage of
[Rosemunda Parvese](rosemunda-parvese.mdx), who presented him as a foundling rescued from the East
Side while quietly preparing to use him in a scheme for wealth, inheritance, and renewed youth.

By the age of sixteen, Benoit had become a floor manager in the Miller Street House and was being
prepared for higher managerial duties. Those close to the Sea Hags affair, however, came to believe
that his apparent advancement was also a form of containment. Rosemunda kept him grateful,
isolated, and dependent while concealing the larger value of his identity.

## Identity

Benoit was said to have been found on the streets of Port Rainoso's East Side and taken in by
Rosemunda. In truth, he was the only known living male heir of the Dubreuil family, the makers of
one of the Apgar Confederacy's best-known cognac brands, and the older cousin whose disappearance
affected [Luana Victorique Dubreuil](luana-victorique-dubreuil.mdx) in her youth. A substantial
bounty had been offered for information leading to his recovery, supported by identifying marks
that could prove his identity.

Rosemunda's agents appear to have found Benoit before the Dubreuil family or its allies could
recover him. She initially had reason to use him as a bargaining piece, but the prospect of soul
transfer changed her plans. Once [Iluvia Wunderplat](iluvia-wunderplat.mdx) described the abilities
of the Soul Stone, Rosemunda saw a path to claim Benoit's body and inheritance for herself.

## Parvese Grooming

Under Rosemunda's care, Benoit was trained to accept increasing responsibility in the Parvese
Houses of Pleasures. He was grateful to Rosemunda and tended to interpret her control as protection
or rescue. This made him difficult to warn, because he had not been openly imprisoned and did not
understand the degree to which his choices were being managed.

Rosemunda's intended endgame was to transfer her soul into Benoit's body once he was sufficiently
attached to her and properly positioned for inheritance. She would then have Benoit, trapped in her
old body, poisoned. The arrangement would allow her to gain the Dubreuil inheritance while also
arranging for Benoit to become sole inheritor of the Parvese estates, consolidating both fortunes
under her stolen identity.

## Raibon Affair

Benoit entered the wider Raibon affair when Rosemunda brought him to the Meeting of the Four as an
apparent reward for good service. During the gathering, he joined the Chicken Chase game and met
[Pecorine](pecorine.mdx). He became enamored with her and made a wager: if he won, he would take her
to dinner the next day; if he lost, he would meet her for lunch wearing a chinchilla costume. Either
outcome would allow him to meet someone outside the House of Pleasures in a setting not defined by
customers or Rosemunda's business.

Benoit lost the wager and met Pecorine and [Anton Lightning](anton-lightning.mdx) for lunch. The
meeting revealed his desire for ordinary friendship and a life beyond the Miller Street House. It
also showed how deeply Rosemunda's influence shaped his loyalties, even when her surveillance and
control were visible to outsiders.

Pecorine and Anton planned to bring Benoit to a
[Mirian Trading Company](../organizations/mirian-trading-company.mdx) Fight Night, where he could
be warned about Rosemunda's plan to take over his body. The plan linked Benoit's rescue to the
broader effort to negotiate with Justus, the company's Port Rainoso liaison, and to the factional
settlement that followed the Meeting of the Four.
