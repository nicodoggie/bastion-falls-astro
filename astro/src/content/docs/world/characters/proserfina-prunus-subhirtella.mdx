**Proserfina Prunus Subhirtella**, usually called **Fina**, is a changeling from the Feywild. She
was a researcher at [Inkwell Academy](../locations/inkwell-academy.mdx), where she specialized in
Fey Crossings. She also loved exploring ruins, making scholarship and expedition work natural
extensions of the same curiosity.

Fina adventured in the Feywild with [Cotto](cotto.mdx) and others after Cotto came there. During
those adventures, Fina and Cotto fell in love.

When Fina was given the choice to go to Cotto's world, she accepted it.

During the later Vengeful affair, Fina and Cotto became trapped in Thrantorbury's time loop with
[Minfilia Diaz](minfilia-diaz.mdx) and [Oscar Savoy](oscar-savoy.mdx).
