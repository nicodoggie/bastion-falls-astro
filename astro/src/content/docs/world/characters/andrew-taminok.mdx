**Andrew Taminok** is the youngest son of a family rooted in farming and mercantile trade. Selling
vegetables remained the family’s primary business even as it diversified into other goods. Andrew’s
father regularly travelled to negotiate sales and expand the business; during Andrew’s lifetime, he
also purchased the brewery that now bears the Taminok name. Andrew’s mother, Eleanor, is in her
seventies and lives separately with servants.

The family's fields produce golden grains and barley. Pixies watching over Eleanor called these
crops the Taminoks' “gold,” referring to the grain itself and the prosperity it brings rather than
to a separate magical substance.

Accounts portray Andrew as exceptionally strong, athletic, and practiced with armor, shields, and
a wide range of weapons. His artisan’s training includes brewer’s supplies, smithing, carpentry,
and masonry. He is recorded as knowing Common, Common Sign Language, and Orc.

Andrew has five older brothers. The eldest, Bramis, became a sailor and captain in Maidenfeld
service; he is married to Satoria, who asks Andrew to call her “Sister,” and the couple have four
children. The second brother, Torgral, won silver in fencing at the ToTO Friendship Games, later
worked at the family brewery, and married Labella, Andrew’s second sister-in-law. The third brother
became a lecturer at the University of the Maltreks, the fourth a successful blacksmith, and the
fifth a jeweler.
