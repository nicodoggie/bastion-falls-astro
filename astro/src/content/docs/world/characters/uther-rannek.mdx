**Uther Rannek** is the Marquess of [Rannek](../families/rannek.mdx), husband of
[Maria Rannek](maria-rannek.mdx), and father of Lance, Laudine, Gwyniviere, Merlin, and
[Angel Rannek](angel-rannek.mdx). As Marquess, he represents House Rannek in the Seneran peerage
and in the Seneran House of Lords.

## Family

Uther is the head of the immediate Rannek household during the Vengeful affair. His wife Maria
appeared at the Travertines as Marchioness of Rannek, while his daughter
[Gwyniviere Rannek](gwyniviere-rannek.mdx) is connected to the Malthreks through her betrothal to
[Mark Malthrek](mark-malthrek.mdx). His youngest daughter Angel became central to events involving
the [Emerald Shield of Spring](../items/emerald-shield-of-spring.mdx), the Bishop of Rannek, and
the Reformist threat around the Rannek Marches.

## Vengeful Affair

During the Vengeful affair, Uther was away from the Travertine wedding because he was quelling a
rebellion in the [Rannek Delta](../locations/rannek-delta.mdx). The rebels were members of
Hickland for Hicks who were destroying flood-control structures in the Delta. The resulting floods
threatened Maiden's Fields and were expected to create wider food security problems in Senera if
left unresolved.[^uther-delta]

Angel sent a message to Uther after returning to Rannek with [Lance Rannek](lance-rannek.mdx),
informing him that they were heading to the Bishop of Rannek and that she had passed important
information to court wizards. Uther's absence from the Travertines left Maria and Gwyniviere to
represent the family at the Malthrek-Travertine wedding while the Rannek crisis remained
active.[^uther-delta]
