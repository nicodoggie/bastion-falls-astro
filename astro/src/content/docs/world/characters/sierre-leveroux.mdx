Sierre Leveroux is a nun of the Sisters of Delight, a sect within Orthodox Divine Masochism that
venerates the Angel of Delight, one of the five Angels of Creation.

She was sent to investigate rumors that the Reformist Masochists were attempting to spread their
heresy across Apgar by first taking root in the demilitarized Raibon Island.
