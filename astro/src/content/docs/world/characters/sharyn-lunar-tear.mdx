**Sharyn Lunar-Tear** is the twin sister of [Raistasi Lunar-Tear](raistasi-lunar-tear.mdx). She is
known for a gentle manner and treating others as equals, and places devotion to the Lady of the
Woods above other concerns. She has pale skin, yellow-green eyes, and light grey to green hair.

Sharyn and Raistasi share the alias **Maerwen Lunar-Tear**, also written **Maerwen Lunartear**.
Sharyn handles outside communication and paperwork for that shared identity.
Daggerheart, their pixie familiar and painter, usually accompanies her. Her associations include
The Management, La Papessa, and the
[Ministry of the Occult](../organizations/ministry-of-the-occult.mdx).
