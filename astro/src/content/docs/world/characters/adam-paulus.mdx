**Adam Paulus** is a [human](../species/human.mdx) adventurer associated with
[La Camaraderie](../organizations/la-camaraderie.mdx).

Accounts of Adam's current abilities describe him as a veteran fighter and artificer with a
shipwright's practical training. He is especially noted for his strength, intelligence, and skill
with tools, and is associated with the use of an eldritch cannon called
**The Scarlet: Thundercannon**. Current records place him at armor class 15, 89 hit points, and a
walking speed of 30 feet. He is known to speak Common and Zemnian.
