_Sandor Kethlas_ is a stowaway who began working for the Sea Crawler.

## Return to Castle Rannek

After his captivity under [Crispin Mallow](crispin-mallow.mdx), Sandor arrived dishevelled during
the court wizards' siege of Castle Rannek and fought for the defenders. He saved Trajan by pushing
his spellcasting beyond his capabilities, leaving himself unconscious from exhaustion. He was
later recovering in his room, where Angel visited him with the transformed Crispin.

How Sandor escaped captivity and Mallow's control remains unexplained, as do the details of his
captivity and how Mallow acquired his copied face. His return does not establish that Angel's
breaking of Mallow's fey deals and curses caused his escape.

See [The Vengeful, September 12](../notes/the-vengeful/2026-09-12.mdx).
