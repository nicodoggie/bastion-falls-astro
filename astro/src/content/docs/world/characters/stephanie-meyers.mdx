**Stephanie Meyers** is a shifter that can transform into a cat. She is also
a hag of the
[Children of the Coven](/world/organizations/children-of-the-coven/).

She travelled from the [Feywild](/world/locations/feywild/) to
[Thera](/world/locations/thera/) with the crew of the
[Rozenmaiden](/world/vehicles/rozenmaiden/) to find a way to get into the good
graces of [Baba Yaga](/world/characters/baba-yaga/) again after she had
accidentally caused the destruction of an important item she was tasked with
protecting.
