**Hellion Blanchimontt** is an eladrin noble and monk associated with
[House Blanchimontt](#house-blanchimontt). He is described as a young man with green eyes, pale
skin, and pale yellow hair.

Hellion is known as a swift and disciplined practitioner of the Way of Mercy, with the bearing and
education expected of a young noble. Accounts of his abilities emphasize his exceptional speed, keen
perception, and familiarity with Common, Elvish, and Goblin. Current records place him at armor
class 20, 71 hit points, and a walking speed of 60 feet.

## Biography

Hellion came to notice in [Flyukishilla](../locations/flyukishilla.mdx) during the events
surrounding the masquerade at Copperlock Mansion. At the Beached Whale, he spoke of
**the Challenger**, a kraken discovered in the Peridot Sea, and was connected to the auctioning of
magical artifacts. After several of those goods were stolen, he appears to have judged the
[Sentinels of Equity](../organizations/sentinels-of-equity.mdx) insufficiently capable of protecting
them.

Reports from Flyukishilla portray Hellion as bored with the city's events yet curious about Proteus.
He is said to have studied business in Nuenmuller and to have lived in the Woods of Veil, a place
associated with the [Spring Court](../locations/spring-court.mdx).

By the time of the voyage on [The Scarlet](../vehicles/the-scarlet.mdx), Hellion was listed among
the passengers quartered in the lower-deck crew's quarters.

## House Blanchimontt

**House Blanchimontt** is the noble house most closely associated with Hellion. Some records use the
spelling **Blanchemont**. Beron and Ingrid are also named among the family, and Beron is said to
bear a resemblance to Hellion. An Uncle Felt Blanchemont is likewise associated with the auction
house in Flyukishilla.
