**Baron Maidenfeld** is the lord of [Maiden's Field](../locations/maidens-field.mdx), the
[Maidenfeld family's](../families/maidenfeld.mdx) territory in the Rannek Delta. He directs the
barony's response to the revolt and the attacks threatening its people, floodworks, and spice trade.
[Tristan Maidenfeld](tristan-maidenfeld.mdx) works directly with him.

## Family

His late wife served as Master of the Fleet for the Maidenfeld Spice Company before
[Steven Maidenfeld](steven-maidenfeld.mdx). The Baron reported that she was implanted with a
parasite during company business at sea and that he killed her. He believed the parasite had
replaced her; this does not establish that she was beyond healing.

Helena Cooper, a local Hicklander leader, is his illegitimate daughter, born of an affair with her
mother.

## Defense of Maiden's Field

During the crisis, the Baron presided over a guarded situation room attended by local nobles.
[Robert Maidenfeld](robert-maidenfeld.mdx) and [Steven Maidenfeld](steven-maidenfeld.mdx) brought
Rannek allies into that council as they sought to rescue Marquess Uther and oppose Crispin Mallow.

The Baron asserted that the protections associated with the
[Emerald Shield of Spring](../items/emerald-shield-of-spring.mdx) should restrict aberrations.
That assertion did not establish their effectiveness against Mallow's forces, nor did it imply
that members of the Reformist Church were magically barred merely by their allegiance.
