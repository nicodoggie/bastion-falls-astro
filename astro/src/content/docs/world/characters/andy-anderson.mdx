**Andy Anderson** is a kobold adventurer recorded in recent Bastion Falls
accounts. He was born human, but was transformed into a kobold after eating a
[Transmogrishroom](../items/transmogrishroom.mdx) while in
[Jing Shen Jiang](../locations/jing-shen-jiang.mdx).

Accounts describe Andy as quick, watchful, and practiced at moving unseen. He is
known to speak Common and Draconic, and has picked up the cant used by thieves
and smugglers.
