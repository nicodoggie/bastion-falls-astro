**Rurika Maidenfeld** is a healer in [The Thirteen](../organizations/the-thirteen.mdx). She is the
daughter of Spearmint Maidenfeld, an itinerant spice and herb merchant whose family had migrated
into [Poasah](../locations/poasah.mdx) from [Senera](../locations/senera/index.mdx) generations
earlier.

After her father was falsely accused of adultery and executed, Rurika was driven from town and
survived in the wilderness through her knowledge of herbs. Her gift for healing magic was later
discovered by Carmain Praisa of The Thirteen's Healing Corps, who took her in and trained her in the
healing arts.

Rurika was eventually deployed to the Tandurian border as a healer and to assist in searches for
magical creatures.
