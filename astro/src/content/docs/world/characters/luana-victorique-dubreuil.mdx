**Luana Victorique Dubreuil** is a young human noble of the Dubreuil family, a house associated with
one of the best-known cognac brands in the Apgar Confederacy. Records connect her with
[La Camaraderie](../organizations/la-camaraderie.mdx) and Gerta Moon's Boarding School.

Luana was sent by her family to a boarding school in hopes of finding a remedy for her
achromatopsia. Accounts also describe her as nearsighted. She is said to have fallen into a brief
depression after learning that an older cousin to whom she was close had been kidnapped when she was
younger. Her older sister, Meredith, had already married into a wealthy family by the time Luana's
own affairs entered the record.

The school Luana attended was Gerta Moon's Boarding School, which began in
[Sanery](../locations/sanery.mdx) as a strict but protective refuge for girls with unusual magical
importance. A Reformist attempt to take the girls and the magical resources guarded by Gerta Moon
and Atomina Russell forced the school to leave Sanery. The subsequent move toward
[Sentimental Island](../locations/sentimental-island.mdx), where Gerta had bought land for a safer
home and orphanage, ended in disaster when the students' vessel capsized during a storm and was
rescued by the [Rozenmaiden](../vehicles/rozenmaiden.mdx).

More recent accounts say Luana learned she was under a curse, though neither she nor those close to
her remembered when or how it began. She is associated with an archfey figure known in some records
as Mommy, who appeared to her in a dream beneath a massive tree. In that dream, Luana saw color and
watched a young girl in unusual vestments reminiscent of the
[Divine Masochist](divine-masochist.mdx) hierarchy. The vision ended before Luana could approach the
girl, but it became part of her search for a way to break the curse and is said to have opened a
path for astral projection.

Records portray Luana as thoughtful, kindhearted, and reserved around adults. She is noted for long
blonde hair, gold eyes, and a small build. She is also described as socially graceful, quick-handed,
and unusually persuasive for her age, with training in calligraphy, disguise, cards, and overland
travel.

Luana moved from [Sentimental Island](../locations/sentimental-island.mdx) to
[Port Rainoso](../locations/port-rainoso.mdx) over the objections of her
family lawyer, [Jason McNatt](jason-mcnatt.mdx). This was because she was approached by
[Mystine Nightingale](mystine-nightingale.mdx) with the idea of handling a column for
[Legally Bare](../organizations/legally-bare.mdx) dedicated to girlhood, inspired by the dormitory
sisters she had known at school.

She modelled for Legally Bare, which were attached to the column she contributed to called
_Ask Cinna_ under the pseudonym **School Girl Cinna Moan**. Since most models for the publication
were given a choice of painters they were comfortable with, she chose [Snow Skalla](snow-skalla.mdx)
to paint her material.

Her personal tastes are recorded with unusual specificity: white lilies, cognac beef stew, chocolate
truffles, journalism, writing, reading, stickers, cosmetics, secret meetings, and favorable reviews
of the magazine. She is said to dislike mathematics and shrimp.
