Lyla Durant is the youngest daughter of [Degare Durant](degare-durant.mdx) and
[Elodië Bentayga](elodie-bentayga.mdx), and was named after Degare's younger sister
[Lyla Savoy née Durant](lyla-durant.mdx).

Two years ago, due to a congenital heart condition, Lyla abruptly died. After a particularly active
play session with her mother, she began breathing heavily. Within moments, she gasped her last
breaths, then collapsed.

Her mother Elodië was inconsolable after her death, and took her life soon after. The tragedy also
affected Degare Durant, the girl's father, immensely. He became erratic, suddenly spouting
conspiracies about the death of Lyla's namesake, blaming the elder Lyla's late husband
[Percival Savoy](percival-savoy.mdx).
