**Baba Yaga** is an archfey of the [Feywild](../locations/feywild.mdx) and considered as the Mother
of all Witches by her followers, the
[Children of the Coven](../organizations/children-of-the-coven.mdx).

## False Crone

A small subset of the Children of the Coven believe that Baba Yaga is not an individual person, but
rather a title given to the most powerful witch in the Feywild. The strongest of these witches
styled herself as Baba Yaga and attempted to overthrow the archfey and take her place, but she was
severely defeated and almost died in the process. Little is shared about this witch, but some say
that she was able to escape the Feywild in the confusion of the
[Draco-Giant War](../events/draco-giant-war.mdx). Those that do know of the story call her the
[False Crone](false-crone.mdx).
