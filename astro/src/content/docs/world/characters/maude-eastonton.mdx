**Maude Eastonton** was a member of the [Eastonton](../families/eastonton.mdx) family and the wife
of [Garward Eastonton](garward-eastonton.mdx). She was the mother of Gareth Eastonton,
[Aigral Eastonton](aigral-eastonton.mdx), and [Nandor Eastonton](nandor-eastonton.mdx).

Maude died within a year of the Eastonton men's return from the
[Poasan-Apgarian Conflict](../events/poasan-apgarian-conflict.mdx). Her death left the religious
and military formation of her younger children increasingly in the hands of Garward, Gareth, and the
Eastonton Paladinate.
