Andrei Delarde was a founding member of the infamous mercenary group
[Free Theater](../organizations/free-theater.mdx) along with its leader,
[Paula Abdul](paula-abdul.mdx). He was once a fellow at the venerable
[Apgarian Magical Military Academy](../organizations/apgarian-magical-military-academy.mdx),
before he was dismissed due to charges of embezzlement of public funds.
