**Justin Pasteur** is a [human](../species/human.mdx) adventurer associated with
[La Camaraderie](../organizations/la-camaraderie.mdx), the later Gerta Moon circle, and
[Lime Schulze](lime-schulze.mdx). He first appears in surviving accounts as a shipboard servant of
the Raibon circle and later became known as a monk of the Way of the Astral Self, a crewman of the
[Rozenmaiden](../vehicles/rozenmaiden.mdx), and one of Lime's closest companions during the events
that followed Gerta Moon's attempted relocation to
[Sentimental Island](../locations/sentimental-island.mdx).

## Raibon and the Rozenmaiden

Justin first served La Camaraderie as a laundry boy aboard
[La Carnalis](../vehicles/la-carnalis.mdx). After Hedwig Rosenkrantz acquired the
[Rozenmaiden](../vehicles/rozenmaiden.mdx), she persuaded [Somme Privatea](somme-privatea.mdx) to
become her first mate and brought several favored La Carnalis staff into the new crew, including
figures such as [Dominguez Tailleur](dominguez-tailleur.mdx). Justin was one of the people Hedwig
wanted to take from Jean-Paul's ship, and he belonged to that wider shipboard circle before he
became involved with Gerta Moon's students.[^carnalis]

Justin's earliest preserved family history centers on his mother and the curse that shaped his
life. A recovered vision describes her as a worn Mehicanian refugee woman living in the Freehold of
Turin. She bought a carved rattle from the street, returned to a poor house in the slums, and gave
it to her malnourished infant son. She later left the child in a basket before the Temple of
Minerva and died after coughing blood in the streets. The Rozenmaiden circle later searched for her
body while preparing magic against the curse that had passed into Justin's line.[^mother]

By the time Justin entered the better-preserved shipboard records, he was blind, though never
helpless. He adapted through a severe training regimen shaped by [Minfilia Diaz](minfilia-diaz.mdx)
and [Bartholomew IV Strandiz](bartholomew-iv-strandiz.mdx), whose military habits taught him to
move, fight, and serve without depending on ordinary sight. Earlier Raibon-era accounts preserve the
beginning of this turn in dramatic form: Minfilia first promised her left eye if Justin learned to
fight in a week. After she learned <Spell name="greater restoration" src="phb" />, the wager shifted
toward a longer period of training and the possibility of replacing the eye by magic. The spell did
not cure Justin, however, because his blindness came from a powerful curse placed on his mother
rather than from an ordinary injury or disease.[^blindness]

Justin's connection to the revived [Hags of Hama](../organizations/hags-of-hama.mdx) came
primarily through [Hedwig Rosenkrantz](hedwig-rosenkrantz.mdx), who had revived the Hags in their
current incarnation. He therefore stood near the Hags through service to Hedwig and the Rozenmaiden
before he became personally tied to Lime and the Gerta Moon students.[^moon]

## Gerta Moon and the Feydark

Justin became involved with Gerta Moon's students after the Rozenmaiden rescued them from the storm
that interrupted their attempted move to Sentimental Island. Lime first became close to him then,
when she felt a responsibility to help him despite his own competence. That protective concern
became friendship and soon developed into romance.

During the rites after the Sentimental Island crisis, Lime accepted the office or burden connected
to the Spirit of the Moon and chose Justin as her protector or token-holder. This choice deepened
Justin's place in the circle around the Hags of Hama, but he was not himself one of the girls marked
by a spirit.[^moon]

Justin then traveled with the group displaced into the Feydark after the
[Parade of the Spirits](../events/parade-of-the-spirits.mdx). Surviving route and crew records
place him among the party in Flyukishilla and later aboard
[the Scarlet](../vehicles/the-scarlet.mdx), where one roster names him as a deckhand. His role in
these accounts is practical rather than ceremonial: he appears as one of the people who keeps
moving, carries what must be carried, and does the unglamorous work needed for the group to
survive.[^feydark]

## Lime Schulze

Justin's relationship with Lime was later complicated by separation, magical obligation, and Lime's
duties as the holder of the Spirit of the Moon. Lime's anxiety over being trapped in the Feywild was
central to the separation. Her connection to the Spirit of the Moon, a power tied to
[Thera](../locations/thera.mdx) rather than the Feywild, made her feel increasingly displaced in
comparison with the others, and she justified the split through Gerta Moon's emphasis on duty before
personal need. During the Vengeful affair, Lime privately told Justin that she was three months
pregnant and that he was the father. The child had been conceived shortly before the group was
pulled into the Feydark, though Lime and Justin only realized it after their return. Unlike the
time-distorted pregnancy associated with [Aika Miura](aika-miura.mdx) and
[Hans Kohler](hans-kohler.mdx), Lime's pregnancy was not altered by planar time distortion. Justin
did not pressure her into resuming a romantic relationship, but promised to support Lime and the
child however she wished. Lime later said that they would face the pregnancy together without
assuming that this restored their relationship to what it had been.[^lime]

The same conversation revealed the depth of a bond created when Lime had inadvertently given Justin
a [Hag Token](../items/hag-token.mdx). The token provided a deep telepathic link through sight,
hearing, feelings, and sensations, letting Justin see through her eyes, feel the wind, and sense the
baby's life energy. Justin's own bloodline curse became urgent because Minfilia warned that it might
pass to the child. Ordinary healing,
<Spell name="remove curse" src="phb" />, and <Spell name="greater restoration" src="phb" /> had not
solved the matter, and the two agreed to seek a cure after the Seneran crisis.[^curse]

## Malthrek Operation

Justin accompanied Lime and the allied investigators during the Malthrek phase of the Vengeful
affair. On the mountain approach to [Malthrek](../families/malthrek/index.mdx), he helped or carried
Lime during difficult descents. At one point he lost his balance while carrying her, spent ki to
recover, spun through the movement, and landed safely. He also offered ship rations and Seneran
cheese bread when Lime became hungry along the route.[^malthrek]

Within the castle operation, Justin worked effectively under cover. He disguised himself as a
laundry boy, remained with Lime near the kitchens when the party split, and later handled a captive
through the cramped cellar and hidden-route passages below the castle. When Lime used
<Spell name="grasping vine" src="phb" /> to help the climb, Justin managed the ascent while still
controlling the captive. At the hideout, he was already with Lime when the others arrived, and he
later dragged an unstable restrained man into Emily Hazeldine's teleportation circle.

Justin also assisted during attempts to treat parasite victims. After celestial blood was poured
into a man's mouth, Justin massaged the man's throat to make him swallow. The violent healing that
followed helped eject a parasite, placing Justin among those present for one of the clearest
demonstrations that the Reformist crisis had become an aberrant infestation as well as a political
and religious conspiracy.[^parasite]

    state that Hedwig wanted to take Justin from Jean-Paul's ship.

[^mother]: Raw Sea Hags transcription notes describe Justin's rattle, his mother as a Mehicanian
    refugee in the Freehold of Turin, his abandonment at the Temple of Minerva, and later efforts to
    find his mother's body.

[^blindness]: Later accounts identify Justin as blind, describe the condition as curse-related,
    and connect his competence to training under Minfilia and Strandiz. Raibon accounts preserve
    the initial left-eye wager, the later training arrangement after Minfilia learned greater
    restoration, and the discovery that the magic did not cure Justin because the curse was rooted
    in his mother.

[^moon]: Lime's own account identifies Justin as the protector or holder she chose when accepting
    the moon-linked office after the Parade of Spirits, while the current Hags of Hama trace their
    revival primarily through Hedwig Rosenkrantz.

[^feydark]: Escape-from-the-Feydark notes place Justin in the displaced company and later list him
    among the Scarlet's deckhands.

[^lime]: Vengeful accounts record Lime's pregnancy disclosure, Justin's response, and their decision
    to face the pregnancy without treating it as a simple restoration of their relationship.

[^curse]: The same discussion records the shared sensory bond, the Hag Token explanation, and
    concern that Justin's bloodline curse might pass to the child. Minfilia jokingly called this
    kind of bond the "69 connection" because Somme and Hedwig had used an earlier version for
    intimate long-distance contact.

[^malthrek]: The Malthrek approach notes repeatedly place Justin assisting Lime, carrying her
    through risky terrain, and providing food from ship rations.

[^parasite]: Later Malthrek notes place Justin with the captive, the teleportation party, and the
    celestial-blood parasite treatment.
