**Iris**, also known as **Fai Vaikhari**, is a face stealer associated with the
[Revivalist Church of the Divine Masochist](../religions/divine-masochism/revivalist/index.mdx).
She was trained as a spy and assassin by agents opposed to the
[Reformist Church of the Divine Masochist](../religions/divine-masochism/reformist/index.mdx).

During the events around the [Death of a God](../notes/death-of-a-god/session-01.mdx),
Iris assumed the identity of Fai Vaikhari, a young Ansa connected to the Kolhan
prince's retinue. Accounts portray the assumed identity as unusually important
to her, reshaping her outward manner into someone more cheerful, present, and
friendly than the withdrawn agent she had been trained to become.

Iris later stole [Yanara](yanara.mdx)'s face and identity, but had too little
time to fully learn the original woman's habits. Her version of Yanara became
more cheerful and assertive, resembling the person the original Yanara had
wanted to become rather than an exact imitation.
