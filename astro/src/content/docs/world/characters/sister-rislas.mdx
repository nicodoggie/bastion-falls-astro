**Adelaide Schulze**, better known as **Sister Rislas**, was a religious sister associated with the
[Congregation of the Angel of Civilization](../organizations/the-congregation-of-the-angel-of-civilization.mdx)
and the Reformist operation around the [Travertines](../locations/travertines.mdx). She was
connected to [Father Charles Renaldi](father-charles-renaldi.mdx), the compromised clergy invited by
Duchess [Antoinette Malthrek](antoinette-malthrek.mdx), and the Schulze branch used by the local
Reformists to draw [Lime Schulze](lime-schulze.mdx) into their confidence.[^travertine-chapel]

Adelaide was her legal identity, while Rislas was the name she took as she became a Reformist
agent. This dual identity made her one of the more direct links between the Travertine clergy, the
Reformist hierarchy, and Lime's investigation into the fate of her parents, Abraham and Juliette
Schulze. Lime was not Adelaide's daughter, but Adelaide and Germaine Schulze recognized her as a
Schulze relative and continued the fiction that she was their child when it suited the Reformist
operation.

## Malter Valley

Rislas appears in the same Malter Valley passage as Renaldi. [Angel Rannek](angel-rannek.mdx), her
mother [Maria Rannek](maria-rannek.mdx), and her sister
[Gwyniviere Rannek](gwyniviere-rannek.mdx) stayed at the
[Battlement Inn](../locations/battlement-inn.mdx) while on the way to the Travertines for
[Luke Malthrek](luke-malthrek.mdx) and [Pallia Travertine](pallia-travertine.mdx)'s wedding.
Renaldi, Rislas, and other members of the Congregation of the Angel of Civilization were present
during that stay.

Rislas showed unusual interest in Angel and brought her to the ruins attached to the inn, where
there was a stained-glass image of the Green Lord. Her party's presence at the Battlement Inn was
not incidental: Rislas knew the ruins were significant, though she and her associates could not
see the shard hidden there themselves. She may have suspected that Angel could perceive what they
could not. At the time, Rislas did not know Angel's celestial nature, which was veiled by an amulet
given to Angel by [Kelthyr Kulok](kelthyr-kulok.mdx), but she appears to have felt that there was
something unusual about her. Angel later returned to the ruins with [Sir Brastok](hilis-brastok.mdx)
and found her first shard of the
[Emerald Shield of Spring](../items/emerald-shield-of-spring.mdx). This made Rislas part of the
earliest known chain connecting the false Angel of Civilization clergy, the Green Lord imagery at
the Battlement Inn, and the Reformist interest in the shield shards.[^malter-valley]

## Schulze Family

Adelaide's Reformist name concealed her place in the Schulze-related Reformist network around the
Travertines. Duchess Antoinette brought Lime to the outer-city chapel to meet Germaine and
Adelaide Schulze after presenting them as Lime's supposed parents. Germaine met Lime in the
rectory and opened the way into a pocket dimension, where Adelaide and Renaldi were waiting.
Renaldi identified Adelaide as a high-ranking local Reformist, possibly a bearer of the whip.

The deception depended on Lime's real place in the Schulze family. She was the daughter of Abraham
and Juliette Schulze, not of Germaine and Adelaide. Germaine was connected to Abraham's branch of
the family, and both he and Adelaide recognized enough truth in Lime's claim to continue the
daughter facade. Lime used that recognition to remain inside the meeting, learn about the
Reformists' interest in the Emerald Shield of Spring, and delay their planned move against Angel
Rannek.[^lime-infiltration]

Lime later returned to the same chapel for her Candle Bearer test. Germaine administered the test
and revealed that he still retained affection for Abraham Schulze, prompting Lime to recall her
true parents before her years at Gerta Moon's Boarding School. Rislas was therefore not merely a
clerical accomplice to Renaldi, but part of the family-facing cover through which Lime pursued
answers about Abraham and Juliette.[^candle-bearer]

## Chapel Confrontation

After Pallia Travertine was exposed and Travertine Keep temporarily stabilized, Rislas and Renaldi
remained targets of the allied investigators. Renaldi appeared to be protected by a pocket dimension
connected to the chapel in the outer city, and Rislas became one of the likely keys to reaching
him.

When the party converged on the chapel, Rislas used a beacon of necromantic energy to raise and
summon undead in a wide area around the building, including undead insects, animals, and bodies
from a nearby graveyard. She was eventually incapacitated by Minfilia Diaz's own undead. A vial of
pixie dust and a single pixie wing were found in her pocket, and the investigators used them with
the chapel brazier to open the portal to Renaldi's pocket dimension.

## Aftermath

Rislas was taken alive after the chapel confrontation. The Renaldi sample and later parasite
discoveries made her valuable as a witness to the same network that included Renaldi, Germaine
Schulze, Duchess Antoinette Malthrek, and Pallia Travertine. The final disposition of her
interrogation or captivity remains unrecorded.
