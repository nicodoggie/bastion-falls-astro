**Sister Rislas**, sometimes rendered **Rizlas** in later notes, was a religious sister associated
with the Angel of Civilization and the Reformist operation around the Travertines. She was connected
to [Father Charles Renaldi](father-charles-renaldi.mdx), the compromised clergy invited by Duchess
Antoinette Malthrek, and the chapel network used by the local Reformists.

## Malter Valley

Rislas appears in the same Malter Valley passage as Renaldi. Angel Rannek, her mother Maria, and
her sister Gwyniviere stayed at the [Battlement Inn](../locations/battlement-inn.mdx) while on the
way to the Travertines for Luke Malthrek and Pallia Travertine's wedding. Renaldi, Rislas, and
other members of the Congregation of the Angel of Civilization were present during that stay.

Rislas showed unusual interest in Angel and brought her to the ruins attached to the inn, where
there was a stained-glass image of the Green Lord. Rislas apparently did not notice the significance
of the place, but Angel later returned there with Sir Brastok and found her first shard of the
Emerald Shield of Spring. This made Rislas part of the earliest known chain connecting the false
Angel of Civilization clergy, the Green Lord imagery at the Battlement Inn, and the Reformist
interest in the shield shards.

## Chapel of Humility

After Pallia Travertine was exposed and Travertine Keep temporarily stabilized, Rislas and Renaldi
remained targets of the allied investigators. Renaldi appeared to be protected by a pocket dimension
connected to the chapel in the outer city, and Rislas became one of the likely keys to reaching
him.

When the party converged on the chapel, Rislas used a beacon of necromantic energy to raise and
summon undead in a wide area around the building, including undead insects, animals, and bodies
from a nearby graveyard. She was eventually incapacitated by Minfilia Diaz's own undead. A vial of
pixie dust and a single pixie wing were found in her pocket, and the investigators used them with
the chapel brazier to open the portal to Renaldi's pocket dimension.

## Aftermath

Rislas was taken alive after the chapel confrontation. The Renaldi sample and later parasite
discoveries made her valuable as a witness to the same network that included Renaldi, Germaine and
Adelaide Schulze, Duchess Antoinette Malthrek, and Pallia Travertine. Surviving notes do not yet
record the final disposition of her interrogation or captivity.
