**Rebecca** is a young girl who the crew of the
[Rozenmaiden](../vehicles/rozenmaiden.mdx) found in the
[Feywild](../locations/feywild.mdx). She is afflicted by a mysterious disease
called *Wonderland Syndrome* which causes her to experience vivid hallucinations
about giant cats, card soldiers, and a wondrous kingdom called Wonderland.

Accounts of her abilities describe her as a human warlock with AC 15, 22 maximum
hit points, 30 feet walking speed, and knowledge of Common, Elvish, Halfling,
and Sylvan.
