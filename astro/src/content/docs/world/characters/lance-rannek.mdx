**Lance Rannek** is a Seneran nobleman of [Rannek](../families/rannek.mdx), son of
[Uther Rannek](uther-rannek.mdx) and [Maria Rannek](maria-rannek.mdx), and brother of
[Angel Rannek](angel-rannek.mdx). He appears chiefly in connection with
Angel's return to Rannek and the crisis around the Bishop of Rannek.

## Bishop of Rannek

Angel's messages to her parents state that she and Lance returned to Rannek and headed together to
the Bishop of Rannek. The same Rannek episode involved the rescue of
[Magnus Bramok](magnus-bramok.mdx), the Bishop of Rannek, from a cursed token and the discovery of
Reformist activity connected to Sister Rislas. Lance and Angel entered the bishop's room during the
investigation.[^lance-bishop]

## Travel With Angel

Lance is also associated with the Malterdale leg of Angel's journey. His horse was named Gawain,
and Angel later bought a Malterdale souvenir mug customized for "Lance and Gawain." The same travel
sequence placed Angel, Sir Brastok, and allied retainers under disguise while Reformists sought the
Emerald Shield shard in Angel's possession.[^lance-travel]
