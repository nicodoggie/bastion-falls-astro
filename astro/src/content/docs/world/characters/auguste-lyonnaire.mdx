Auguste Lyonnaire, aka _Le Petit Tigre_, is the captain of the cog
[Souris Marine](../vehicles/souris-marine.mdx). During the
[Poasan-Apgarian Conflict](../events/poasan-apgarian-conflict.mdx), he was the captain of a pirate
ship that regularly brought supplies from the [Parpelissian Coast](../locations/parpelisse.mdx)
through the Seneran blockade, into [Sanery](../locations/sanery.mdx) to augment the resistance
forces within.
