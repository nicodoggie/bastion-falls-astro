**Liana** is a [hamadryad](../species/hamadryad.mdx) adventurer recorded in recent Bastion Falls
accounts.

Within [Rebecca's](rebecca.mdx) Wonderland, Liana is her vassal. Rebecca's fantasy assigns her the
role of spreading Wonderland's seeds; this does not establish an independent magical purpose or
ability for Liana.

Accounts describe Liana as a druid with exceptional dexterity and wisdom. Current records place her
at armor class 14, 16 hit points, and a walking speed of 30 feet. She is known to speak Common,
Druidic, and Halfling.
