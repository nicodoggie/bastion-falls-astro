**Angel Rannek** is an aasimar bard associated with the Vengeful affair. Accounts of her abilities
describe her as a glamour-trained performer with AC 15, 51 maximum hit points, 30 feet walking
speed, darkvision, and command of Celestial, Common, Common Sign Language, Elvish, and Sylvan.

Records connect Angel to [Gwyniviere Rannek](gwyniviere-rannek.mdx) and
[Maria Rannek](maria-rannek.mdx). She is also associated with [Hilis Brastok](hilis-brastok.mdx),
who is recorded as one of her guards.
