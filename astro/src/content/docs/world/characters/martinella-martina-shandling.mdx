_Martinella Shandling_, or more infamously _Martina_, is the proprietress of
_[The Balsawood Boad](../locations/the-narrows.mdx#the-balsawood-boat)_, and one of the
councilmembers of the Smugglers' Guild in [the Narrows](../locations/the-narrows.mdx). Through her
money-lending services, and her partnership with [Margaret](marguerite-margaret-underhand.mdx), she
had muscled out all competition in the prostitution business in the Murmals district.
