_Martinella Shandling_, or more infamously _Martina_, is the proprietress of
_[The Balsawood Boad](/world/locations/the-narrows#the-balsawood-boat)_, and one
of the councilmembers of the Smugglers' Guild in
[the Narrows](/world/locations/the-narrows/). Through her money-lending
services, and her partnership with
[Margaret](/world/characters/marguerite-margaret-underhand/), she had muscled
out all competition in the prostitution business in the Murmals district.
