**Matim** is a graduate student at the
[University of Calamutz](../organizations/university-of-calamutz.mdx),
associated with its Department of Extra-Human Abilities. Records describe him as
a young noble-born scholar whose magical aptitude is most often associated with
draconic sorcery.

Accounts of Matim emphasize a tension between aristocratic upbringing and a
desire for independence. He is described as refusing to place himself above
common folk, while still carrying the expectations and consequences of noble
birth.
