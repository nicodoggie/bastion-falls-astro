**Raistasi Lunar-Tear** is half elf and half nymph, the twin sister of
[Sharyn Lunar-Tear](sharyn-lunar-tear.mdx), and a follower of Oran the Green Lord.
She crafts charms and talismans, favors lavender and forest green,
and enjoys turnip salad and turnip juice.

Raistasi and Sharyn share the alias **Maerwen Lunar-Tear**, also written **Maerwen Lunartear**.

## Captivity and Rescue

Raistasi was among the rescued elven children cared for at
[Rannekford](../locations/rannekford.mdx).
She remained frightened and weakened by captivity, during which her magic had been drained.
Trauma impaired her concentration, and the absence of her stag antler band, her spell focus,
further prevented her from casting.

[Hellion Blanchimontt](hellion-blanchimontt.mdx) noticed her resemblance to Maerwen Lunartear while
she comforted another crying child. He began saying Maerwen's name, cut himself off, and left
without pressing her. His recognition of a resemblance did not establish her identity to him.
