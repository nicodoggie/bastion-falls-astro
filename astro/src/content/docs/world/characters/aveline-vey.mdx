**Aveline Vey** is an occasional contributor to the Encyclopaedia of Thera. Her writing concerns
public institutions, cultural practice, and the arrangements by which authority presents itself as
natural. Her place of residence, education, access to sources, and other affiliations remain
unsettled.

Her first surviving draft is [*On the Civic Usefulness of a Scandal*][civic-usefulness], an essay
on the Apgarian periodical *Legally Bare*.
