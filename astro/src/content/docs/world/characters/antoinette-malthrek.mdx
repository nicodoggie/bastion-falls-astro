**Antoinette Malthrek** is the Duchess of Malthrek and the wife of
[Duke Ellington Malthrek](ellington-malthrek.mdx). She is well-known for her
charity work and her piety to
[Orthodox Divine Masochism](../religions/divine-masochism/orthodox/index.mdx).

## Early Life

Born Antoinette Thralmal, she was the eldest daughter of the Viscount Ludinum
Thralmal with is second wife Deirdre. Her mother was once a novice in the Circle
of the Angel of Nature before she met her father, so Antoinette was raised as a
devout follower of the sect's teachings.
