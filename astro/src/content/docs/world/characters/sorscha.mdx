**Sorscha** is a spy associated with the
[Revivalist Church of the Divine Masochist](../religions/divine-masochism/revivalist/index.mdx) and,
later, the Band of the Hawk. Records describe her as a product of Reformist breeding grounds who was
trained from youth as a valuable covert asset.

Accounts portray Sorscha as socially inexperienced rather than truly emotionless. After traveling in
Ahuria, she appears to have questioned whether she had to remain a tool of the organization that
raised her.
