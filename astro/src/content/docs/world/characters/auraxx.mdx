**Auraxx** is a gold dragon whose defiance during the
[Draco-Giant War](../events/draco-giant-war.mdx) brought the ancestral fey population of
[Senera](../locations/senera/index.mdx) out of the [Feywild](../locations/feywild.mdx). A curse
imprisoned him within an innocent-looking gold canary, and his body was later dismantled and divided
between [Crispin Mallow](crispin-mallow.mdx) and [Stacy Cartwright](stacy-cartwright.mdx).

## The Realm Bridge

As the war between dragons and giants displaced fey communities across the Feywild, someone foresaw
or studied an approaching fey crossing whose destination remained unknown. Auraxx persuaded the
Archfey to send the refugees through this crossing, which became known in Senera as the
[Realm Bridge](../novelty/the-realm-bridge.mdx).

An unnamed superior within the draconic forces ordered Auraxx not to accompany them. Unwilling to
leave the refugees undefended wherever the crossing carried them, he assumed his humanoid form and
entered it with them. The Realm Bridge opened into Senera, where the refugees became the ancestors
of the island's fey communities.

## The Gold Canary

Auraxx's choice to accompany the refugees had been anticipated. A curse prepared for that
contingency imprisoned him within the form of a
<Item name="Gold Canary Figurine of Wondrous Power" src="FTD" />. The trap depended upon his decision
to cross despite his orders; had he remained behind, it would have had no use.

The identity and purpose of the curse-maker remain unknown, as does the exact relationship between
the curse and whoever had studied the approaching crossing. The canary was a prison imposed upon
Auraxx rather than a chrysalis he created to survive the disappearance of magic.

The figurine survived the extraction of Auraxx's body because the curse still binds his soul within
it. Without that remaining prisoner, the damaged canary would be destroyed rather than persist as an
empty shell.

## The Gilded Division

Mallow eventually found a way to invoke the canary's adult-dragon form under controlled conditions.
He beheld Auraxx's true body and held the manifestation long enough to extract it, although the
means by which he controlled the transformation remain unknown. The figurine recorded the extraction
in its own damage: each chipped loss corresponded to a bone removed from Auraxx's manifested body.

Mallow first used Stacy Cartwright as a testbed for the incorporation, turning her attempted
exposure of him into an opportunity for punishment and experimentation. He implanted the least
magically powerful portions of Auraxx into her and observed whether the graft would take. These
peripheral structural tissues included scale-bearing skin, distal forelimb bones and tendons with a
claw root, terminal wing digits and membrane, and pieces of rib, sternum, cartilage, and marrow. The
smaller graft compelled her magic and preserved her through an excruciating transformation while
leaving her conscious inside a body controlled against her will.

After testing the process on Stacy, Mallow melded the majority of Auraxx into himself, creating the
authentic draconic foundation of Crispin's counterfeit Gilded Anatomy.

## Unresolved Identity

Auraxx's physical substance persists across Crispin and Stacy, while his soul remains bound within
the canary. Whether that imprisoned soul remains conscious is unproven. If his awareness survives,
it is unknown whether it is dormant, continuously aware of the figurine's surroundings, or capable
of acting independently when the canary assumes a living form.

A later *Legend Lore* revelation presented parts of Auraxx's history through a voice identified as
his own. The divination's dramatic form did not establish that Auraxx personally spoke through
Stacy or that he remains separately conscious within either host.

Auraxx's body has been too thoroughly dismantled and incorporated into others to be restored or
revived. His soul remains imprisoned within the canary, but releasing it remains possible in
principle. If the figurine is invoked in its giant-canary form, Auraxx may be able to speak through
that body. He would remember at least Mallow's desecration and dismantling of his manifested body,
but the extent of his other memories remains unsettled. Whether he remains conscious while the
figurine is dormant and what would be required to free him also remain unknown.
