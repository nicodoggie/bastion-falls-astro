**Madeline** is the former wife of [Garward Eastonton](garward-eastonton.mdx), the mother of
[Nandor Eastonton](nandor-eastonton.mdx), and the stepmother of
[Nelson Eastonton](nelson-eastonton.mdx). Garward married her after the death of his first wife,
[Maude Eastonton](maude-eastonton.mdx), but the later marriage ended in divorce.

During the Thrantorbury crisis, information reached Garward's family that Madeline was being
abused and enslaved until she became weak. This report prompted
[Minfilia Diaz](minfilia-diaz.mdx) to travel to Thrantorbury. A table reference identifying the
captive as Maude is retconned to Madeline; Maude's earlier death remains unchanged.
