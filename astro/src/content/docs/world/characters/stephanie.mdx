**Stephanie** is a [shifter](../species/shifter.mdx) that can transform into a
cat. She is also a hag of the
[Children of the Coven](../organizations/children-of-the-coven.mdx).

She travelled from the [Feywild](../locations/feywild.mdx) to
[Thera](../locations/thera.mdx) with the crew of the
[Rozenmaiden](../vehicles/rozenmaiden.mdx) to find a way to get into the good
graces of [Baba Yaga](baba-yaga.mdx) again after she had
accidentally caused the destruction of an important item she was tasked with
protecting.

Stephanie studied magic in Scholomance, though she never had any affinity with
Necromancy she uses the school to learn magic nonetheless. She grew up in
Murkendraw along with the fey folks who worships the Archfey Baba Yaga. After
reaching a certain age, she exchanged years of her life for magical artifacts
and knowledge with Baba Yaga. Baba Yaga gifted her a broomstick to commemorate
this exchange as she reduced to a child. She once travelled with a group of
adventurers whom she sacrificed and betrayed in the name of Baba Yaga but a hunt
was enacted against her so she is currently hiding. She plans to find an
otherworldly artifact and return to Baba Yaga one day.
