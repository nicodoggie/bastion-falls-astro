**Gwyniviere Rannek** is a Seneran noblewoman of [Rannek](../families/rannek.mdx), daughter of
[Uther Rannek](uther-rannek.mdx) and [Maria Rannek](maria-rannek.mdx), and sister of
[Angel Rannek](angel-rannek.mdx). During the Vengeful affair, her betrothal to
[Mark Malthrek](mark-malthrek.mdx) drew the Rannek household into the Malthrek and Travertine
crises.

## Family and Betrothal

Gwyniviere traveled with Maria and Angel to attend the wedding of
[Luke Malthrek](luke-malthrek.mdx) and [Pallia Travertine](pallia-travertine.mdx). The Ranneks were
invited because Gwyniviere was betrothed to Mark Malthrek, Luke's brother. Early reports noted that
Mark was absent from the Travertines after being called back to the Maltreks for unclear reasons,
which later became one of the signs that he might have been magically compromised. [^gwyn-betrothal]

When Gwyniviere learned that Mark was in the dungeon, she reacted badly and demanded details along
with Angel. The sensitivity of Mark's condition, and the possibility of enchantment or coercion,
made her betrothal a political and personal concern. The Marchioness of Rannek was later considered
able to rescind the match if Mark's condition made the marriage untenable.[^gwyn-betrothal]

## Artistic Work

Gwyniviere is repeatedly associated with painting and portraiture. She paints portraits of Angel,
and at Travertine Keep she offered to paint Oscar Savoy. She described herself as an artist who
posed subjects and manipulated the surrounding
environment, suggesting a practical and theatrical approach to portrait work rather than a purely
passive one.[^gwyn-art]

Her skill was supported by an unusually precise visual memory. Gwyniviere could remember anything
she saw and reproduce it in paint almost immediately, though longer study allowed her to capture
more details from a scene. This gift also shaped her portraits of Angel. Gwyniviere sometimes
claimed to glimpse wings on her sister, despite Angel having no physically visible wings, a detail
later read as a sign that Gwyniviere could perceive something of Angel's celestial nature.

Gwyniviere was also the person who persuaded Angel to become a model for
[Legally Bare](../organizations/legally-bare.mdx). After Angel adopted the public persona
**Angel of Delight**, Gwyniviere painted the originals used for Angel's pages. Mystine Nightingale
later contacted Gwyniviere about providing regular art for Miya as well, contingent on an upcoming
collaboration between Miya and Angel proving fruitful.

Gwyniviere also helped with wedding arrangements after the Travertine crisis disrupted the planned
ceremony. She enjoyed creative work and was capable of managing local affairs.[^gwyn-art]

## Vengeful Affair

Gwyniviere remained with Maria and Angel through the Travertine crisis. She helped guard Pallia in
the infirmary after Pallia was exposed as a dangerous hag and Reformist agent. She also provided a
personal focus that helped the party teleport to Mark after he was found missing, feverish, and
apparently abandoned. [^gwyn-crisis]

Subsequent planning treated Gwyniviere and Angel as figures who might have personal influence with
local combatants in the Rannek Marches. This placed Gwyniviere among the family members whose
social position could matter if the Rannek crisis broadened beyond the Travertines.[^gwyn-crisis]
