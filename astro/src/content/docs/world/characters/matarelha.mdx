**Matarelha** is a noble-born priest and martial ascetic associated with the
[Death of a God](../notes/death-of-a-god/session-01.mdx). He served as mentor
and priest to Fai Vaikhari during the voyage toward the Isle of the Gods.

Accounts often call him **Mat**. During the voyage, he was attacked by a giant
squid and later became suspicious of the changes in Fai's behavior and magic.
