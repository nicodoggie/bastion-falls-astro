**Sapphire** is an aasimar adventurer recorded in recent Bastion Falls accounts.

Accounts describe Sapphire as a sorcerer and ranger with exceptional agility.
Current records place her at armor class 15, 76 hit points, and a walking speed
of 35 feet. She is known to speak Celestial, Common, Elvish, Mapach, Orc, and
Sylvan, and some accounts associate her with telepathic communication at short
range.
