**Sapphire** is an aasimar adventurer recorded in recent Bastion Falls accounts.

Accounts describe Sapphire as a sorcerer and ranger with exceptional agility. Current records place
her at armor class 15, 76 hit points, and a walking speed of 35 feet. She is known to speak
Celestial, Common, Elvish, Mapach, Orc, and Sylvan, and some accounts associate her with telepathic
communication at short range.

During the confrontation at the Rannek Admiralty facility, Sapphire cast *calm emotions* around the
presiding Marshal and argued that impostors would not steal Marchioness Maria Rannek's signet merely
to surrender themselves voluntarily.

A temporary shipboard roster assigned Sapphire deckhand duties aboard
[the Scarlet](../vehicles/the-scarlet.mdx) after her recent arrival from the Feywild. That
assignment did not establish whether she considered herself a formal member of either the Scarlet
or [Rozenmaiden](../vehicles/rozenmaiden.mdx) crew, or of
[La Camaraderie](../organizations/la-camaraderie.mdx).
