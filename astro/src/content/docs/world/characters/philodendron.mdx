**Philodendron** is a figure recorded in recent Bastion Falls accounts. Some records identify them
with the name **Trajan Dupe** and with a face-stealing identity.

Under the name **Trajan**, Philodendron identifies as a self-trained
[Revivalist](../religions/divine-masochism/revivalist/index.mdx). They received no formal magical
schooling and regard broad magical education as one of Reformism's better doctrines and a point on
which Revivalists agree.

Accounts of Philodendron's abilities describe a gloom-stalking ranger and scout rogue. Current
records place them at 61 hit points and a walking speed of 30 feet.
