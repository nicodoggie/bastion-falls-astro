**Gravy** is a former soldier associated with the events surrounding the
[Death of a God](../notes/death-of-a-god/session-01.mdx). Accounts describe him as a confident
combatant with latent magical power, later associated with wild sorcery.

Records connect Gravy with a small black-purple orb and a fragment of a banner. He is remembered for
a fierce independence and for the defeat suffered by a company to which he once belonged.
