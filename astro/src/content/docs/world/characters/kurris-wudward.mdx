_Kurris Wudward_ is a personal servant of [Henry](henry-surrata-spellvig.mdx) of the
Spellvig branch of the House Surrata. Sold as a slave to the Spellvigs as a young man, he was later
freed by Sonia Surrata-Spellvig prior to the Seneran Great Slave Emancipation in 1235 AI.

## Early Life

Kurris was born in of Senera. As a young man, he was sold as a slave to the Spellvigs of the House
Surrata.
