**Matthew Malthrek** is the youngest known son of
[Duke Ellington Malthrek](ellington-malthrek.mdx) and
[Antoinette Malthrek née Thralmal](antoinette-malthrek.mdx), and a member of the
[Malthrek](../families/malthrek/index.mdx) ducal family. During the Vengeful affair, he was
studying maritime cartography at the [University of Thrantorbury](../locations/thrantorbury.mdx)
while his parents and elder brothers became entangled in the
[Reformist](../religions/divine-masochism/reformist/index.mdx) occupation of
[Castle Malthrek](../locations/the-maltreks.mdx).

Matthew's importance grew after the discovery that his mother and brothers had been targeted by
aberrant parasites. At first, allied investigators were uncertain whether he was safe at
Thrantorbury or whether the conspiracy had already reached him. He later became strategically
important because of questions around Antoinette's old blood bargain and whether any inherited
obligation might have passed to him or another Malthrek son.

## Overview

Matthew was younger than [Luke Malthrek](luke-malthrek.mdx),
[Mark Malthrek](mark-malthrek.mdx), and [John Malthrek](john-malthrek.mdx). His absence from the
immediate Travertine and Castle Malthrek crises initially protected him from the worst events at
home, but it also made his status uncertain. Once Antoinette's parasite was discovered, Luke, Mark,
and John feared that both Duke Ellington and Matthew might also be compromised.

The first priority was Ellington, whose rank and decisions could immediately benefit the
Reformists. Matthew nevertheless remained a live concern, especially once hostile hag and Reformist
interests around the Thralmal pools became clearer.

## University Studies

Matthew studied maritime cartography at the University of Thrantorbury. Later searches of his room
at Castle Malthrek supported this interest: it contained a drafting table, rulers, tools, art
supplies, and a stylized world map marking Senera far to the west, eastern lands, and Bastion as a
deep purple-black circle. Paintings of monsters near Bastion resembled aberrations.

The same room was large, well kept, and little used, consistent with Matthew's residence away at
university. When the party needed a personal focus to reach him, John entered the room under
disguise and recovered Matthew's favorite fine-liner pen.

## Strategic Importance

Matthew's strategic importance increased after Antoinette's cure. Discussions around the
Thralmals suggested that Antoinette's old blood bargain had been broken by celestial blood, but
that some remaining obligation might have passed by blood or next of kin. Matthew was therefore
treated as a possible target of the Faithful hags or other factions hoping to claim power through
the Malthrek line.

[Tiphanie](stephanie.mdx) used <Spell name="sending" src="phb" /> to contact Matthew, drawing on
her connection to one of his brothers. Matthew answered that he was alive, doing homework, and
confused. This answer confirmed that he could still be reached, but it did not remove the risk
that hostile agents might reach him first.

## Extraction

The plan to secure Matthew centered on locating him at Thrantorbury and removing him before the
Reformists or hostile hags could act. After John recovered the fine-liner pen,
[Kelthyr Kulok](kelthyr-kulok.mdx) provided teleportation sigil notes, and
[Minfilia Diaz](minfilia-diaz.mdx) and [Oscar Savoy](oscar-savoy.mdx) teleported to Matthew while
invoking the
[Great Erected One](../religions/great-erected-one.mdx).

Matthew was found reading [Legally Bare](../organizations/legally-bare.mdx). Startled by the
sudden arrival of strangers, he asked whether they were devils, succubi, or summoning him to
another plane. He was told to pack and informed that he was being returned to Antoinette and
Ellington. Matthew remained skeptical, invoked stranger danger, and only agreed to leave after
being given proof from John.

He was later returned to his parents. His recovery secured the youngest Malthrek son and reduced
the risk that a surviving blood obligation, if it existed, could be exploited before the family
understood it.

## Personality

Matthew appears cautious, studious, and somewhat literal-minded. His reaction to Minfilia and
Oscar's arrival suggests a young noble educated enough to recognize supernatural danger and
sensible enough to distrust implausible rescue claims. His room and studies show a serious
interest in cartography, while later notes describe him as a fan of Legally Bare, with access and
memorabilia that he treated with notable seriousness.
