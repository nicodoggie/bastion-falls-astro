**Rima** is a student in the Department of Extra-Human Abilities at the
[University of Calamutz](../organizations/university-of-calamutz.mdx). Accounts describe her as a
shy and timid young woman from Dalmutz, raised in a wealthy but isolated household.

Rima is said to have left home at sixteen, partly from a belief that her absence would relieve her
mother of the burden of raising an unusual child. She is associated with telepathic gifts and other
magical abilities, which she practiced in secret with the help of a butler named Branson.

Despite her sheltered upbringing, Rima is portrayed as curious, sincere, and kind-hearted. Some
records note that her questions can be misunderstood, but that she tends to look for the good in the
people around her.
