**False Crone** is a hag that led a small group of witches within the
[Children of the Coven](../organizations/children-of-the-coven.mdx) that believe that the
[Baba Yaga](baba-yaga.mdx) is not an individual person, but rather a title given to the most
powerful witch in the Feywild. She attempted to seize the "title" for herself, but was soundly
defeated and almost died in the process.

She used the confusion of the [Draco-Giant War](../events/draco-giant-war.mdx) to escape the
Feywild, and brought her followers to [Thera](../locations/thera.mdx), hidden among the refugees
from the war.
