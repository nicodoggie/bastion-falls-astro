**False Crone** is a hag that led a small group of witches within the
[Children of the Coven](../organizations/children-of-the-coven.mdx) that believe that the
[Baba Yaga](baba-yaga.mdx) is not an individual person, but rather a title given to the most
powerful witch in the Feywild. She attempted to seize the "title" for herself, but was soundly
defeated and almost died in the process.

She used the confusion of the [Draco-Giant War](../events/draco-giant-war.mdx) to escape the
Feywild, and brought her followers to [Thera](../locations/thera.mdx), hidden among the refugees
from the war.

## Borrowed Bodies

The Crone currently inhabits an elven body. She can survive the defeat of a vessel by emerging
as a spirit and attempting to escape. Defeating or killing the body does not permanently kill
or capture her: keeping her requires magic that binds her spirit or a hag's Soul Bag.

[Madeline](madeline.mdx) is a prospective vessel, not an established completed possession.
An encounter in her body would be weaker than one in the Crone's current elven vessel. Those
two situations are alternatives, not a predetermined transfer during combat. Neither the
Crone's alignment nor her possessing abilities establish Madeline's allegiance or change her
underlying identity.

## Encounter Statistics

These are provisional homebrew statistics. The **elven vessel is CR 20**; the **Madeline vessel
is CR 15** and is used only if that possession occurs. Personal histories, the means of the
transfer, and other proposed parts of the Ascension project are not settled by these blocks.
Neither form automatically has the Sapphire Brooch of Winter or coven reinforcements.

### Defeat, Rescue, and Capture

The blocks distinguish an occupied vessel's defeat from the Crone's escape. They give a visible
spirit, a normal initiative window, and an explicit Soul Bag capture attempt. The bag check,
spirit statistics, and host death-saving-throw procedure are encounter-design choices, not a
claim that these exact mechanics were already demonstrated in play.

Bindings retain their own spell requirements. The spirit gains neither an immediate free turn
nor another attacking phase, and ordinary damage cannot secure lasting captivity. Madeline can
be stabilized or healed after the Crone is expelled; defeating the possessor does not make her
an automatic monster casualty. Effects of excess damage and deliberate nonlethal melee attacks
still apply normally.

See [Three Hag Triads](../misc/children-of-the-crone-triads.mdx) for her followers, disruption
tactics, advance clues, and the separate objectives of saving a host and capturing a spirit.
