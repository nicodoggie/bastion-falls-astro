**Goldilocks Westfall**, often called **Goldie**, is a soldier and member of
[The Thirteen](../organizations/the-thirteen.mdx). She was born into a military family, the only
child of Brannon and Cresseida Westfall. Her father was remembered as a field tactician and veteran
of the Poasah-Apgar War.

Accounts describe Goldie as bold, brazen, stubborn, and fiercely protective of the people she holds
dear. She is especially noted for wielding a greatsword nearly as tall as herself, and for her rare
golden hair, yellow eyes, and striking appearance.
