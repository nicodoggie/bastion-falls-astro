**Merlin Rannek** is a Seneran nobleman of [Rannek](../families/rannek.mdx), son of
[Uther Rannek](uther-rannek.mdx) and [Maria Rannek](maria-rannek.mdx), and brother of Lance,
Laudine, Gwyniviere, and [Angel Rannek](angel-rannek.mdx). He is recorded as a professor of
economics at the University of Thrantorbury.

## Family Position

Merlin belongs to the immediate Rannek household but was less directly involved in the Vengeful
affair than Maria, Gwyniviere, Lance, or Angel. His position at Thrantorbury places him outside the
main Travertine wedding party, while his family remained involved in the Rannek Marches, Malthrek,
and Emerald Shield matters.
