**Maria Rannek** is the Marchioness of [Rannek](../families/rannek.mdx), wife of
[Uther Rannek](uther-rannek.mdx), and mother of Lance, Laudine, Gwyniviere, Merlin, and
[Angel Rannek](angel-rannek.mdx). During the Vengeful affair she attended the
Malthrek-Travertine wedding in the company of Angel and [Gwyniviere Rannek](gwyniviere-rannek.mdx).

## Family

Maria is recorded as the principal maternal figure in Angel's correspondence during the Rannek
crisis. When Angel returned to Rannek with [Lance Rannek](lance-rannek.mdx) to assist the Bishop of
Rannek, she sent Maria a message saying that she was safe, missed her, and was heading to the
bishop. Maria's answer expressed relief that Angel had survived and concern for her
safety.[^maria-letters]

## Travertine Crisis

Maria and Gwyniviere continued to the wedding of [Luke Malthrek](luke-malthrek.mdx) and
[Pallia Travertine](pallia-travertine.mdx) while Uther remained occupied in the Rannek Delta. At
Travertine Keep, Maria was lodged in the Rannek wing and was among those briefed on the Reformist
threat after the crisis around Luke and Pallia escalated. She and Gwyniviere were initially told
that Pallia was dead rather than merely unconscious, reflecting the confusion and secrecy around the
Travertine incident.[^maria-travertines]

Maria was also present when Pallia, exposed as a dangerous hag and Reformist agent, was guarded in
the infirmary by Angel, Gwyniviere, and Sir Brastok. Angel's mother was later resting after attacks
by aberrations and religious extremists, placing Maria among the family members directly affected by
the wider Reformist crisis.[^maria-travertines]

## Authority Over Gwyniviere's Betrothal

During discussions of [Mark Malthrek](mark-malthrek.mdx)'s possible magical compromise, the
Marchioness was treated as someone who could rescind Gwyniviere's betrothal if necessary. This made
Maria's household authority relevant not only to family matters, but also to the political
management of the Malthrek-Rannek connection.[^maria-betrothal]
