Renier de Pardieu Simbal, or more commonly known as, Papa Simbal, is a self-styled shaman who
operates a fortune-telling business in the outskirts of
[Le Pharé Phallique](../locations/le-phare-phallique.mdx) in the western portion of
[Raibon Island](../locations/raibon-island.mdx). He was exposed as a charlatan right before he
perished of mysterious circumstances on a ship bound for [Sanery](../locations/sanery.mdx).
