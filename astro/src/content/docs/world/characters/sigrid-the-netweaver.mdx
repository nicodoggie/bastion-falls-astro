**Sigrid the Netweaver** was a [hexblood](../species/hexblood.mdx) associated with Gerta Moon's
Boarding School, which began in [Sanery](../locations/sanery.mdx) and later attempted to relocate
to [Sentimental Island](../locations/sentimental-island.mdx). She was connected to the
[Hags of Hama](../organizations/hags-of-hama.mdx) as the holder of the Spirit of the Spider, one of
the protector spirits tied to the older White Witch traditions of Raibon.

Sigrid was known for weaving colorful nets and baskets. Accounts describe her as quiet, fragile,
often ill, and somewhat clumsy, but also motherly, understanding, and inclined to leave small
crafted gifts for her friends. She preferred knitting, sewing, and weaving old clothes to more
public activity.

## Gerta Moon's Boarding School

Sigrid was one of the girls connected to Gerta Moon's Boarding School, alongside
[Lime Schulze](lime-schulze.mdx), [Luana Victorique Dubreuil](luana-victorique-dubreuil.mdx),
[Snow Skalla](snow-skalla.mdx), [Bianca Bentayga](bianca-bentayga.mdx), [Melki](melki.mdx), and
[Fawn Plaisir](fawn-plaisir.mdx). In the school records, she was associated with the spider spirit,
while other girls were connected to the spirits of the moon, rain, snake, and sea.

The school had been forced out of Sanery after Reformists attempted to seize both the students and
the magical resources guarded by Gerta Moon and Atomina Russell. During the attempted move to
Gerta's new land on Sentimental Island, the students' vessel capsized in a storm. The early
Sentimental Island accounts describe Sigrid as a young woman found washed ashore afterward. A curse
obscured her true identity, and after rescue she spoke little, continuing instead to weave nets and
ropes. She identified Gerta Moon's Boarding School as her place of origin, which helped draw later
attention toward the school and its students.

## Death

Later Vengeful accounts state that Sigrid held the Spirit of the Spider and died knowingly after
her rescue. She was remembered as wanting peace rather than further intervention. Her death left
the status of the spider spirit and its future vessel as part of the unresolved legacy of Gerta
Moon's school and the revived Hags of Hama.
