**Elizabeth Lime Schulze**, usually known as **Lime Schulze**, is a
[hexblood](../species/hexblood.mdx) druid associated with
[Gerta Moon's Boarding School](#gerta-moons-boarding-school),
[La Camaraderie](../organizations/la-camaraderie.mdx), and the
[Hags of Hama](../organizations/hags-of-hama.mdx). She is identified in recent accounts as the
holder of the Spirit of the Moon, a manifestation of lunar power rather than a case of ghostly
possession.

Lime is best known among her companions for her orderly habits, protective temperament, and
practical courage. She likes cleaning, neat rooms, marshmallows, stuffed toys, and her boarding
school uniform. In later Seneran accounts, she became a key infiltrator in the investigations
around the [Travertines](../locations/travertines.mdx), the
[Reformist](../religions/divine-masochism/reformist/index.mdx) movement, and the Malthrek crisis.

## Overview

Lime was left by her parents, Abraham and Juliette Schulze, at Gerta Moon's Boarding School while
it still operated in [Sanery](../locations/sanery.mdx), and did not see them for roughly five
years. Although the school shaped many of her habits, Lime's later actions show that her obedience
and neatness were never passive traits. She often acted as leader among the girls when they went
out, looked after her friends, and was willing to place herself in danger to protect them.

During the Vengeful affair, Lime's Schulze bloodline and history with Gerta Moon's school became
important in Senera. She operated under the name Elizabeth Schulze and exploited the fact that
Germaine and Adelaide Schulze recognized her as a relative, though not as their daughter. This gave
her access to Reformist meetings and let her investigate both her parents' fate and the Reformist
conspiracy around the Malthreks.

## Gerta Moon's Boarding School

Gerta Moon's Boarding School began as an all-female boarding school in Sanery. It served publicly
as a strict school for girls and, more privately, as a refuge for magically gifted children whose
families or communities had become dangerous to them. Its headmistress, Gerta Moon, taught
cleanliness, obedience, and feminine self-command with unusual severity, while also protecting girls
whom she believed were threatened by the
[Reformists](../religions/divine-masochism/reformist/index.mdx) or by hostile social expectations.

A Reformist attempt to seize the girls, together with the magical resources guarded by Gerta and
Atomina Russell, forced the school to abandon its Sanerian site. Gerta's plan was to relocate the
children to land she had bought on [Sentimental Island](../locations/sentimental-island.mdx), where
the school could be remade as, or joined to, a protected orphanage. That relocation became the
central movement of the Gerta's Home affair rather than a simple change of address.

Lime's time at the school explains several of her most consistent traits. She learned to value
cleanliness and order, took responsibility for younger or more vulnerable girls, and kept a strong
attachment to the school's sailor-style uniform. She was part of a cohort that included
[Luana Victorique Dubreuil](luana-victorique-dubreuil.mdx), [Snow Skalla](snow-skalla.mdx),
[Sigrid the Netweaver](sigrid-the-netweaver.mdx), [Bianca Bentayga](bianca-bentayga.mdx),
[Melki](melki.mdx), and [Fawn Plaisir](fawn-plaisir.mdx).

The school also placed Lime within the older traditions of the White Witches. The Gerta Moon
accounts describe her as representing the Spirit of the Moon, while other girls were connected to
the spirits of spider, rain, snake, and sea. Later Vengeful accounts clarify that Lime was the
holder of the moon spirit's power, not a body possessed by a dead spirit.

## The Rozenmaiden and Sentimental Island

Lime first entered wider adventuring records through the failed relocation from Sanery to
Sentimental Island. The vessel carrying the students capsized during a storm, and their rescue by
the [Rozenmaiden](../vehicles/rozenmaiden.mdx) drew Lime into the circles of La Camaraderie and the
revived concerns of the Hags of Hama. The Sentimental Island accounts describe her as one of the
girls recognized from Gerta Moon's Boarding School and as a protective presence among the students.

Her connection to the moon later became politically and magically significant. During the Malthrek
operations, companions warned [Mark Malthrek](mark-malthrek.mdx) to be careful around her because
his lycanthropy had begun at the new moon and might become unstable under full-moon conditions.
Oscar Savoy's use of <Spell name="moonbeam" src="phb" /> was later associated with revealing or
confirming Lime's moon connection.

After the Rozenmaiden saved Sentimental Island from undead attacks, the surviving Gerta Moon
students were brought to the bog for the Parade of Spirits. The ritual revealed that Hedwig
Rosenkrantz could not properly lead the rite because she was not the Moon Witch. Lime was named as
one of the candidates for that office and accepted, choosing [Justin Pasteur](justin-pasteur.mdx)
as her protector or token-holder. The same sequence preceded the group's later displacement into the
Feydark.

## The Vengeful

In the Vengeful affair, Lime participated in the investigation of the Travertines and the Malthrek
household. [Antoinette Malthrek](antoinette-malthrek.mdx) took her to meet Germaine and Adelaide
Schulze at a chapel associated with the Angel of Humility, where
[Father Charles Renaldi](father-charles-renaldi.mdx) identified Adelaide as a high-ranking local
Reformist. Lime maintained the fiction that she was Germaine and Adelaide's daughter, using the
role to keep access to the Reformist meeting after they recognized that she was at least a Schulze
relative.

Lime's infiltration gave the allied investigators several important pieces of information. She
learned that Duchess Antoinette had no formal title in the Reformist hierarchy and that the
Duchess's value came from her access to the [Thralmals](../locations/thralmals.mdx) and her
Malthrek sons. She also heard the Reformists discuss the shards of the Emerald Shield of Spring and
encouraged them to delay direct action against Angel Rannek so that Angel could collect more shards
before they attempted an ambush.

Lime later returned to the chapel for a Candle Bearer test of devotion. The test demanded acts of
arson, domination, magical manipulation, and eventually the killing of an urdefolcin. Lime subverted
the test rather than accepting its moral terms: she bound Germaine with vines, used the situation
to gather information, refused to kill the urdefolcin, and instead offered Germaine to the spirit
of the forest. During this encounter, Germaine's mention of Abraham Schulze caused Lime to recall
her true parents before she was left at Gerta Moon's Boarding School.

After the chapel operation, Lime continued to help track the Thralmal and hag dimensions of the
crisis. She argued against confronting Antoinette too early, preferring to follow the Thralmal pool
lead. At the volcanic pool site, she parleyed with hags connected to the Mother or Crone, identified
herself with the Hags of Hama and the White Witches, refused to make careless oaths, and sought
help against the Children of the Crone. She explained that Antoinette had been corrupted by an
implanted aberrant parasite brought into Senera by the Reformist Church.

During the Castle Malthrek operation, Lime used the practical skills and social discipline that had
defined her since her school years. She operated under cover near the kitchens, served tea properly
in Duke Ellington Malthrek's study, identified a deliberately shrouded fey presence around
[Oren Bell](oren-bell.mdx), and recognized Feywine or Feywine-making signs. When a suspicious
paladin-like figure threatened the group, Lime identified him as a bad paladin and Reformist. She
also used <Spell name="grasping vine" src="phb" /> to help the party move through the hidden route
below the castle.

## Justin Pasteur

Lime had a complicated personal relationship with [Justin Pasteur](justin-pasteur.mdx). Their
separation followed Lime's growing anxiety over being trapped in the
[Feywild](../locations/feywild.mdx). Her connection to the Spirit of the Moon made her feel
increasingly displaced in comparison with the rest of the company, and she justified the split
through Gerta Moon's emphasis on duty before personal need. During the Vengeful affair, she
privately told Justin that she was three months pregnant and that he was the father. The pregnancy
had begun shortly before the group was pulled into the Feydark, but Lime and Justin only recognized
it after their return. Unlike the time-distorted pregnancy associated with
[Aika Miura](aika-miura.mdx) and [Hans Kohler](hans-kohler.mdx), Lime's pregnancy was not altered by
planar time distortion. Justin did not pressure her into resuming a relationship and promised to
support Lime and the child however she wished. Lime later said that they would face the pregnancy
together, without assuming that this restored their relationship.

The same conversation revealed Lime's real name as Elizabeth and showed the depth of the bond
created when she had inadvertently given Justin a [Hag Token](../items/hag-token.mdx). The token
provided a shared connection through sight, hearing, feelings, and sensations; Lime demonstrated it
by letting Justin see through her eyes, feel the wind, and sense the baby's life energy. Justin's
bloodline curse became urgent because Minfilia warned that it might pass to the child, and Lime and
Justin agreed to seek a cure after the Seneran crisis.
