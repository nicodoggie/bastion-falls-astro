## Recorded Vocabulary

**maerwen** — “young girl” or “little girl” in a Seneran elven dialect. A common noun used both
for reference and direct address, particularly by an elder speaking of or to a younger female
as “girl.” The particular dialect has not yet been identified.

[La Papessa](../organizations/ministry-of-the-occult.mdx#known-holders) has been referred to with
this term rather than by a personal name. This usage does not establish her chronological age.

A possible connection to [Early Hick](./hickic/seneran/early-hick/index.mdx) remains unconfirmed;
no specific derivation has yet been established.
