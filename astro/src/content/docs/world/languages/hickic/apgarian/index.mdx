Apgarian Hickic covers the mainland and near-mainland Hickic branch associated with Apgar and its
surrounding regions. It is the likely source area for several conservative Hickic features, but its
exact relationship to Proto-Hick, Proto-Maritime, and later Seneran evidence remains provisional.

This branch is expected to include Estregan, Anasaian, Sanerian or Standard Apgarian, and related
varieties once those languages are reconstructed in more detail.

## Reconstruction Role

Apgarian Hickic is useful for testing whether a form is:

- inherited directly from Proto-Hick;
- a conservative mainland retention later lost in Maritime or Seneran branches;
- a mainland reborrowing into Early Hick or another Seneran variety;
- part of a broader Apgarian sprachbund rather than strictly Hickic inheritance.
