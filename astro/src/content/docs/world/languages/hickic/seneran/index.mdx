Seneran Hickic is the branch of the Hickic language family that settled on Senera and later formed
the substrate layers behind Seneran languages, place names, ritual vocabulary, and maritime
terminology.

Early Hick is currently the best-attested Seneran Hickic stage, but the branch also includes
reconstructed pre-Early-Hick layers and later Middle, Late, and Modern Seneran developments.

## Historical Stages

<SeeAlso href="/languages/hickic/seneran/pre-seneran/">Pre-Seneran Hickic</SeeAlso>

The working reconstruction stage between maritime settlement on Senera and the attested Early Hick
reference dialects.

<SeeAlso href="/languages/hickic/seneran/early-hick/">Early Hick</SeeAlso>

The primary reference grammar and lexicon for pre-Kalassarian Senera.

<SeeAlso href="/languages/hickic/seneran/middle-hick/">Middle Hick</SeeAlso>

The later Seneran Hickic stage associated with post-Early-Hick regional developments.

<SeeAlso href="/languages/hickic/seneran/late-hick/">Late Hick</SeeAlso>

The late Hickic stage before full replacement by Kalassarian and later Seneran language layers.

<SeeAlso href="/languages/hickic/seneran/modern-seneran/">Modern Seneran</SeeAlso>

Modern Seneran preserves Hickic substrate material through names, place names, and inherited
semantic patterns.
