## Overview \{#lexicon-top}

The Middle Hick lexicon records inherited Early Hick vocabulary alongside
Iutlandish maritime, trade, and landscape borrowings from the period of
increased seafaring contact.

For grammatical information and language overview, see [Middle Hick](index.mdx).

## Usage Notes

1. Cross-references are marked with →
1. Etymologies are shown in parentheses
1. Compound derivations are marked with \<
1. Apostrophes (') are used to transcribe glottal stops in phonemic
   transcriptions

## Linking to entries

Each lemma is wrapped in a definition list with `id` equal to its lexicon id
(for example `mhk:hafel` for _hafel_ “ocean”). You can deep-link from anywhere
on the site to a headword on the paginated page that contains it:

`/world/languages/hickic/seneran/middle-hick/lexicon/alpha/1#mhk:hafel`

The same `id` appears on both alphabetical and by-field pages, so either browse
mode works for fragment links.
