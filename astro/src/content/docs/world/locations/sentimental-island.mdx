The Island is small, much smaller than Racos Palma. A small settlement was
established by fishermen whose primary source of work is harvesting pearls and
oysters. They sail to Sanery every Friday to sell the oysters and the pearls and
to also do trades. The houses and buildings in Sentimental Island are painted,
decorated with bright colors, there’s a child-like essence that radiates from
every corner of the island. Blossoming flower pots hang from lamp posts and
hand-painted signs decorate the businesses. Legend has it the houses are painted
in this way so that when sailors came back from the fishing grounds, all they
had to do was look up to find their home shining in the harbor like a beacon.
Sanery is two and half days sail from Sentimental Island. The locals rely on
trade through sea with Raibon Island. The island’s security relies heavily on
its occupants. Shared patrols between capable men and women day and night. The
private pearl and oyster farms have their own guards. People on the island are
very accommodating.

### High tide beach

South of the mansion, there’s a little groove in the coastline where the sea
rises up into the estate, roughly once every season. Following a storm, this
beach often yields rare treasures, beautiful shells and tinted sea glass. The
reef can be restored so the beach can be used for swimming or fishing.

Loot:

- Necklace worth 100 gp
- Glass bottle worth 2 gp
- Gemstone (Carnelian) worth 50 gp.
- If the party spends an hour picking up sea glass, 1-10 they get a bag of
  common sea glass worth 200 gp, 11-20 they get a bag of uncommon sea glass
  worth 500 gp.
- The hound
  - A small cove that used to be a dock. It can dock small boats and ships. It
    has a small cottage for tools and maintenance of boats.
  - Loot: Fishing tools, ropes and nets.
