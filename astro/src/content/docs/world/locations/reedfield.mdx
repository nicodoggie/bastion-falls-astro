**Reedfield** is a territory of the [Rannek Delta](rannek-delta.mdx) held by
[House Risen](../families/risen.mdx).

[Mercer County](mercer-county.mdx), held by House Marcellan, lies to the south.

Its name pairs with [Reedpool](reedpool.mdx), held by Risen's rival, House Malris. The two
families dispute their seniority and each claims the other as a cadet branch. The paired names do
not establish either genealogy. Reedfield's seat and boundaries are not recorded here.
