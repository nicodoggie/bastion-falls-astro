**Queilei**, known in Common as **Marim**, is an elven settlement in the northern portion of the
[Footbowl Basin](footbowl-basin.mdx), at the foot of the Northern
[Maltreks](the-maltreks.mdx). Its inhabitants include elves, nymphs, dryads, and pixies. Its
protected interior contains shops, gardens, woodland homes, and a shrine to Oran the Green Lord.
Elderberry, a druid called the **Oakfather**, leads the settlement.

## Access and Protection

The entrance is known as **Elsewhere**. Protective fey magic reverses directions and confuses
non-elven creatures attempting to enter. Its protections include **Obliviate Rain**, **Oblivion
Moss**, and **Befuddling Mist**.

Some facilities are openly accessible. Foxglove's fruit and
vegetable stand and Rook's game and hunting supplies are not hidden. Non-fey visitors may also use
the campsite, a small clearing encircled by trees. The Mallard's Tavern, Pennyroyal's tea shop,
Fairy Wand, the Green Lord's shrine, and Oran's Herb Garden are reserved for fey.

## Customs and Government

Elderberry lives in a small cottage with Snowdrop. As Oakfather, he is the settlement's leader.

Queilei observes rules of reciprocity and hospitality. A gift from a friend, enemy, or stranger
must be accepted and answered with a gift or service of comparable value, though repayment need
not be immediate. Anyone entering a home is entitled to gracious treatment until their words or
actions show them undeserving of it. Harming a resident is dishonorable.

Oaths and promises made within the settlement have the power to bind their makers in strange ways,
regardless of how seriously the speaker intended them. Local trade uses goods and favors rather
than coins.

### Seasonal Celebrations

- **Spring:** Hatching Day, the Festival of Spring, and the Wild Hunt.
- **Summer:** Midsummer's Night.
- **Autumn:** the Harvest Festival and the Festival of the Spirits.
- **Winter:** the Yuletide Festival.

## Shops and Cultivated Grounds

### Foxglove's Stand and Farm

Basil Foxglove manages the fruit and vegetable stand. The Foxglove family grows seasonal produce
at its farm, including an orchard of **star apples**, a fruit unique to Queilei. Their peak season
is Imbolc, in spring. They may be eaten or made into **star wine**.

### Rook's Game and Hunting Supplies

Sparrow Bird's Nest manages Rook's game and hunting supplies, one of the settlement's openly
accessible businesses.

### The Mallard's Tavern

Talon Bird's Nest manages the fey-only tavern. Its menu lists a glass of **Smell Like Rain** at
two copper pieces and a plate of **Sunrise over the Mountains** at seven copper pieces.

### Pennyroyal's Tea Shop

The dryad Pennyroyal serves tea selected according to the customer's emotions. The shop is
reserved for fey.

### Fairy Wand

[Sharyn Lunar-Tear](../characters/sharyn-lunar-tear.mdx) and
[Raistasi Lunar-Tear](../characters/raistasi-lunar-tear.mdx) manage Fairy Wand, assisted by
Snowdrop. The shop sells potions and charms made and enchanted by Sharyn. The charms take the form
of bracelets, key charms, and pageholders. Fairy Wand is reserved for fey.

### The Green Lord's Shrine and Herb Garden

The Lunar-Tear family tends the Green Lord's shrine, a massive oak tree. An artifact bearing
**Banish Blight** is embedded within it, a gift from a girl who introduced herself as
**La Papessa**. The family also owns **Oran's Herb Garden**. Both sites are reserved for fey.

## Woodlands and Waters

- **Forest of Isolation:** the home of Queilei's dryads.
- **Balam Falls:** a hidden waterfall inhabited by nymphs, with jewelfish in its waters.
- **Heartroot Forest:** a foraging ground.
- **Glade of Echoes:** a hunting and foraging ground.
- **Sparkling Waters:** a natural resource protected by nymphs.
- **Eagle's Nest:** a series of canopy houses concealed in tall trees, home to the Bird's Nest
  family.

The fishing spot provides trout, rainbow trout, freshwater shrimp, and silverling. Rainbow trout
are available throughout the year; seasonal catches include sweetfish in summer, gobi in autumn,
and pike in winter.

### Venn's Hollow

Statues at Venn's Hollow commemorate an elven hero who fought in the
[Draco-Giant War](../events/draco-giant-war.mdx) and protected Queilei in the Green Lord's name.

family includes the following households and members:

- **Robin-run-in-the-grass**, his elven wife **Poppy**, and their daughters **Pansy** and
  **Pepper**.
- **Scaldhead**, a guard, his elven wife **Ivy**, their sons **Snapdragon**, **Fern**, and
  **Yarrow**, and their daughter **Holly**.
- **Bark**, a guard.
- **Starweed**, an herbalist and cleric, Starweed's elven husband **Cypress**, their daughters
  **Maple** and **Peach**, and their son **Mandrake**.
- **Torches**, a scout, with **Charlotte Highbury** and **Dorothy Highbury**.
- **Tarragon**, a merchant and cleric, and **Henna**, a human lover.
- The twins **Sharyn** and **Raistasi**.
- **Hawthorn**, a toddler, and **Chicory**, an infant.

### The Foxglove Family

The Foxglove family includes **Resin**, **Valerian**, **Basil**, **Cypress**, **Thyme**,

and daughter **Feather**. Thyme's household includes Thyme's husband **Sparrow** and sons

### The Bird's Nest Family

The Bird's Nest family includes **Eagle**, **Camellia**, **Violet**, **Hawks**, **Sparrow**,
**Talon**, and **Corven**.

### River Nymphs

Queilei's river nymphs include **Vanira**, **Dilara**, **Shell**, **Inara**, **Kitra**, **Cherie**,

### Dryads

The dryads include **Bride-of-the-Sun**, **Burning Bush**, **Pennyroyal**, **Catmint**,
**Tigerlily**, and **Bliss**.

### Pixies

The pixies include **Dagger Flower**, **Mimi**, **Upelkuchen**, and **Fright**. Elderberry and
Snowdrop also live in the settlement.
