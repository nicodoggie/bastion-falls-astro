**Reedpool** is a territory of the [Rannek Delta](rannek-delta.mdx) held by
[House Malris](../families/malris.mdx).

Its name pairs with [Reedfield](reedfield.mdx), held by Malris's rival, House Risen. The two
families dispute their seniority and each claims the other as a cadet branch. The paired names do
not establish either genealogy. Reedpool's seat and boundaries are not recorded here.
