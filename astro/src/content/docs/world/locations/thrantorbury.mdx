_Thrantorbury_ is the capital city of the [Kingdom of Senera](senera/index.mdx) and the seat of the
[Royal House of Dragonheart](../families/dragonheart.mdx). The city occupies a magically carved
shelf on the lower flanks of [Mt Thraltor](mt-thraltor.mdx), beneath the separate, higher shelf
of [Castle Dragonheart](castle-dragonheart.mdx).

## Mountain Shelves

Mt Thraltor has three peaks, regarded as a single mountain within the
[Ranterg Mountains](ranterg-mountains.mdx). Its highest summit rises behind Castle Dragonheart;
the castle does not occupy the mountain's summit.

The two inhabited shelves have different origins. The lower plateau, where Thrantorbury stands,
was magically carved by the local _mathrantorgralok_. The higher castle shelf was carved by the
[Kalassarians](../organizations/kalassarian-empire.mdx). The town and university district are near
the royal seat, but neither shares its terrace.

## The Crown Road and Town Defenses

The **Crown Road** approaches from below, climbing the exposed mountainside in broad switchbacks
before entering Thrantorbury through the **Crown Gate**. It connects the mountain capital with
[Surrey-on-the-Brams](surrey-on-the-brams.mdx).

Retaining walls support the road where needed. These are distinct from the raised defensive
walls and crenellated wall-walks commanding the final ascent and flanking the gate. The defenses
concentrate on the accessible approach rather than enclosing the entire plateau in a continuous
curtain wall; the cliffs themselves provide much of its protection.

Magical shaping may have been used in the supporting works, but their construction is not tied
to a particular spell. A wall made through magic need not depend on a continuing enchantment
to hold up the road.

### Royal Guard Presence

Town buildings gather near the approach to the castle tunnel. A controlled buffer separates
civilian streets from its mouth, with Royal Guard facilities inspecting castle-bound goods,
services, and visitors. Passing through the town gate does not grant access to the royal seat.

An **auxiliary Royal Guard military complex**, including a lookout tower, stands on the
Thrantorbury plateau. It is separate both from the tunnel's inspection facilities and from the
main Royal Guard complex within Castle Dragonheart.

## Castle Dragonheart

Castle Dragonheart occupies the higher shelf beneath Thraltor's greatest summit. Its site
overlooks Thrantorbury and the approaches through the mountains. The royal seat is distinct
from the Haven on Senera's western coast.

### Imperial Residence and Royal Seat

The Kalassarian predecessor of the present royal complex housed the governor of **Seneria**,
the imperial name for Senera, and was intended to accommodate the Emperor should he visit.
It was a palatial seat of government as well as a defensible residence, not simply a fortress
made habitable afterward.

Its architecture favors substantial wings, smaller internal courts, sheltered galleries,
and protected entrances rather than an uninterrupted skyline of battlements. The established
royal complex includes the **High Keep**, associated with the Maidenfeld period and housing the
Dragon Throne Room; the **Crown Cathedral**, built in the early Dragonheart reign and connected
to the keep by a covered bridge; the **Court Quarter**, with chambers, archives, treasury vaults,
and administrative offices; and the **Royal Ward**, with residences, diplomatic quarters,
guard barracks, and service buildings.

Kalassarian engineering allowed for magical spectacle and magical fallbacks alongside durable
physical works. The defensive problem included teleporting intruders, magically flying casters,
and mounted air cavalry: a high cliff or tall perimeter wall was not sufficient by itself.
Sheltered circulation and internal divisions therefore mattered alongside guards and magical
protection. The particular wards and their workings remain unspecified.

### The Castle Tunnel

A large arch in the mountain face opens onto the magically created tunnel that is the primary
route from Thrantorbury toward Castle Dragonheart. This passage is distinct from the exposed
road bringing travelers up to the town.

The tunnel is neither dank nor stifling. Numerous small air passages cut by the **Kalassarian
magical engineering corps** provide ventilation through ordinary airflow. Non-magical lamps can
burn steadily inside without their flames dimming for lack of air. Magic made the excavation
possible; the ventilation does not require a continuously active enchantment.

The route through the mountain, its upper exit, and the positions of its ventilation passages
are not shown in the elevation.

## University of Thrantorbury

The city is home to the prestigious
[University of Thrantorbury](../organizations/university-of-thrantorbury.mdx), particularly known
for cartography. Its campus plan, colleges, and student facilities are described in the university
article.

The college-town area occupies part of the lower plateau, separate from the castle grounds.
Within the university, the Grounds descend toward a **lagoon** used for maritime practical
studies. The College of Maritime Studies stands near the lagoon and the College of Evocation;
the latter also uses the lower grounds for practice away from the academic core.
