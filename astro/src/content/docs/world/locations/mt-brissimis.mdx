**Mt Brissimis** is a mountain in the northern portion of the Western Maltreks. It is where the
protected pools of the [Thralmals](thralmals.mdx) are located, a politically and spiritually
important site tied to local hag bargains and the Thralmal family.

The mountain lies within the volcanic and geothermal landscape where hot springs, geysers, and
spirit-haunted pools shape local travel and ritual. The Thralmals pools are protected under an old
blood-bound bargain involving the hags and the Malthrek-Thralmal line, though the exact terms and
current obligations of that protection remain contested.
