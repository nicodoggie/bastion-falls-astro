**Malterford** is a town in the
[Western Lowlands](/world/locations/western-lowlands/) of
[Senera](/world/locations/senera/).
It is a trade hub for the region,
as it connects the mining operations from
[the Maltreks](/world/locations/the-maltreks/) with the deeper portions of the
upper arm of the [Malter River](/world/locations/malter-river/).
