**Malterford** is a town in the
[Western Lowlands](western-lowlands.mdx) of
[Senera](senera/index.mdx).
It is a trade hub for the region,
as it connects the mining operations from
[the Maltreks](the-maltreks.mdx) with the deeper portions of the
upper arm of the [Malter River](malter-river.mdx).
