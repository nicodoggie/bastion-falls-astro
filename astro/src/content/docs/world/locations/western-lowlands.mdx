The _Western Lowlands_ is a major region in the western part of [Senera](senera/index.mdx), and is
nominally administered as part of the Seneran Royal Family's domain, but separately from
[Central Senera](senera/index.mdx#central-highlands-ranterg-range).

## History

### Pre-Kalassarian Period (1200-110 AI)

The [Hicks of the Maasamgralok tribe](../ethnicities/hick.mdx#maasamgralok) were scattered
throughout the hilly landscape of the region. They ranged as far north as present-day
[Malterdale](malterdale.mdx), as far south as the [Rannek Karsts](rannek-marches.mdx#rannek-karsts),
and as far east as the feet of [Thraltorgral](mt-thraltor.mdx).

The region was once A large part of the region was once the capital region of Senera, as it included
the lands of the former royal house of [Maidenfeld](../families/maidenfeld.mdx). After the events of
the [War of Thyme and Fire](../events/war-of-thyme-and-fire.mdx), the region was formally named the
"Western Lowlands" by the Dragonhearts, and its administration was transferred to the Marquessate of
Rannek.

## Geography

The majority of one of the two major watersheds of Senera, the Western Lowlands is fed by the
[Malter River](malter-river.mdx), which originates in the [Maltreks](the-maltreks.mdx) and flows
into underground rivers carved through the rich marine limestone that underpins the hilly landscape.
