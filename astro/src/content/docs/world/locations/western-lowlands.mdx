The _Western Lowlands_ is a major region in the western part of
[Senera](/world/locations/senera), and is administered as a part of the
[Marquessate of Rannek](/world/locations/rannek). It is a region of rolling
hills and valleys, with its system of underground rivers and karsts as a source
of fresh water for surrounding regions, as well as its own burgeoning
agricultural economy.

# History

## Pre-Kalassarian Period (1200-110 AI)

The [Hicks of the Maasamgralok tribe](/world/ethnicities/hick/#maasamgralok)
were scattered throughout the hilly landscape of the region. They ranged as far
north as present-day [Malterdale](/world/locations/malterdale/), as far south as
the [Rannek Karsts](/world/locations/rannek-karsts/), and as far east as the
feet of [Thraltorgral](/world/locations/mt-thraltor/).

### Administration by the Marquessate of Rannek

### Dissolution of the Earldom of the Lowlands and the Creation of the Viscounty of Malterdale

A large part of the region was once the capital region of Senera, as it included
the lands of the former royal house of
[Maidenfeld](/world/families/maidenfeld/). After the events of the
[War of Thyme and Fire](/world/events/war-of-thyme-and-fire/), the region was
formally named the "Western Lowlands" by the Dragonhearts, and its
administration was transferred to the Marquessate of Rannek.

# Geography

The majority of one of the two major watersheds of Senera, the Western Lowlands
is fed by the [Malter River](/world/locations/malter-river), which originates in
the [Maltreks](/world/locations/maltreks) and flows into underground rivers
carved through the rich marine limestone that underpins the hilly landscape.
