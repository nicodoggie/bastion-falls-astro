The _Western Lowlands_ is a major region in the western part of
[Senera](/world/locations/senera), and is nominally administered as part of the
Seneran Royal Family's domain, but separately from
[Central Senera](/world/locations/senera/#central-highlands-ranterg-range).

## History

### Pre-Kalassarian Period (1200-110 AI)

The [Hicks of the Maasamgralok tribe](/world/ethnicities/hick/#maasamgralok)
were scattered throughout the hilly landscape of the region.
They ranged as far north
as present-day [Malterdale](/world/locations/malterdale/),
as far south as the [Rannek Karsts](/world/locations/rannek-karsts/),
and as far east as the feet of [Thraltorgral](/world/locations/mt-thraltor/).

formally named the "Western Lowlands" by the Dragonhearts, and its
administration was transferred to the Marquessate of Rannek.

## Geography

The majority of one of the two major watersheds of Senera,
the Western Lowlands is fed by the
[Malter River](/world/locations/malter-river), which originates in the
[Maltreks](/world/locations/maltreks) and flows into underground rivers carved
through the rich marine limestone that underpins the hilly landscape.
