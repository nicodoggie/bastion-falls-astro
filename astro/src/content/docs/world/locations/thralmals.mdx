**The Thralmals** is a barony in [the Maltreks](./the-maltreks.mdx) located in the northern regions
of its western arm. Its protected pools are at [Mt Brissimis](mt-brissimis.mdx), where
old hag bargains, local healing traditions, and Malthrek-Thralmal family obligations intersect.
