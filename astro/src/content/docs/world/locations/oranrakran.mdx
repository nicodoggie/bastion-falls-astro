**Oranrakran**, known in Common as **Oran's Piss**, is a region of the northern
[Footbowl Basin](footbowl-basin.mdx) around [Queilei](queilei.mdx), also called Marim.
Its twin rivers turn golden under certain conditions, giving the region its name.

## Geography

Oranrakran lies beneath the Northern [Malthreks](the-maltreks.mdx). Its northern
boundary follows that arm of the mountains. To the east, an unnamed pass between the
Maltreks and the [Rantergs](ranterg-mountains.mdx) leads toward Skelwick.

The Western Maltreks provide its western boundary. To the south, Oranrakran opens
into the rest of the Footbowl Basin; it is part of that basin, not a separate basin
alongside it.

### Woodlands and Settlement

Queilei lies at the foot of the northern mountains, amid the region's woodlands and
upper river courses. The settlement's Common name, Marim, appears on the vicinity map.

## Name and Waters

The name combines **Oran** with **rakran**, the decorous Hickic term for urine,
historically derived from an expression meaning "yellow stream". The blunt Common
rendering preserves the bodily reference in the regional name.

The rivers' golden appearance is intermittent. Its cause and the conditions under
which it occurs remain undescribed.
