**Jing Shen Jiang** is a city in the Feydark founded by changelings. It has become a haven where
shapeshifters of all kinds can live in peace.

The entrance to Jing Shen Jiang is concealed at **Holt Settlement**, a separate settlement in
the Feydark that once housed renegade humans. No humans currently live there: its residents are
changelings and doppelgangers posing as humans and elves to conceal Jing Shen Jiang's existence.
