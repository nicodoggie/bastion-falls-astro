**Rannekford** is the capital of the [Rannek Marches](rannek-marches.mdx) in western
[Senera](senera/index.mdx) and the seat of [House Rannek](../families/rannek.mdx). It is a river
city with a working barge port, guarded warehouses, and normally well-maintained streets.

## Geography and Connections

Rannekford was founded around two streams of the same river. Upstream, the river emerges from an
underground system at Bogral Airur, where there is no settlement at the headwaters. Rannekford
serves as a starting point for river trade, rather than a stop on an established route from a
headwater town.

A nearly straight road was built specifically to connect the capital with the naval base at
[Port Mibram](port-mibram.mdx).

## River Port

River barges gather beside the city's warehouse district. Guards watch the warehouses, which
include private storage as well as the facilities of the river port.
[Lance Rannek](../characters/lance-rannek.mdx) maintains a concealed storage room in this district.

## Institutions and Landmarks

Castle Rannek is the seat of the ruling household. Magistrates' offices form part of the city's
administrative establishment. The Spring Dancer Inn stands near the castle.

Rannek Cathedral is associated with [Bishop Magnus Bramok](../characters/magnus-bramok.mdx) and
Head Deacon [Rufus Kethmaris](../characters/rufus-kethmaris.mdx).

The Lord's Judgment, in southern Rannekford, is an inn and mercenary gathering place.

## Recent Unrest

Reformist infiltration and [Crispin Mallow's](../characters/crispin-mallow.mdx) activities brought
impostors and controlled combatants into the city, compromising officials and threatening the
ruling household. Castle Rannek was besieged, but its defenders survived the assault.

The unrest damaged streets along the route to the river port. This damage was a recent disruption
to the city's usual maintenance, not its ordinary condition.
