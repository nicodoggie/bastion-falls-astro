**Maiden's Bay** is a protected harbor in Western [Senera](senera/index.mdx) that shelters
[Port Mibram](port-mibram.mdx) and the [Seneran Royal Navy](../organizations/seneran-royal-navy.mdx)
Western Command. It facilitates ruutine trade from the [Maidenfeld](../families/maidenfeld.mdx)
spice trade network, as well as local maritime trade between the
[Rannek Marches](rannek-marches.mdx) and the [Western Lowlands](western-lowlands.mdx) via
[Port Sera-by-Malterdale](port-sera-by-malterdale.mdx).

## Hydrology

Maiden's Bay is a naturally shallow embayment opening westward onto the
[Havmals Ocean](havmals-ocean.mdx). Its waters are brackish, with tidal exchange from the ocean
mixing with freshwater from the Rannek coastal plain. Some of that freshwater enters through the
[Rannek Delta](rannek-delta.mdx), but much of the bay's steady inflow comes less visibly from the
Malter River system, whose waters pass underground through the Western Lowlands' limestone karsts
before re-emerging through springs, seeps, and submerged vents around the inner bay.

This karst-fed inflow gives the bay a more reliable freshwater contribution than its surface rivers
would suggest, but it also leaves the harbor prone to shoaling. Fine sediment from the Rannek
lowlands and mineral deposits carried through the karst system settle readily in the bay's sheltered
waters, especially near the approaches to [Port Mibram](port-mibram.mdx).

The deep-water harbor used by Port Mibram and the
[Seneran Royal Navy](../organizations/seneran-royal-navy.mdx) Western Command is therefore partly
artificial. A stone mole protects the main anchorage from ocean swell, while dredged channels and
basins keep the naval dockyards and merchant quays open to heavier vessels.

## History

For most of recorded history, Maiden's Bay saw little importance aside from local trade between
coastal settlements. Its shallow waters made it impractical for larger ships, and its location
away from the [Beteran](betera/index.mdx) mainland diminished its usefulness for maritime trade at
the time.

### Maiden's Belt

During the reign of the Maidenfelds, a stone
[mole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mole_(architecture)) was built to control tidal effects and
silt from the open ocean. This allowed for more drastic dredging and larger harbors to be built. It
was at this time that Port Mibram was founded under the supervision of the Ranneks, and was made to
anchor larger vessels. [Maiden's Port](maidens-port.mdx) was expanded as well, but the debris and
silt from the Rannek Delta redeposited onto the bed too often, which still limited its usefulness.
