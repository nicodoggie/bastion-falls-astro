**Ranlis County** is a Seneran county in [The Maltreks](the-maltreks.mdx), held by the
[Ranlis](../families/ranlis.mdx) household. Its capital and chief known settlement is
[Faltir](faltir.mdx), a walled salt town whose mines and trade form the county's practical center of
power.

The county's known public life is dominated by Faltir's salt economy, the authority of
[Count Godwin Ranlis](../characters/godwin-ranlis.mdx), and the influence of
[Comtesse Aveline Ranlis](../characters/aveline-ranlis.mdx) during Godwin's absences. Further
settlements, boundaries, and older legal history remain sparsely recorded.
