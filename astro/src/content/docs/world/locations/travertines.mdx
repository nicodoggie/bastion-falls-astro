_The Travertines_ are the headwaters of the Malter River,
and are called such due to the nearby travertine formations atop large basalt
fissure formations where the river proper emerges.
Though geographically, the Travertines are part of the Maltrek mountain range,
the region is administered
as part of the [Western Lowlands](/world/locations/western-lowlands).

Traditionally the region was named _Asatogar_, from early hickic, it was
translated literally into _Tiburtinus_ by the Kalassarians during its conquest
of [Senera](/world/locations/senera/). When the Maidenfelds held power following
the
[Fall of the Western Kalassarian Empire](/world/events/fall-of-the-western-kalassarian-empire),
the region was renamed to its current name, and was administered as part of the
Maidenfeld's core territory in the Western Lowlands as a check on the
[Duke Malthrek](/world/families/malthrek).

## Geography

The travertine formations that give rise to its name, are a series of delicate,
striated formations of limestone,
which are a result of the water seeping through the basalt
and depositing calcium carbonate as it flows over the surface.
