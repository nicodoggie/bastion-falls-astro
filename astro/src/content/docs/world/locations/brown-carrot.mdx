Brown Carrot Village is a small-sized agricultural, timber production, and
equestrian settlement in the [Acquan Region](../locations/acqua.mdx) of
[Poasah](../locations/poasah.mdx), in the outskirts of
[Acqua Termina](../locations/acqua-termina.mdx).

The village itself, and the lands that surround it are the possession of the
[Caxton Baronetcy](../families/caxton/index.mdx) led by
[Lord Preston Caxton](../characters/preston-caxton.mdx).

One of the most notable features of the village, is that it is the base of
operations of the
[Hardpebble Trading Company](../organizations/hardpebble-trading-company.mdx),
owned and operated by the eponymous
[Hardpebble](../families/hardpebble/index.mdx) family.
