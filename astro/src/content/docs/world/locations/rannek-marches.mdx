_The Rannek Marches_ are a rich agricultural region in the western-most end of Senera. It is famous
for the massive flood control systems of weirs and dams that have been built over the centuries to
keep tidal and seasonal flooding at bay. The reclaimed land is some of the most fertile in the
entire island. It used to be the administrative center of the Kingdom of Senera after the collapse
of the Kalassarian Empire, when the [Maidenfelds](../families/maidenfeld.mdx) held the Crown. The
region has been reorganized as the Rannek Marches by the
[Dragonheart Dynasty](../families/dragonheart.mdx) after the
[War of Thyme and Fire](../events/war-of-thyme-and-fire.mdx).

### Rannek Delta

The Rannek Delta is crossed by distributary channels, canals, drainage works, weirs, sluices, and
embankments. Its roads commonly follow raised levees and the available bridges, ferries, and
controlled crossings rather than direct lines. Maintained flood-control works can provide good
carriage roads in ordinary conditions, while a damaged weir or lost crossing can impose long detours
or halt carriage travel entirely.

#### Maiden's Field

Maiden's Field is a large managed agricultural territory measuring approximately 27 miles east to
west and 27 miles north to south. It covers approximately 729 square miles and spans approximately
38 miles from corner to corner. [Maiden's Port](maidens-port.mdx) lies immediately east of its
northern lands.

The territory's unusual extent reflects the [Maidenfelds'](../families/maidenfeld.mdx) former royal
position and continuing importance to the herb and spice trade. The name refers to a jurisdiction
containing settlements, subordinate estates, wetlands, flood basins, and water-control
infrastructure rather than to one uninterrupted private manor.
