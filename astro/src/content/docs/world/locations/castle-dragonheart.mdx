**Castle Dragonheart** is the palatial royal seat of the
[Kingdom of Senera](senera/index.mdx) and its reigning
[House Dragonheart](../families/dragonheart.mdx). It stands above
[Thrantorbury](thrantorbury.mdx) on a magically carved mountain shelf. Its site previously
served as the residence of the Kalassarian governor of Seneria and was intended to receive
the Emperor as well as accommodate the provincial government.

## Site and Approaches

The castle occupies the higher of two inhabited shelves on
[Mt Thraltor](mt-thraltor.mdx). The mountain's highest summit rises behind the royal seat;
the castle itself does not stand at the summit. The Kalassarians carved its terrace, while
the local _mathrantorgralok_ carved the separate, lower plateau occupied by the town and
university district.

The **Crown Road** climbs the exposed mountainside from the direction of
[Surrey-on-the-Brams](surrey-on-the-brams.mdx), entering Thrantorbury through the **Crown Gate**.
The principal onward approach to the castle is a tunnel through the mountain, not a
continuation of that exposed ascent. The Crown Gate and the castle-access checkpoint are
separate thresholds.

The royal seat is distinct from the **Haven** on Senera's western coast.

## History

### Kalassarian Residence

Under the [Kalassarian Empire](../organizations/kalassarian-empire.mdx), Senera was known as
**Seneria**. The predecessor of the present castle complex housed its governor and provided
for the Emperor's accommodation should he visit. Imperial residence, provincial administration,
and defense were therefore part of its purpose, rather than comforts added to a purely
military fortress.

The Kalassarian design combined magical spectacle and magical fallbacks with durable physical
engineering. Its tunnel and carved terrace express this combination: magic made substantial
works possible, while their ordinary operation need not depend entirely on an uninterrupted
enchantment.

### The Royal Complex

The royal complex includes structures associated with both the
[Maidenfeld](../families/maidenfeld.mdx) and Dragonheart periods. The High Keep predates
Dragonheart rule, while the Crown Cathedral belongs to the dynasty's early reign. These
later royal elements form part of a site whose use as a seat of government reaches back to
the imperial residence.

## Principal Divisions

### High Keep

The **High Keep** is the ancient stone fortress associated with the Maidenfeld period. It
houses the **Dragon Throne Room** and remains a distinct part of the larger palatial complex.

### Crown Cathedral

The **Crown Cathedral** was built during the early Dragonheart reign in the architectural
tradition of the
[Orthodox Church of the Divine Masochist](../organizations/orthodox-church-of-the-divine-masochist.mdx).
A covered bridge connects it to the keep.

### Court Quarter

The **Court Quarter** contains the **Small Court chambers**, royal archives, treasury vaults,
and administrative offices. It serves the business of the royal government as well as the
residential and ceremonial functions of the site.

### Royal Ward

The **Royal Ward** contains noble residences, diplomatic quarters, guard barracks, and
service buildings.

### Wizard's and Signalling Towers

The complex includes a **wizard's tower** and a separate **signalling tower**.

The signalling tower uses **flags** for short-range communication and **beacons** for signals
across the [Ranterg Mountains](ranterg-mountains.mdx). Its flagstaffs communicate with the
lookout at Thrantorbury's auxiliary Royal Guard base. They carry general orders for the castle
garrison and relay orders to the Guard below; acknowledgements and reports return from the
auxiliary lookout. This avoids sending every message through the castle tunnel.

Flags and beacons serve in place of a Kalassarian signalling system that was probably magical
and has not yet been rediscovered. The local flag link and the beacons' longer-range outlook
have different sightline requirements; the diagrams do not establish surveyed bearings or
the locations of relay stations.

## The Castle Tunnel

A large arch in the mountain face at Thrantorbury opens onto the magically created tunnel
that provides the principal route toward Castle Dragonheart. Civilian streets gather near
its approach, but a controlled Royal Guard buffer separates them from the tunnel mouth.
Facilities there inspect castle-bound goods, services, and visitors.

The tunnel is neither dank nor stifling. The **Kalassarian magical engineering corps** cut
numerous small air passages to provide ventilation through ordinary airflow. Non-magical
lamps can burn steadily inside without their flames dimming for lack of air. The excavation
is a magical engineering work; its ventilation is not a continuously maintained enchantment.

## Teleportation Court

The castle's large **teleportation circle** has a **15-foot radius**, spanning **30 feet** across.
Its full transfer area accommodates multiple squads or troops accompanied by supplies, rather
than only a small group packed closely together.

The imperial installation was originally fed through conduits connected to a large
[Orb of Enhancement](../misc/orbs-of-power/orb-of-enhancement.mdx). It has since been adapted
to consume diamonds as a substitute power source. The retrofit does not restore the full
imperial transfer area: diamond-fed operation is limited to the ordinary
<Spell name="teleportation circle" src="phb" /> spell's **5-foot-radius** opening and brief
portal window. The full circle has nine times that opening's area.

Creatures already standing within the active area are transported when the circle activates.
Additional travellers can enter before the opening closes. Capacity depends on the active
area, the load, and the opportunity to enter, rather than a fixed passenger count.

## Architecture and Defense

Castle Dragonheart is a defensible seat of government and a palatial residence, not simply
a ring of high curtain walls. Substantial wings, smaller internal courts, sheltered galleries,
and protected entrances divide its functions and circulation.

The imperial defensive problem included teleporting intruders, magically flying casters,
and mounted air cavalry. A cliff or tall perimeter wall could not by itself keep such
opponents outside. Protected movement between buildings and internal divisions mattered
alongside guards and magical protection.

The castle's main Royal Guard complex is separate from the auxiliary military compound and
lookout on the town plateau below. The town's military base, the tunnel checkpoint, and the
castle garrison have distinct locations rather than forming a single gatehouse establishment.

Structural support, ordinary enclosure, and military defenses serve different purposes.
Retaining works support raised terraces or made ground where needed; the carved bedrock
itself need not be faced everywhere with a retaining wall. Parapets and enclosures also
serve safety, privacy, and access control without making every terrace edge a battlement.
