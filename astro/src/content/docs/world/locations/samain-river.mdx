The main river on [Raibon Island](/world/locations/raibon-island). It bisects
the island almost equally in a north-south direction. It sources its water
primarily from groundwater collected in a _[freshwater
lens](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lens_(hydrology)>)\_, the
[Dustain River](/world/locations/dustain-river), as well as snow-melt from the
