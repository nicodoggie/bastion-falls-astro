**Le Phare Phallique** is a lighthouse and small settlement on [Raibon Island](raibon-island.mdx).
Its name comes from the lighthouse itself: the first people to settle around the tower thought its
shape looked unmistakably phallic, and the joke became the name of both the landmark and the village
that grew up around it.

The settlement is quiet and tree-filled, with private local meeting places used by nearby residents
and visitors. In Seneran records and local speech, the name is sometimes rendered phonetically as
**Le Faerfalik**.
