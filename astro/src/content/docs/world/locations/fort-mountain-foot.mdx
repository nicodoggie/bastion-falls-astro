Fort Mountain-Foot is a fortified settlement in [Poasah](poasah.mdx) and the base of its Northeast
Garrison. [Sirra Kita](../characters/sirra-kita.mdx) served at the fort while investigating the
source of people being trafficked into [Calamutz](calamutz.mdx) and its surrounding areas.
