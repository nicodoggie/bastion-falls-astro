## Regions

[Oranrakran](oranrakran.mdx), known as Oran's Piss, occupies the northern part of the basin
around Queilei. Its twin rivers turn golden under certain conditions.

## Settlements

[Queilei](queilei.mdx), known in Common as Marim, lies in the northern portion of the basin,
at the foot of the Northern [Maltreks](the-maltreks.mdx).
