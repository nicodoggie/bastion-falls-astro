The Longissian Mountains are a major north-south mountain range
that bisects the Mirian Peninsula,
forming the natural border between present-day [Kolha](kolha.mdx)
and [Southern Poasah](southern-poasah.mdx).

Historically, these mountains were home to
[Kureran](../ethnicities/koradin-kurerans.mdx) settlements, referenced in the
[Kalassus Foundation Myth](../novelty/kalassus-foundation-myth.mdx) as the home
of the "Magi Diminuti."
