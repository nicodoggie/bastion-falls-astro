**Green Barrel** is a ruined village in [Maiden's Field](maidens-field.mdx), in
[Senera](senera/index.mdx). It was the childhood home of
[Crispin Mallow](../characters/crispin-mallow.mdx), whose parents were Lilandra and Tempo Mallow.
The village lies about a week's caravan journey from Rannekford.

## Ruins

Crispin Mallow burned Green Barrel. The local magistrates' official account attributed its
destruction to bandits.
Its former agricultural fields are now overgrown, with broken dikes and thick mud turning portions
of the paths into quagmires.
A dilapidated house stands among the ash-covered remains of other buildings, where mushrooms
and vegetation have begun to reclaim the settlement.

An underground laboratory beneath the settlement held the preserved body of Tempo Mallow.
[Uther Rannek](../characters/uther-rannek.mdx) was imprisoned there by the adult Crispin.
After Crispin's return to childhood, his memories of home guided the expedition that returned
to Green Barrel in search of his journal.
