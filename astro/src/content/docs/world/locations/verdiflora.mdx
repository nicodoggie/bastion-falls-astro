**Verdiflora** is the territory held by [House Bellacourt](../families/bellacourt.mdx), a baronial
house of the [Rannek Marches](rannek-marches.mdx). Verdiflora names the lands, not the family.

Hogshead lies on Verdiflora's border. It is distinct from Goat's Head. The barony's seat and full
boundaries are not recorded here.
