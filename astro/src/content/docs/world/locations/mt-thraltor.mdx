Mt Thraltor is the highest peak in the [Ranterg Mountain Range](ranterg-mountains.mdx) and in the
entirety of Senera Island. Its three peaks are regarded as a single mountain. The highest summit
rises behind [Castle Dragonheart](castle-dragonheart.mdx), which stands on a magically carved
shelf below it rather than at the summit.

The city of [Thrantorbury](thrantorbury.mdx), the capital of Senera, occupies a separate lower
shelf on the mountain's flanks. The local _mathrantorgralok_ carved the town plateau through
magic; the Kalassarians carved the higher shelf supporting the royal seat.
