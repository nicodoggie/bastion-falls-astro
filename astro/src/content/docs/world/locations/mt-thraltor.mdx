Mt Thraltor is the highest peak in the
[Ranterg Mountain Range](ranterg-mountains.mdx) and in the entirety
of Senera Island.
The base of the mountain is home to the city of
[Thrantorbury](thrantorbury.mdx), which is the capital of Senera.
