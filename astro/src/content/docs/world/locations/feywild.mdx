**The Feywild** is an alternate plane of existence in close proximity to the one
that contains the world of [Thera](./thera.mdx). The place is a land of whimsy
and wonder--where concepts like logic and continuity doesn't necessarily apply.
It's thought that many of the "real world's" folktales and legends arose from
events that took place in the Feywild.

### The Unseelie Court

## Notable locations

1. Goblin Market (Ruled by Nachtur of the Goblin Kingdom)
1. The Moondrop Flower (A sailing ship owned by tiny fairies who had a pact with
   the White Witches)

## Alliances

1. Spring, Summer and Autumn are known allies.
1. Winter Court is allied with most of the people in his realm. The Prince of
   Frost is Queen Verenestra’s Brother whom he always plays pranks with. He is
   searching for his son named “Karya”. He believes that his son was stolen by
   the Raven Queen. This is his weakness that Queen Valdas is exploiting. Both
   of the courts are in an early stage of forming an alliance. In truth, Queen
   Titania and King Oberon, put him in Feydark as the protector of the artifact.
   “Ring of Winter”.
1. Coral Court is allied with Summer Court, Day Court and Court of the Stars.
