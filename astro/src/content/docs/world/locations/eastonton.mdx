_Eastonton_ was a township in the Northern
[Inespell Marches](../locations/inespell-marches.mdx) of the
[Eastern Seneran](../locations/eastern-senera.mdx) region of
[Senera](../locations/senera/index.mdx). The territory had since grown as the
prestige of the [Eastonton House](../families/eastonton.mdx) grew, and now
basically encompasses the northern portion of the Marches.
