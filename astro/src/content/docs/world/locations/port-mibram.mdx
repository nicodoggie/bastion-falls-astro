## Admiralty Teleportation Circle

The Admiralty facility's teleportation circle was already fully constructed when it became
unusable for lack of capable spellcasters. [Angel Rannek](../characters/angel-rannek.mdx) restored
its operation using installed Orbs of Enhancement and taught a military caster the technique.
What happened to its former operators remains incompletely explained.

Angel also changed the circle's destination sigil, changing who could teleport directly to Port
Mibram through knowledge of that sigil. Outbound destinations follow the Teleportation Circle
spell; the installation is not restricted to a fixed route list. Neither restoring operation nor
changing the sigil required additional circle construction.

See the [August 16 account](../notes/the-vengeful/2026-08-16.mdx#activating-the-port-mibram-circle).
