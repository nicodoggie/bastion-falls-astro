_Port Tressemer_ is a port town built on the mouth of the [Samain River](samain-river.mdx) as it
flows out into the [Bay of Squids](bay-of-squids.mdx) on the northern portion of
[Raibon Island](raibon-island.mdx). Settled originally during the
[Kalassarian invasion of Senera](../locations/senera/index.mdx#early-kalassarian-period).

## Student Life and Cafés

Port Tressemer's academy quarter supports a lively student trade around
[Tressemer Academy](../organizations/tressemer-academy.mdx). One of its best-known cafés is **Warp
Star Café**, a cute, star-themed food shop owned by Ashmodai. It is especially popular among teenage
and young-adult students, and its packaged meals and drinks are fashionable enough to travel beyond
the town through gifts, cravings, and school gossip.

Warp Star Café also advertises in [Legally Bare](../organizations/legally-bare.mdx), placing it
among the youth-facing businesses and sponsors tied to that publication's growing Apgarian and
Seneran audience.
