## Vortex Cove

A hidden cove located a quarter-mile trail from a hidden path at the western
edge of [Malthren Manor](/world/locations/malthren-manor).
It was once a sacred ritual site
where the Malthrenok of the Magarisok could foresee the future—
participants could see visions in the "swirls" of the cove.

### Current Use

- Used by the **Murmal smugglers** as a hidden harbor
- Rumored to house ships belonging to
  [Hickland for Hicks](/world/organizations/hickland-for-hicks) separatists
- The Smugglers Guild maintains tight control over the area

### Access

- Hidden from view even to those who travel the Ear's Canal
- Entrance only visible to those who have been shown the secret path
- Known entrance is from the Murmals Market
- A "Vortex Labyrinth" is associated with the cove; Lord Malthren may share the
  secret entrance

### Features

- Deceptively wide berths
- Deep channel allowing unexpectedly large vessels to dock
