**Vortex Cove** is a hidden cove located a quarter-mile trail from a hidden path at the western edge
of [Malthren Manor](../locations/the-narrows.mdx#malthren-manor). It was once a sacred ritual site
where the Malthrenok of the Magarisok could foresee the future.

The cove can be accessed three ways:

1. By boat, from the Ear's Canal. However, the entrance is nearly invisible from the water. Only
   those who know the secret path can find it.
1. By foot, from the hidden path at the western edge of the Malthren Manor, through the
   [Murmal Market](../locations/the-narrows.mdx#murmal-market) lagoon.
1. By teleportation, from the basement of the Malthren Manor.

The deceptively wide berths of the cove allow for the docking of surprisingly large vessels, and it
became a popular hiding place for ships owned by the Narrows'
[Smugglers Guild](../locations/the-narrows.mdx#smugglers-guild).

## History

In the distant past, the Vortex Cove was considered a sacred site by the
[Hick](../ethnicities/hick.mdx) people who inhabited [Senera](../locations/senera/index.mdx) (c.
2000 PF-200 AI). Wise folk from all over the island held pilgrimages to the tide-pools within the
cove in order to watch as the [Magarisok](../ethnicities/hick.mdx#magarisok) tribe's
Malthrenok--literally pool-watchers--walked into the tumultous waters of the pools, to ride, as was
said, the very ebbs and flows of time itself.

## Current Use

In the present day, the Vortex Cove is used by the **Murmal smugglers** as a hidden harbor. Tunnels
were made connecting it to the **Murmal Market**. Some say that the place was rented out to the
members of the [Hickland for Hicks](../organizations/hickland-for-hicks.mdx) rebellion.

A magical portal connects Vortex Cove to the basemet of the
[Malthren Manor](../locations/the-narrows.mdx#malthren-manor) as well, presumably used by the Lord
Malthren to travel to the cove in order to perform divinations.
