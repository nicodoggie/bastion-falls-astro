**Herb of the Harem** is a magically potent medicinal and aphrodisiac plant whose effects vary
sharply between processed pills, leaves, and flowers. It grows naturally in the orb-rich soils of
[Ahuria](../locations/ahuria.mdx). Druids can reproduce those conditions with refined or synthesized
orbs, while natural deposits are also believed to exist in Senera.

The [Maidenfeld family](../families/maidenfeld.mdx) controls the established trade into Senera and
Apgar. Its pills are typically processed from leaves, carefully measured, and adulterated to make
their cardiac use safer. Raw leaves retain much more of the plant's aphrodisiac potency, while the
flowers express it fully.

Ahurian accounts often describe affected people becoming broadly charmed by several desirable
companions rather than fixating on only one. This appears to reflect personal and cultural
disposition rather than a chemically distinct Ahurian variety.

The herb has a separate and spectacular effect on cadaver-based undead. Decoctions boiled from
leaves or flowers rupture retained circulatory anatomy in zombies, ghouls, and even undead locusts,
remaining effective when diluted into enough water to douse entire formations.

[Poasah](../locations/poasah.mdx) developed the discovery into a proprietary agricultural technology
during its defense against a necromantic attack on the kingdom’s Northwestern corridor, widely
considered the country's breadbasket. Herb-water treatments applied to crops keep at bay the undead
locust swarms sent by the
[Reformed Church of the Divine Masochist](../organizations/reformed-church-of-the-divine-masochist.mdx).
The preparation and application process remains closely held.
