A **Handkerchief of Candid Courtesy** removes the stains and small indignities that make a
formal visit uncomfortable. Unfortunately, its concern for appearances does not extend to tact.

The next compliment its user offers may acquire a truthful qualification that would have been
considerably more courteous left unspoken.
