**Essence of Nymph** is a fey-infused vial associated with nymphs, beauty, sensuality,
revelry, and natural abundance.

The essence encourages a nymph-like outlook: desires feel more compelling to act upon, but
the consumer still chooses their partners, just as nymphs do. Greater inclination to pursue
those desires is not indiscriminate attraction or an obligation to act on them.

It intensifies feelings and reduces inhibitions without removing agency, compelling sexual
activity, or overriding willingness. Unlike the coercive effects of the more potent forms of
[Herb of the Harem](herb-of-the-harem.mdx), its influence leaves the consumer able to choose.
Heightened desire is not itself consent to any particular act.
