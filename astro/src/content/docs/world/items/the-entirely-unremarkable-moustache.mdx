**The Entirely Unremarkable Moustache** is a crooked scrap of paper, darkened unevenly with
lampblack ink and curled at one corner. Close inspection reveals no hidden stitching, fine material,
secret markings, or unusual craftsmanship. It looks precisely like something cut in haste for a
cheap marionette performance.

The best-known specimen lies among the uncatalogued props of an otherwise unremarkable marionette
theater in the [Rannek Marches](../locations/rannek-marches.mdx). The same drawer holds fourteen
containers labeled **MOUSTACHE GUM**. Every one of them has dried solid.

When properly affixed beneath a performer's nose, the moustache makes its wearer impossible to
recognize as anyone previously known to an observer. It does not conceal its own nature: witnesses
plainly see cheap blackened paper, but regard it as completely appropriate on the face of the
stranger before them. Actors quickly learn that the enchantment is more reliable than whatever
paste, wax, or spirit gum happens to be available backstage.

Old cast ledgers name performers whom no surviving member remembers hiring: Señor Mustachio, Lord
Blackwhisker, Doctor Papercut, and the Marquis de Moustache. Accounts disagree about whether these
were stage names, former company members, visiting performers, or the same actor returning in
different costumes. This disagreement has never been helped by demonstrations of the moustache.
