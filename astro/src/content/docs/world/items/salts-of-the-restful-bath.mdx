**Salts of the Restful Bath** turn an ordinary pool into a restorative bath. One dose grants
bathers the benefits of a long rest after an hour in the treated water.

The bath's capacity depends on whether everyone could fully submerge together, not on a fixed
number of bathers. They need not actually submerge or hold a rigid bathing posture: the magic
requires an hour in the water, not an hour underwater.
