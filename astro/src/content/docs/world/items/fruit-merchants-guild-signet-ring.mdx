The **Fruit Merchants Guild Signet Ring** is a magical signet associated with the
[Fruit Merchants Guild](../organizations/fruit-merchants-guild.mdx), colloquially known as the
Fructarians. Its magical form is known as the **Ring of Elemental Chaos**.

The ring was recovered during the party's investigation of the burned grove and logging-site
compound tied to the Reformist route near Castle Malthrek. Miya found it while scanning the ruined
carts, carriages, crates, and trade goods with _Detect Magic_; the ring survived on a desiccated
burned right hand and carried an evocation signature.

The signet appeared to mark a member or honored affiliate of the guild. Its presence connected the
Northcross trade-goods clue and the Fruit Merchants Guild to the Reformist site, though whether the
guild itself was compromised, an individual guild member was involved, or the signet had been
misused remained unresolved.
