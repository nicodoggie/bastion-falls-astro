A **Love Beat** is a small enchanted intimate toy associated with En Chanté Workshop and
[Legally Bare](../organizations/legally-bare.mdx)'s adult novelty line. It is discreet enough to be
worn, removable, and more narrative spice than adventuring tool.

Its vibration follows the wearer's arousal: quiet when they are calm, stronger as they grow more
excited. The magic is noticeable when spells like <Spell name="detect magic" src="phb" /> or similar
are used, but its use is intimate and sensory rather than combat-oriented.

The Love Beat is distinct from Kelthyr's intimate ring, the
<Item name="Variable Cock Sheath" src="BF" />.
