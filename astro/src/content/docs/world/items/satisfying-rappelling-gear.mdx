**Satisfying Rappelling Gear** is genuinely useful for difficult cliffside travel, including
terrain in the [Ranterg Mountains](../locations/ranterg-mountains.mdx) around the [University of
Thrantorbury](../organizations/university-of-thrantorbury.mdx).

Its harness also produces pleasurable vibrations that intensify as the wearer approaches a
descent. A fitting in a courtyard may reveal little about what it will feel like at the edge of
a drop.

## Drafting Notes

Advantage on difficult cliffside traversal and increasingly pleasurable vibrations near a
descent are established. The check types, ordinary rigging requirements, no attunement, uncommon
rarity, and absence of extra forced actions or penalties are provisional. No save or fixed
vibration distance was supplied, so none has been added.
