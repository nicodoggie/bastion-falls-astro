**Boots of Courteous Passage** suppress their wearer's footfalls. Their enchantment is admirably
considerate of everyone nearby, including people the wearer hoped not to disturb or attract.

An empty corridor provides an excellent demonstration of their useful property. Testing near
another person establishes how the boots understand courtesy.
