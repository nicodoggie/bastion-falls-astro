A **Pen of Revealing** functions as an ordinary writing pen and produces magical light that
reveals hidden writing. Invisible inks and magically concealed text become readable under its
light without changing the message itself.

The pen is not cursed. It reveals writing, but does not translate unfamiliar languages, decipher
codes, or restore text that has been destroyed.
