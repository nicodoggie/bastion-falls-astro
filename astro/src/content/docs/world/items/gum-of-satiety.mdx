**Gum of Satiety** genuinely supplies nourishment rather than merely suppressing hunger. Its
apparent convenience conceals a compulsion to keep chewing after the wearer is already full.

Prolonged chewing continues to provide nourishment, so repeated excess gradually causes weight
gain as ordinary overeating would. It does not cause instantaneous swelling, and becoming fuller
does not make the gum easier to discard.

## Drafting Notes

The DC 20 Wisdom save, real nourishment, and gradual weight gain are established. Meal timing,
