The **Twin Door of Instantaneous Travel** are an identical pair of oak wood doors, with
distinctively-grained olive wood inlaid into a two-panel design. Strange runes are carved into the
cornices that run along each panel.

## Examples

### Walburton Doorway

The _Walburton Doorway_ is an example that connects the
[Turbiere de Hubar](../locations/turbiere-de-hubar.mdx) in
[Raibon Island](../locations/raibon-island.mdx) with the
[Walburton Mansion](../locations/walburton-mansion.mdx) in [Hugo](../locations/hugo.mdx), about 1000
miles away. However, because there is a _power source_ attached to it, the distance cost is only one
1st-level spell slot.
