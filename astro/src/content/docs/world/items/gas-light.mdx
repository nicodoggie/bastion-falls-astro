The **Gas Light** illuminates an entire room regardless of its size. Its curse makes a single
visible detail appear subtly false, with the most consequential distortions concerning whatever
the perceiver is trying to establish.

An altered key profile might seem to match a sought-after lock. A name and photograph on a
most-wanted notice might appear to identify someone entirely different. The physical key,
document, or person does not actually change.

## Drafting Notes

The room-wide illumination, DC 20 Perception check, and d20 bands 1-10 / 11-17 / 18-20 are
established. This draft uses a distortion on every lighting, rolled separately for each
perceiver; an additional chance of entirely truthful illumination remains unsettled.
The per-room persistence and current-illumination requirement, action costs, outdoor range,
uncommon rarity, no attunement, and the treatment of magical darkness are provisional.
