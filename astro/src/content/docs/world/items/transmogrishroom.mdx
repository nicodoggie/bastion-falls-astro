The **Transmogrishroom** is a strange magical fungus associated with unstable bodily transformation.
Records from the [Rozenmaiden](../organizations/rozenmaiden.mdx) crew's expedition into
[Jing Shen Jiang](../locations/jing-shen-jiang.mdx) connect one such mushroom to
[Andy Anderson](../characters/andy-anderson.mdx)'s transformation into a kobold.
