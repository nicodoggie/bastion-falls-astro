A **Pillow of Public Dreams** makes it possible to sleep through ordinary noise and on an
uncomfortable surface. It is a promising traveling companion for anyone who considers privacy
less important than a good night's sleep.

Its silent dream pictures are not reliable evidence of what the sleeper remembers, desires, or
has actually done. Their audience may nevertheless be tempted to interpret them.
