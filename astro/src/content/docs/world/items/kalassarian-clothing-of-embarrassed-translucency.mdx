**Kalassarian Clothing of Embarrassed Translucency** resembles ordinary, exceptionally
well-fitted clothing. Surviving garments include dresses and masculine and feminine styles; each
somehow fits whoever puts it on.

Its curse responds to embarrassment, not to an observer's judgment. A genuinely self-confident
wearer might use a garment for years without discovering its other property, until a particular
person or situation finally makes them blush.

## Drafting Notes

Uncommon rarity, no attunement, ordinary removal, and the absence of additional numerical
penalties are provisional rules. The embarrassment trigger and exceptional fit are established
concepts; no universal embarrassment DC is imposed.
