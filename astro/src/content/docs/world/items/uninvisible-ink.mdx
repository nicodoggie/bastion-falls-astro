**Uninvisible Ink** behaves like invisible ink and can be read with a
[Pen of Revealing](pen-of-revealing.mdx). The pen's magical light reliably reveals the actual
message; the pen itself is not cursed.

The ink's defect is unreliable secrecy rather than false information. At unpredictable moments
it reveals what was written without any revealing light, potentially exposing an otherwise
authentic private message.
