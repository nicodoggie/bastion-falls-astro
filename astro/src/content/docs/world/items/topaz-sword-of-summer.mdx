The _Topaz Sword of Summer_ is one of the four gifts bestowed by the Archfey of
the Seasonal Courts collectively known as the
[Elven Boons](../novelty/the-elven-boons.mdx). The Summer Queen Titania gave it
to the elven host that crossed into [Senera](../locations/senera/index.mdx) via
the [Realm Bridge](../novelty/the-realm-bridge.mdx); the elves later entrusted
it to the [Mathrantor'al](../ethnicities/hick.mdx#mathrantoral) tribe.
