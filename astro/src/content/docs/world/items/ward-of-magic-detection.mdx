A **Ward of Magic Detection** is a standing magical sensor used to watch for magical auras,
spellcasting, and similar magical intrusions. It is often installed near doors, offices, waiting
rooms, or other controlled passages where suspicious magic would matter more than mundane movement.

The ward normally needs a daily 3rd-level spell recharge. When an
[Orb of Enhancement](../misc/orbs-of-power/orb-of-enhancement.mdx) or similar enhancer orb operates
nearby, the ward can draw on the condensed raw magical energy instead.
