A **Page Finder** is a small archive-moth-like charm used to search a particular book by intent
rather than by index. It is especially useful with thick binders, collections, and heavily annotated
volumes where the reader knows what they want but not where it was filed.

When set on a book, the charm can unfold from a cocoon-like shape into a tiny moth-like or
butterfly-like seeker. Its user guides it telepathically, and the charm finds the page or passage
that most closely matches that intention.
