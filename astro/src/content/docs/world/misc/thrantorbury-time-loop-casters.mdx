These are provisional encounter statistics for the unnamed **Abjurer** and **Conjurer** at the
[University of Thrantorbury](../organizations/university-of-thrantorbury.mdx). Their names,
ancestries, genders, ages, and individual histories remain unset. Medium humanoids, language
choices, abilities, and all combat features below are design assumptions, not additional canon.
The alignment field remains unrestricted rather than assigning a moral identity.

The established context is that both belonged to the radicalized part of an underground research
community. They discovered a power source in the Kalassarian ruins beneath the Library and used
it for experiments directed by Reformists toward taking over Senera's religious institutions.
Their religious beliefs and grievances are genuine; those facts do not settle their individual
motives, relationship, or willingness to negotiate.

## Encounter scope

The requested target is **CR 10 each**, with **four allied participants inside the time loop** and
**three outside** seeking to reinforce once it comes down. The outside group is not counted as
present from the start. Their arrival timing is unresolved, not an automatic extra wave on a
particular round. Party levels, current resources, and the exact encounter sequence are unknown,
so these are CR estimates, not playtested encounter ratings.

Both blocks use short daily-use spell lists and original combat abilities rather than full
player-character spell-slot progression. Spell references use the 2014 rules, particularly for
Counterspell. Neither caster has legendary actions, legendary resistance, or automatic regeneration.

## The Abjurer

<StatBlock creature={frontmatter.creatureStats.abjurer} />

**Role:** hold a defensible position, keep the Conjurer working, and spend limited protection
carefully. The Arcane Ward is one shared pool: protecting an ally consumes the same protection
that would otherwise absorb damage to the Abjurer. Dim its visible sigils as it runs out.

Projected Ward and Counterspell compete for the same reaction. Baiting one opens a window for
other attacks or spells. Projected Ward does not protect concentration automatically: any damage
that gets through still requires the recipient's normal saving throw. Counterspell should be
used for a concrete priority, not to repeatedly deny one player's every attempt to participate.

Raise Partition costs the whole attack action. The panel blocks the Abjurer's side too; it is
not a transparent firing loophole. Describe its edges and give its AC and damage feedback when
attacked. Going around, climbing, teleporting, breaking it, or waiting out its short duration are
all real answers. There is no invisible second wall closing every alternative route.

## The Conjurer

<StatBlock creature={frontmatter.creatureStats.conjurer} />

**Role:** create one tangible obstruction, move around it, and maintain pressure while protecting
concentration. The sentinel is the Conjurer's principal combat summon, not another CR 10 enemy.
All of its statistics and turn rules are in the block; it requires no additional creature sheet.

The sentinel adds one short turn after the Conjurer, not a swarm of independent initiatives. Its
arrival and tether are visible. Attacking it, dispelling it, breaking concentration, moving it
beyond the tether's range, or incapacitating its caster can all remove it. It cannot make
opportunity attacks, pin someone without a check, summon reinforcements, or act after vanishing.

If combat begins with no sentinel present, summoning costs the Conjurer's first action. A sentinel
already present must have been cast within its actual one-minute duration; it does not receive a
free attack before initiative. Track one shared concentration slot across the sentinel, Web, and
Fog Cloud. Casting either of the latter spells ends an existing sentinel rather than layering
another control effect over it.

Spatial Deflection is one reaction, not an answer to every hit. Apply the remaining damage and
resolve concentration before moving. Misty Step and Dimension Door obey the established loop
boundaries and other teleportation restrictions; neither grants a new way out of the loop.

## Running them together

Start with the pair and at most the Conjurer's single sentinel. Do not add unbudgeted aberrations
or research assistants merely because a summoning specialist is present. Existing creatures from
prior scenes require their own accounting if they remain in this encounter.

The Abjurer can protect the Conjurer's concentration, but only within 30 feet and at the cost of
its own finite ward. The Conjurer can reposition without automatically moving its partner. Forced
movement, separation, breaking sight lines, and drawing out reactions therefore matter. Neither
caster supplies the other with permanent invulnerability or immunity to a character's abilities.

Arcane Bolt, Rift Bolt, Raise Partition, and Spatial Deflection are magical abilities, not spells.
Conjure Sentinel and the named spells are spellcasting and can be interrupted when Counterspell's
normal requirements are met. Dispel Magic has the explicitly stated interaction with the panel;
it is not a general off switch for all of either caster's features.

## Reasoning with them

Their objections to Orthodox restrictions do not have to be fraudulent for their current actions
to be harmful. Characters can take their work and convictions seriously while challenging the
Reformists' use of it. Stopping immediate harm, agreeing to a pause, and abandoning a religious
or political commitment are different outcomes; one conversation need not accomplish all three.

Do not assign a single automatic persuasion DC that converts both researchers. Their individual
stakes and relationship remain for development. They may disagree, accept different terms, or
choose differently without one being secretly insincere. Neither block grants immunity to
ordinary conversation or forces a fight to the death.

## The loop and the ruins

These blocks do not define the power source, how the loop is sustained, the reset's timing, what
resets restore, or which act ends it. Defeating either caster does not automatically settle those
questions. Do not add a personal reset, free revival, or immunity to death to either sheet merely
because the encounter takes place in a loop.

Likewise, the arrival of the outside group does not by itself heal anyone, restore daily uses, or
start a new combat phase. Apply any actual reset rules separately when those rules are established.
No unrestricted power-source recharge or special weakness has been assumed.

## Provisional balance notes

Both casters have **150 HP** from **20d8 + 60**, a **+9 spell attack**, and **DC 17** spell saves.
The Abjurer has **AC 17** and a **60-point ward**. Its three bolts deal **54 damage** if all hit.
The Conjurer has **AC 16**, two bolts for **36 damage**, and one sentinel strike for **23 damage**:
**59 damage** per round if everything hits and the summon remains active. These are raw averages,
not expected damage against a particular AC.

A rough three-round durability budget gives the Abjurer **210 effective HP** if its ward is used
on itself. The Conjurer's upper planning budget is also **210**: 150 personal HP, 30 damage
prevented by three successful uses of Spatial Deflection, and 30 sentinel HP if opponents spend
damage destroying the summon. That is not 210 personal HP. Attacking the caster directly or
breaking concentration can bypass the sentinel's durability; save-based damage bypasses Spatial
Deflection. The Abjurer cannot spend its ward on itself and count those same points again as
additional protection for its ally.

Using 2014-style monster benchmarks, the Abjurer's defense is roughly CR 10 and its offense
roughly CR 9 after attack-bonus adjustment, giving a rounded CR 10 estimate. The Conjurer's
upper-budget defense and established-summon offense are roughly CR 10. A cold start lowers its
first three rounds to **47 raw damage per round**; losing concentration lowers output further.
These estimates do not fully price cover, mobility, spell interruption, or the synergy of the pair.

The pair's base XP is **11,800**. Under the 2014 two-enemy multiplier, the nominal adjusted value
is **17,700** before terrain or other participants. The sentinel's contribution is already inside
the Conjurer's estimate, not a separate XP award or another monster multiplier. No safe difficulty
verdict follows without the four insiders' levels and resources. Reinforcements, an already
weakened research team, or other active enemies can substantially change the result.

Track rounds to defeat, ward consumption, sentinel uptime, and how many actions the party spends
on obstacles. Choose any changes before play; do not secretly refill wards or inflate HP because
the group finds an effective tactic. Spell lists, defenses, and CR remain open to revision.
