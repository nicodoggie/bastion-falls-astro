These are three proposed encounter covens serving the
[False Crone](../characters/false-crone.mdx), not the Hags of Hama. Each contains three distinct
hags. The labels below describe combat roles, not established personal names or institutional
ranks. Their appearances, individual histories, exact assignments, and willingness to bargain
remain open. All statistics and custom capture procedures are provisional homebrew.

**CR applies to each sister, not to the entire triad.** The lower triad has CR 6, 7, and 8 members;
the middle triad has three CR 10 members; the senior triad has three CR 15 members. These are not
three consecutive mandatory fights, and the Crone is not included in any triad's rating.

## Shared operating rules

Each hag has one turn, one bonus action, and one reaction under the ordinary rules. None of these
nine sisters has legendary actions, Legendary Resistance, automatic healing, or a free summon.
The blocks contain their cooperation benefits; do not add another shared coven spell list or
extra coven turn on top. Named spells use the 2014 rules. Concentration is one shared limit across
spells and custom abilities, not one slot for each category.

All three sisters must be conscious and within 30 feet of the acting hag when a feature says
that both other triad members must be present. That extra benefit is checked on activation;
separating them afterward does not retroactively undo an effect unless its text says so.
The movement feature Coven Step requires just one other conscious allied hag nearby. Distance,
barriers, forced movement, and incapacitation therefore have concrete effects without making
isolation an automatic victory.

Named magical attacks and custom workings are not spells unless their text says otherwise.
Counterspell can interrupt actual spellcasting, not every magical action. Dispel Magic has the
explicit interactions printed in the knots, bars, circle, and stolen-voice features.

[Yielding](../species/hag.mdx#yielding) is a reaction usable once per long rest. It prevents one
instance of damage to the hag and leaves her at 1 HP. It does not shield a sister, refill a ward,
remove a condition, or create another health pool. A hag who has already spent her reaction
cannot Yield until that reaction is available again. It is usually a last desperate choice,
not an additional full-health shield.

## Lower triad — The Briar, the Ash, and the Knot

A mobile hunter draws someone out of position, a hex-worker interferes with reactions and
attacks, and a binder tries to keep the victim there. They are threatening together but have
limited answers to separation, broken sight lines, and focused pressure.

### Briarwife — CR 6

<StatBlock creature={frontmatter.creatureStats.briarwife} />

**Role:** pursue and pull. Barbed Invitation can draw a target toward the Knotkeeper, but it costs
the Briarwife her whole action. With the full triad present it can pull two targets. The shears
have reach; Coven Step is only movement, not passage through walls. Thorn-Scent aids tracking
without revealing a hidden creature automatically.

### Ash-Tongue — CR 7

<StatBlock creature={frontmatter.creatureStats.ashTongue} />

**Role:** spoil a response. Bad Word competes with Coven Step for her bonus action. It can remove
an opportunity attack before the hunter withdraws, but requires hearing and a failed save.
Smothering Reproach is short-lived fear, not repeated minute-long incapacitation. Her illusion
and disguise spells do not grant automatic surprise.

### Knotkeeper — CR 8

<StatBlock creature={frontmatter.creatureStats.knotkeeper} />

**Role:** hold a lane or isolate a rescuer. Tying two victims needs the full triad nearby and
spends her whole action; she cannot also use Threadbite on that turn. Both knots share one
concentration. Each victim gets an end-of-turn save, a helper can cut the cords, and dispelling
one knot does not automatically dispel the other. Web and Silence replace the knots because
all three require concentration.

## Middle triad — The Spite, the Step, and the Hearth

These sisters protect one another's opportunities. Their ward is finite, their position exchange
consumes two creatures' action resources, and their strongest interruptions compete for reactions.

### Spite-Matron — CR 10

<StatBlock creature={frontmatter.creatureStats.spiteMatron} />

**Role:** prepare one consequential failed save. Speak Spite replaces a damaging attack, affects
only the next save, and does not stack with another use. She can set up a sister's working but
cannot guarantee it succeeds. Chorus of Accusation is usable once per day, not a repeatable
opening burst. Fear and Bestow Curse must respect their ordinary concentration requirements.

### Crooked-Step — CR 10

<StatBlock creature={frontmatter.creatureStats.crookedStep} />

**Role:** change who is standing where. Sister Exchange uses her bonus action and the willing
sister's reaction, leaving that sister unable to Yield or protect someone until it refreshes.
Wrong Door offers a save, triggers on completed movement rather than every attack, and cannot
place someone directly into damaging terrain. Fold the Threshold costs the whole action.
Teleportation restrictions apply to all three features; ordinary walls obstruct Coven Step
but not otherwise legal teleportation.

### Hearth-Gaoler — CR 10

<StatBlock creature={frontmatter.creatureStats.hearthGaoler} />

**Role:** keep an important sister working. Stitched Ward has only three uses and can prevent at
most 36 damage in total, whether spent on herself or allies. The same reaction cannot also cast
Counterspell or Yield. Her bars are visible, destructible, climbable, and permeable to attacks
with cover. They are not a sealed, invulnerable cage and do not prevent teleportation.

## Senior triad — The Circle, the Shears, and the Stolen Reply

This is a dangerous encounter in its own right. The Circle-Sovereign holds targets, the Red-Scissor
closes to attack, and the Name-Thief interferes with spoken spellcasting. Their workings still
cost actions, permit saves, and compete with other concentration effects. Their authority is not
an ability to rewrite the rules of another character's identity.

### Circle-Sovereign — CR 15

<StatBlock creature={frontmatter.creatureStats.circleSovereign} />

**Role:** hold the battlefield. Closed Circle restrains its initial failed-save targets; it does
not repeatedly catch everyone entering afterward. Victims can spend actions attempting escape
as well as making their end-of-turn saves. Breaking concentration or dispelling the circle ends
the working. The visible boundary is not a wall or a teleportation prohibition.

### Red-Scissor — CR 15

<StatBlock creature={frontmatter.creatureStats.redScissor} />

**Role:** capitalize on someone else's restraint. Her pursuit reaction grants movement, not an
extra attack. Taking Hold replaces one attack and occupies the hand needed for Great Shears,
so the hag must use Severing Glance while maintaining that grapple. She has no automatic
execution, decapitation, or bonus damage merely because a victim is helpless.

### Name-Thief — CR 15

<StatBlock creature={frontmatter.creatureStats.nameThief} />

**Role:** interrupt spoken coordination. Steal the Reply takes her whole action and concentration;
it suppresses voice, not memory, selfhood, telepathy, or every possible action. It offers repeat
saves, help from an adjacent ally, Remove Curse, and Dispel Magic. A two-target use still has only
one concentration slot. Hold Monster cannot be layered over her own stolen-voice working.

## The Crone and her vessels

See the [Crone's encounter statistics](../characters/false-crone.mdx#encounter-statistics) for
**CR 20 in her current elven vessel** and **CR 15 if she takes Madeline**. These are alternative
states, not an automatic CR 20 fight followed by a fresh CR 15 phase. Do not give her a spare
vessel, the Sapphire Brooch, extra sisters, or a replacement health pool without separately
establishing and accounting for them.

Defeating a vessel expels the Crone. It does not permanently kill or capture her, and it does not
automatically kill the person whose body she occupied. The two blocks give the same escaping-spirit
profile: she remains on her initiative, has no legendary actions or possession action, and must
actually move to escape. Spirit damage can disperse her and end the encounter, but cannot keep her.

Lasting containment requires magic that specifically binds or imprisons her spirit, or a hag's
Soul Bag. A prepared empty bag offers a reaction check within 10 feet on emergence, followed by
possible action attempts within 5 feet. The provisional DC is 20 Intelligence (Arcana). This is a
specific encounter procedure, not an assertion that a standard Soul Bag normally captures every
kind of soul. Ordinary sacks cannot substitute. Opening or destroying the bag releases her.

A binding spell keeps its actual casting time, creature-type requirements, saving throws,
components, duration, and release conditions. Both possessed forms and the escaping spirit are
fey for effects targeting the Crone; this does not change Madeline's own species. A spell limited
to a humanoid soul does not become eligible merely because the abandoned vessel is humanoid.
A long ritual cannot be declared completed as a reaction. Temporary restraints, wards, and
barriers can buy time if applicable, but are not a permanent soul-binding victory.

Give the players evidence before a confrontation: descriptions of her borrowed body, an earlier
spirit departure, a witnessed binding, or information about a Soul Bag. Which clue they obtain is
not predetermined. Defeating the body, rescuing the host, escaping, disrupting the project, and
capturing the Crone are separate achievable outcomes; do not require all of them for a meaningful
success or hide the capture condition until after an otherwise successful fight.

## Provisional balance notes

These are 2014-style CR estimates, not playtest results or a promise of difficulty for a particular
party. Party levels, resources, terrain, surprise, and other active enemies are unknown. Control,
mobility, successful concentration, and coordinated targets make the covens more dangerous than
raw damage alone suggests. The cooperative benefits above are already part of the intended
ratings; they do not grant another free CR increase or another set of spells.

Raw sustained damage below means the average of the damage dice if every listed attack hits,
before resistance or saves. Fractional dice averages are retained here even when the block's
printed damage is rounded down. Area estimates assume two targets, not every party member.

| Sister or vessel | HP | AC | Raw sustained damage per round |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Briarwife, CR 6 | 156 | 15 | 35 |
| Ash-Tongue, CR 7 | 169 | 15 | 41 |
| Knotkeeper, CR 8 | 182 | 16 | 46 |
| Spite-Matron, CR 10 | 204 | 17 | 66 |
| Crooked-Step, CR 10 | 204 | 17 | 66 |
| Hearth-Gaoler, CR 10 | 162 | 17 | 66 |
| Circle-Sovereign, CR 15 | 255 | 18 | 96 |
| Red-Scissor, CR 15 | 255 | 18 | 96 |
| Name-Thief, CR 15 | 255 | 18 | 96 |
| Crone, elven vessel, CR 20 | 285 | 19 | 121.5 including three damaging legendary actions |
| Crone, Madeline vessel, CR 15 | 255 | 17 | 85 including two damaging legendary actions |

Using Magic Resistance's approximate effective-AC adjustment, the lower sisters have defensive
estimates near CR 7, 8, and 9 and sustained offensive estimates near CR 5, 6, and 7. The Briarwife's
two-target opener stays within that offensive band over three rounds. The middle sisters fall
near CR 10 on defense and offense; count the Hearth-Gaoler's 36-point ward only once across the
whole encounter, giving her an upper self-protection budget of 198 HP. Her bars are an optional
obstacle, not another creature or another pool that opponents must destroy.

The senior sisters have defensive estimates near CR 14 and attack-based offense near CR 16,
averaging CR 15. The Circle-Sovereign's two-target sweep does not increase her three-round damage
above that attack-based offensive band. Abandoning attacks for control lowers direct damage but
can enable a sister's attacks. The Red-Scissor's weapon attacks use her Strength-based bonus;
her ranged attacks and spells use Charisma.

For the elven vessel, two Legendary Resistances give an upper planning HP budget of 345; Magic
Resistance then puts defense near CR 20. Its full damaging action economy is about CR 20 offense
after attack-bonus adjustment. The Madeline vessel has one Legendary Resistance, giving a 285 HP
planning budget, defense near CR 15, and offense near CR 14 after attack-bonus adjustment. Their
rounded intended ratings are CR 20 and CR 15. The spirit is a capture/escape state, not an
additional attacking boss or a second XP award. Yielding is not counted as another full HP pool.

For three enemies and a standard three-to-five-character party, the 2014 encounter multiplier
is 2. The figures below are encounter-accounting aids, not a party-specific difficulty verdict.

| Triad | Base XP | Adjusted XP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lower, CR 6/7/8 | 9,100 | 18,200 |
| Middle, CR 10 each | 17,700 | 35,400 |
| Senior, CR 15 each | 39,000 | 78,000 |

Do not add the Crone to one of these totals without recalculating the encounter. Do not count
barriers as additional monsters. Group size changes the multiplier; objectives, depleted party
resources, and a prepared enemy position can matter more than the nominal figure. Track actual
rounds, control uptime, ward use, and escape attempts when revising these drafts after play.
