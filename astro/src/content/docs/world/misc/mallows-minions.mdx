These thread-controlled minions support [Crispin Mallow](../characters/crispin-mallow.mdx),
intercepting rescuers or protecting him during a confrontation. The following encounter designs
and deployment guidance are provisional, not established appearances in play. Challenge ratings
are estimates, not playtested ratings; party levels and available resources remain unknown.
The control-knot weakness applies to these designs, not universally to Mallow's needle.

Routine damage assumes all attacks hit; it is not expected damage against a particular AC.
No minion has legendary actions, legendary resistance, regeneration, or a spell list.

## Threadbound Snarer

<StatBlock creature={frontmatter.creatureStats.threadboundSnarer} />

Its arms unfold into hooks while its shoulders draw thread across a passage. Its job is to cost
movement and choices, not remove whole turns. Pulling requires a save, happens only once on its
turn, and cannot drag an entire party into a hazard through three successful hits.

Stitch the Crossing costs its entire action: it deals no damage that turn. Describe the threads
before anyone enters them. Players can cut a section, go around, fly above, teleport past, or
interrupt the snarer. Do not fill every alternative route with another invisible trap.

## Needlehand Skirmisher

<StatBlock creature={frontmatter.creatureStats.needlehandSkirmisher} />

Extra finger joints spread into throwing needles; its backward-stepping legs keep its face toward
pursuers. It makes a nuisance of itself from ledges or the far side of a crossing. Its withdrawal
is movement, not teleportation: grappling it or blocking its route matters.

Pinning Volley trades one attack for two possible short-lived tethers. Tethered characters retain
their actions, reactions, and attacks. An ally can free them without a roll. No special penalty to
concentration is added: ordinary damage already pressures concentration.

## Misstitched Bulwark

<StatBlock creature={frontmatter.creatureStats.misstitchedBulwark} />

Broad grafted shoulders and bracing limbs make this a living obstruction. Its protection transfers
rather than deletes damage, so attacking Mallow still advances the fight. It must remain close to
him and gets only one reaction per round. Forced movement can separate them.

Clear the Master's Space consumes the beneficiary's reaction. Mallow must choose between accepting
that movement and keeping his reaction available for other uses. It grants neither an attack nor
an extra spell. The bulwark should protect Mallow, not chase the party's most vulnerable member
around the map while somehow continuing to intercept attacks at a distance.

## Living victims and the knot

These drafts assume living altered humanoids, not corpse constructs or willing Reformist agents.
Their original identities and graft sources are deliberately unspecified. Confirm that choice
before promoting the blocks into canon.

Each has a visibly exposed control knot. An adjacent character can spend an action on a DC 16
Medicine or Sleight of Hand check. The first success interrupts the puppet through the end of its
next turn; another success during that window ends control. That rewards coordinated rescuers
without requiring a particular spell. Failed checks do not injure the victim. At 0 HP the victim
is stable unless killed outright, leaving a second practical rescue route.

Describe a slackening limb or the victim struggling to speak after the first success so players
can recognize the opportunity. A disabled victim is not automatically healthy or a combat ally.
The control-knot rule belongs to these encounter designs only; it does not establish the magical
needle's universal mechanics. Do not silently substitute the existing replica puppet's healing
vulnerability for these living victims.

## Deployment

### Approach: delay with a purpose

Start with one snarer at a crossing or narrow approach. Its objective is to hold the party back,
not fight to the last hit point. Put a skirmisher in a subsequent interception rather than stacking
both immediately. If the party is comfortably overwhelming the encounter, the skirmisher can
instead appear as a visible reinforcement with an approach route the party can disrupt.

Give a reason to advance: Mallow is moving captives or preparing his defenses. Do not attach an
unannounced automatic failure timer. Successful bypass, rescue, or forced retreat counts as
beating the interception; killing every creature is not required.

### Boss: one supporting turn, not a second boss

Begin with one bulwark alongside Mallow. Keep any surviving interceptor offstage initially. Do
not assume CR 17 plus a CR 10 support creature is safe merely because each block fits the requested
range: party resources, NPC turns, and the two-form boss sequence materially change the difficulty.

A second support creature is an optional escalation, not the default. If used, bring it in visibly
and only after assessing the actual fight. Avoid overlapping pinning effects on Angel every round.
Spread interference so several players get decisions rather than one player losing mobility all
night. Support creatures retain their current damage and conditions across Mallow's transformation;
no automatic healing, replacement waves, or free reactivation of freed victims.

For a faster fight, deploy fewer creatures or let interception survivors withdraw. Do not secretly
change hit points mid-fight. Choose any reduced-HP variant before initiative and mark it on
the sheet.

## Balance checks and limits

The snarer favors control over damage and sacrifices its attack routine to create its obstacle.
The skirmisher has more sustained damage but loses output when pinning, and has no defensive
immunities. The bulwark's damage transfer accelerates its own defeat while preserving Mallow.
Their durability and routine damage broadly target the requested tiers; mobility, interruption of
control, terrain, and party rescue tactics can substantially alter their effective difficulty.

Watch the first encounter for rounds-to-defeat, actions spent freeing allies, and whether anyone
loses meaningful choices. Use that information when selecting the boss support, not as a reason to
inflate the boss's minions. Schema and arithmetic checks are not playtesting.
