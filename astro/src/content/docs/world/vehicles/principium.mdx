The _Principium_ was a galley working under
[La Camaraderie de la Mer](../organizations/la-camaraderie.mdx), and was its
"flagship" in that it was led by the organization's Grand Master. On paper, it
was a cargo ship that hauled iron from
[Raibon Island](../locations/raibon-island.mdx) to
[Estrega](../locations/estrega.mdx).

## Crew

### Captain

The Captain of the _Principium_ was always the Grand Master of La Camaraderie de
la Mer. At the time of its sinking, the captain was
[Jethro Duvague](../characters/jethro-duvague.mdx).
