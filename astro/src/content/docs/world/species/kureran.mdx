Kurerans were a mysterious people said to have inhabited the lands surrounding
[Korad Pass](../locations/korad-pass.mdx). They may have coexisted with the
[Kalassarian Empire](../organizations/kalassarian-empire.mdx) in its infancy before coming into
conflict with it shortly after the Flowering. Sources describe them as magically powerful and of
short stature and slight build.

Scholars who have visited the [Feywild](../locations/feywild.mdx) note that artistic depictions of
Kurerans bear a remarkable resemblance to [gnomes](gnome.mdx). Some hypothesize that the first
Kureran settlers fled the [Draco-Giant War](../events/draco-giant-war.mdx) through the
[Realm Bridge](../novelty/the-realm-bridge.mdx). Critics note that the Seneran fey arrived much
later than the earliest known Kureran settlements, while proponents cite reports of unpredictable
temporal displacement during travel to and from the Feywild.

Many powerful ancient magical artifacts are attributed to the Kurerans, who are believed to have
brought with them a deep understanding of various magical arts. This knowledge may have allowed them
to adapt quickly to the natural energies of the [Theran](../locations/thera.mdx) plane. They
eventually formalized a magical tradition called [Ritma](../concepts/ritma.mdx), which the
Kalassarians later adopted and extended into their own magical tradition.

The Kurerans expanded from [Korad](../locations/korad-pass.mdx), their greatest city, to become a
major power across the Beteran continent. They founded cities scattered across the continent rather
than settling it continuously. From these enclaves, they made rounds among local populations and
reportedly helped cultivate magical practice.

## History

Much of Kureran culture has been lost. Only fragments have been recovered from expeditions into
Kalassarian ruins. Attempts to study magical artifacts attributed to the Kurerans with a
<Spell name="legend lore" src="phb" /> spell have been unsuccessful. The artifacts themselves are
believed to interfere with divination, and no one has successfully reached the documented ruins of
Koradin culture to conduct further archaeological study.

### Kureran Culture (c. 3,000 PF - 70 AI)

A partial reconstruction of fragments found in Kalassarian ruins places one of the greatest Kureran
cities near Korad Pass. The fragments do not preserve the city's name, but historians commonly call
it Korad. It is believed to have been founded about 3,000 years before Kalassar's Flowering.

The Kurerans are believed to have mastered the art of _Ritma_ during Korad's formative era. This art
formed the basis of the Kalassarian magical tradition, which in turn became the foundation of
magical scholarship recovered after the [Fall of Bastion](../events/fall-of-bastion.mdx).

### Kalassarian Conflict and the Kureran Genocide (15 - 70 AI)

The rise of the Kalassarian Empire brought the Kurerans into direct conflict with the expanding
human civilization. The [First Kalassarian-Kureran War](../events/first-kalassarian-kureran-war.mdx)
began in 15 AI, marking the beginning of a
dark period for Kureran civilization. Despite their mastery of _Ritma_ and deep understanding of
Thera's magical nature, the Kurerans became increasingly isolated and overwhelmed by the Empire's
military might.

The conflict reached its tragic conclusion in 70 AI with what became known as the Kureran Genocide.
The Kalassarian Empire's systematic campaign against Kureran settlements destroyed many of their
great cities, including several magical academies that had been centers of _Ritma_ study.

#### The Koradin Resistance

The Kurerans of Korad proved more resilient than most. Protected by the surrounding mountains and
generations of accumulated magical fortifications, the Koradin Kurerans resisted long after other
settlements fell. No records of a final confrontation with the Kalassarians survive, and no
survivors of the genocide have been documented.

Recent but unconfirmed reports allege that Kureran ruins have been found in the
[Alsatian Mountains](../locations/alsatian-mountains.mdx). Some researchers propose that groups of
Kurerans escaped the purge into the difficult Alsatian terrain, but it remains unclear whether the
ruins represent a separate Kureran settlement or are Kureran at all.

### Legacy

Following the first translation of Kalassarian fragments that mention the Kurerans, many have
claimed to have found Kureran settlement sites throughout the
[Beteran](../locations/betera/index.mdx) continent. These sites have yielded valuable magical
artifacts considered sophisticated even by present-day standards. However, because no confirmed
Kureran remains have been excavated, neither the sites nor the artifacts have been conclusively
identified as Kureran.
