**Face-Stealers** are creatures that can change their form to any other humanoid
body. Unlike other shapeshifters, they can only change their shape after
incorporating biological fragments of a person they wish to impersonate.

Face-stealers are sterile, as they are unable to reproduce sexually. They also
physically do not have any sexual organs, and are unable replicate them when
they transform into a new body. Organizations trying to root out face-stealer
spies often resort to spot checks to ensure that their members have genitalia.

Their classification as a subspecies of shapeshifter is also debated, as they
can be children of exclusively human parents. It's theorized that the ability to
shapeshift evolved independently from mutations from exposure to large amounts
of magical energy, instead of being in the same lineage as
[Doppelgangers](doppelganger.mdx).
