**Face-Stealers** are creatures that can change their form to any other humanoid body. Unlike other
shapeshifters, they can only change their shape after incorporating biological fragments of a person
they wish to impersonate.

Face-stealers are sterile, as they are unable to reproduce sexually. They lack primary genitalia and
cannot replicate it when transforming into a new body. Organizations trying to root out face-stealer
spies therefore often use anatomical spot checks. This physical absence is ordinarily sufficient to
identify a face-stealer, barring rare anatomical conditions, but ordinary anatomy cannot clear a
suspect because other kinds of shapeshifters may retain it.

Their classification as a subspecies of shapeshifter is also debated, as they can be children of
exclusively human parents. It's theorized that the ability to shapeshift evolved independently from
mutations from exposure to large amounts of magical energy, instead of being in the same lineage as
[Doppelgangers](doppelganger.mdx).

## Faces

Face-Stealers call their assumed forms **faces**. Realizing a face involves more than reproducing
the original person's body: a Face-Stealer may still lack the person's personality, opinions, mores,
and other social knowledge. During the Rannek Admiralty infiltration, one captive called such an
incompletely realized face “spare.” This described that specific face's incompleteness and is not a
general Face-Stealer term for assumed forms.

Face-Stealers serving the
[Reformed Church of the Divine Masochist](../organizations/reformed-church-of-the-divine-masochist.mdx)
commonly carry false teeth filled with poison. If captured, they are expected to bite down on the
tooth and die rather than divulge information. This is an operational practice associated with
Reformist agents, not an inherent Face-Stealer trait.
