**Changelings** are cross-breeds of
[Doppelgangers](doppelganger.mdx) and a different humanoid.
Unlike doppelgangers, they do not have telepathic and empathic abilities
that allow doppelgangers to more completely transform into the character they
wish to impersonate.
They are also limited by the number of different persons they can transform into
over their lifetime.
