**Doppelgangers** are creatures that can change their form into any other shape,
typically, but not limited to, humanoids. They have short-range telepathic and
empathic abilities that allow them to delve deep into the mind of the creature
they wish to impersonate so that they might act more closely to that creature.

- Have telepathy
- can transform to anything at any time
