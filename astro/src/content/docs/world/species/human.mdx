Humans are the most adaptable and ambitious of the common races. Whatever drives
them — ambition, idealism, wanderlust — humans are the innovators, the
achievers, and the pioneers of the world. Their drive and diversity allow them
to spread across nearly every corner of the world.

## Common Human Stats

Guards are the most common armed humans encountered in settlements, posted at
gates, patrolling streets, or protecting merchants.

<StatBlock creature={frontmatter.creatureStats.guard} />

Veterans are seasoned warriors who have survived many campaigns. They are often
hired as bodyguards, mercenaries, or city watch captains.
