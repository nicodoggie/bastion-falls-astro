The _Drekavac_ are undead creatures made from aborted fetuses or the bodies of
dead infants. They are created by Hags of the 
[Children of the Coven](../../../organizations/children-of-the-coven.mdx)
by covering the cadavers in a special dough, then baking them in a special oven.

The Drekavac are used as servants by the Hags, to gather raw materials, 
information, or perform assassinations on moonless nights.
