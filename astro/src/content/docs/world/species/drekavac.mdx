The _drekavac_ are shadow-bound undead creatures that resemble small children, but with limbs and
heads shaped like rotting animals like chickens, dogs and cats.

They are said to be made by hags from aborted fetuses or the bodies of dead infants.
They replace the most decayed parts of the body with various animal parts and cover them with a
special dough. After baking in a hag's oven, the grotesque creature is brought to unlife as servants
of the night. They're often given unsavory tasks by their masters, such as gathering rotten meat and
ingredients from noxious swamps and graveyards.

While typically solitary, drekavacs can congregate into ravenous swarms that overwhelm their prey
with sheer numbers, acting as a single monstrous entity driven by a collective hunger.
