An _urdefolcin_ is known in Seneran tradition as an ancient protector of forests and the communities
that depend upon them. Those who have encountered one describe a large creature tied to living wood,
capable of slipping between trees and tearing prey apart with tooth and claw.

When a forest requires defenders, an urdefolcin may “plant” Shambling Mounds within it. How this is
done—and whether it resembles cultivation, creation, or some other process—remains unknown.

Their full nature remains uncertain. Their origins, biology, and masks are unresolved; the creature
statistics below record observed capabilities and a game-mechanical classification rather than a
complete in-world taxonomy.
