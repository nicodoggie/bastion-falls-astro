## Meeting of the Four Pillars

[Rosemunda Parvèse](../../characters/rosemunda-parvese.mdx) called for
a [Meeting of the Four](../../events/meeting-of-the-four.mdx) a the
behest of [Minfilia Diaz](../../characters/minfilia-diaz.mdx) as
a condition in their [Machado](../../families/machado.mdx) estate.

The activities of the [Free Theater](../../organizations/free-theater.mdx) in
[Raibon Island](../../locations/raibon-island.mdx) Including, but not limited to their involvement
with:

- Apgarian Military
- Mehicanian Military
- [Monsignor Leonidas](../../characters/adelberto-leonidas.mdx)

  [Reformed Church of the Divine Masochist](../../organizations/reformed-church-of-the-divine-masochist.mdx).

[L'Escargot](../../locations/lescargot.mdx), in
[Port Rainoso](../../locations/port-rainoso.mdx)'s Main Street. The entire place was closed to the
public in such short notice, as it was agreed upon only at 7 in the morning of the same day.

### Who is Benoit Marconne?

[Benoit Marconne](../../characters/benoit-marconne.mdx) is an employee of the
[Parvese Houses of Pleasures](../../organizations/parvese-houses-of-pleasures.mdx) in Miller
Street. Rosemunda brought him along supposedly as a reward for a job well-done. He had fully
intended to enjoy the entire experience, so he joined the Chicken Chase game. There he met Pecorine,
who so enamored him, he made a bet with her. If he won, he would take her to dinner the next
day--otherwise, at her request, he would meet her for lunch wearing a chinchilla costume. Either
way, he would meet her outside the House of Pleasures, so he considered either a win, minor
embarrasment nonwithstanding. He lost of course, and so he went to meet with her and Anton.

They had found that Benoit is a normal boy who just wants to experience what life was like outside
the House of Pleasures. He'd like to meet new people who were not paying customers, but people who
had a genuine interest in friendship. It seemed to the pair, that Rosemunda was monitoring almost
his every movement, and that he was being repressed, but he was almost absolute in his graciousness
towards his "savior". So they thought to invite him to the
[Mirian Trading Company](../../organizations/mirian-trading-company.mdx) Fight Night--there, they
plan to tell him about Rosemunda's plan to take over his body.
