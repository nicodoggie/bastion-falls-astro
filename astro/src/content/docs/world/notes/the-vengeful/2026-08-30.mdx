## Sanctuary and alliance

Hellion's hag bargain created a protected gathering point where allies, mercenaries, and noble
relatives assembled, shared intelligence, and arranged magical transport.

## The Maiden's Field crisis

Evidence increasingly reframed the Hicklander uprising as a Reformist-engineered conflict involving
stolen wages, infiltrators, parasites, sabotage, black powder, and Crispin Mallow's personal
vendetta against the Maidenfeld bloodline.

## Rescues and infiltration

The party secured Robert's children and their mother, disabled a scrying token, captured attackers,
found Lance operating under the Rance disguise, and learned that Castle Rannek and its court wizards
had been compromised.

## Search for Angel's father

Locate Creature repeatedly pointed east or northeast toward Maidensport and Maiden's Bay. A second
connection made from Mallow's biological suturing thread gave the same bearing, supporting the
belief that Mallow and Angel's captive father were traveling together.

## Departure

The party prepared to teleport toward the search area, leaving defenders at the keep while Angel,
Oren, Sir Brastok, Malmann, and still-disputed additional companions readied themselves to intercept
Mallow and rescue the captives.

## Boundaries

- The apparent Maya Bank, Maya credit, GCash, food-ordering, and similar table-side exchanges are
  treated as out-of-character unless later evidence establishes otherwise.
- The redacted scene claim remains redacted and is not reconstructed or inferred.
- Physical speaker labels do not establish represented-character identity; uncertain character
  attribution remains explicitly unresolved.
- Conflicting or phonetically unstable proper nouns are preserved as confirmation needs rather than
  normalized into canon.
- Allegations about Hicklanders, Maidenfeld violence, Valyriot's complicity, Garward's family, and
  other disputed conduct are reported as claims made in the scene, not settled fact.
- The Baron and other possibly controlled individuals are not treated as willing Reformists without
  confirmation.
- Locate Creature provides directional evidence, not proof of an exact destination or proof that its
  target is uncompromised.
- Reports about Balimbano, Victor Woodward, Isana Walburton, Rothschild, and planar cosmology remain
  attributed where the underlying truth is disputed.
- Sexual, reproductive, torture, parasite, and forced-reproduction material is summarized only to
  the extent necessary to preserve plot and threat information.
- Decoder loops, prolonged silence, missing speech, and acoustically unrecoverable phrases are not
  promoted into narrative facts.
- The scene summaries contain extensive playful, sexual, publication-related, and gambling table
  talk that should not be treated as a mission objective unless explicitly connected to one.
- Caller-owned cache identity, provenance, campaign, and session date must be replaced or derived by
  the caller.
