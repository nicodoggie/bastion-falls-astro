## Recruitment and the Road from the Cave

- The group asked after Matthew Malthrek, a university geography student whom their contact seldom
  saw, then recruited a self-described wild-magic sorcerer despite immediate skepticism about the
  recruit's control.
- Conversation ranged across Angel's guards, a favorite Dorothy Marie Campbell doll, five
  rechargeable orbs, and the search for the local hags.
  - One bargainer said their fourth child was promised in marriage to a hag's child and wondered
    whether the hags had foreseen or engineered that future.
- A Spring Court informant recalled a former ruler, a human archfey who became queen, a vast
  carnival at probably Grandiflora Island, and a woman left in a state neither death nor ordinary
  sleep.
- Before departure, the sorcerer disabled exhausting cave wards. Outside, the group found many
  aberration corpses and heard of an experimental farm that fed aberrant remains to plants.
  - A researcher shared a chibi-like beholder sketch copied from an immovable family book; it seemed
    to omit disintegration and death rays.
  - The researcher offered notes, while another person mentioned a magical teacher with an unclear
    connection to Baba Yaga.
- Discussion of Rebecca established little securely beyond her presence in Wonderland, knowledge of
  Hick, and competing offers of care or university refuge. Allegations that a proposed caretaker
  might experiment on her remained unconfirmed.
- Near Castle Malthrek, the travelers reached hot, misty, pockmarked volcanic highlands.
  - Lime confirmed she was a druid.
  - The group considered Wild Shape, a flying broom, a rare flying carpet, and summoned transport.
  - After learning that the apparent Gerta Moon was actually her successor among the white witches,
    they selected giant spiders as discreet all-terrain mounts and departed with the spiders
    following the carpet.
  - Gerta had been the previous Spirit of the Moon, had been involved in the war, and was deceased.

- The travelers confirmed that frogfolk truly protected the bog and revisited suspicions that a
  traveler's mother had intended to implant or deliver an aberrant parasite before Minfilia
  intervened.
- Riding the carpet toward volcanic pools near an uncertainly named mountain, they discussed the
  hags' continuing protection of the pools despite the Celestial Blood's broken agreement.
  - The operator tested the giant spiders' speed while assuring alarmed passengers that the carpet,
    found in ruins, would not drop them.
- Along the way, the group discussed an Orb of Absorption, Eastonton's unusual undead, Minfilia's
  spellcasting dead, a mysterious resurrection method, Fliokyshelan runecrafted prosthetics, and an
  exceptional bard protected by an amulet of nondetection and a
  <Item name="ring of mind shielding" src="dmg" />.
- The carpet crossed increasingly craggy mountains and geysers before reaching a soot-black volcano
  and interconnected springs surrounded by extraordinary vegetation.
  - Peaceful blue globular sprites gathered moisture and protected the travelers from scalding
    geyser spray.
- At the main pool, the Duchess entered the warm water and three women appeared, confronting her
  over celestial blood, old promises, and a broken deal.
  - Lime argued that someone of celestial blood had broken the agreement, not the Duchess
    intentionally.
  - The hags at the Thralmal Pools were descendants of the Children of the Coven, the Feywild
    Faithful Hags in Senera—not the Hags of Hama, also called white witches.
- The visitors sought help and shared discoveries from Senera: a concealed parasite had corrupted an
  unnamed woman, the Reformist Church had brought it, and hags aligned with the Children of the
  Crone had knowingly cooperated.
  - Whether mind flayers or an elder brain controlled the parasites remained unknown.

  the Crone. They still intended to honor an earlier blood pact inherited by a boy.
  - If he chose to keep the inherited promise, they offered one of their most desirable.
    The exchange included an objection to glamours and the reply that desire was a glamour
    unto itself; neither acceptance nor fulfillment was established.

  before embracing the visitors.
  - Moonlight pulled at three visitors' true forms, but she prevented the transformations.
  - The witches recognized a human visitor as touched by the Mother's essence and clarified that
    Emily was an elf from the Feywild.
- The witches longed to return to the Mother and mentioned Tiffany, who had bargained with hags
  while seeking a way for an unidentified group to return to her.
- The visitors warned that piercing the veil could admit aberrations.
  - Emily described parasites that spread through hosts over months or perhaps a year.
  - She cited research associated with [Murkendraw](../../locations/murkendraw.mdx), naming the
    Awakened Library and Marshview. The exchange did not securely establish the library's exact
    location or the subject of the access restriction.

    complete a mind-flayer transformation.
  - The group recalled a Reformist woman becoming a grick after an infiltration involving a Seneran
    diplomat and a maddening mushroom from Moreaux Island.
  - Of the two high-ranking diplomats, one had died. The survivor,
    [Edmar Pritchett](../../characters/edmar-pritchett.mdx), had been returned to Senera.

  guardian had visited unspecified lands.

    serenity of mind; no quantified healing effect was established in this exchange.

  had an opposite personality and chose a different name. Emily associated the duplication with
  a candy from Sentimental Island.

- Discussion of hexbloods established that six had existed, two remained in the Feywild, and two

  eventually to return to the Mother's embrace.
