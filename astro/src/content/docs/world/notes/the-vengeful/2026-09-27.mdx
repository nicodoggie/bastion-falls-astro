## After the Ready-Room Meeting

- Uther Rannek, Marquess of Rannek, and Marchioness Maria Rannek remained with the party and offered
  practical support.
  - The Marquis would contact the Marshal and arrange investigative sailors if wanted, while
    intending to send a contingent even without the party.
  - He also offered to seek other capable allies.
- The crew invited the nobles to visit its wider network through the old teleportation circles.
  - The visitors learned that the crew includes foreign elves, fiendish and celestial members, and
    effective young-looking casters.
  - Tiphanie had left to help the Crown Prince with an undisclosed objective.
  - Minfilia Diaz was described as an ancient pregnant necromancer bonded to Oscar Savoy. She had
    given Gwyniviere a small emerald object that controlled skeletons, used undead labor to build an
    Apgar hospital, and had also employed undead in more dubious enterprises.
- Captain Hedwig Rosenkrantz sought information about local elves and possible drow, but the local
  nobles had only recently learned that elves were real; even Kelthyr Kulok's half-elven ancestry
  surprised them.
- Discussion ranged across rare archfey ascensions, uncertain divine avatars, and a distant
  southeastern ruling class whose members claim godhood.
- The party planned to travel to Eastonton with the Duke, Duchess, court wizard, marshal, and a
  small retinue.
  - Minfilia hoped to make Eastonton a central base for associates linked to La Camaraderie de la
    Mer.
  - The port lay beyond the Gulf of Inespaul's tighter controls, though it was exposed to severe
    weather.
  - Angel remained behind to rest.
- Oren Bell initially declined the trip so he could continue investigating people connected to
  Tristan. He had not met Minfilia, who was in Thrantorbury, but later joined the Eastonton
  delegation.
- The group remained suspicious of the Maidenfelds, their secrecy, and rumors that they possessed
  dragon eggs.
  - Stories of an Orb of Dragonkind created by Tiamat were treated as uncertain lore, not as
    information connected to Tiphanie.
  - Someone had apparently been returned to the age when their wizard training began while retaining
    power, following a request to Baba Yaga connected to locally trapped dragons.
- On the way to the base, Hellion found Crispin in a tree singing his dragon lullaby and spoke to
  him in Draconic.
  - Hellion invited him to Eastonton and warned that his old acquaintances there would neither
    recognize him nor be recognizable to him.
  - Hellion mentioned Miya Campbell (Dorothy Marie Campbell). Crispin agreed to accompany the
    group only if Sandor Kethlas also came.
  - Crispin descended acrobatically, catching another branch and swinging down.
  - Hellion suggested that he could become a monk rather than a caster. Crispin misunderstood
    “monk” as “monkey,” then assumed monks were bald and prayed. Hellion identified himself as a
    monk and cautioned Crispin against judging others by appearance.

- Crispin argued that a shapeshifted dragon mother would have been the monster, not him, and
  suspected magic was compelling his confessions.
  - He was warned that Auraxx's boon may have prevented him from killing Angel.
  - Sandor confirmed that he had received a <Item name="ring of mind shielding" src="dmg" />.

  - The marshal was briefing troops on an undisclosed operation and agreed to accompany the

  - Hellion used five enhancer orbs to power the prepared teleportation circle without exhausting
    the party's spellcasters.

  - Beyond them lay Oberon's oasis, with pixies and wary dryads. The dryads were especially

  Uther Rannek, warned that approaching the orb could be fatal, and used

## The Orb of Absorption

- The delegation discussed the Maidenfelds' spice network, their senior contacts, and the secrecy
  surrounding their sources.
  - No communication had arrived from Thrantorbury while Lieutenant Lilia's operation remained under
    radio silence.
  - The surviving Reformist prisoners remained restrained or magically asleep after several captives
    died by suicide; resistance to the Essence of Dream appeared to be growing.
- Marcus Parnassus, an intelligent Kalassarian skeleton raised by Minfilia, was said to understand
  the vast local Orb of Absorption.
  - A skeletal dog beside him was variously called Dottie, Dorothy, or Dummy.
  - Someone claimed knowledge of a weakness that could defeat Minfilia but refused to disclose it.
- Hellion led the visitors toward the control room.
  - Everyone removed magical equipment and placed it in identity-bound lockers before approaching.
  - Hellion produced numerous concealed items; Sandor stored a ring and rope; Crispin stored an
    amulet; the nobles removed rings, runes, and other possessions.
  - Miya stayed in the control room while Jessica guided the others toward the boundary.
- An unidentified girl explained that the orb drains magic and that Reformists had fed children and
  captured adult spellcasters to it.
  - She identified herself as a sorcerer and rejected assumptions that her relationship with a god
    defined her magic.
- The party speculated that the Maidenfeld keep might conceal Kalassarian machinery, underground
  chambers, submarines, or access to an underwater haven.
- Five figures represented the pagan goddesses Minerva, Justicia, Pomona, Volupta, and Amarina,
  associated respectively with civilization, justice, nature, delight, and humility.
  - Divine Masochism syncretizes these goddesses as angels; Minerva is the Angel of Civilization,
    and Justicia the Angel of Justice.
- Jessica pointed out the orb's invisible boundary.
  - Hellion crossed it and convulsed as red wisps were drawn from his spine until a companion pulled
    him back.
  - The effect resembled antimagic and was especially painful to strongly magical beings.
  - The nobles experienced differing levels of discomfort, with the Marquis and Marchioness seeming
    least sensitive.
- Crispin reacted differently: he felt lighter near the orb, resisted being removed, and wanted to
  go beneath it.
  - The group persuaded him to withdraw by reminding him that Angel wanted him safely returned and
    still intended to reach Bastion.
  - His apparent affinity with the orb remained unexplained.

## The Paladinate and the Rescued Elves

- Jessica explained that the Orb of Absorption continuously drains raw magic and routes it through
  conduits to power the ruins and their traps.
  - Reformists had used it to charge other orbs and feed an aberration.
  - The orb was currently controlled, though it had previously run wild.
  - Ranking Reformists were now imprisoned in the ruins; Crispin, projecting the office of Bearer of
    the Candle, was the notable exception.
- The party left the orb, recovered its equipment, and inspected the Paladinate.
  - Miya's seized golem stood among the paladins; creating such constructs normally required
    advanced magic and a specialized manual.
  - Crispin had reportedly built still more sophisticated golems, including one indistinguishable
    from a human.
- Jessica warned that Angel intended to reach Bastion, which she believed was tied to the origin of
  magic.
  - Bastion was surrounded by an exponentially growing Orb of Power reportedly fifty miles across.
  - Reformists were consolidating control nearby as refugees fled.
  - Jessica urged the party to delay Angel rather than support an expedition too small to succeed.
- The Paladinate had been rapidly rededicated from the Divine Masochist to the Great Erected One.
  - Stained glass, livery, and movable fixtures had been replaced within days.
  - Lord Boyle was mortified, while Garward's mother—reportedly another hag—served the paladins.
- At Rannekford, Hellion visited survivors living in tents.
  - Sister Pira led healing at the pool with Gwyniviere's help.
  - Hellion intervened when clergy hesitated to treat a devilish-looking girl and personally gave
    her food and drink.
  - He then approached frightened rescued elves, speaking Elvish and offering food without
    pressuring them to respond.
- Hellion recognized a mud-covered child who closely resembled Maerwen Lunartear and quietly called
  her “Meru” before dismissing the resemblance as impossible.
  - The familiar-looking children were young twins with sandy green-tinged hair.
  - Sharin normally handled outsiders and was usually accompanied by the pixie Daggerheart.
  - One twin was secretly a cleric of the Green Lord, but her missing deer or stag headgear was her
    focus. Magical draining and trauma also prevented her from casting.
  - Their original belongings had not been returned, and they remained weak after unsuitable food
    and captivity.
  - They fed the youngest children first and appeared to communicate silently through eye contact.
- Hellion contacted Emily because Kelthyr and Luana were unavailable.
  - Thrantorbury was engulfed in an enormous antimagic field.
  - Oren was supposedly barred from a possible elven refuge but might guide the group while
    remaining outside its protections.
- Sir Aldric Torward had been captured without his northern forces knowing.
  - The group considered exploiting that ignorance and interrogating him about northern settlements.
  - A reported plan to convert drow into Reformists was condemned as dangerously naïve.
  - Speculation connected the rescued children to a shard of the Emerald Shield of Spring and
    continued to question why one looked almost exactly like Maerwen.

## Boundaries

- Player identities, account identities, and private speaker labels are excluded from the
  reader-facing notes.
- Uncertain names, relationships, locations, motives, magical identities, and creature types remain
  explicitly qualified rather than canonized.
- Rumors about nobles, marriages, incest, hidden identities, and conspiracies are recorded as
  speculation or invented hag-barter, not established fact.
- The rescued people's captivity and abuse are described non-graphically while preserving the
  consequences relevant to play.
- Crispin's child identity is distinguished from his adult history without resolving moral or
  metaphysical questions not settled in the evidence.
- The Maidenfelds' ownership of submarines was already acknowledged. The Haven's underwater
  approach and unhatched chromatic dragon eggs had been disclosed before this session, but the
  party had not reached the sanctuary; its full protections, power source, and the family's
  intentions remained unresolved.
- No source code or compendium tag is invented for uncertain custom items, artifacts, class
  features, or unidentified magic.
