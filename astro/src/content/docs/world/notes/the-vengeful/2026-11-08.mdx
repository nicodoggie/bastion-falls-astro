## Descent to the Hidden Pools

- Luana tried to cover herself with a cloak, only for it to disappear; using her hair instead was
  suggested.
- Tiphanie asked where the group should go next. Marlion directed them downward toward a nearby
  pool, although nobody knew whether the king or his enemies remained there.
- A female companion lent Kelthyr <Item name="dimensional shackles" src="dmg" /> to prevent the king—or another dangerous opponent—from teleporting.
  - The group understood the shackles to block teleportation generally, not only planar travel.
  - Their ally claimed the remaining enemies below, and perhaps their souls or body parts, for
    herself.
- The party discussed Narmaya's supposed sons.
  - Marquez was said to count as her son because she turned him.
  - Dante Le Fanu, Narmaya's adopted child, was recalled as having died.
  - The shackles had reportedly been used on Marquez when he could not control his feeding on
    guards; the speaker said this was no longer a problem.
- The lightly defended route made the party suspicious that its occupants had underestimated
  infiltration or trusted a powerful guardian.
- Old Kalassarian architecture gave way to a natural cavern of bare rock, lichen, mushrooms, and
  audible running water.
- Tiphanie recognized an arch of tangled branches and vines as a portal. Beyond it lay a cavern
  filled with clear, refreshing spring water.
- Under water falling from the ceiling stood King Alitar I Dragonheart, emaciated, dressed in ruined
  royal clothing, and muttering to himself.
  - Kelthyr and Marlion hurried toward him; Alitar is Marlion's father and Kelthyr's stepfather.
  - Tiphanie carried Luana on her broom and watched their approach.
- A bubble-like magical barrier repelled Marlion's hand while allowing the waterfall through.
  - The man repeatedly defied an unseen presence, insisting it would never possess his land or mind.
  - The party hesitated to break the barrier because doing so might expose his infected mind or
    cause him to lose the struggle.
- Marlion addressed him as Father, but he suspected an enemy was using Steinar's face—Steinar being
  Marlion's true name.
  - Marlion drew the Topaz Sword of Summer from its sheath as proof, but even its correctly placed
    topaz failed to convince the king.
  - He said the barrier had resisted three triads and would protect him until his frail body failed.
- During a partly unclear Draconic exchange, someone threatened to call through an orb of dragonkind
  and break his mind. The king asked whether that would kill him before he became an undead
  abomination or enemy servant.
- The king confirmed that a parasite crawled inside his mind, while his questioner said he had begun
  protecting himself too late.
- Kelthyr asked after his mother, Queen Murwes Dragonheart, but received no clear answer.
  - In a separate exchange about a daughter, the king insisted that she would never be found; the
    woman's identity remained unclear.
- A woman invoked dragon eggs hidden near Maiden's Field. The king's startled response appeared to
  confirm that the eggs, or a related secret, were real.
- The woman offered to extract the parasite without killing him. He accused her side of implanting
  it and warned that hags exploit loopholes between their powers and promises.
- Tiphanie declared that she wanted neither his land nor his world, only justice for her
  own kind after his people hunted and mistreated them.
- Tiphanie condemned the transformation of Auraxx into a mortal body, challenged the king over
  horrors inflicted on dragons, and transformed into a silver dragon.
  - The pools radiated intense raw magic. Kelthyr suppressed a wild-magic surge, while another
    companion resisted magical sleep through her patron's protection.
  - Much of the confrontation occurred in Draconic, which Kelthyr barely understood.
  - Companions expressed surprise and questioned whether the dragon was truly Tiphanie. She had
    already taken that form in the preceding landing battle, but their dialogue still reflected
    unfamiliarity with her draconic nature.
  - Marlion noted that Dragonhearts are taught Draconic and accused others of withholding
    important secrets.
- Tiphanie named several dragons connected with her Feywild history. Their names were not
  transcribed reliably, and which of them might be in Senera remained unconfirmed.
  - Some reportedly assumed their draconic forms only as a final act and died trying to preserve
    peace.
  - She said they had attempted what she was now doing, but she would not die for the mortals'
    realm.
- Tiphanie declared her intention to go to the rumored eggs and roared; the exchange with the king
  continued.

## The Deep Levels and the Conduit-Fed Blight

- The party followed a strange green, forest-like impression deeper underground. It intensified into
  overwhelming nature magic mixed with raw wild energy, with an undead cambion guiding the descent.
- The party healed and cleaned the rescued woman's facial injuries.
  - Probably Minfilia introduced herself, Oscar as her soul mate, Erik Malmann, and Eskabeth.
  - The woman identified herself as Madeline and was distinguished from Maude, Garward's earlier
    wife who had died.
- Someone sensed another entity inside Madeline, raising the possibility of a parasite; Madeline
  denied being pregnant.
  - The party told her that Garward had abused and abandoned her, invited her to accompany them as a
    witness, and announced their intention to kill him.
  - <Spell name="lesser restoration" src="phb" /> eased some of her distress, but she still needed physical support.
  - Probably Eskabeth noticed something unusual about her, and the group tentatively proposed that
    Madeline might be a hexblood.
- The basement had been constructed over an older concealed passage whose protections no longer
  functioned.
  - Someone below activated magical conduits, launching bolts from the walls.
  - Oscar and a nearby companion narrowly avoided them while Eskabeth and Madeline remained farther
    back.
- Minfilia anchored <Spell name="hallow" src="phb" /> to each of three gems, intending to prevent Garward from teleporting.
  - The gems then served as power sources to maintain the effects, rather than storing spells for
    later release.
- The party entered a deep cylindrical chamber resembling the complex beneath Eastonton and saw an
  enormous unidentified object glowing black and purple below.
- Ten animated armors ambushed them from both sides of the balcony.
  - Some threw expanding spheres capable of enclosing and paralyzing victims.
  - One sphere was deflected, Erik Malmann was trapped, and Eskabeth resisted another.
  - Other armors struck Oscar for at least 8 damage and later landed a critical hit whose damage was
    reported inconsistently; attacks against Eskabeth missed.
- The party expected its undead forces to handle the armors.
  - After Eskabeth floated clear, two armors tried to push the sphere holding Malmann over the
    balcony.
  - The undead disrupted them, a caster blew the enclosure away from the edge, and a woman dispelled
    it to free Malmann.
- Once Malmann recovered, a companion harshly taunted someone about moving on before marrying Sister
  Pira.
- Leaving the armor battle behind, the party found a familiar mechanism with two sets of buttons.
  - It was a magical lift marked with Kalassarian numerals, offering nine levels and initially
    displaying 1.
  - The group selected 9 and descended rapidly.
- Level 9 opened onto rotting, lesion-covered plant growth.
  - Oscar compared it to plants he had produced when first made a revenant.
  - Roots clung to copper conduits and drew magic from them.
  - Thick, throbbing branches blocked a partly open doorway.
  - <Spell name="speak with plants" src="phb" /> produced only scrambled, incoherent answers.
  - An observer detected hag magic but rejected the idea that the growth itself resulted from
    another hag bargain.
- Madeline clung to a companion and breathed heavily despite showing no obvious immediate injury.
- Oscar cast <Spell name="blight" src="phb" /> on the growth.
  - Conduit-fed resilience kept it from drying completely, but he cleared most of the obstruction
    and began hacking through the rest.
- The party considered planting a seed from Dorothy Marie Campbell, known as Miya, to counter the
  blight, but had not yet chosen its location.

## Garward in the Ruined Library

- The party concluded that the conduit-fed plants wanted magic but were receiving the wrong kind.
  - With Eskabeth Kilsward's cautious approval, likely Oscar planted a seed from the Green Lord's
    shrine.
  - He watered it with water identified as coming from the pools of Thralmals and germinated it with <Spell name="druidcraft" src="phb" />.
  - The seed rooted successfully but was expected to grow slowly.
- Beyond the doorway lay an extensive former library whose shelves and books were being torn apart
  by invasive roots.
  - The destruction prompted concern about Hedwig Rosenkrantz's likely reaction.
  - Living entities watched the party and hid when noticed. They appeared frightened, although
    contradictory narration left their disposition uncertain.
- The party found Garward Eastonton alone and reading.
  - He recognized Minfilia Diaz and Madeline, whom he called his wife.
  - He claimed he had invited the parasite willingly rather than losing to it.
  - He said his soul was too fractured to permit undeath, although another visitor sensed that it
    remained intact.
  - He claimed to have an escape option and one of the boons associated with the Dragon Hearts and
    Maidenfeld.
  - Discussion suggested a seasonal artifact or brooch linked to winter, but Garward said he did not
    have it on his person. A visitor proposed that an unfinished bargain prevented it from becoming
    his; that was not a confirmed account of its ownership.
- Threats escalated into combat while Minfilia grinned at Garward.
  - Erik Malmann received <Spell name="freedom of movement" src="phb" /> before his turn.
  - The table later ruled that it granted him advantage against a frightened effect.
- A combatant summoned a reaper-shaped <Spell name="spiritual weapon" src="phb" /> and hit Garward for 26 force damage with an attack total of 27.
- A separate reaction forced an unidentified character to make a Wisdom save. Despite a reported 27,
  the character took 6 damage, reduced to 3 by an unidentified resistance.
- Likely Garward cast <Spell name="banishment" src="phb" /> on Oscar, making him disappear.
  - Garward then used Ringing Decree to command Malmann to flee.
  - Malmann failed despite his granted advantage and became frightened.
- Malmann attacked Garward reluctantly, held back by the loyalty and respect he had once owed him.
  - He still hoped that, once Garward's parasite was removed, Garward would prove to be the decent
    man he remembered.
  - An object clanked to the floor.
  - Garward threatened to break Malmann's neck unless he stood down; another speaker protested that
    Malmann was only a rookie.
- Garward succeeded on a DC 18 Constitution save against <Spell name="blight" src="phb" /> and took 23 damage from the halved 47-point roll.
  - A <Spell name="spiritual weapon" src="phb" /> attacked again with a resolved total of 22.
  - Garward used <Spell name="misty step" src="phb" />, drank a green liquid, and raised fears that he might ascend or escape accountability.
    - Minfilia's Hallow gems had not yet been deployed around him when he teleported.
  - A woman attempted <Spell name="hold person" src="phb" />, but Garward spent his final legendary resistance after rolling a 3.
- Garward used Lay on Hands, charged Minfilia, missed once with his longsword, and hit with a 25 on
  the second attack; its final damage was not preserved reliably.
- Minfilia goaded Garward about drinking the green liquid.
- Malmann used Inspiring Smite and landed a critical Divine Smite for 33 damage.
  - Inspiring Smite granted 13 temporary hit points to an unidentified ally.
- Eskabeth launched a projectile-like attack formed from her lengthened fingernails, striking and
  knocking Garward down.
  - He succeeded on the Constitution save and avoided its apparent poison-related vomiting effect.
  - Oscar remained banished and was expected to return after seven more rounds.
- The party attempted to use <Spell name="protection from evil and good" src="phb" /> against Garward's condition.
  - A table ruling required a DC 10 check despite the spell normally requiring no roll, and Garward
    resisted it.
- An unidentified character restrained Garward with a Strength total of 19 against his 14, allowing
  the group to administer Sapphire's vial of celestial blood.
- Garward shook as though fighting something internally.
  - Green fluid foamed from his mouth and evaporated.
  - A parasite forced its way out through his ear, badly injuring him and causing bleeding.
