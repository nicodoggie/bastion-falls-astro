## The Vengeful Open Hooks

This is the living tracker for unresolved Vengeful campaign questions. Session notes still keep
their own `Open Hooks` sections as point-in-time records of what was open at that point in play;
this page tracks the current carry-forward state and can be updated when later sessions settle,
narrow, or retire a hook.

Use this page for questions that need future attention, not as a planning scratchpad. Broader prep,
scenario design, and speculative campaign planning can stay in `threads/`.
Read each question alongside its current boundary when planning: editorial deferrals are not
in-world objectives, and settled milestones are not promises of a future repeat.

### Status Key

- **Active:** still relevant to the current campaign state.
- **Pending confirmation:** likely answer exists, but needs transcript, audio, or Nico confirmation.
- **Dormant:** unresolved, but not currently driving play.
- **Settled:** resolved by later play or canon; keep only when the resolution is useful to remember.
- **Rejected artifact:** transcription noise, table chatter, or a false hook.
