## Summary

### Castle Malthrek and Eastonton

- Castle Malthrek is substantially under party control after the capture and sedation of its
  Reformist personnel.
  - Father Karam Dunweather, Comtesse Aveline Ranlis, Count Godwin Ranlis, Carissa, and scores of
    other Reformists and infiltrators are in custody.
  - The party is separating willing collaborators from parasite hosts, changelings, and coerced
    staff rather than punishing everyone indiscriminately.
  - Their detection vial contains a living parasite preserved with *Death Ward*. The captive
    parasite reacts to the presence of other hosts.
  - Mark Malthrek is being considered as Duke Ellington's successor, with Emily Hazeldine's future
    role in the family and duchy increasingly entwined with that question.
- Emily and her allies began moving roughly ninety captured Reformists to Eastonton in guarded,
  sedated teleportation-circle batches.
  - Henson Eastonton and nineteen prisoners were confirmed in one successful transfer.
  - Miya and Lord Boyle escorted a later batch, while Henson and Jessica coordinated arrivals in
    Eastonton.
- Duke Ellington Malthrek's parasite was removed at the Travertines.
  - Kate Helsinki protected him with *Death Ward* while Emily used the yellow Orb of Absorption; the
    weakened parasite left through his ear and was secured in a flask.
  - Emily followed with *Greater Restoration*. Ellington briefly recognized his family before
    falling unconscious, and Antoinette received an *Encode Thoughts* rune to help preserve and
    manage his memories.

### Restoring Malthrek

- The party's intended restoration of the duchy includes fairer taxation, relief for Faltir's salt
  miners, support for workers, screened military rebuilding, and religious freedom without Reformist
  control.
- Emily has begun fortifying Castle Malthrek.
  - She has not yet cast *guards and wards* over the main interior.
  - A smaller, inactive *teleportation circle* was found in the cistern-side storage space. Emily
    left it intact and protected it with an alerting *glyph of warding*.
  - At the training grounds, she created a new concealed circle and prepared an Orb of Enhancement
    to renew it.
  - Senera may have inherited laws for such circles from Kalassarian tradition, but any codified
    rules have largely been forgotten or ignored after centuries without magic. Most surviving
    large circles, including Eastonton's, remain within Kalassarian ruins.
  - A circle's practical capacity depends on its size and how many travellers can fit within it.
    Larger Kalassarian circles increased the number transported at once.
  - Orbs of Enhancement slowly recharge from ambient raw magical energy. A Wild Magic sorcerer's
    interaction with that energy can accelerate the process.
- Mark has manifested *Hunter's Mark*, confirming to Emily that his unusual hunting and tracking
  instincts are ranger magic. His **Glass Eye of True Seeing** has also begun revealing hidden
  people and magical auras.

### Faltir and the Ranlis Operation

- Luana Victorique Dubreuil, working publicly as Cinna Moan, and Kelthyr infiltrated Faltir and
  uncovered Reformist exploitation of its salt miners.
  - The Reformists were transmuting salt into black powder and moving it by a prototype submarine.
  - Luana's dream magic enabled nonlethal captures, while Podron—a parasitized changeling tortured
    into impersonating others—impersonated Aveline to help sedate the town's Reformist personnel.
- The party freed Podron, captured Aveline and her household, and recovered the miniature submarine
  in Luana's Bag of Holding.
  - On the road to Castle Malthrek, Podron's impersonation of Ellington and Luana's assumed
    Reformist authority led Godwin, Carissa, and their retinue into submission and capture.
  - Strong brining remains a possible countermeasure against parasite contamination, but its
    reliability is unconfirmed.

### Hag Road, Oren Bell, and the Black Box

- Miya/Dorothy, Lord Boyle Eastonton, and Henson entered the looping Feywild Hag Road of Helga,
  Lucinda, and Kara, the Children of the Crone.
  - Miya invoked old hag law to defeat Kara and protect Boyle and Henson.
  - After Kara's death ended the duel's protection, Helga and Lucinda were bound by their mother;
    Miya granted them mercy deaths and kept their blood and relics in hope that some part of them
    might endure.
- Born Orellian of the Highguard and now choosing the name Oren Bell, he remains burdened by the old
  elf-human war and the Black Box.
  - Emily and Strandiz had found the lead-lined box during the Sea Hags affair. It contains an item
    that slowly draws life, magic, and other resources within a one-mile radius into the Shadowfell.
    Emma once pushed Emily away from the fey crossing during the Parade of the Spirits because the
    box endangered it.
  - Emily's blood-and-breath bargain asks Oren to try living again, form connections, and relinquish
    his fatalism. In return, she agreed to give him the box.
  - If Emily dies before finding a way to return the fey stranded in Senera to the Feywild, Oren may
    open the box and resume his old purpose of destroying humanity.
  - Emily has also called the box her only way to help Aika Miura, whom she regards as a sign of
    hope for fey creatures. Aika's connection to the box and bargain remains unresolved.
  - Luana and Justin entered his repeating war-memory, protected a young elven boy, and cut the
    magical threads through which an elven puppet master directed armies.

### Thrantorbury, the Rannek Marches, and Wider Deployments

- The party's immediate rescue priority is the Thrantorbury time loop involving Minfilia, Fina,
  Oscar, Tiphanie, and the Crown Prince.
  - Lime and Justin are expected to join the rescue. The team plans to arrive outside Thrantorbury
    University and enter quietly rather than risk its defenses.
  - Lord Boyle may remain behind because Garward Eastonton is his brother.
- A parallel Seneran operation may support Angel Rannek and extract Gwyniviere Rannek. Luana,
  Kelthyr, Trajan, Sapphire, and possibly Oren are considered for that side.
- Angel Rannek is reported safe with Branrum Malterdale. John, Rebecca, Trajan, and Sapphire are
  reportedly travelling toward the Rannek Marches.

### Feywild, Family, and Allies

- Sapphire continues to grieve the loss of her right wing to Oberon, the Green Lord. An attempted
  prosthetic does not function while attached, and Emily still sees the shadow-like aura of the
  missing wing.
  - Sapphire's role in a massacre during Arcadia's absence appears tied to a *geas*-like compulsion.
    She remains subject to Feywild punishment while the responsible court, caster, and path to
    redemption remain unresolved.
- Emily is pregnant with twins, likely magical children. Mark's next full moon is fourteen days
  away, and the group intends to let him endure it in an isolated place so that he can learn control
  rather than rely on dimensional shackles.
  - The plan draws on general lycanthropic traditions recorded across multiple books, not a single
    identified manual. Lime, holder of the Spirit of the Moon's power, offered to ease Mark's
    transformation.
- Luana dreams of a colossal green tree where elves live. Oren considers it neither a Tree of Life
  nor a Feywild crossing, though he senses an unusual fey quality in Luana.
- The party remembers that dead spirits in this world gather at four places nearest the Feywild
  instead of crossing into it; Mt Brissimis, Skelmark, and Skelwick are among the named sites.

### Eastonton, Ruins, and Resources

- The Eastonton receiving site lies in or beside Kalassarian ruins, where Kelthyr detected a hidden
  or otherwise unseen *teleportation circle*.
  - Minfilia's undead and Dark Archons hold the site, and the Green Lord's grove stands near the
    circle.
  - Marcus Parnassus, the Kalassarian scholar Minfilia raised as an intelligent skeleton beneath
    Eastonton, may be able to interpret the ruins and their older knowledge.
  - Matzka and Vivian have recovered and returned to work at the orphanage.
- Luana retains the prototype submarine and her Wingwear Glider. Jessica traded rings and a Bag of
  Holding for dolls intended for refugee children, while the group also acquired an Amulet of Proof
  against Detection and Location and a Hat of Disguise.
- *Legally Bare* issue 3.5 is being developed as **Phantasmagoria**, though Luana has deferred work
  during the current crisis.

### Boundaries

- Treat parasite removal as potentially destructive to memories. Ellington's removal was survived
  only with careful protection and restoration; the same procedure is not established as safe for
  every host.
- Preserve the distinction between parasite hosts, changelings, coerced personnel, and willing
  Reformist collaborators when deciding guilt, custody, or treatment.
- Treat Sapphire's massacre as a grave act possibly committed under a *geas*-like compulsion; do not
  settle responsibility on Sapphire alone while the court and caster remain unknown.
- Emily's bargain with Oren says that he may open the Black Box if she dies before returning the fey
  stranded in Senera to the Feywild. It does not explicitly distinguish a revivable death from a
  final death; leave Oren's interpretation open, potentially shaped by whether he has found new
  meaning and connections.
- Do not restrict Aika's title as the Mother to pixies. Many fey accept the title, and Day Court
  statuary also presents her as the Mother, but the title's exact scope and relationship to Baba
  Yaga and the False Crone remain unsettled. Feywild-side canon may require adjudication from the
  player who runs Emily and DMs those Feywild games.
- Use **Mapach** for the Feywild people, some of whom reside in Maple Bridge. Tiphanie's displacer
  beast is named **Shadow**.
- Treat `Rowana` as a likely mistranscription of Luana. Do not create new characters from the
  garbled `Milo` and `Wills` transfer roster; the known escorts were Miya and Lord Boyle. **Matzka**
  is the correct orphanage name.
- Do not treat the interrupted hag terminology, the Thrantorbury/Scalewit discussion, or social and
  comic speculation about dolls, occult practices, and Feywild customs as settled world lore.
