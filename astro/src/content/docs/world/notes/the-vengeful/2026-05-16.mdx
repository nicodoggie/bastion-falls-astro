## Minfilia

- The night after the wedding chaos, the group regathers around Luke's chamber
  around 9:30 PM.
  - Jessica is found in human form; John Malthrek is awake practicing
    handwriting for a spell scroll.
  - Crown Prince Marlion Dragonheart lets Oscar handle the Topaz Sword of
    Summer; it hurts Oscar being an undead revenant.
- Marlion confirms the death of the Bishop of Surrey.
  - He had given the order to release the crew of the Principium from
    the Splits.
  - The group prioritizes finding the keep's teleportation circle, then
    traveling to the University of Senera to investigate intellect devourer
    threats to mages.
- Minfilia and Oscar explore the Kalassarian ruins under Travertine Keep with
  Marlion, split into three paths.
  - Minfilia and Oscar take a spiral staircase; Tiphanie goes down the center;
    Minfilia's skeletons scout the crossbow gallery.
  - An illusion hides a broom closet as a long hallway; Marlion's sword reveals
    it.
  - They find inactive crossbows of energy (+3 heavy crossbows dealing force
    damage), copper conduits, a scrying mirror, an empty infinite-food chest,
    and a magical latrine.
  - Oscar in bear form fails to breach a barricaded door; Minfilia casts
    <Spell name="legend lore" src="phb" /> on the chest.
- Following conduits to the main power source, they find a collapsed cavern
  blocking access.
  - Minfilia clears debris with <Spell name="animate objects" src="phb" />
    and <Spell name="stone shape" src="phb" />.
- Night hags attack near the crossbow gallery with undead babies.
  - Minfilia wrests necromantic control of the babies from the hags.
  - Oscar casts <Spell name="stinking cloud" src="phb" />; Minfilia casts
    <Spell name="insect plague" src="phb" />.
- The party finds and partially restores an old Kalassarian teleportation
  circle with diamond cups and conduit power.
  - Marlion has Tiphanie change the circle's sigil; Minfilia teleports briefly
    to The Scarlet via Andy Anderson, then returns.
  - Minfilia's skeletons and drekavacs guard the working circle afterward.
- After resting, Minfilia casts <Spell name="hallow" src="phb" /> on the keep's
  main building, barring aberrations, hags, infernals, and undead unless
  allowed, with a courage effect so allied hags are not frightened.
- At breakfast, Minfilia assigns Angel to recover the five Emerald Shield of
  Spring shards gifted by Oberon.
  - Angel already holds two; the Reformists hold at least one.
  - Sir Brastok adn Branrum Malterdale will her through Malterdale to locate the
    remaining shards.
- Oscar and Minfilia spend the night near Angel's mother in the Rannek wing;
  Oscar is caught and nearly seized by guards until Angel intervenes.

## Tiphanie

- Deeper halls include a dining chamber and a chapel-like hall with Divine
  Masochist imagery.
  - Marlion and Tiphanie discuss the disappearance of magic in year 466 and its
    return about fifty years ago, coinciding with the Bastion disaster.
  - They theorize about magic being absorbed by growing orbs rather than truly
    annihilated.
- A gem stalker and gem spirits attack; Tiphanie tries to spare them and learns
  they eat gems and burrow.
- An ancient amethyst gem dragon mother blocks the cavern.
  - She fled the Draco-Giant war, survived the waning of magic, and is
    sustained by a central gemstone orb.
  - Tiphanie performs a Rite of the Dragon; gemstalkers devour the mother's
    body after Marlion's breath weapon loosens her grip.
  - The orb radiates power, but copper conduits were eaten; new wiring is
    needed before defenses can be fully restored.
- Tiphanie requests one of the Faithful hag sisters she'd met to find a
  potential wife for Luke. Someone who looks like Katalin Helsinki perhaps
  - Reformists may be letting the party power the keep for their own ends.
  - Luke may attune to the keep's magical systems once the power source is
    restored
- Minfilia proposes a "book club" meeting in one week with Hedwig
  Rosenkrantz to discuss aberrations and the green ascension liquid.
- Marlion offers to garrison a navy battalion before the wedding
  - Tiphanie offers to repair the broken Rannek teleportation circle.
- Jessica, Donmar, Tiphanie, and Marlion will head to the University of
  Thrantorbury; the party waits on Lime before moving on Father Charles Renaldi.

## Lime's group

- While the keep meeting continues, Lime returns to the outer-city chapel of
  the Angel of Humility where she first met Germaine and Adelaide Schulze.
- She is tailed by at least three watchers; Andrew Taminok intercepts one
  assailant before Lime reaches the chapel.
- Germaine Schulze administers Lime's Candle Bearer test of devotion,
  renounce Orthodox heresy and prove commitment to the Reformist creed.
  - The first test is to burn down a random house that holds an Orthodox family.
  - The second test was to find a person in town, of her own choice, and
    dominate them.
    - Lime chooses to dominate Germaine in the chapel, binding him with vines
      and using Herb of the Harem as Oscar taught her.
  - The third test is to get burly, highly-visible man, seduce him, and trick
    them magically into stealing a key to a prison in the local constabulary
    office.
    - The point is to test her ability to use magical manipulation to achieve
      her goals.
    - The fourth test is to kill an urdefolcin and bring its heart to Germaine.
      - Lime refuses to kill the urdefolcin, instead offers Germaine to the
        spirit of the forest as a sacrifice.
  - Germaine mentions that he still has affection for Abraham Schulze.
  - Lime recalls her actual parents, Abraham and Juliette Schulze, before she
    was left in the Gerta Moon's boarding school.

## Finale

- The party converges on the Chapel of the Angel of Humility to confront Father
  Charles Renaldi and Sister Rislas
  - Minfilia beat Rislas as she used the Beacon of Necromantic Energy to raise
    and summon undead in a 200 foot radius. She summons undead insects, animals
    and some zombies from a nearby graveyard.
  - Rislas is incapacitated by Minfilia's undead.
  - She finds a vial of pixie dust in Rislas's pocket. Included is a single
    pixie wing inside as well.
  - They use the pixie dust on a brazier in the chapel's rectory to open a
    portal to a pocket dimension.
  - Inside the pocket dimension are Father Charles Renaldi and Duchess Malthrek,
    who were expecting Germaine and Lime to announce Lime's completion of her
    tests
    - Minfilia and Oscar kill Father Charles Renaldi and incapacitates Malthrek
    - Minfilia summons the pixie who owns the wing, and tells them to collapse
      the pocked dimension.
    - They pull Renaldi and Malthrek out of the pocket dimension.
  - They discover that Renaldi has a parasite in his brain. Tiphanie is
    convinced that it is a Mind Flayer parasite.
