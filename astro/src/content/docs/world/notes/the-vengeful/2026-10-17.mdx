## The Barrier Around Thrantorbury

- Four companions examined the barrier enclosing Thrantorbury University.
  - <Spell name="identify" src="phb" /> revealed an unusually large <Spell name="wall of force" src="phb" /> that was not technically an abjuration.
  - The wall directly adjoined the time loop, leaving no physical gap, and appeared to be
    continuously powered.
  - The devastated town remained visible outside, but conditions within the university were unknown;
    the professor controlling the time-loop object was considered hostile.
- The group considered entering with <Spell name="dimension door" src="phb" /> and deploying the Orb of Absorption inside, but could not establish that this was safe.
  - They feared the orb could grow uncontrollably and eventually consume the town.
  - Emily had once redirected its energy through conduits during the pocket-dimension incident, and those conduits remained in a <Item name="bag of holding" src="dmg" />.
  - They discussed using conduits with repeated <Spell name="teleportation circle" src="phb" /> castings or a <Spell name="globe of invulnerability" src="phb" />. Concern about repeatedly using seventh-level magic led them toward the circle plan, although neither Eastonton nor a Malthrek-associated destination was selected.
  - The tentative procedure was to construct the apparatus, connect a conduit, introduce the Orb of
    Absorption, and leave it guarded from Reformists with repulsion magic.
- The Orb of Repulsion briefly opened a narrow hole, but the wall regenerated when the orb was
  withdrawn.
  - The opening was too small for the group; sending Luana or another small person through would
    leave them isolated.
  - The party recalled that Minfilia held both an Orb of Absorption and an Orb of Repulsion,
    accounting for two of the four orbs under discussion.
- Because the distortion blocked <Spell name="sending" src="phb" />, the group considered holding a hole open so the spell could pass through.
  - Earlier Thrantorbury research suggested that a hole through lead could admit <Spell name="sending" src="phb" />, but nobody knew whether the repulsion orb would reject the spell.
  - They also lacked the antimagic field needed to test a related hypothesis.
- A second plan involved letting the Orb of Absorption weaken the wall and then pulling it away with repulsion, since casting <Spell name="dispel magic" src="phb" /> on the absorption orb would merely feed it.

## Luana Enters the Loop

- Luana entered alone with the red Orb of Repulsion, believing it would protect her while she
  recovered the loop's source.
  - She refused to remove it to cast <Spell name="sending" src="phb" /> and left most of her equipment with Lime in a <Item name="bag of holding" src="dmg" />.
  - She retained an unlit green censer and its Essence of Dream brew; the barrier conformed around
    her as she passed through.
- Outside the four-story northeastern dormitories, Luana lit the censer.
  - Nearby creatures and people rapidly fell asleep.
  - The repulsion orb impaired her magical senses and color vision, but she continued despite her
    fear of the familiar effect.
  - When the loop reset, daylight became darkness and the falling creatures vanished. Luana remained
    mobile and unaffected, then fled after seeing a sleeping guard begin shedding his skin.
- Remembering that her objective lay in the library, Luana followed signs past the Public
  Administration Building, gardens, parks, and a fountain.
  - The censer put robed inhabitants to sleep while she avoided breathing its fumes.
  - Matthew was established to be in the southwestern dormitories.
- Near the library, Cotto and Fina were fighting enormous fire and water elementals.
  - The censer incapacitated the allies but not the pure elementals. The group discussed their
    immunity to poisoning and unconsciousness separately from Herb of the Harem, whose reproductive
    effects were said not to affect pure elementals.
  - Luana also tried burning Essence of Ether, described as a poison that induced unconsciousness;
    this was distinct from the Essence of Dream brew and Herb of the Harem.
  - Luana evaded the fight with exceptional stealth, spotted the conjurer directing the elementals
    from an upper balcony, and climbed behind him with a grappling hook.
  - The conjurer fell asleep; his elementals disappeared when he could no longer maintain
    concentration.
- Luana took an Orb of Enhancement from the conjurer's left hand and another magical object from his
  right.
  - Her touch dispelled an active effect on the second object without damaging it.
  - Bringing the Enhancement and Repulsion orbs together produced an unexpected compatible reaction.
  - Identification named the conjurer as Orlis Cornelius, a middle-aged College of Conjuration
    professor. His tattoos suggested Reformist ties, but his lack of expected piercings left Luana
    unsure of his affiliation at that point. He was later confirmed to be a relatively low-ranking
    Reformist.
- Beyond an indistinct presence at an open balcony door, Luana passed another protective barrier and
  sneaked past wards and a heavily coated person.
  - The censer put that person to sleep; she was the abjuration professor Wenwen Asturias.
    Her single piercing identified her to Luana as a Reformist.
  - Luana took her journal of time-control diagrams. The professor's unconsciousness did not stop
    the loop.
  - The journal explained that folding time and space required enormous power, possibly from an Orb
    of Absorption or a massive enhancement source, and pointed toward Kalassarian ruins beneath the
    library's restricted sector.
- Using the professor's key, Luana descended alone through protected books and an authorized
  repository of lead-lined containers, magical clothing, and low-risk cursed objects.
  - She borrowed a cloak for cover and followed footprints through a burned-away doorway into
    ancient ruins.
  - The trail passed a huge, unpowered <Spell name="teleportation circle" src="phb" /> with broken conduits and deliberately cleared passages.
  - In an atrium, Luana found a black-purple bestower connected to four newer, crude conduits.
    Touching one disabled only that line; the other three remained active.

  and risk another Eastonton disaster.
