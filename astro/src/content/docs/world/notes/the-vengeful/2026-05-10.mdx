## Angel's group

- Angel's group arrives at the Travertines for the wedding of Luke Malthrek and
  Pallia Travertine.
  - The Malthrek party is also present, including Lime, Tiphanie, John Malthrek,
    and Andrew.
  - Reformists have infiltrated the keep staff.
  - Duchess Antoinette Malthrek is confirmed to be a Reformist, matching Angel's
    earlier suspicion from the feeling of death wrongness.
- Angel meets with her mother after returning from Rannek and the Bishop of
  Rannek situation.
- Gwyniviere Rannek says her fiance, Mark Malthrek, is not present.
  - Mark was called back to the Maltreks for unknown reasons.
- Angel is present in the hall after Crown Prince Marlion kills the Reformist
  spy watching Luke.
  - Angel considers using _zone of truth_, but instead casts
    <Spell name="speak with dead" src="phb" /> on the severed head.
  - The dead Reformist confirms that she herself is a Reformist.
  - She does not know whether Pallia Travertine is a Reformist.

## Lime's group

- Viscount Palladium Travertine, Lord of the Travertines
- Duchess Malthrek tells Lime that her supposed parents are Germaine and
  Adelaide Schulze.
  - Lime is still passing as their daughter, Elizabeth Schulze.
- During the chaos around Crown Prince Marlion's audience with Luke, Duchess
  Malthrek decides Lime should meet her "parents."
- Lime goes with Duchess Malthrek to a chapel in the outer city, outside the
  walls of the Travertines.
  - They meet Germaine Schulze in the rectory.
  - Germaine sprinkles a powder into a brazier-like device, opening a portal to
    a pocket dimension.
- Inside the pocket dimension are Adelaide Schulze and Father Charles Renaldi.
  - Duchess Malthrek identifies Adelaide as Lime's supposed mother.
  - Father Charles Renaldi calls Adelaide a high-ranking local Reformist,
    possibly a bearer of the whip.
- Lime convinces Germaine and Adelaide to pretend that she is their daughter,
  even though they know she is not.
  - They recognize that she is a Schulze relative.
- The Reformists discuss recent failures and the shards of the Emerald Shield of
  Spring.
  - Angel has two of the seven shards.
  - The Reformists can sense the shards, but cannot reliably locate them.
  - Lime persuades them to let Angel collect more shards so they can ambush her
    later.
- Lime is told she may have to take a test of devotion.
  - She may be offered the position of Candle Bearer in the local Reformist
    hierarchy.

- Casts an alarm <Spell name="alarm" src="phb" /> in the Crown Prince's

  liege lord's wedding boon.

  hair.

- Tiphanie accompanies Marlion under the guise of being one of his court
  wizards.

    Reformist beliefs.

  spell.
