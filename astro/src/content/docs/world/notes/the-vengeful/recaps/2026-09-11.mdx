## Previously, in *The Vengeful*…

Angel came to Maiden's Port to find a way to her father. She found her sister displaced, the
Maidenfelds frightened, and Crispin Mallow's work already inside their defenses.

The teleportation brought Angel, Gwyniviere, and their companions into Laudine's cramped rented
apartment, not the residence Angel remembered. Broken weirs and sluice gates had flooded the
estate. Laudine had moved closer to her atelier; beyond the port, the countryside remained at war.
Uther was a prisoner. Lance had disappeared. The family could offer neither a safe road nor a
reliable account of where they had gone.

Laudine could, however, take them to her husband.

With Bertram Dorand and a small mercenary escort, the sisters crossed the anxious city to the
Maidenfeld Spice Company. Behind its guarded doors and above its clattering lift, Robert heard an
account that commercial discretion could no longer keep at arm's length: infiltrated households,
copied faces, parasites carried in food, and living people stitched into weapons. Angel produced
the Crown Prince's seal. When the adults questioned why an eleven-year-old had come to intervene,
she reminded them whose rescue she had paid for. She was there for her father and House Rannek.

The Maidenfelds trusted their safeguards against magical intruders. Angel and Gwyniviere had
already learned how little a familiar face could guarantee. During tea, while Laudine contacted
Maria and the conversation wandered through family codes, perfume, and birthday preparations,
the flan arrived with a warning nobody had intended to serve.

The servant's legs were wrong.

Angel noticed the mismatched limbs and alerted Sir Hilis Brastok, who restrained her. Examination
revealed stitches and living replacement flesh, not a changeling's disguise. Whatever had allowed
her through the building's wards, Mallow's handiwork had reached Robert's office. His magical
needle and ordinary thread could turn altered bodies into puppets. The woman before them was
also a victim, and earlier recoveries gave the party reason to hope such victims could be saved.

Elsewhere, Hellion secured a place where the scattered allies could gather. At a stream outside
Maiden's Field, a hag answered a summons from the water. Questions became negotiation; negotiation
became an alliance with Esmeralda and her sisters against the Reformists and the Children of the
Crone. Within a defined circle on the riverbank, those owing allegiance to the protected party
would receive sanctuary and protection.

Hellion offered his firstborn for that alliance. The obligation would survive distance between
planes and even death; any conception was to be consensual and uncoerced. The sanctuary was real,
and so was the debt. Its eventual fulfillment remained ahead of him.

Orbs of Enhancement and coordinated teleportation brought mercenaries, the Rannek sisters, Robert,
and his spellcasting brother Steven into the gathering. Oren Bell placed himself at Angel's
disposal. Reports described broken approaches, isolated troops, and a barony supplied by ships
where roads and bridges had failed. Searchers began probing the sanctuary. The defenders were
asked to mislead them rather than kill them and advertise the company's presence.

Still, reaching Maidenfeld Keep presented another problem. Old bargains supposedly restricted
hag access and magic within Maiden's Field, but the people invoking those restrictions could not
fully explain them. Hellion called Captain Hedwig of the *Scarlet* to test what could actually be
done.

Hedwig arrived with Jane and Jennifer. She drew the teleportation circle with her blood and sealed
the bargain with Esmeralda in a blood handshake. Esmeralda kept her blood out of the circle,
fearing her prior bargain with the Maidenfelds would obstruct their entry. Hedwig and her sisters
carried the company into a preserved Kalassarian chamber beneath the keep. Whatever the old
restrictions meant, they had not stopped these sisters. That success did not explain the larger
veil around Senera, or make every hag free to cross every boundary.

Above the ancient chambers, Hedwig and her sisters spread a vast storm across the barony, hoping
to hinder Mallow's movements and his puppets. Inside, the party confronted the Baron and his
assembled nobles.

Their account of the defeat described a trap. The force sent after Hicklander leader Victor
Woodward had reached a tavern that exploded. Sabotage struck the floodworks, water overwhelmed the
fields, and attacks disabled the wizards who might have helped evacuate the soldiers. Uther was
isolated and surrounded. Lance had been fighting elsewhere, attempting to free a hostage village.
Accusations of inside help followed, but accusation alone did not settle which nobles had served
the enemy willingly, which had been coerced, or which understood what had happened.

Hellion produced a living Reformist parasite. The visitors described stolen bodies, stolen
memories, and families held hostage to secure obedience. As the questioning continued, the
Baron's account gave Mallow's vendetta a more personal shape: Crispin wanted him to watch the
Maidenfeld people die and grieve as Crispin had grieved for his wife. The threat extended to the
surviving bloodline, including relatives abroad.

The workers' grievances also refused to disappear beneath talk of outside agitators. The union
leader admitted withholding wages and blaming the lord; Baron Maidenfeld corroborated the account,
rather than confessing to withholding the pay himself. Reformist involvement did not make the
suffering that fed the revolt imaginary, or settle every participant's allegiance.

For Robert, the danger to the bloodline meant an immediate rescue of his own.

His marriage to Laudine was a protective political arrangement between friends, not the love
match Maria believed it to be. Robert loved a common woman whom family tradition prevented him
from marrying. Their children lived away from the keep, beyond the protection now gathering
around the Baron.

Personal keepsakes gave the rescuers a way to reach them. At their cottage, the children ran to
embrace their father—but someone had been watching through a doll. Divination was dispelled,
and a hidden token was later removed from its stuffing. It belonged to a system linked to a
scrying pool; removing it did not reveal who watched from the other end.

The children's mother had gone to a bakery. Hidden followers shadowed the rescuers, and windows
shattered as they approached. Inside, attackers held her bound and blindfolded. A charm failed
against its intended target; rapid strikes and magical exchanges of position broke the captors'
hold. Outside, Robert fought while the children were shielded from the attack. The rescuers
brought the family back to the keep and returned with two enemies in custody.

Then Helena Cooper received a seat among the nobles. The Hicklander leader was also the Baron's
illegitimate daughter, born of his affair with her mother.

The Hicklander leader described wages stolen in transit and recruits whose food had made her
violently ill while others continued eating. The party suspected contamination or compulsion,
though it could not yet name the cause. Helena agreed to help them reach the local movement and
an old black-powder delivery site. She also remembered a man who had asked after Victor before
the flood: someone calling himself Rance.

After a necessary stop to deal with the brazier left burning in Helena's cooperage, the party
reached an abandoned depot among coconut trees. There was black powder—and an armored stranger
with a thoroughly convincing paper mustache.

His name was Rance. Naturally, he was entirely different from Lance.

Magic made that absurd distinction difficult to question until the rain loosened the mustache.
Angel perceived her brother and embraced him, believing Rance had vanished rather than recognizing
the disguise. Lance had staged his disappearance to investigate undercover. He reported that
Castle Rannek's infiltrators had used Maria's face and the family's codes. Angel's knowledge of
Mallow beating Uther came from Lotan's memories, not from a vision supplied by *Locate Creature*.
Lance also reported that Mallow had attended Hicklander meetings, though their gathering places
had since changed.

Angel used her father's signet ring to seek him with *Locate Creature*. The bearing led toward
Maiden's Port and Maiden's Bay. Later discussion fixed the direction from the keep as east or
northeast, not west. It was a direction, not a discovered prison: a ship, submerged ruins, or a
pocket dimension remained possibilities.

Hedwig used *Freedom of the Waves* against the powder cache. Lance's disguise reasserted itself,
and Rance resumed explaining that he merely worked with Angel's brother. Flan, wine, and fresh
glue helped preserve his cover. Back at the keep, Hellion recognized the trick and played along.

The company returned to the problem of Mallow's speed. Hedwig demonstrated how *Tree Stride*
could explain travel between matching trees; reports connected that ability to the Heart of the
Urdefolcin. It was a working explanation, not proof that every sighting came from the same method.
Nor did the party yet know whether Mallow wanted Angel's Emerald Shield shards, her celestial
nature, or something else entirely.

To find him, they turned to something he had left behind.

Hellion and Gwyniviere went to the dungeons to obtain thread from an altered prisoner. The
procedure required care: the transplanted tissue had begun integrating, and Mallow's sutures had
rerouted blood vessels to keep it alive. Gwyniviere performed the extraction with Hellion's medical
guidance, Sister Pira controlling bleeding, and Sir Erik Malmann healing afterward. They recovered
material that might serve as a magical connection without abandoning the prisoner to the wound.

Outside, Angel cast again. Supported by guidance and inspiration, she used the recovered thread
as a conduit for the search. It pointed in the same direction as her father. Steven fixed the
bearing with compass and astrolabe.

They still did not have a cell door to open. They did not know whether Mallow was moving, whether
his trail was a lure, or how much time the storm had bought. But the two searches agreed.

Oren proposed teleporting ahead to intercept him. Allies would remain to protect those at the
keep while the rescue moved forward. Exhausted, Angel repeated that she would save her father.
Hilis, Oren, Gwyniviere, and Lance disguised as Rance would accompany her, with Malmann agreeing
to join. Angel believed Rance was going and Lance was staying behind; the disguise still held
from her perspective.

The destination anchor was the Maidenfeld Spice Company's circle. Steven would escort them
briefly before returning, while Dimitri guarded the circle and facilitated teleportation traffic.
Hedwig, Jane, and Jennifer offered standby teleportation and healing support at Maiden's Port.

Lance had been found. Robert's family was safe at the keep. Uther, Sandor, the court wizards, and
other missing people were still waiting.

The group teleported.

This time, they had a bearing to follow—and Mallow's own thread to lead them.

## At the Table: Where We Left Off

- **Immediate objective:** follow the matching bearings toward Maiden's Port and Maiden's Bay,
  intercept Mallow, and rescue Uther and the other captives. The session ended on teleportation,
  before an arrival scene established the destination or the outcome.
- **Lance is alive:** he has been investigating as the magically convincing, paper-mustached
  Rance. His disappearance was deliberate. He joins the rescue disguised; Angel believes Rance
  goes while Lance stays behind.
- **Robert's family is at the keep:** the bakery rescue succeeded, two attackers were captured,
  and the doll's scrying token was removed. The watcher remains unidentified.
- **The war is not simply nobles against Hicklanders:** withheld wages are a real grievance;
  testimony and evidence point to Reformist manipulation, but do not settle every allegiance.
- **Allies and obligations:** Hedwig, Jane, and Jennifer entered Maiden's Field and raised the
  storm. Hellion's alliance with the river hags carries his firstborn obligation.
- **Rescue party:** Angel, Oren, Hilis Brastok, Gwyniviere, Lance disguised as Rance, and Malmann.
  Steven briefly escorts them to the company circle before returning. Dimitri guards and operates
  that circle; Hedwig, Jane, and Jennifer offer standby support at Maiden's Port.
- **Still unresolved:** the captives' exact location, Victor's fate, and Mallow's full motives and
  powers. Someone remains to protect the Baron and children, without an established name here.
