## Previously, in *The Vengeful*…

The rescued children were playing in the gardens, but the companions still had to get them home.
An old teleportation circle could shorten the journey north. Nobody had secured its arrival point.

Hellion, Gwyniviere, and Lance weighed the alternatives. Traveling by river and overland could take
months through dangerous country; teleportation meant trusting a route into the northern Maltreks.
Oren could guide them, though his ban from the elves' settlement would keep him outside its borders.
Angel would negotiate.

Returning the children was an obligation and an act of goodwill. The party agreed to keep it
separate from any price for an alliance. For gifts, they bought five weapons of warning from
Jessica, two longbows and three shortswords, and paired them with Feywild seeds. Ordinary iron or
steel might offend their hosts; Jessica said enchanted weapons could adapt to their wielders.

The council had little time to spare. Thousands needed food, and only two or three days of supplies
remained. The companions discussed bringing food through teleportation circles, inspecting and
purifying it, and preserving what they could. Marquis Rannek was willing to accept help from the
Maidenfeld fleet even if it earned the family political favor. Hellion insisted that any supplies
still needed inspection: Eastonton had shown what contaminated food could carry.

Matthew's rescue might also secure the aid promised by the hags at the Thralmals pools. Meanwhile,
Minfilia and her companions remained trapped in Thrantorbury. With the next full moon only two days
away, Mark's surviving lycanthropy and Jessica's uncertain condition added another deadline.

For now, the expedition north could move.

Farther north, two young travelers had already left home without permission.

Sharyn, Raistasi's twin, believed a merchant had abducted her sister. Snowdrop, the young changeling
raised alongside them, went with her. Their elders had been slow to recognize that missing children
might mean something worse than an extended visit to relatives. Sharyn and Snowdrop were not
prepared to wait.

They entered Torris stacked beneath one cloak, hoping to pass as a single adult. Their Elvish and
enthusiastic gestures did little to help them explain themselves. Hunger became confused with
pregnancy or a missing baby. Snowdrop smelled meat she was forbidden to eat. The pair fled their
first attempt at human hospitality without abandoning the disguise.

Sharyn's <Spell name="locate creature" src="phb" /> pointed south. At the river port, however, the
travelers had neither tickets nor money. An unsuccessful <Spell name="charm person" src="phb" />
and offers of work only made the boat attendant more suspicious. He called the guards.

The escape acquired a horse. With animal speech and the promise of a star apple, the travelers
persuaded a black stallion to leap his fence and carry them away. He had troubles of his own: humans
had stolen his elder foal, and he was reluctant to leave the filly waiting at home. They persuaded
him not to abandon two elflings in the wild.

He carried them through Norlis and onward. Sharyn found a map in his barding and confidently misread
it, placing distant Tumult's Landing conveniently near their route. Audrey, a hag, offered food,
directions, and an introduction to Mother. They remembered their elders' warnings and declined.
They continued south.

Back with the expedition, Raistasi helped the rescued children wash and prepare. She accepted the
short name Stas and reassured a frightened child that Angel would not put them back in the crates.
Angel invited her along as a recovered cleric of the Green Lord. The children would remain behind
until the advance force secured the route.

At Port Mibram, twenty soldiers and Angel's twenty mercenaries awaited them. Hellion and Oren would
go first, Lance and the soldiers would follow, and the support group would come later. The orders
were to preserve Reformist lives where possible, bind their hands, and gag their spellcasters.

Oren channeled the circle through an orb. The advance party arrived at Tuck.

The enclosure around the circle had been destroyed. Buildings stood damaged and deserted. A Dispel
Rune neutralized an alarm glyph, but no bodies explained the blood around the settlement's edges.
Most of it was human and a few days old. One sample was elven, unfamiliar enough to raise suspicion
of drow.

While Hellion guarded the arrival point, Oren investigated. Five Reformist scouts had just reached
the ruins after losing contact with the settlement. They reported black elves, missing personnel,
and halted mining. Their reinforcements were still several days away. They had also lost contact
with Sir Aldric Torward.

The party struck before nightfall. Lance and the soldiers came through the circle and overwhelmed
all five scouts. Hellion's black eyes and tendrils terrified one captive into addressing him as
Master.

The prisoners marked a mine entrance roughly a hundred miles away. Oren suspected another entrance
must lie much closer. The scouts also named an elven settlement, Marim, from which their own people
had not returned, and described a local Reformist hierarchy gathering at Leeward as the mines fell
silent.

Hellion then drained the prisoners one by one, killing them and putting their bodies in his bag
of holding. Lance watched in horror. Hellion said Minfilia had requested three hundred bodies.
He still needed two hundred and sixty-three.

The soldiers cleaned the blood from the route and circle with runes. Hellion sent word that the
arrival area was safe enough to bring the children through, though remaining there overnight was
another matter. About twelve children arrived from Port Mibram and went into Lance's care.
Hellion departed with most of the soldiers; the escort took the children and diplomatic gifts
south toward Torris. Before leaving, Raistasi hung black dahlias on a ruined building.

On the road, maps showed Raistasi how little of the world she knew. Her settlement restricted
contact with humans and traded in favors rather than money. Oren explained that his name came from
his human adoptive parents; Oran was a name of the Green Lord. Asked about the world beyond her
protected home, Raistasi had as many questions as answers.

As darkness approached, the escort chose to press onward. Their cleric fed and befriended the
horses, secured the vehicle with vines, and used <Spell name="freedom of movement" src="phb" />
and <Spell name="lesser restoration" src="phb" /> to help them continue.

Elsewhere, Sharyn and Snowdrop's stallion needed rest. They stopped beside trees and a stream, only
to discover blighted leaves and worse corruption deeper in the woodland.

Sharyn remembered La Papessa's advice and cast <Spell name="plant growth" src="phb" />. Fresh growth
surrounded damaged limbs; trees cast off diseased branches. The travelers had made a healthy pocket
large enough to camp in, but the forest beyond remained blighted.

The spring stank. Dead animals and deformed fish occupied its waters. Sharyn cleared the water,
and the horse drank safely. The travelers settled for goodberries.

Through <Spell name="speak with plants" src="phb" />, a recovering tree told them what had happened.
The forest's Urdefolcin guardian was dead. Hags had consumed its heart, felled its trunk, and
desecrated its roots. Older trees farther in were dead, dying, or caught in an evil the tree could
not explain.

Their elder had forbidden interference in human forests. Snowdrop argued that the blight would
not respect that boundary. For the moment, the travelers rested to recover their magic.

Around midnight, the children's escort reached Torris. Lance had scouted ahead without finding
the strange nighttime behavior reported in Eastonton. The party hid the children's ears and used
Marchioness Maria Rannek's cover story: these were children from a troubled mountain orphanage,
being relocated south.

Oren erased their trail. A sleepy boy led them to lodging, where Miss Mika had keys and warm soup.
Angel and Raistasi settled the children. Sandor and Mal were exhausted; Sir Brastok kept watch,
with Angel nearby.

Raistasi left her <Item name="boots of elvenkind" src="dmg" /> with a note thanking Angel,
then climbed out a window.
She found Oren outside and asked him for the route to another elven settlement. She needed an
archfey.

Her elders, she explained, no longer hoped to return bodily to the Feywild. They wanted to create a
tree that could carry their souls to the Tree of Life. Sharyn possessed the rare life magic they
wanted. Raistasi and Oren feared the elders intended to put her inside the new tree.

Raistasi defended them as desperate. They had watched generations of elven souls become restless
dead or fall into others' hands. Oren warned that their proposed tree might offer no protection
against the blight or the hags. He urged her to save Sharyn and give the elders a credible reason
to hope for another way home.

Raistasi also explained how she had left. She had paid a merchant with an elven cloak to smuggle
her out. The children she babysat had followed without her knowing. The merchant carried a cursed
object she could not identify.

Oren reassured her that she had not intended their kidnapping. Returning alone, however, would
not stop the elders' plan. He suggested approaching Lance and the Legally Bare associates who had
traveled to the Feywild and returned. A warlock among them could reportedly reach an archfey patron
across Senera's barrier. Oren's own promise to Emily Hazeldine was that every elf should have the
choice to go home.

Their conversation lasted toward dawn. Oren revealed that he had once been Orellian of the
Highguard, before banishment and his life among humans. At the river, Raistasi's skin and hair
turned blue, and gill-like markings appeared as she swam against the current.

They spoke of confinement and grief. Raistasi described her brother Torches' forbidden love for a
human, his collapse, and his isolation in the forest. Oren had suffered decades of grief after
losing his own human family. He questioned whether Torches' wish to contact merchants and send
letters proved madness, or whether confinement was keeping him from the answers he needed.

Raistasi described dreams of an archfey reaching through darkness and blighted forests, apparently
held back by a barrier. She regretted leaving her twin behind. She did not know that Sharyn had
already escaped into an adventure of her own. Raistasi returned unnoticed to the inn before sunrise.

When Sharyn and Snowdrop woke, <Spell name="locate creature" src="phb" /> pointed north, back along
the way they had traveled.

A woman hidden among the trees offered pies, cakes, caramel, and candied apples. She looked human;
the travelers suspected a hag beneath an illusion. Sharyn cast <Spell name="awaken" src="phb" />
on the tree concealing her. Grateful for the forest's healing, it entangled the woman and urged
the children to run.

They escaped north to a caravan clearing. After resting, Sharyn used
<Spell name="commune with nature" src="phb" /> and sensed blight nearby, with a cave and a portal
to another plane somewhere in the affected area.

They sent a rabbit with <Spell name="animal messenger" src="phb" />: a warning for Raistasi
to escape any captors, avoid leading them home, and return for a painting session. Snowdrop added
a request for meat.

Then they went toward the cave. The frightened stallion asked to return to his filly, and Sharyn
released him. Snowdrop agreed to a quick look. A plant near the blight could no longer feel its
roots or its kin beyond the sickness.

Sharyn searched for druidic markings. On a fallen tree, she found a warning of danger that
Snowdrop could neither see nor read.

Someone had written it in blood.

  planar portal detected by Sharyn's magic. Their stallion has been released. A druidic warning
  written in blood is their last discovery; they have not entered the cave.

  conceals their identities. The party carries weapons of warning and Feywild seeds as gifts.

  knowledge. She fears the elders intend to sacrifice Sharyn to a replacement Tree of Life. Oren
  proposes finding help through Lance's Feywild-traveled associates. Raistasi returned to the inn
  before dawn; Sharyn does not know her account, and Raistasi does not know Sharyn has left home.
- **The sisters' search has changed direction:** Sharyn's latest spell pointed north. A rabbit
  carries their message, but delivery has not been witnessed.
- **Tuck's attackers and the blight remain unresolved:** Reformist scouts reported black elves,
  disappeared personnel, and halted mines. A recovering tree separately blamed hags for killing
  its forest's Urdefolcin. Neither account establishes how the wider threats connect.
- **The other deadlines remain:** food for thousands, the approaching full moon, the rescue in
  Thrantorbury, and Matthew's rescue to secure the Thralmals hags' promised support.
