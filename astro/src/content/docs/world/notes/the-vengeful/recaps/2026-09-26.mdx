## Previously, in *The Vengeful*…

The inquiry paused. Oren followed someone toward the castle exterior, and Angel took Crispin
Mallow inside for pudding.

For the boy, the immediate mystery was not the Maidenfeld fleet. It was why he was in a castle,
what had happened to his family, and whether someone had sold him.

Back in the garden, Angel tried to explain the years he could no longer remember. He had become
an officer of the Reformist Church. He had hurt people and imprisoned a dragon within himself.
She had freed the dragon, accepted its boon, and chosen to return him to childhood. Her spellbook
and the date offered evidence for a story that sounded impossible.

Crispin had a terrible story of his own. He remembered his mother strangling him and calling him
a monster. He had killed her while defending himself; his father blamed him for her madness.
Angel told him that surviving did not make him deserving of death. Whatever his older self had
done, she intended to protect the child before her and give him a different future.

He wanted to garden. He was afraid of unfamiliar adults. Angel agreed that he could travel with
her, while the others helped keep his identity concealed.

His childhood nevertheless offered clues. He distrusted the Maidenfelds, vaguely connected
Tristan with an uncle or relative, and recognized the crown prince's emblem from a dress his
mother had treasured. None of that yet supplied a family tree.

The conference was supposed to resume. Instead, Baron Maidenfeld arrived with another missing
person to explain.

Steven had answered his adjutant's summons to a fleet emergency and teleported away. Now Steven,
his adjutant, and the ship he had gone to had vanished. Ordinary searches, magical detection,
and the fleet's own navigation instruments had found nothing.

Hellion supplied a potion that would let Angel breathe and swim beneath the sea, instructing her
to drink it before attempting teleportation. Rather than teleport into an unknown danger, she cast
<Spell name="legend lore" src="phb" />.

Warmth, light, and ethereal wings surrounded her. Steven was alive. He was endangered and under
coercion, in a place of obscure legend hidden for centuries.

Pressed for answers, the Maidenfelds disclosed what their ships and submarines had helped conceal.

The Haven was a dragon sanctuary.

It lay within Senera, yet occupied a stable realm of its own. Reaching it required a particular
underwater approach; teleportation could not simply bypass the route. Its protections followed
Maidenfeld blood and name; shared Dragonheart ancestry alone would not preserve them if another
house took ownership. A hidden magical source had sustained spellcasting there even during the
magicless era.

Inside were unhatched chromatic dragon eggs. There were no living dragons to tend them. Their
caretakers feared what might hatch, and feared destroying them as well.

The companions argued over whether the hatchlings' futures were already determined by their
nature. They also had a more immediate concern: what the Reformists might do with such a clutch.
An <Item name="orb of dragonkind" src="dmg" /> might let an evil spellcaster hatch and command
dragons, but whether the Reformists possessed it remained unknown.

To learn how much Mallow had known, they needed something less guarded than the Baron's answers.
Uther remembered his captor's journal.

Hellion used a <Spell name="teleport" src="phb" /> scroll, with Crispin's memory of home as the
conduit, to bring the group to Green Barrel, the burned settlement in Maiden's Field where Uther
had been held. Mud, broken dikes, and ash-covered ruins surrounded the surviving dilapidated house.
Hellion carried the party across
the quagmire. Angel questioned a squirrel with <Spell name="speak with animals" src="phb" />
and an offering of bread.

The squirrel proved useful. A large stone concealed an entrance beneath deliberately arranged
rubble.

Crispin began to shake. Angel stayed outside with him while the others descended.

Below lay rough passages, a laboratory, and a chemically preserved body the explorers suspected
was Crispin's father.
A dragon coin had been stitched inside its mouth, supposedly to purchase the soul's silence.
Uther recognized the place of his captivity. He remembered repeated contact between the Reformists
and Mallow, which he interpreted as coordination. He believed he himself had been bait. Angel was
the person his captor had repeatedly said he needed.

True sight revealed concealed specimens among the laboratory's preserved remains. A stuffed
squirrel held something magical—and an evocation trap. A <Spell name="dispel magic" src="phb" />
rune made it safe to open, revealing a decoder-like device that the party believed the Duchess
could operate.

Another illusion hid a many-colored box. Forcing its magic apart risked destroying whatever
lay inside, so the explorers carried it out. They also recovered an elaborate Dragonheart dress,
which Angel carefully folded away.

The box recognized Crispin. Its recess reshaped itself around the purple Dragonheart coin.
His hand satisfied another condition. Something still had to be spoken or sung.

They tried words, family references, humming, and songs. Dancing was suggested, but Crispin said
he did not dance. As he repeated his mother's lullaby, the familiar sounds became Draconic snarls
and roars. His mother had taught him the language without his knowing its name. Hellion recognized
the song as Flight of Dragons, also known in the Feywild; Crispin understood and answered his
Draconic speech.

The right song alone was not enough. It needed Crispin's own imperfect pronunciation.

With the coin held against the box, he sang again.

Inside was a leather-bound book.

Its hidden writing appeared when Crispin touched it. A hand worked. So did a foot. Once the words
were visible, someone else could read them.

The journal described a life spent taking pieces of other people to remake its writer. Elven
longevity, stolen organs, altered shapeshifters, and obedient puppets appeared alongside plans
for a throne. Mallow wrote of a curse in his blood and a final sacrifice that would purge it.
The party understood why Angel's celestial blood had mattered—and what he had intended to do
after obtaining it.

The journal revealed that Mallow had burned Green Barrel, overturning the official attribution
to bandits. He claimed his mother came from an expelled Maidenfeld cadet branch with Dragonheart
ties, and blamed the recognized family for his branch's
loss of property and standing. The journal offered a grievance and a claim to a birthright, not
independent proof that either justified his ambitions.

It also offered an account of the stolen submarine: a Maidenfeld captain made into a puppet,
a prototype redirected, and the vessel retained after the puppet's discovery. Other entries
described years of raids by puppets dressed as Maidenfeld forces, intended to provoke anger in
the Hicklands.

The Reformists appeared in his writing as tools. Whether they had regarded him any differently
was another question.

Even his own record could not capture everything. Attempts to write the terms of a bargain
produced only the admission that he had made a deal. In entries Hellion read privately, Mallow
suspected the Maidenfelds concealed dragon eggs, but still intended to discover exactly what was
there. What he had passed to the Church remained unclear.

Hellion acknowledged the patience behind the planning. He also observed what the planner had
become: someone like the people he hated.

Before returning, Hellion gave Crispin a history of the Draco-Giant War and a rune that let him
understand its Elvish text, taking the journal in exchange. The history supplied another account
to examine, not a settled explanation of the dragons' past.

Angel used Hellion's <Spell name="teleport" src="phb" /> scroll to return the group safely, with
her mother as the conduit. Back at the castle, the Baron reported that Steven was not in the Haven.
They suspected
someone had anticipated Angel's divination and used the search to force the Maidenfelds to
reveal their secret. The missing man's actual location and captor were still unknown.

Nor did the recovered journal become a straightforward piece of evidence to pass around.
Hellion retained it but lied to the Baron that it had burned when forced open. At the Baron's
request, Tristan used <Spell name="encode thoughts" src="ggr" /> to inspect Hellion's recollection.
Hellion consented while attempting to control what was extracted. The selected memories showed
the moth-like page finder and the words “Claim the throne.” The phrase was genuine, but the
extraction did not provide the full account the Baron demanded.

The danger to the fleet could not wait for that dispute to end.

After Hellion reported the puppeted captain and the enemy's knowledge of the Haven, the Baron
announced that the fleet would be ordered back to port for inspection. Missing ships would be
treated as AWOL; their beacons would allow them to be located. Hellion proposed sending Jessica
to Maiden's Port as a spy whose affiliation with the companions the Maidenfelds would not recognize.
Sapphire and Andrew would continue their assignment at the settlement.

A submarine might still provide a way along the hidden approach. Hellion knew the sigil of the
Maidenfeld teleportation circle at Maiden's Port. Uther proposed commandeering it or requiring
continued access, though they worried the Baron might change its sigil on grounds of a leak.
Whether to proceed with the Baron or without his knowledge remained undecided.

Beyond the ships and dragon eggs, the rescued captives needed homes to return to. The marshal's
people would investigate the human captives' identities. An elven settlement might receive
the displaced elves; Angel hoped to find Maerwen. Kelthyr was ruled out as a guide. Oren might
lead an escort there if willing, remaining outside because of his ban from elven settlements.

Rannek also needed food. The flooded fields could not feed it, Malthrek depended on its supply,
and the reported stores would last only about three days. Nobles had been summoned to account
for their lands and provisions. Druidic assistance was still needed, not yet secured.

The discussion widened to alliances and the possibility of another struggle for the throne.
The crown prince would need support beyond assurances from a few noble houses. Minfilia,
Tiphanie, Lime, local peoples, and other regional powers all entered the reckoning. Uther
reminded the company that his adult children would choose their own parts in what came next.

Steven had not been rescued. The Haven had not been reached. A fleet recall had been announced,
a spy's assignment proposed, and Mallow's ambitions partly exposed.

For now, there were people to bring home, a delta to feed, and another war to prevent.

## At the Table: Where We Left Off

- **Steven is still missing:** divination established that he was alive, endangered, and coerced.
  The Baron reports he is not in the Haven. His captor and location remain unresolved;
  deliberate manipulation of the divination's clues is their theory.
- **The Haven is disclosed, not reached:** the sanctuary contains unhatched chromatic dragon eggs.
  Its underwater approach cannot be bypassed by teleportation. Its full history, power source,
  and the right course for the eggs remain open.
- **A fleet recall is announced:** ships are to return for inspection for puppets. Hellion proposed
  sending Jessica to Maiden's Port; Sapphire and Andrew would continue their settlement assignment.
  These plans were not shown completed. The next route and whether to act with the Baron's
  cooperation have not been settled.
- **The journal was found and read:** it describes fleshcraft, a stolen submarine, staged raids,
  dynastic ambitions, and a planned sacrifice involving Angel. Its lineage and bargain claims
  need corroboration. Hellion retained it after giving Crispin a history book and comprehension
  rune in exchange. His claim that it burned was a lie, and the selected memories extracted by
  Tristan did not supply a full record.
- **Crispin remains in Angel's care:** his childhood memories and lullaby helped open the box.
  His possible ancestry is unresolved, and his former self's deeds do not settle the boy's future.
- **Recovery continues:** rescued captives remain under care while arrangements for their return
  are pursued. Food and druidic aid are urgent; Rannek's shortage also threatens Malthrek.
  Political alliances are being sought, not yet secured across all the proposed factions.
