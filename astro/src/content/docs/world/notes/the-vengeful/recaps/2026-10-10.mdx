## Previously, in *The Vengeful*…

Angel rested after the return from Green Barrel and the meeting with the Baron. Hellion proposed
showing the visiting nobles something of the allies and resources available to them in Eastonton.
Uther and Maria offered support, while the unanswered questions about Steven and the Maidenfelds
remained waiting.

On his way out, Hellion found Crispin in a tree, singing Flight of Dragons.

The boy did not want to go without Angel. He feared Hellion might sell him. The suggestion that
he could represent Angel and find something for her helped, but he set a condition: Sandor had
to come too. He barely knew Sandor; he trusted him more nevertheless.

They walked through increased patrols and checkpoints to the military base. The nobles arrived
with their honor guard, Oren joined them, and the marshal finished briefing his troops before
coming along. Trajan remained behind. Hellion replenished the teleportation circle's surrounding
orbs from his own supply, then opened the way.

Eastonton greeted the delegation with a ruined chamber, a tree, and figures dressed as Dark Archons.
The Duke drew his weapon. These were not new attackers: Minfilia had raised their former enemies
as undead guards.

Beyond them, pixies and wary dryads inhabited Oberon's oasis. Hellion offered candy and reassurance;
the dryads returned his bow and left acorns, which he collected, leaving seeds in exchange.
Jessica brought the visitors into the restored living quarters with
<Spell name="dimension door" src="phb" />.

In the cafeteria, Sandor startled at the horned, red-skinned baker. Sensodyne introduced herself
as a devil, learned that the children were Angel's friends, and invited them to take bread.
Maria praised her baking. Miya, meanwhile, protested that Hellion had brought Angel's parents
without Angel. She had dressed up expecting to meet her.

Boyle could offer contacts in Port Surrey, but no easy answer to the Maidenfeld mystery. Their
spice sources remained closely guarded, and their trading arrangements restricted resale.
There was still no news from Thrantorbury.

The delegation had come to see another guarded source of power: the Orb of Absorption.

Marcus Parnassus, the Kalassarian skeleton raised by Minfilia, recommended the control room and
warned anyone approaching the orb to remove magical equipment. The visitors stored their items
in lockers bound to their identities. Jessica led them toward the invisible boundary, keeping
her own equipment because she would not cross it.

Miya described what the Reformists had done here: feeding children and captured adult spellcasters
to a device that drained their magic. Jessica explained that its conduits powered the ruins and
their traps; when the place was captured, the system had also been nourishing an aberration.
The orb was now controlled. That did not make standing beneath it safe.

Crispin advanced. Hellion went with him.

Red wisps drew out of Hellion's spine as he shook under the draining effect. He caught Crispin
by the collar and dragged him back. The boy resisted. He felt lighter near the orb and wanted
to go farther.

Part of that willingness was despair. Crispin said people hated who he had been, or might become.
Hellion asked who he was now, reminded him that Angel would be sad if he did not return, and
suggested coming back with her. Crispin eventually agreed to leave. Feeling better near the orb
had not answered what it would do to him.

The prisoners elsewhere in the ruins posed a different urgency. Ranking Reformists were being
kept asleep with Essence of Dream, but some resisted it. Jessica wanted a decision from Minfilia
or the Crown Prince about their fate. No new sentence was passed.

After recovering their equipment, the visitors went to the Paladinate. Miya's seized golem stood
outside among the paladins. She had fought it and taken control of its magic, not built it herself.

Inside, the old religious imagery was rapidly giving way to the Great Erected One. An enormous,
unmistakable stained-glass depiction finally explained the name to Hellion. The Duchess had
thought its meaning already clear. Jessica described Boyle as mortified.

Bastion was less amusing. Hellion intended to accompany Angel there; Jessica urged him to distract
her from going for now. She had heard of an Orb of Power fifty miles across and still growing,
and described Reformists consolidating control while people fled the destruction. Her report
was alarming, but secondhand. Neither an expedition nor a postponement was agreed.

With help from Hedwig and the Hags of Hama, the group returned to Rannekford. Hellion made rounds
among the rescued captives. Sister Pira and Gwyniviere helped tend the worst injuries, with few
healers to share the work.
Hellion intervened when clergy hesitated to help a Cornups girl because of her appearance,
and gave her food and drink himself.

The rescued elves were quiet and suspicious. Hellion left food and sweet water, spoke Elvish,
and offered to listen. They did not tell him where home was. After he moved away, they examined
the food and fed the youngest first.

One girl, comforting another child, looked remarkably like Maerwen. Hellion began to say the
name, stopped himself, and walked away without pressing her.

Trajan sought help through Emily, but Kelthyr and Luana were unavailable. Oren might guide an
escort while remaining outside a settlement that barred him. Miya and Natalia were assigned to
learn what the captured Sir Aldric Torward knew about northern settlements and drow; answers
would have to wait.

Hellion and Trajan also agreed to protect Crispin's identity and give him room to decide how to
handle his new life.

Back in the Eastonton ruins after dinner, Crispin found a way to make a decision of his own.

He introduced himself to Miya as Chris Mallory. When she identified herself as a hag, he asked
for a bargain. Learning magic was taking too long. He wanted older people to leave him alone,
especially those who did bad things to the people around him.

Miya suggested invisibility. He offered his name, expecting to forget it if he traded it away.
She corrected that expectation and said she had no use for his name. Gardening proved more useful:
she wanted a grove in Eastonton and hoped to help Natalia rebuild a coven.

In her room, Miya worked hair, blood, and magic into a simple bronze ring. She offered a
<Item name="ring of invisibility" src="dmg" /> for future gardening services, with no requirement
that he stay in Eastonton now. Wearing it would also connect them mentally across any distance
on the same plane. To reach her, he should use her real name: Dorothy.

Crispin gave his own name and accepted.

Dorothy recognized the name of a serial killer. She assumed the killer had stolen it from the
child. Crispin let that explanation stand.

They tested the connection: thoughts, shared sight, and the sensation of Dorothy pinching her
cheek. She lent him A Fulminating Treatise, an evocation book, as a separate gesture of friendship.
Being able to understand its words did not mean he knew how to cast its spells; he hoped Sandor
could teach him.

They agreed to keep each other's private information. Dorothy celebrated her first hag bargain.
Crispin returned to the others and refused to explain what had happened. When Hellion questioned
Miya, she said she had only asked him to promote her magazine.

Jessica accompanied the returning party. By the carriage ride back from the military base,
Sandor was struggling to stay awake and Crispin was fidgeting with his ring. The meeting could
wait until morning.

By breakfast, the Maidenfelds and several other nobles had left. Their departures encouraged
suspicions, but did not settle their loyalties; some had instructions to accompany the family
and play along.

The council considered waiting another day before mounting a rescue for Minfilia and Crown
Prince Marlion in Thrantorbury. The prince's advice mattered to the Maidenfeld crisis, and the
group chose to help Thrantorbury before pursuing the larger operation. No rescue had launched.

Angel joined the meeting and learned that Hellion had met Miya. She wanted a visit, a gift,
and a joint birthday celebration. A language rune appeared on her pancake; told to hold it rather
than eat it, she answered Hellion's Elvish greeting.

Jessica was less visible, but made her presence felt when a joke about her biting necks earned
Hellion an unseen punch that sent his head into his plate. He insisted he was fine and kept eating.

Angel had tried to contact Steven before sleeping and received no response. Hellion urged her
to concentrate on helping the rescued elders and children home while he pursued Steven's safety.
Jessica would help investigate the Maidenfelds, with Uther offering to reimburse operational
expenses. Borrowing a submarine, finding an insider, and tracing possible Kalassarian machinery
remained proposals, not discoveries.

The companions debated celestial goodness and freedom, the danger of someone exploiting the
Haven's eggs, and what hags might know. They even invented gossip to barter for information.
But the first people they chose to approach were already in the gardens.

They also told Jessica who Crispin had been and how Angel had used Auraxx's boon to return him
to childhood. They supported giving the boy another chance; a closer magical examination was
left for a later opportunity.

Angel met the girl who resembled Maerwen and addressed her by that name. The girl said she did
not know Legally Bare. Angel believed her, apologized, and introduced herself.

The girl recognized one of her rescuers. Her own name was Raistasi.

She could not find her mother or explain her home's location in human terms. An unnamed merchant
had agreed to bring her outside; Angel later described the encounter as a kidnapping involving
a deal. Raistasi remained hesitant to trust. She asked for her missing stag antler band, without
which she could not currently use her magic.

The party promised to bring her and the others home.

With the recovered band back in place, Raistasi entered a tree and emerged from another through
<Spell name="tree stride" src="phb" />. She called herself a child of the Green Lord. Around Angel,
she perceived a resonant aura, without identifying the Emerald Shield shards themselves.

Her home, she explained, was a distant forest with a waterfall and a glade. The castle was harder
to explain: to her, it was a very large house or cabin.

Raistasi invited the children to play a game of singing, climbing trees, and escaping an Earth
Guardian below. They adapted it into tag. Sir Brastok watched nearby; Gwyniviere sketched the
children, and Angel arranged a picnic on the grass.

Meanwhile, Lance reported to Hellion that he had confirmed with the guards that Raistasi was
Maerwen. Hellion told Gwyniviere, hoping their existing connection might help negotiations with
the elves. Gwyniviere pointed out that Raistasi had denied the identity to Angel, who believed her.
The confirmation had not yet become an alliance, or a conversation Raistasi chose to have.

For the moment, the children were playing after captivity. Getting them home was still ahead.

Torris offered a possible route: a forest region near a small human settlement, a hunting lodge,
lumberjacks, and a river. An old teleportation circle might shorten the journey, but its sigil,
arrival point, and condition were uncertain. The companions proposed sending capable casters and
fighters ahead before bringing the children through.

They chose to try the sigil first.

No advance party had secured the destination. Thrantorbury still needed help, Steven was still
missing, and the Haven remained unreached. Beneath those larger dangers lay the immediate promise:
bring the rescued people home.
