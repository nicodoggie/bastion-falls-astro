## Previously, in *The Vengeful*…

The search for Uther Rannek brought Angel and her companions back to the Maidenfeld Spice
Company. Dimitri checked Steven's codes, accepted his assurances, and discovered that the
visitors were not the most alarming people inside the building.

Near the elevator stood a robed figure with his feet switched.

A <Spell name="wall of force" src="phb" /> contained him and sent his thrown needles rebounding.
Then Gwyniviere opened the elevator to reveal two more altered Maidenfelds. The company's defenses
had already been breached. Below, its people were fighting others of their own kind.

The rescuers chose the fifth-floor window.

Rance broke the glass, <Spell name="feather fall" src="phb" /> carried the escape, and Brastok dove
after Angel with his hammer. A butcher's hook trailing something like sinew narrowly missed Rance.
Whatever safety the company had promised, there was little of it left indoors.

Angel's search spell led onward to the harbor. Guards lay fallen, and crates were being loaded
aboard a ship. The bearing did not tell her which box held her father.

<Spell name="shatter" src="phb" /> opened several at once.

Inside were metal cages, prisoners—and Uther.

Gwyniviere went for the locks while the others fought to reach and protect her. Hooks dragged
at the rescuers; stitched enemies crowded the cages. Brastok exchanged places with Gwyniviere
to put himself between her and an attacker. Rance fought beside her and broke a padlock when
more delicate methods failed. Angel healed prisoners, slowed the enemies aboard the ship,
and suggested that one particularly dangerous opponent take its friends swimming.

It obliged, with more force than its companion would have preferred.

The party freed Uther and checked him for seams and signs of substitution. But Angel went
farther, flying into the ship's hold. There she found more chained prisoners and a submarine
unlike the earlier designs they knew. As the vessel moved, she began freeing captives and
helping those who could still help one another.

Then she used the recovered thread to search for Mallow.

The spell found him at a distance, lost him, and found him much nearer. A prisoner carrying a
strange thread injured himself; his body burst apart, and a tall man emerged. With threats
against Angel's companions and family, the captor forced her into the submarine. The apparent
corpse worked the lever that dropped them into Maiden's Bay.

Underwater, Mallow described his puppets and strings. He threatened the castle and Angel's
mother, and claimed that the Maidenfelds possessed a hidden key.

Angel tried to reach someone beneath those threats.

She let him taste her celestial blood and silently prayed for his freedom from curses and
bargains. Golden scales appeared. Her blood broke Mallow's fey deals and curses; the people
under his control collapsed. His body began a transformation it could not complete, scales
and forming wings falling away.

Auraxx emerged—a gold dragon whose soul Angel had freed.

In thanks, he offered her a boon. Angel did not ask merely to escape. She
wanted the horrors undone. Auraxx warned that reversing everything could break reality again.

Instead, she asked for a childhood.

The boon returned Crispin Mallow to ten years old, with his memories up to that age and none
from afterward. His magic and unnatural attachments were removed. Auraxx had warned that his
nature would remain damaged; Angel chose to take responsibility for raising him anyway.

That left her holding an unconscious boy inside a moving submarine she did not know how to
operate.

Hellion's answer to her <Spell name="sending" src="phb" /> brought news of attacks on Castle Rannek.
At Maidenfeld Keep, Nick of Bede's supposed reinforcements had turned against the defenders. Angel
found the submarine's brake, but its magical propulsion kept driving it toward a reef.
<Spell name="dispel magic" src="phb" /> stopped that problem and began another: the vessel failed
and flooded.

She carried Crispin through <Spell name="dimension door" src="phb" /> into the sea.

Angel survived the ascent, kept them afloat, and contacted Brastok. Hedwig used her familiarity
with Angel to reach her; three women appeared and brought Angel and Crispin back. Brastok
embraced her. Jane dried her. At the harbor, Gwyniviere held their unconscious father while
Lance freed prisoners.

Uther was alive. Angel was back. Mallow's control had broken.

There was still a ship full of people to retrieve, a castle full of wounded, and a child who
would wake without knowing what his older self had done.

Hedwig undertook the ship's recovery. The rescuers brought Uther through shelter and treatment
before returning to Castle Rannek. There they found casualties preserved for revival, healers
working through their exhaustion, and Sandor unconscious after saving Trajan during the siege.
He had returned to fight for the castle; how he had escaped captivity and Mallow's control
remained unexplained.

The immediate battles at the castle and keep ended. Most of the castle's attackers had been
controlled by Mallow. When they collapsed, those still acting freely fled or were captured.
Nick of Bede also appeared to have been controlled, though that did not settle every question
about his allegiances. Ordinary followers had gone into battle under leaders whose choices
were not their own.

Recovery demanded distinctions the fighting had allowed little time to make.

Angel reunited with her mother beside Uther, and explained what she had done. The family
weighed responsibility, justice for Mallow's victims, and the work his transformation had not
repaired. Sister Pira's pool offered a possible treatment for afflicted survivors, but its
water worked there: the patients would have to be brought to it.

When Crispin woke, he feared his father and aunt had sold him.

He expected pursuit, disliked being touched, and wanted to leave. Angel offered food, shelter,
and a place with her, though she would not let him go. He liked plants. Hunger and pudding
proved more immediately persuasive than reassurance. Angel lent him Kelthyr's protective
amulet, requiring its eventual return, and asked the others to treat him as a friend.

He knew his childhood in Maiden's Field. He did not know how far beyond it he had come.

Sandor woke as well, objected to Angel's method of administering a potion, and agreed to help
against the Reformists after resting. He described the Sandor-shaped creature used against
the party: in his account, Mallow had assembled it using a murdered young changeling's copied
face. That was separate from Trajan's friendly impersonation of Hellion.

Between treatment and the next gathering, Angel pursued another family mystery: Lance's
secret storage. He led her to a guarded warehouse in Rannekford's river port. The supposedly
dull errand concealed his collection of *Legally Bare* issues, merchandise, and artwork.
Its top VIP, “Rance,” had evidently been very busy. Secrecy was promised, copies were signed,
and the party returned to the castle around noon.

At lunch, Uther was awake and much improved. Kate and the others discussed continued healing,
while Hellion remained at Maidenfeld Keep and allies searched the countryside. Ellington's
treatment was still ongoing. He and Antoinette reiterated their intention to abdicate after
helping repair the damage, with Mark rather than Luke named to succeed him.

Angel still intended to travel to Bastion, despite her family's warnings, and wanted
Gwyniviere with her. More immediate questions crowded the table: the Emerald Shield shards,
the king's resistance to a parasite, Reformist access to places of power, and the advanced
submarines the Maidenfelds had concealed.

Uther wanted a meeting.

Getting the parties together required arguments about security and neutral ground, but the
formal inquiry convened at the castle. Ellington and Antoinette spoke about the movement
they had renounced. They had restored the memories held by their parasites so that they
would retain responsibility and guilt. They offered those memories as evidence, accepted
the possibility of execution, and called for reparations without expecting forgiveness.
Hellion showed the living parasite extracted from Ellington.

The Maidenfelds faced questions of their own.

A binding bargain restricted what the family could disclose. That restriction did not make
every explanation they could give true. Baron Maidenfeld accepted responsibility for
withholding information, defended his attempts to contain the danger, and recounted his
wife's infection and death. He had killed her believing the parasite had replaced her;
his belief did not establish that rescue had been impossible.

Confronted about the submarine aboard the *MS Paprika*, the family acknowledged ownership.
The Baron said the vessels principally protected the secrecy of their spice source, and
argued that commercial silence had not meant inaction against the Reformists.

The cages aboard the *Paprika* demanded a better account than that.

The ship had carried people of varied ages and species, reportedly without criminal records.
At least one was dead. Mallow's controlled sailors established his involvement, but not the
family's suggested hijacking, its timing, or whether someone inside had helped. Uther invoked
his authority over the Western Fleet and treated the affair as a national-security matter.

Nick of Bede said he had not controlled his actions. He described Crispin as a gifted gardener who
denied knowing magic and disappeared periodically. The inquiry also heard that Crispin had served as
a Reformist candle bearer. Testimony also supplied a name for Mallow's passage through his victims:
“meat stride.” The interruptions in Angel's search had an explanation more terrible than travel
between trees.

Questions about the spice trade led to questions about hidden cultivation, orbs, and the
Maidenfelds' old royal knowledge. Hellion inferred that the Baron understood Draconic and
preferred not to advertise it. He let that knowledge remain discreet.

The investigation would proceed. Hellion made clear that he intended to accept, but not to
undertake it alone. Terms, leadership, and military companions still needed to be settled.

Beyond the inquiry waited the flooded delta. Broken weirs and sluice gates, ruined crops,
and the prospect of winter hunger could give the Reformists another opening. Powerful
<Spell name="plant growth" src="phb" /> might help, if the party could find a capable caster.
Returning the rescued elven children to their settlements offered a possible way to make contact
with local elves. The party did not want that approach to become a bargain in which children were
exchanged for assistance.

Meanwhile, the Baron sent Tristan to find out what had delayed Steven.

Tristan went to the courtyard. Later, he was located inside a cone of silence.

Angel tried to coax Crispin down from a tree with flowers she did not have. He knew perfectly
well that blood orchids were out of season. Pudding remained the stronger offer. The others
agreed to conceal his identity, while Angel guided him by the edge of her skirt.

Oren tried to follow Tristan.

The inquiry was still unfinished. So was the business in the courtyard.

## At the Table: Where We Left Off

- **Immediate scene:** the formal inquiry remains in progress. Oren is trying to follow Tristan,
  whose presence in the courtyard inside a cone of silence is established; his business there
  is not. Steven's delayed arrival also needs explaining.
- **Uther is rescued:** he is recovering and has asserted Western Fleet authority over the
  *MS Paprika* investigation. The battles at Castle Rannek and Maidenfeld Keep have ended.
- **Crispin is ten:** Auraxx's boon left him with memories only up to that age. Angel has taken
  responsibility for him; his identity is being kept secret. Breaking Mallow's fey bonds with
  Angel's blood caused the controlled fighters to collapse before the transformation.
- **The investigation will proceed:** Hellion intends to accept. Its terms, personnel in charge,
  and military companions remain undecided. Maidenfeld submarine ownership is acknowledged;
  the Baron's explanation and the proposed hijacking remain unverified.
- **Recovery continues:** Hedwig has undertaken retrieval of the ship and remaining captives;
  that rescue is not an unresolved outcome. Treatment, accounting for the court wizards, and
  returning the elven children remain important. Sandor is recovering after saving Trajan.
- **The delta needs help:** floodworks and crops require restoration to avert winter hunger.
  Contact with the local elves is proposed, not secured; returning their children must not
  become leverage for assistance.
- **Longer commitments:** Mallow's victims still need care and restitution. The Malthreks'
  corrective work and announced succession remain ahead, and Angel still wants to go to Bastion.
