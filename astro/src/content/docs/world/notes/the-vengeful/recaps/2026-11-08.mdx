## Previously, in *The Vengeful*…

The university's time loop had stopped. Outside its barrier, Thrantorbury had endured a week of
battle.

Crown Prince Marlion and his companions emerged into a capital held by Reformists. Castle
Dragonheart remained ahead of them, along with unfinished business: rescue King Alitar, Queen
Murwes, and Madeline, and find Garward Eastonton. The king was Marlion's father. The queen was
Kelthyr's mother. Neither son knew what he would find.

On the way to seek the surviving Royal Guards, the party passed mutilated bodies missing their
hearts and eyes. A teenage lookout fled when Oscar revealed himself. Marlion pursued him and
returned with a frightened boy who eventually gave his name as Pat.

Pat described his parents leaving their children alone at night, guards turning on civilians,
and old women taking his mother's heart. He had survived to watch over a refuge. When the
companions offered healing and water, he led them there.

The camp held wounded people with spell burns and ruined limbs. Beneath a pavilion, the dead
were being prepared for burial. Lime summoned a <Spell name="healing spirit" src="xge" />;
Luana handed out food. The companions stabilized survivors, but closing a wound could not
restore everything they had lost.

Marlion field-promoted Sergeant Serge to colonel, subject to the recovery of his senior officers.
Then the guards told their story.

Strange behavior had spread through the city before open fighting began. Suspect supplies had
slipped past purification. Soldiers sent to control gatherings had been overwhelmed; healers
and other spellcasters were singled out and killed. Expected paladin reinforcements from
Eastonton attacked the defenders instead. The guards accused Garward of betrayal, although
none had seen him in the battle.

They also described victims erupting into creatures. Survivors believed a green liquid had
forced parasites inside their hosts to mature. Some refugees remained isolated for exorcism.
The party recognized possible echoes of Eastonton, but the whole mechanism was still unclear.

The refuge had held. Its messages had not gotten help. Pigeons went unanswered, the beacon had
been doused, and the people who could cast <Spell name="sending" src="phb" /> were dead.
The tunnel into the castle had collapsed behind the attackers who used it.

The companions would need allies—and another way through.

Minfilia contacted Eskabeth Kilsward. Hags prepared to fight the Children of the Crone arrived
near the refuge, fought Reformists, and brought back prisoners. One conjurer, Matisse, said the
king was still in the castle and would be killed after serving some unknown purpose. The queen,
he reported, was intended for Sanery once her captors learned how to take elves out of Senera.
He had heard that Garward would be present, but had not seen him.

The allied hags had enemies of their own. They asked for help destroying a false Baba Yaga whom
they described as tainted by aberration. Old divisions could wait. Tiphanie invoked an existing
blood deal, and more sisters answered.

The coalition formed around a rescue. Civilians were to be evacuated to Eastonton, where the
badly wounded could receive further treatment. The Royal Guards would follow the Crown Prince.
Minfilia and the hags would draw the enemy's attention while the rescuers approached the castle.

First, Minfilia brought back the healers.

She stacked crates of orbs, invoked the Great Erected One, and cast
<Spell name="resurrection" src="phb" />. Three dead healers returned to life. Sarwen's missing
eye did not regrow; Tiphanie supplied a temporary prosthetic. Their orders were to follow the
rescue party and heal, not fight.

Then Minfilia raised the rest of the dead.

Humanoids, dogs, and other animals rose around the camp. Some crawled on broken limbs. Soldiers
and civilians panicked until Marlion ordered them to hold: these undead would not harm them.
Minfilia advanced beside Oscar, cackling at the head of her army.

The undead struck the Reformist lines. Hags overwhelmed the stronger defenders. Buildings fell
beneath their spells while the prince's force slipped past the main fighting toward the tunnel.

At the blockage, the undead collapsed under the force of buried Orbs of Repulsion. The party
brought forward Eastonton's great enhancement orb, and Tiphanie cast
<Spell name="transmute rock" src="xge" />. Part of the rubble became air. With Luana's help,
they recovered seven small repulsor orbs.

Seven Royal Guards each received one. Magic would have difficulty reaching them; steel would not.

When the remaining rocks fell away, hostile casters waited beyond. Luana, Tiphanie, and Kelthyr
answered three incoming spells with <Spell name="counterspell" src="phb" />. The orb-bearing
guards charged through an attempted <Spell name="hypnotic pattern" src="phb" /> and killed the
four opponents.

The tunnel was open.

Minfilia sent the undead ahead to spread chaos, keeping Malmann with her as an honor guard.
Marlion used the Topaz Sword of the Summer to dispel a hidden barrier. Tiphanie directed Luana,
Kelthyr, and the seven protected guards through the opening. She also lent Luana the Red Dragon
Mask she had received in exchange for Ashpitel.

Marlion guided the party along the castle's quieter passages, past marked traps and routes that
could deposit an intruder over a cliff or into lava. At a concealed broom-closet entrance, he
inserted a Dragonheart sigil. The wall opened, nearly sending a broom and buckets down the steps.

Beyond lay the basement beneath the High Keep. The divining pool was farther below. They hoped
the king would be there.

Kelthyr distributed masks. Luana filled her censer with Essence of Dream.

The first hags fell asleep before they could act. Farther down, an intricately shaped stone
staircase opened onto a broad landing. More hags waited there, expecting the fighting to remain
above them. The weaker ones succumbed to the smoke.

The others had time to discover that Tiphanie could become an adult silver dragon.

She drove them toward the landing's edge. Luana cast <Spell name="hunger of hadar" src="phb" />,
blinding the defenders and hindering their escape while Tiphanie attacked with her breath.
Marlion ordered the seven guards forward, then entered the darkness himself. The Summer Sword
protected him even from the dragon's breath.

Tiphanie eventually returned to her usual form to give her companions room. The surviving hags
retreated, but they were still dangerous. Kelthyr used <Spell name="thunder step" src="xge" />
to carry himself and Luana across the landing. Attempts to imprison the guards and prince behind
iron bars failed against the orbs and Summer Sword.

A powerful attack brought both Kelthyr and Luana down.

Marlion stepped toward them, giving Luana enough temporary strength to rise before turning his
rage on a hag. Luana refilled her censer. Three more enemies fell asleep. Tiphanie unleashed
powerful twinned spells against those still fighting.

When the defenders were down and Tiphanie said she had no further need for them, Marlion
beheaded them.

Luana gave Kelthyr a healing potion. His return to consciousness triggered a Wild Magic Surge.
Suddenly, he appeared dressed in a mankini, while Luana acquired an equally revealing sling-style
outfit. Their equipment still worked. Kelthyr had no idea how long the illusion would last.

Tiphanie was not waiting for a wardrobe change.

The landing was cleared. The castle was not. Somewhere farther below, they still hoped to find
the king.

## At the Table: Where We Left Off

- **The rescue party is beneath the High Keep:** the secret approach and landing battle are
  complete. The next move is toward the divining pool. Neither arrival at the pool nor finding
  King Alitar has happened yet.
- **The royal rescue remains urgent:** Matisse reported that the king would be killed after an
  unknown use and that the queen was intended for Sanery. Those are leads, not witnessed outcomes.
  Madeline's whereabouts and Garward's actual presence remain unresolved.
- **The party has seven orb-bearing Royal Guards:** their protection worked against the hags'
  magic and attempted barriers, but does not prevent physical injury. Minfilia and the allied
  hags launched the wider assault; the whole castle has not been secured.
- **Kelthyr is conscious again:** Luana healed him after the fight. Their outfit illusions are
  temporary and leave equipment functional; the campaign table gives them ten minutes, although
  Kelthyr does not know that duration. Tiphanie wants to continue immediately.
- **The survivors still need follow-through:** evacuation to Eastonton and advanced healing were
  planned, not confirmed complete. Minfilia resurrected three healers, including Sarwen, and sent
  them with the rescue force under orders to heal rather than attack.
