## Arrival in Maiden's Port

- Angel used an item formerly belonging to her sister
  [Laudine Maidenfeld née Rannek](../../characters/laudine-rannek.mdx) as a teleportation anchor.
  - Angel, Gwyniviere, and their companions arrived in Laudine's cramped rented wooden apartment in
    [Maiden's Port](../../locations/maidens-port.mdx) rather than the larger residence Angel
    remembered.
  - An Arcana check found distant magic but none inside the apartment.
  - Laudine explained that the quarters stood near the atelier and closer to Maiden's Field than
    Rannek, although the field remained about a day's travel away; the group had followed the advice
    of Angel's mother, [Marchioness Maria Rannek](../../characters/maria-rannek.mdx), to use
    Laudine's home while attempting to rescue Father.

  or disappearance unconfirmed.
  - Barges could not approach, but Laudine thought her husband Robert at the Maiden's Field Spice
    Company might secure passage.
  - The family rejected teleporting directly to Father because a powerful enemy caster opposed
    them.
  - They discussed house keys as possible teleportation anchors and explained that Angel and her
    sister had arrived with hired mercenaries after Father, Lance, and their forces were defeated
    by an enemy they did not yet understand.
- Angel presented a Purple Dragon royal seal, said she served the Crown Prince, and had Gwyniviere
  attest that he gave it to her.
  - Recent events made Angel's previously dismissed stories credible.
  - Maria was with Duchess Malthrek, and Angel named Master Kelthyr Kulok as her tutor and
    Hicklander connection.
  - After Robert's changeling warning, Laudine tested Angel with a birthday question.
  - Angel explained that operational security had kept Laudine uninformed about her
    deputization and argued that, despite being eleven, her magic, Maria's authorization, and her
    determination to save Father and Lance justified her role.
- Gwyniviere reminded Laudine that Angel had hired the mercenaries, Maria had vouched for them,
  Tyrion Donmar had become marshal, and Sir Brastok assisted Angel.
  - Laudine offered to ask Robert about reinforcing Angel's roughly twenty-person force.
  - Teleportation was scarce, while Maidenfeld spellcasters might possess better intelligence.
- They blamed the Maidenfeld family's treatment of farmers and employees for creating the
  Hicklander uprising and an opening for Reformist infiltration.
  - Angel said the Crown Prince alone grasped the full threat and was conducting parallel work in
    Chantaburi with other friends.
  - Angel's group had recently captured Reformist parasites and extracted them from hosts, with
    Gwyniviere participating.
- To avoid alarming Maiden's Port, they approached the Spice Company with only Bertram Dorand, who
  introduced himself as leader of the Lord's Judgment, and three other mercenaries.
- Gwyniviere underwent an identity check using the remembered perfume name Angel's Breath and a
  drawing request.
  - The party said Gwyniviere had been copied and that her duplicate traveled to a military base
    with a copy of Mark.
- The family also discussed Pallia Travertine, a literal hag captured by another hag with a
  different appearance.
  - They inferred that the Travertines had made a bargain when Pallia was younger and said her
    proposed marriage to Luke Malthrek had failed.
  - Luke had instead married [Katalin Helsinki (Kate)](../../characters/katalin-helsinki.mdx), a
    Tanduarian. After Tiphanie killed Pallia, the Crown Prince arranged Kate's adoption into a
    cadet branch of House Dragonheart so she could marry Luke.
- The party then departed with four mercenaries in noble-guard formation.
  - The buildings of Maiden's Port appeared generally newer than Port Mibram's, though Port Mibram
    had newer Admiralty and harbor construction beside older slums.

## The Maidenfeld Spice Company

- Maiden's Port proved to be a fashionable and wealthy chartered city where the Maidenfelds
  resupplied and unloaded trade goods, although unrest had emptied the streets and frightened
  residents.
  - Its harbor accommodated galley-sized ships larger than ordinary Port Mibram traffic, where
    similar vessels were mainly military and civilian trade tended toward Port-Sera-by-Malterdale.
  - The Maidenfeld fleet visibly exceeded all other trader organizations' fleets combined in size
    and maintenance.
- The party reached the distinctive brick-and-stone Maidenfeld Spice Company near the harbor,
  surrounded by cedarwood and thriving herbs.
  - Two guards stood openly outside, while Sir Brastok detected four more concealed through the
    mental connection.
  - Laudine whispered to a guard and gained entry.
  - The broad-windowed lobby received green-tinted light from courtyard plants.
  - A receptionist addressed the escort as Lady Laudine, and she led the party onward despite a
    man's weak objection.
- She showed them a door or lift-like installation built into a marble-sided pillar and
  commissioned from spellcasters.
  - Laudine operated its crude, noisy mechanical lift, moving from L for lobby to the fourth floor.
  - The device imitated more advanced technology linked to ruins and Eastonton; Minfilia had said
    Eastonton's systems needed no levers.
  - The group fondly remembered Minfilia and discussed skeletons associated with her that had
    apparently been left in the Travertines.
- Laudine guided them through Accounts, past employees who called her Lady Laudine, and into Robert
  Maidenfeld's office.
  - Robert recognized Lady Gwyniviere and Lady Angel and told Angel that her elder sister
    was preparing an extravagant birthday present.
  - Laudine intended to advertise her products at the celebration, while Lord Brandon Malterdale
    had offered catering and a large cake.
  - Brandon was called the family's youngest son despite contradictory statements about his birth
    order.
- Members of the Malterdale family had aided the party's first investigations into the Reformist
  Church.
  - Those present agreed that the Reformists were evil and damaging to trade, but openly opposing
    them could obstruct travel.
  - They therefore concealed their knowledge and resistance and accepted temporary compromise
    despite some desire for open defiance.

## The Revolt, the Spice Trade, and Crispin Mallow

- The parties disputed the roots of the Hicklander revolt.
  - One side blamed low wages and excessive Maidenfield labor demands, while the nobles argued
    that worker cooperatives negotiated and distributed wages according to computed values.
  - The nobles believed anti-royal and anti-nobility agents were inflaming discontent, leading the
    group to conclude that Reformists could have infiltrated both cooperatives and Hicklanders.
- The Reformists allegedly supplied Faltir-linked explosives by transmuting salt into black
  powder, an explanation supported by nonmagical destruction of weirs and sluice gates.
  - A captured submarine containing black powder had been reported lost six months earlier, around
    the time local explosives appeared, suggesting that Maiden's Field itself had been infiltrated.
- Crispin Mallow was identified as an enemy necromancer who posed as a gardener, had recently been
  hired by the household, and carried instructions granting access before its withdrawal.
  - Normal safeguards against Face-Stealers and changelings had failed to stop him.
  - He mutilated people by switching legs and combining limbs, muscles, organs, and other living
    parts into unwilling amalgamations.
  - Stacy Cartwright, identified here as the Maltreks' court wizard, had been captured and fused
    with dragon anatomy.
  - Forty-eight castle employees were feared missing and possibly used in Mallow's work, although
    only two creatures containing parts from roughly five bodies each were confirmed.
- Angel remained focused on rescuing her increasingly thin father, Uther, seen beside a court wizard
  and Sandor, while Hellion was suggested as a better adviser on mature operational decisions.
- The party directly warned Robert that Mallow hated Maiden's Field, intended to destroy the
  Maidenfeld family, and still possessed access to Laudine's home after she knowingly hired him as
  guardian.
  - Angel and Gwyniviere had teleported to Laudine's residence in Maiden's Port, while Robert and
    the mounted travelers were near Maiden's Field's opposite end, roughly two days away by horse.
  - Mr. Bell's augmentation accelerated the horses, but the riders planned an hour-long break for
    water, grass, and restoration.
  - Robert resolved to alert the Baron and other Maidenfeld relatives.
- The group concluded that destroying Maiden's Field would also let the Reformists seize the spice
  trade.
  - They were using the Fructarian Guild, whose authorized trade covered limited goods and imported
    exotic produce such as mangoes and pomelos but not Maiden's Field herbs and spices.
  - Fructarian produce carried parasite eggs.
- Hicklanders had discovered how to kill the eggs, but their regional groups were not unified;
  known groups operated in Bramsgate, the Narrows, Port Surrey, the Maltreks, and Rannek.
  - Maltrek Hicklanders had helped retake Castle Malthrek from the Reformist Church, while
    Reformists were infiltrating Rannek's Hicklanders and supplying gunpowder.
  - The party cited a captured Hicklander Reformist in Malterdale, questioned by the court wizard,
    as evidence of that manipulation.

## Mallow's Biomancy and the Rescue Debate

  joining still-living parts from different creatures.
  - The restrained woman remained alive despite mismatched replacement legs.
  - <Spell name="cure wounds" src="phb" /> could stop Mallow's stitched creatures; the party had
    encountered them while fighting Stacy, whose body Gwyniviere and Sister Pira later carried to
    a healing pool.
    - Earlier discussions distinguished victims transformed into monstrosities from ordinary
      Face-Stealers, who are not harmed by healing magic. The precise limits remain unresolved.
  - Eevee warned that Mallow could oppose an army with constructed creatures.
  - Many changelings and rare Face-Stealers under his command were coerced through captive relatives
    instead of serving willingly, and he did not seem to exploit their ordinary abilities.
- Hellion was reportedly near Maiden's Field's western edge and able to teleport to one participant.
  - The group considered asking him to contact the Baron.
  - Angel had only one teleportation available.
  - Eevee insisted on going to the keep to rescue her father, but others urged waiting for her
    brother's intelligence about the Reformists' naval campaign.
- Mallow could track and command his puppets and notice failures to respond, although he apparently

  - Suspected agents carried information around Maiden's Port and Castle Rannek, so the party

- Robert wished he had inherited yellow eyes and was reassured that the Reformists manipulated
  nobles through power, wealth, and family influence.

## Negotiating with the River Hag

- An Insight result of twenty-five exposed the apparently human young woman as a hag concealed by
  glamour; the observer's innate sensitivity pierced the disguise.
- An unidentified eladrin offered her a potion in exchange for answers, and she accepted it as
  payment for one response.
  - She explained that a nearby cave had flooded again because elementals were destroying sluice
    gates installed jointly by the Ranneks and Maiden's Field.
- The eladrin then offered a <Item name="ring of protection" src="dmg" />.
  - She joked that local proposals usually used chokers and asked whether he was proposing marriage.
  - He offered to show her the Feywild if she provided a safe meeting place for him and his
    companions.
  - She doubted his ability, reasoning that she and her associates would already have returned if
    return were possible.
- After an eighteen Charisma result, he intensified his flirtation and identified himself as an
  eladrin.
  - The hag answered that the figure she called "Mother" had taught her none of his kind should be
    here.
  - He demonstrated seasonal fey magic, changing from warm green, grasslike Spring traits to
    bright-yellow Summer, then autumnal features, and finally a solemn Winter aspect with
    silver-white hair, silver-blue eyes, and an aura of cold.
- He requested sanctuary and then offered his firstborn in exchange for an active alliance.
  - The unfinished clause apparently required him to relinquish his firstborn to the hags.
  - The bargain would endure planar travel and death, while conception was required to be
    consensual and uncoerced.
  - A negotiating party warned that older bargains took priority and that this deal would be off
    if the hags were already allied with the eladrin's enemies.
  - He named the Reformist Church, its allies, and the Children of the Crone as enemies.
- He also said he had brought a hag loyal to Baba Yaga from Murkendraw who was searching for hags
  lost during the Draco-Giant War, although he did not know her exact origins.

## The Riverside Alliance

- The three local negotiators accepted an alliance against the Reformists and those of the Crone.
  - The compact granted sanctuary, protection, and permission to establish a settlement within a
    circle extending two hundred feet from the specified point on the riverbank.
    - Protection extended to people owing allegiance to the protected party while they remained
      inside that area, not unconditionally to everyone entering it.
  - The Reformists were tentatively compared to changelings or doppelgangers who assumed
    appearances, twisted words, and could replace people or their children.
- The party showed the hag a fragment of the Heart of the Urdefolcin and explained that Crispin
  Mallow used such material in experiments and for <Spell name="tree stride" src="phb" />.
  - They suspected he had bargained with one of the crones, declared their intention to kill him,
    and received the hag's acceptance.
- Afterward, an unidentified character returned to Malmann, who confirmed he had sensed the hags,
  and they prepared to leave together.
  - Someone urged the paladin to practice Divine Sense against celestials, aberrations, hags, and
    other unfamiliar creatures; he admitted he had previously used it mainly against undead.
- At the table, Malygos was described as a dragon turtle associated with monks and a monastic
  tradition rather than shrines or churches.
  - The fey creatures who still remember Malygos think of them as the Archfey of Magic; that is
    their understanding, not a definitive account of Malygos's true nature.
