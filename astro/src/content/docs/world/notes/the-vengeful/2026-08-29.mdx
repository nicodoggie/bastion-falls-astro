## Convergence on Maiden's Field

The recap and renewed play establish Mallow's captives and fleshcrafted victims, Reformist
manipulation of refugees and institutions, Castle Rannek's losses, and the allied rescue force
converging through Maidensport and Port Mibram.

At the Spice Company, the party exchanges intelligence with the Maidenfelds about Mallow, Reformist
infiltration, regional uprisings, sabotage, magical impostors, trade routes, and endangered
captives.

## Tea and the Altered Servant

Planning pauses for family and magical-device discussions until Angel discovers that a servant has
Mallow's switched-leg alterations and may have bypassed the household's magical screening.

## Operational Planning and Hag Summons

The allies refine their understanding of Mallow and Reformist logistics, coordinate Maidenfeld and
teleportation support, and split attention between Castle Rannek, Maidenfeld Keep, and a streamside
attempt to summon a hag.

Children of the Crone, after which the group connects Mallow to Urdefolcin material and prepares to
depart.
