## The Broken Capital

- The party emerged from Thrantorbury University after stopping its time loop.
  - The university’s barrier appeared to have spared it from the week of battles that devastated the
    surrounding capital.
  - The group included Crown Prince Marlion, Tiphanie, Minfilia, Oscar, Matthew, Lime, Justin,
    Kelthyr, and Luana.
- Kelthyr reported Reformists covering the road, wall, and nearby gate, with a base near
  the cliffside.
- The party reviewed its objectives: kill Garward and rescue Madeline, King Alitar I Dragonheart,
  and Queen Murwes Dragonheart, Marlion’s father and Kelthyr’s mother respectively.
  - Madeline was initially the highest priority, but the group later argued for rescuing the king
    first, fearing an open assault would prompt his transfer to Sanery.
  - Marlion thought Garward might be with his father, but their location was unconfirmed.
- Possible approaches to Castle Dragonheart included a tunnel, flight, or teleportation by Tiphanie.
  - The castle’s immense teleportation circle had been broken for years, the airspace was probably
    watched, and Tiphanie had never visited the destination.
  - The party therefore considered scouting with a familiar before committing.
- The group expected defenses tailored against them, possibly including <Spell name="hallow" src="phb" />, <Spell name="glyph of warding" src="phb" />, anti-undead measures, paladins, and hags.
  - They believed the enemy probably knew about their necromancer and the Summer Sword, but these
    defenses remained predictions.
- Oscar confirmed Reformists on the battlements with a spyglass.
  - A defender cast a fireball at a passing bird, apparently suspecting a familiar or other spy.
  - The party considered a rear or sewer entrance normally illuminated by repeated magic, as well as
    Reformist disguises, though the enemy might recognize that tactic.
- The university appeared evacuated, and the rest of Thrantorbury was sparsely populated.
  - The party considered searching the university basements and dormitories, the ruined town, and
    the Royal Guard auxiliary base for Hicklander contacts, survivors, and allies.
  - Missing residents might have hidden, fled, died, or been transformed into aberrations.
- An earlier attempt to find the king through an associated object had revealed nothing.
  - The group considered nondetection, heavy shielding, confinement in caverns or pools beneath the
    high keep, or removal from the castle.
  - They also speculated that the king’s blood might connect elven and Hick matters with recent
    dragon developments.
- The party decided to find the Royal Guards before finalizing the operation.
  - Tiphanie detected magic resembling Murkendraw and the Feywild warmth associated with Baba Yaga,
    the coven, and other hags.
  - Something altered and wrong was intermingled with it. She did not recognize it as the Children
    of the Crone and feared its source was not one of her sisters.
- On the road toward central Thrantorbury, the party found scattered, mutilated bodies whose hearts
  and eyes had been removed.
- Near the Crown Gate, Minfilia led the cautious advance through territory reportedly patrolled by
  Reformists.
  - A Perception result of 21 revealed someone peeking out and watching the party.
  - When Oscar exposed himself, the watcher fled. The Crown Prince pursued with an apparently
    step-like magical ability and returned with a boy of about fifteen or sixteen.
- Luana told Minfilia that she and the Crown Prince had formed a partnership, prompting companions
  to worry that he might be a bad influence.
- The teenage lookout gave the name Pat after being challenged over his first answer, Dennis.
- The boy said cloaked Reformists had taken over while the townspeople began behaving strangely.
  - His parents repeatedly left him and his younger sister alone at night.
  - His father eventually surrendered his mother to old women who removed her heart.
  - The party identified the women as hags and tentatively connected them to the Children of the
    Crone.
- According to the boy, the upheaval began about five days earlier.
  - Royal Guards fought one another and attacked civilians.
  - Armed men advanced toward the castle through a tunnel amid explosions and heavy casualties.
  - He could not confirm the guards’ current condition or what was happening inside the castle.
- The boy was serving as a lookout for the remaining survivors.
  - After the party identified themselves as healers, offered aid, and gave him water, he described
    refugees sheltered at a base protected by some Royal Guards.
  - He warned that they would have to sneak past Reformist harassment, then guided them safely to
    the camp.
- The Crown Prince appeared deeply guilty while looking at the boy, then steeled himself as though
  reaffirming that his earlier actions had been necessary.
  - Another companion promised that the boy’s family would be avenged.
- The boy described the Reformist Church as a rogue faction exploiting the Divine Masochist’s name,
  transforming innocents into controllable monsters, and possibly maintaining an Apgarian
  connection.
  - Paladins he expected to fight the Reformists instead acted as their allies and called themselves
    soldiers of the Divine Masochist.
  - These remained the boy’s allegations rather than independently established facts.
- At the survivor camp, a large pavilion held bodies being cleaned for dignified burial.
  - Living refugees suffered severe wounds, spell burns, and damaged or missing limbs.
- The party immediately rendered aid.
  - Lime cast <Spell name="healing spirit" src="xge" />, Luana distributed food, and others used measures including <Spell name="spare the dying" src="phb" />.
  - Healing stabilized many survivors and closed wounds but did not restore every lost function; some would need <Spell name="greater restoration" src="phb" /> or comparable treatment.
- Sergeant Serge served as the camp’s effective contact.
  - Several lieutenants were alive but initially unable to command, though healing and rest might
    restore them.

## The Royal Guard’s Account

- The Crown Prince field-promoted Serge from sergeant to colonel, subject to any recovered senior
  officers resuming authority.
- Minfilia Diaz introduced herself as a necromancer, claimed to be pregnant, named Oscar as her
  soulmate, and presented Tiphanie as the new court wizard.
  - Although necromancers were normally arrested on sight, the guards postponed enforcing that rule.
  - Separately, Lime was identified as sixteen and pregnant, with the man helping her described as
    the baby’s father.
- The guards said residents had behaved strangely for weeks.
  - Taverns began operating like brothels, overt sexual gatherings spread, and misconduct became
    common during the day.
- The group suspected a tactic resembling the tainted vegetables allegedly used to make people
  susceptible to beliefs in Eastonton.
  - Suspect food might have reached Thrantorbury.
  - Officials quarantined, purified, or burned supplies they detected, but cleric shortages let food
    from trusted suppliers bypass purification.
  - This included goods from the fruit merchants’ guild, whose cooperation with the Reformist Church
    had only recently become known.
- A squad sent to control the gatherings was reportedly drawn in and killed.
  - Witnesses described dazed victims, apparent thorn crowns, interpersonal violence, and effects resembling <Spell name="crown of madness" src="phb" /> and <Spell name="hypnotic pattern" src="phb" />.
  - Dispatched spellcasters retained their hands but became unable to cast before their throats were
    cut.
  - <Spell name="silence" src="phb" /> was proposed as an explanation, and later testimony implicated chanting hags, but neither was confirmed.
- The Royal Guard signaled and organized for battle, but enemies teleported directly into rooms.
  - Fighting became chaotic, casualties mounted, and a planned pincer failed.
  - Expected paladin reinforcements from Eastonton attacked the defenders instead.
  - The paladins were described as having grown up under Garward in a Reformist environment and
    converted without realizing it.
  - Garward, the Lord Commander, was accused of having betrayed the Crown long ago, although no
    witness saw him in the battle.
  - Hostile paladins targeted spellcasters and killed the remaining healers, while rapidly healing
    attackers and infiltrators inside the Royal Guard compounded the losses.
  - Hundreds of bodies were later gathered for burial in the pavilion.
- Noncombatants who could not be removed from the gatherings began exploding and leaving strange
  creatures behind.
  - Survivors believed a green liquid reacted with parasites already inside victims’ heads, forcing
    the parasites to mature, grow within their hosts, and manipulate their minds.
  - Refugees and sentries were screened. Most were cleared, while some wounded survivors were
    isolated for exorcism after a detector sensed parasites.
- The attackers withdrew after a loud call roughly two or three days earlier.
  - The defenders fortified the refuge and evacuated some people, but Reformists blocked a breakout
    through the Crown Gate.
  - Carrier pigeons received no response, <Spell name="sending" src="phb" /> casters had been killed, and a signal beacon was doused.
  - Royal Guard patrols lower on the slopes or the Navy might otherwise have helped, but the
    defenders believed the enemy had anticipated those options and disabled the Navy.
- The group considered staging a false attack to rally the remaining forces and divert attention
  while another team rescued or seized the king.
  - Minfilia proposed waves of undead as the diversion while the prince’s force infiltrated.
- The known tunnel to the castle had collapsed through its middle section, cutting off resupply.
  - Its builders reportedly included secret entrances opened by particular magical items.
  - An ally familiar with the tunnels said their sword could dispel the entrances’ magic.
  - Other ideas included climbing with conjured spiders, using tremor-sensing moles, excavating or
    transmuting the blockage, and employing skeleton labor.
  - Justin sensed what he associated with Orbs of Repulsion hidden in the rubble and doubted
    ordinary magic could clear it.
  - The party considered extracting an orb both as valuable loot and as a weapon against the enemy.
- Paladins and Royal Guards who turned against the defenders had fought through the tunnels and
  collapsed the route behind them.
  - Explosions and fighting followed inside the castle, which had been quiet for about a day.
  - The defenders did not know whether their former friends were mind-controlled.
  - A companion recalled Sir Essen’s account after his parasite had been removed in Eastonton:
    Essen remembered Garward ordering the paladins to withdraw from the war and using his men as
    a shield.
  - Minfilia asked that Garward be left to her and [Sir Malmann](../../characters/erik-malmann.mdx).
- Lime continued treating survivors through a Luana-shaped <Spell name="summon fey" src="tce" /> spirit; the spirit was not Luana herself.
- The party feared that the king or queen would be teleported away once the assault began.
  - If the king remained in the castle but locating magic could not find him, the tunnels beneath
    the high keep seemed the likeliest location.
  - The group also feared he was being prepared as a hostage.
- The party believed local Reformists and hags were cooperating but did not know their objective.
  - Theories involved Crispin Mallow’s journal, the pools, dragons, valuable dragon remains, a
    dracolich ritual, Auraxx, or the Elven Boons.
  - Another theory held that destroying Thrantorbury was intended to terrorize the population and
    break captives’ resistance.
  - The king might be near pools resembling those at the Thralmals while the enemy sought
    shards, a dragon, or other boons, but none of this was established.
- Luana was suggested as an infiltration companion because her sleep magic might reduce fighting,
  although it remained unclear whether it would affect hags.
- The party’s uncertain inventory of the Elven Boons placed the Sapphire Brooch of Winter’s boon
  with the enemy and suggested they knew the Summer Sword’s location and possessed at least two
  shield fragments.
  - They corrected a seasonal mix-up: the Emerald Shield belongs to Spring; the Ruby Amulet of Fall
    is the Autumn boon. The discussion did not establish a reliable current custody list.

## Beneath the High Keep — Post-session Recollection

This continuation records the campaign maintainer’s account of the unrecorded ending. It is a
broad-strokes recollection, not a transcript, and has no recording timestamps.

- Marlion’s secret passage emerged into a basement beneath the High Keep, with corridors broad
  enough for an adolescent dragon. The party hoped to find the king at the divining pool farther
  below.
- Wearing masks against the smoke, the party used Luana’s Essence of Dream censer to put nearby
  hags to sleep before they could act.
  - Beyond them, an intricately stone-shaped staircase descended to a broad landing where more
    hags were resting, expecting the fighting to remain above and outside the Keep.
  - The weakest hags succumbed to the smoke, but others remained awake. Before they could react,
    Tiphanie transformed into an adult silver dragon, surprising her own companions as well.
- The attack drove the awake defenders toward the wall at the landing’s edge.
  - Luana cast <Spell name="hunger of hadar" src="phb" />, blinding them and hindering escape while
    Tiphanie assaulted them with her breath weapon.
  - At Marlion’s order, the seven orb-bearing Royal Guards advanced to cover the party. Their orbs
    protected them against the hags’ predominantly magical attacks.
  - Marlion entered the Hunger of Hadar and attacked freely, protected by the Summer Sword even
    from Tiphanie’s breath.
- After exhausting her breath weapons, Tiphanie returned to her usual form to give the others room
  to move. The hags began retreating, but the battle was not over.
- Kelthyr used <Spell name="thunder step" src="xge" /> to take himself and Luana to the other side
  of the landing to cover the retreat.
  - The hags tried to imprison the guards and Marlion behind iron bars, but the orbs and the
    prince’s sword defeated the barriers.
  - A strong attack brought Kelthyr and Luana down. Marlion used a refreshing step in their
    direction, giving Luana temporary hit points, then raged and slashed a hag.
  - Luana got up and replenished her censer. Another three hags fell asleep, leaving only the
    strongest fighting.
- Tiphanie unleashed powerful twinned spells, emboldened by the magical protection covering much
  of the party. Poor rolls for the remaining hags helped bring them down.
  - After Tiphanie confirmed that she had no further need for the hags, Marlion beheaded them.
- Luana gave Kelthyr a healing potion. The shock of regaining consciousness triggered a Wild Magic
  Surge, with rolls of 4 and 44 on the campaign table.
  - Kelthyr received a mankini outfit illusion and Luana a revealing sling-style outfit illusion.
  - The [Wild Magic table](../../misc/wild-magic.mdx) gives the effect a ten-minute duration and
    leaves gear functional. Kelthyr had never experienced it before and did not know its duration.
- Tiphanie urged the party to continue immediately rather than wait for the illusions to end.
  - Amusement and concern for Marlion’s father were suggested as possible motives, not established
    knowledge of her thoughts.
  - The session ended with the party intending to search for the king at once. They had not yet
    reached the divining pool or found him.
