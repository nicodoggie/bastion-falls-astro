## Reformist Goals

- So far, it seems like the Reformists have these goals around the world:
  - Collection and exploration of these [Orbs of Power](../../../misc/orbs-of-power/index.mdx)
  - Abduction and extraction of magical organs
  - Infection of magic users with aberration parasites
  - Transformation of infested magic users into aberrant forms
- What had they done so far to further their goals:
  - Took over [Eastonton](../../../locations/eastonton.mdx)
  - Took over [The Maltreks](../../../locations/the-maltreks.mdx)
  - Took over some cells of the [Hickland for Hicks](../../../organizations/hickland-for-hicks.mdx)
  - Made deals with the Children of the Coven Hags
- These could not and should not be the actual goal, but what is?
