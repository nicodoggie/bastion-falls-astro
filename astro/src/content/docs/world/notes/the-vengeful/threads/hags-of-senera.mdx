The Hags of Senera are not a single entity like the
[Hags of Hama](../../../organizations/hags-of-hama.mdx). It is simply means of describing a group of
hags that have travelled from the [Feywild](../../../locations/feywild.mdx) through the
[The Realm Bridge](../../../novelty/the-realm-bridge.mdx).

```markmap
## The Hags of Senera

## The Faithful

- These hags are the ones who have formed a large closely-knit group of covens
    that still hold to the traditions of the
    [Children of the Coven](../../../organizations/children-of-the-coven.mdx)
- They are children of the Mother, faithful hags who still recognize
    [Baba Yaga](../../../characters/baba-yaga.mdx) as the Mother.
- They wish to return to the Feywild and reconvene with the
    [Baba Yaga](../../../characters/baba-yaga.mdx)

## The Adapted

## The Children of the Crone

- These are hags that followed the
    [False Crone](../../../characters/false-crone.mdx) to Thera to establish a
    new foothold of power, in hopes of being able to rival the Baba Yaga
    herself.
```
