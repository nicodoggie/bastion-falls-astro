# [The Elven Boons](../../../novelty/the-elven-boons.mdx)

In the beginning, as a sign of the gratitude that the Elves gave to the natives
of Senera for allowing them to settle in their lands, the Elves gave these
native tribes considered the Eldandili, each an artifact that they deemed to fit
their natures and temperaments.

## Gems

### Emerald Shield of Spring

- Given to the tribe whose settlement is based in the Western Lowlands

#### The Five Emerald Shards

- The Shield of Spring was broken into 5 shards, and had been lost for
  centuries.
- Locations:
  - One shard was found under a stained glass window with an image of the Green
    Lord Oberon in the Battlement Inn, by Angel Rannek
  - In the possession of the Bishop of Rannek.
  - Somewhere in the Rannek Delta.
  - 2 in the possession of the Reformists.

### Topaz Ring of Summer

- Given to the [Mabrisgralok](../../../ethnicities/hick.mdx#mabrisgralok) tribe
  whose settlement is based in Southern Coastal Plains

### Ruby Amulet of Fall

- Given to the [Magarisok](../../../ethnicities/hick.mdx#magarisok) tribe, and
  was in the possession of the
  [Lord Malthren](../../../characters/marthur-malthren.mdx) until it was
  destroyed by the actions of
  [Lilya Bkorneblume](../../../characters/lilya-bkorneblume.mdx) and
  [Phillipa Vertin](../../../characters/phillipa-vertin.mdx)
- Its fragments were collected by the spirits within the
  [Surrey's Ear](../../../locations/surrey-on-the-brams.mdx#natural-watersheds),
  and are being reconstituted in the altar dedicated to Queen Arcadia.

### Sapphire Brooch of Winter

- Given to the tribe whose settlement is based in the
  [Skelmark County](../../../locations/skelmark-county.mdx) area.

## Persons of Interest

### [Angel Rannek](../../../characters/angel-rannek.mdx)

- Currently has two shards of the Emerald Shield of Spring.

### Prospective Arch-Hag

- Needs one of the Boons to ascend into the role of the Baba Yaga on Thera.
- Trying to make a deal with some
  [Reformists](../../../religions/divine-masochism/reformist/index.mdx) to
  retrieve one of the Boons. She already one of the shards of the Emerald Shield
  of Spring.

## Q & A

### Why were the Elves so easily willing to part with these artifacts?

- Perhaps they did not work as they expected when they crossed over?
  - This can still be true, but it wouldn't be the reason. I think they still
    possessed a fraction of their power, and also perhaps had strange
    interactions with Thera, that they actually gained new ones.
- Perhaps there was some kind of Fey Deal that would unlock some power?
  - Maybe, but I don't think that the Archfey would necessarily give them tools
    that would only work if the refugees were to use them as bargaining chips.
  - If there were this kind of effect, it was an unintended consequence.
  - Though if it were about land-use, then it could work. The issue is that since
    the humans eventually went to war with the elves, then any kind of Fey Deal
    would have been broken.
    - So, if a Fey Deal was broken:
      - could it have been the cause of the war?
      - did the artifacts stop working, break physically, or lost some powers
        as a result of the war?
- Perhaps the artifacts were actually actively harming the elves because of
  something that either happened, or because of some effect during the crossing?
