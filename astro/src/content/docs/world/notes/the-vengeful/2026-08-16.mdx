## Summary

### Leaving Kurtis Settlement

- Sapphire woke after only a few hours of rest but felt recovered from the previous night’s
  injuries. Andrew was less fortunate and began the morning with a headache.
- The party suspected that Reformist forces might be advancing toward both Maiden’s Field and
  Rannekford, including Castle Rannek.
  - Scouts found no reinforcements searching for Pablito Escobar, Torkin, or the other captured
    Reformist operatives.
  - With the captives’ parasites removed, their allies may believe them dead, although a later
    investigation or retaliation remains possible.
- The Band of the Ladle and local mercenaries were briefed and remained available to protect the
  settlement.
  - Arika was told to seek aid from Rannekford if trouble returned.
  - Her mother still intended to present the evidence against the mayor to the town council.
- Andrew and Sapphire adopted a Taminok Brewery cover.
  - Andrew wore his brewery uniform.
  - Sapphire received a fitted golden-yellow dress bearing the family seal, apparently sent by
    Satoria.
  - The dress did not fully conceal the outline of Sapphire’s severed wings, so she kept her cloak
    over it.
- Sapphire introduced herself to Arika by her true name and apologized for previously relying on
  aliases.
  - She explained that attempting to lie causes an unexplained internal jolt.
- Arika’s family provided breakfast and waived payment for the party’s stay in gratitude for her
  rescue.
- The party found a box of provisions in its carriage, including preserves, jerky, marmalade, jams,
  pickled plums, pickled eggs, and sunset-beet soup.
- Sapphire invited Arika to a Taminok Brewery festival featuring wrestling and free beer; Andrew
  agreed with the invitation.
  - The beer could become free-flowing if the organizers bought enough copies of the
    <Item name="alchemy jug" src="dmg" /> from Jessica.
- The party continued directly toward Maiden’s Field rather than detouring to investigate an
  unverified Reformist preacher at a nearby homestead.
  - The abandoned homestead lead could be passed to other authorities.
  - The travelers hoped to reach shelter before an approaching storm.

### Families, trade, and Feywild goods

- Sapphire recalled learning knots and shipboard language from her former crew.
  - Dom was patient while she learned.
  - She also spoke of Aza, Caedus, Mai, and Wilhelm Köhler.
  - Mai willingly left with Wilhelm, who reportedly became a better person because of her.
- Sapphire once worked as a barmaid at her mother’s brothel, Hell’s Kitchen.
  - Her mother’s daughters could propose improvements while retaining the right not to participate.
- Inspired by Feywild media sprites, Sapphire suggested hiring bards or skalds to advertise Taminok
  Brewery.
  - These sprites carry commercial songs between towns using domestic magic popularized by the
    Autumn Court.
- Andrew remained uneasy about his influential merchant family.
  - They possess wealth and connections but no noble title.
  - His father once tried unsuccessfully to remove him from military service after he had reached
    adulthood; the rest of the family was not implicated in that attempt.
- The travelers discussed several Feywild delicacies.
  - Sunset beets and berries grow in lands of perpetual sunset.
  - Sunrise beets also exist but are tougher.
  - Pumpkin Town produces Golden Nectar from golden pumpkins.
- Hellion’s magical quartermaster’s notebook automatically tracks supplies as they are removed.
  - He spent a week cataloguing Feywild goods and jokingly called the notebook his romance book.

### Magic, scholarship, and the Feywild courts

- James criticized General Strandiz’s influential argument that divination is little more than
  unreliable guesswork.
  - Sapphire offered fairy weather magic and *Druidcraft* as practical counterexamples.
  - Strandiz’s thesis was only recently approved in Senera.
  - Wartime Apgarian manuscripts have also begun entering the country, sometimes illegally through
    its ports.
  - Democratic writings are reportedly still burned by the military.
- Necromancy remains prohibited in Senera and condemned by the Church.
  - A theory associated with the University of Thrantorbury claims the Church suppresses necromantic
    scholarship to preserve control over healing and resurrection.
  - The discussion considered *Revivify*, *Spare the Dying*, *Resurrection*, and *True Resurrection*
    as part of the disputed boundary between healing and necromancy.
  - “Rebirth” referred to the Feywild cycle of death and rebirth associated with the Tree of Life,
    not a distinct spell.
- Minfilia is a Death Domain cleric who practices necromancy and possesses a
  <Item name="libram of souls and flesh" src="tce" /> used by Scholomance in Murkendraw to teach
  lichdom.
  - Her faith is directed toward death itself rather than a deity or creed.
  - She cannot use the libram’s magic because it requires a wizard.
- Creating a lich requires rare materials, a soul-separating potion, the removal of part of the
  subject’s soul, and a sufficiently powerful vessel.
  - A phylactery often possesses legendary or sentimental importance, but neither quality is an
    absolute requirement.
  - Destroying a phylactery makes recovery of the separated soul fragment difficult.
- The party discussed several Feywild magical institutions.
  - Megima, in the Summer Court, teaches Evocation.
  - Golaroxy teaches Transmutation.
  - Ionguale, located in a desert, contains a dedicated school of Abjuration.
  - Avium is a general academy rather than a dedicated Divination school.
- Focused institutional study can unlock greater capabilities within a practitioner’s magical
  vocation.
- Secrets can serve as currency or create binding relationships in the Feywild.
  - Named archfey included the Keeper of Secrets, the Cobbler, and Queen Arcadia of the Ashes and
    the Scorched Forest.
- The principal courts were described as Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter, Day, Night, and Dawn.
  - The former Dusk Court was overthrown and became Midnight in the Feydark.
  - Lesser courts include Coral, Crimson, Iron, Crystal, Time, Star, and Dream.
- Captain Hedwig resembles Queen Arcadia, while Minfilia strongly resembles Lady Verenestra.
  - Similar resemblances among Hedwig’s crew prompted speculation about their Feywild connections.
- The group believed Senera’s Dragonheart royal family had concealed the presence of elves from most
  of the nobility, perhaps to protect the elves or because of an earlier agreement with them.
  - They considered the Dragonhearts the people most likely to know the purpose of the secrecy.

### Stacy and Crispin Mallow’s victims

- Stacy survived Crispin Mallow’s alterations and continued recovering under Sister Pira’s care.
  - Her organs had been rearranged and unnaturally connected.
  - Parts incorporated into her body came from Auraxx’s manifested gold-dragon anatomy.
- Mallow appears to leave his victims conscious of their alterations so that survivors and witnesses
  will be horrified.
- A distressed painter attempted another portrait of Branrum, but memories of Stacy intruded upon
  the work.
  - The resulting painting combined Branrum with unnatural anatomy and Stacy-like golden scales.
  - The painter took the portrait into the garden to reflect and seek another emotional outlet.
- Stacy’s recovery offered hope that other victims made into modified composite monstrosities by
  Mallow might also be restored.

### Castle Rannek’s security crisis

- Angel’s mother assumed duties normally handled by Uther while Castle Rannek responded to the
  disappearance of dozens of personnel.
- A guard involved in the previous day’s fire was confirmed as a genuine castle guard rather than an
  impersonator.
  - His legs had been switched before he set himself and a building ablaze.
  - Angel’s mother believed the perpetrator was weaponizing missing personnel to terrorize their
    families and burden survivors with guilt.
- Castle personnel adopted more invasive identity examinations in response to the Face-Stealer
  threat.
- Trajan remained at the castle while wearing Hellion’s face.
  - Hellion had asked him to keep Angel at the castle, but Trajan accepted that she would leave
    after learning Uther was alive.
  - He remained to protect Maria and Gwyniviere because Hellion suspected the enemy was trying to
    divide their defenders.
- Gwyniviere stayed behind to support her mother and confront the distress caused by what she had
  witnessed.
  - She and Angel embraced before parting.
  - Gwyniviere told Angel she was proud of her and hoped they could paint together again after the
    rescue.
- Hellion advised Angel to support Gwyniviere through quiet presence rather than attempting to
  resolve her inner struggle for her.

### The Lord’s Judgment

- Marchioness Maria Rannek sent Angel to investigate the Lord’s Judgment, a registered mercenary
  guild operating from a weapon-decorated tavern.
- Sir Hilis Brastok, Trajan—publicly appearing as Hellion—and Castle Rannek soldiers accompanied
  her.
- Rumors of Angel’s capture had already spread through Maiden’s Field, and patrons recognized her
  when she entered.
- Bertram Dorand, the guild’s leader and a notary, verified the party’s authority and granted access
  to the premises.
- Angel ordered that nobody leave while soldiers secured the exits and counted the occupants.
  - The patrons were unusually subdued, but *Detect Magic* found no enchantment influencing them.
- Magic led the investigators to a locked basement filled with supplies, trophies, gifts, and snow
  globes.
  - Brastok opened the door using Bertram’s key and retained the key.
  - Angel found a black-purple
    [Orb of Enhancement](/world/misc/orbs-of-power/orb-of-enhancement/), a type of Orb of Power,
    hidden among the stored objects.
  - Opening the basement released magic resembling that associated with the earlier parasites or
    “worms.”

### Leland and Lotan

- Angel used *Locate Creature* to pursue whoever had placed the orb.
- Angel, Brastok, and several soldiers chased the moving target onto the roof.
  - *Hold Person* stopped him before he could jump to a neighboring building.
- The captive was Leland, one of Castle Rannek’s missing soldiers. His legs had been switched, and
  Mallow was puppeting him through ordinary thread passed through a magical needle and into his
  altered body.
  - Recovering Leland reduced Castle Rannek’s missing-person count from forty-seven to forty-six.
- *Detect Magic* revealed evocation magic in Leland’s chest.
  - Angel dispelled it and identified the fading symbol as a *Glyph of Warding*.
- A further magical search for the glyph’s caster led Angel, Brastok, Leland, and the guards across
  the river.
  - They confronted a spellcaster wearing Kethbjorn’s face who defended himself with *Shield*.
  - The party used an augmented *Command*—“Drop”—against him and captured him.
- Telepathic examination showed that the second captive possessed neither Kethbjorn’s memories nor
  his affection for Angel.
  - His actual name was Lotan.

### Lotan’s memories

- Hearing Crispin Mallow’s name triggered memories of Mallow’s abuse.
  - Mallow forced Lotan into his Face-Stealer form.
  - He made Lotan consume another person’s facial skin.
  - He drove Lotan through more transformations than Lotan could safely withstand.
- Lotan remembered Mallow as a concealed-faced man with bright golden wings.
- Through Lotan’s memories, Angel saw Uther Rannek, the real Kethbjorn, Sandor, and several court
  wizards imprisoned in separate cages.
  - Uther was badly injured and extremely weak.
  - Sandor had been severely beaten.
  - Their captor had not yet overcome their minds.
- Mallow’s exposed flesh appeared partly golden and draconic, apparently because of his grafts.
- Angel threatened Mallow through her connection with Lotan, invoking Minfilia, Oscar, Hellion, and
  Sapphire and promising to deliver Mallow to Minfilia alive.
  - She ended the connection before learning whether Mallow could answer through Lotan.
- Lotan’s memories also revealed Face-Stealers being tortured and conditioned from childhood.
  - They were stripped of personal identity, trained to adopt other faces, and taught to serve the
    Divine Masochist.
- Angel told Lotan that his body and identity belonged to him and offered to introduce him to
  Trajan.
- Hilis gave Lotan a sleeping pill so he could be transported safely.
  - Trajan accepted responsibility for him and planned to seek treatment from Sister Pira and Castle
    Rannek’s healing pool.

### Lord’s Judgment joins Angel

- Angel returned to the guild and reported that the hidden orb had led her to evidence that Mallow
  held her father.
- She hired all twenty members of Lord’s Judgment for 1,000 gp under a two-month contract.
  - She paid 100 gp upfront.
  - The guild accepted on the condition that it would not participate in persecution.
  - Bertram sealed the contract and placed the company under Hilis’s operational command.
- Bertram revealed that he is an Eldritch Knight and one of three spellcasters in the otherwise

- For the coming operation, Angel requested Bertram and his fighters while leaving the guild’s

### Searching for Lance

  - She found a key marked “Rannekford Storage Company.”
  - She also took one of Lance’s early wooden giraffe carvings.

### The Malthreks arrive

- Duke and Duchess Malthrek, Katalin Malthrek, and two guards arrived through Castle Rannek’s
  private *Teleportation Circle*.
- Tyrion subjected the unexpected arrivals to the castle’s Face-Stealer protocol.
  - Both the duke and duchess passed.
  - The duke successfully recalled recently restored security codes but warned that the Reformists
    probably possessed them as well.
- Katalin was formally introduced as the Malthreks’ daughter-in-law.
- The duke and duchess apologized for the harm they had caused while compromised and promised
  restitution to the Ranneks.
  - They intended to share their recovered memories and help repair the damage.
- Repeated uses of *Encode Thoughts* and *Greater Restoration* were gradually restoring the couple’s
  memories.
  - The duke recalled additional locations in the western Malthreks.
  - His parasite had already been extracted and killed.
- The Malthreks revealed that Tyrion’s superior had been replaced by an infiltrator.
  - They reconstructed a tentative chronology in which the current impersonator had served for
    roughly eighteen months after an earlier infiltrator was punished and replaced for making a
    mistake.
  - They described the current infiltrator as a “copy of a copy,” but the chronology remained their
    inference rather than established fact.
- They also described attempted replacements of Rannekford magistrates and successful substitutions
  involving Gwyniviere, Mark Malthrek, and another family member.
  - A golem duplicate of Mark remains imprisoned in the military dungeons.
  - Mark lost roughly a year of memories after prolonged parasite exposure.
  - Gwyniviere released him from their engagement rather than hold him to a relationship he could no
    longer remember.
- Emily Hazeldine is expected to marry Mark and help lead the Malthrek court wizards.
- Duke Ellington and Duchess Antoinette announced their abdication after helping repair the damage
  caused during their rule.
  - Mark, rather than his elder brother Luke, would succeed Ellington as Duke of Malthrek.

### The war-room council

- Maria received the unexpected Malthrek delegation and moved the gathering to the situation room at
  the duke’s request.
- Angel reported the Lord’s Judgment investigation, Lotan’s rescue, the imprisoned Ranneks and court
  wizards, and the search for Lance.
- Katalin identified Crispin Mallow as the current Reformist Candlebearer.
  - Mallow may be an ascended Dark Archon answerable directly to Sovereign Rothschild, although this
    remained a theory.
  - Rothschild was described as an archlich and the Reformist patriarch.
  - The intelligence reportedly originated with the celestial Deathengel.
- Mallow was credited with the Faltir operation that transformed salt into black powder and with
  constructing exceptionally precise golems.
- Katalin described Mallow’s magical needle, which can use ordinary thread to control people through
  his fleshcrafting.
  - Mallow threaded altered bodies together with the needle to turn them into puppets.
  - The same control could be applied to Face-Stealers whom Mallow had unnaturally modified; it was
    not an inherent vulnerability of ordinary Face-Stealers.
  - His experiments employ rare magical materials, including dragon bones, scales, and fragments of
    the Heart of the Urdefolcin.
  - Sister Pira now possesses recovered material from the heart.
- Mallow may be exploiting the Hicklander rebellion to pursue a vendetta against the Maidenfelds.
- The council concluded that the Face-Stealers serving Mallow should be treated as captives and
  victims.
  - The rescue force intends to disable and restrain them rather than kill them.
- Angel may herself be one of Mallow’s targets.
  - The council considered whether he intends to use *Planar Binding* on her.
  - Directly teleporting to Uther was rejected as too dangerous because Mallow may have prepared

- Katalin refused to leave the duke and duchess despite the urgency of the rescue.
- Antoinette offered healing, *Greater Restoration*, and teleportation support.

### Auraxx and the Gold Canary

- The council identified Auraxx as the gold dragon imprisoned within the Gold Canary.

- Gwyniviere and Hilis had both seen Stacy manifest golden wings and dragon-like scales.

### Regional infiltration and forbidden magic

- The Reformists appear to have infiltrated the Admiralty, Malthrek forces, Eastonton’s Paladinate,
  and several Hicklander movements.
  - Reformist activity in Eastonton was estimated to have begun about fifteen years earlier, while
    the separate Malthrek operation had run for almost ten years.
  - Reformist cells sometimes acted independently; the Eastonton and Malthrek operations were not
    coordinated with each other.
  - While acting as a Reformist under parasite influence, Duke Ellington tried to coordinate as many
    otherwise independent operations as possible because his military judgment recognized their
    fragmentation as a weakness.
  - His efforts did not give him authority over every cell. Some Reformists may not have recognized
    his command at all; Garward may have been among them.
- The Malthreks reported that their northern lands contain natural mines used to manufacture Orbs of
  Power under Reformist control.
- Lord Garward may have helped introduce Reformist influence to Eastonton.
  - Lord Boyle Eastonton discovered that Garward had not been the same since the war.
- Deathengel’s intelligence identifies Isana Walburton as a simulacrum.
  - Tanduaria publicly executes unaffiliated arcane casters while its Church secretly preserves
    restricted magical knowledge, including *Simulacrum*.
- The council compared different regional systems for controlling spellcasters.
  - Tanduaria channels them through the Church.
  - Poasah places them under the Thirteen and the Crown.
  - Apgar distrusts them and reportedly relies on forced drafts.
  - Senera remains comparatively accepting despite its own prohibitions.
- Duke Malthrek connected regional fear of magic to events at the Bastion and the rise of magically
  empowered warlords in nearby Burtz.
- Katalin warned that suspected spellcasters in the
  [Banate of Summa](/world/locations/summa/), including children, were allegedly killed without
  inquiry.

### Activating the Port Mibram circle

- Port Mibram possessed the largest *Teleportation Circle* Angel had seen.
  - Hellion’s Orbs of Enhancement had already been installed in its conduits, vessels that
    ordinarily held diamonds, but the military casters did not know how to use them.
- Angel altered one corner of the sigil into a triangle.
- Although she could not cast *Teleportation Circle* herself, Angel channeled energy from the orbs
  through herself and activated the installation without using a spell slot.
- She taught a military caster to direct the spell through the orbs rather than the circle itself.
  - Angel had learned the technique by watching Minfilia in the Travertines.
- The orbs should recharge naturally if left in place.
- Angel ordered the circle guarded against unauthorized use.
  - Its sigil would be shared with allies, including Hedwig and Safar.
  - The circle could move reinforcements, supplies, refugees, and rescued captives whenever a
    capable caster was available.

### Boundaries

- Trajan and Hellion are distinct people. Trajan was deliberately wearing Hellion’s face and using
  his name during these events.
- Leland is not a Face-Stealer. He is a genuine Castle Rannek soldier whom Mallow altered and
  puppeted. Lotan is a separate Face-Stealer victim who was forced to wear Kethbjorn’s face and
  voice.
- Auraxx is the gold dragon imprisoned in the Gold Canary and is not allied with the Reformists.
  Katalin identified Mallow as the current Candlebearer, but that is the office he projects; his
  actual rank remains unresolved and may be higher.
- Oscar is a revenant. Angel included an image of him in her threat to Mallow, but the scene does
  not establish whether she consciously knows his nature or deliberately revealed it.
- The comparison between baelnorns and “archliches” was a simplified explanation, not a settled
  equivalence.
- *Locate Creature* established only Lance’s last detected direction before the spell failed; it did
  not establish whether he died, hid underground, entered an extradimensional space, or became
  magically concealed.
- Healing magic is dangerous to Face-Stealers transformed into monstrosities, as Stacy was. Hilis
  explicitly distinguished them from ordinary Face-Stealers, who are not harmed by healing magic.
- Mallow’s needle can control Face-Stealers he has unnaturally modified and threaded together; this
  is not an inherent vulnerability of ordinary Face-Stealers.
- Face-Stealers coerced by Mallow are to be treated as victims and rescued where possible, even when
  they are encountered as hostile operatives.
- Mallow’s supposed vendetta against the Maidenfelds, Dark Archon status, and intention to bind
  Angel remain theories or hostile reports rather than confirmed motives.
- Katalin and Gwyniviere’s council-chamber encounter was consensual, but the transcript does not
  reliably assign most individual actions or dialogue.
