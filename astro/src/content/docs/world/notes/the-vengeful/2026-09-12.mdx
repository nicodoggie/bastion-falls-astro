## Arrival and the Switched-Limb Infiltration

- Angel's group arrived at the Maidenfeld Spice Company's teleportation circle.
  - Dimitri verified Steven through personal questions and codes before accepting the arrivals.
  - Steven vouched for Angel, Hedwig, Jane, and Jennifer, while Dimitri reacted with alarm and asked
    how they had entered.
  - Lance accompanied the rescue party disguised as Rance. Angel still believed Rance was a
    separate person and Lance had stayed behind.
- The group discussed hags, local teleportation rules, and whether the hags of Estrega could help.
  - An ally accepted responsibility for the visitors.
  - Their available support included healing, curses, and crowd control, but they did not intend to
    remain long.
- They considered a multi-pronged response to the Reformists.
  - Bellacourt had professed Reformist sympathies, though the sincerity and duration of those
    beliefs were uncertain.
  - The Count of Bede's claim that he was handling local insurgencies remained unverified.
  - Investigation of seaside Bede was deferred until after the marquis's rescue.
- A fleet-bound ally ordered Dimitri to fortify the spice company, gather its spellcasters, and warn
  the others before departing through the teleportation circle.
- Discussion of hag bargains established only conflicting possibilities: local hags seemed
  temporarily bound to the site, yet bargains might be made through even slight contact at a
  distance.
- Near the elevator, the group encountered a robed company figure whose feet were switched.
  - A <Spell name="wall of force" src="phb" /> trapped him, and his thrown needles rebounded from
    the barrier.
  - The group warned Dimitri that additional switched-limb creatures had probably infiltrated the
    site.
- Gwyniviere opened the elevator and revealed two more switched-limb Maidenfelds.
  - The first captive was pushed inside, and the spherical barrier blocked the entrance.
- The group escaped through a fifth-floor window under <Spell name="feather fall" src="phb" />.
  - Rance broke the window, and Hedwig encouraged Gwyniviere to jump.
  - Fighting had spread below, with Maidenfelds exchanging spells against others of their own kind.
  - Sir Hilis Brastok dove after Angel with his hammer, while Rance narrowly avoided a butcher's
    hook tethered by tissue resembling sinew.
- The party renewed its search for Crispin Mallow.
  - Hedwig probably cleared the weather and cast <Spell name="fly" src="phb" /> on Angel, Brastok,
    and probably Rance, though the exact targets remain uncertain.

  - The spell led them across Maiden's Port to a harbor where guards had been laid low and crates
    were being loaded aboard a ship.

  harbor forces.

- Several construct-like enemies went berserk as Gwyniviere began opening the cages.
  - Rance stayed near her, fought the constructs, and knocked one unconscious with an opportunity
    attack.
  - Gwyniviere avoided a pursuing construct's hook, but later broke her lockpick on a natural 1.
- Brastok used Step of the Wind to reach Gwyniviere, struck her attacker, and exchanged positions
  with her using Bait and Switch.
- Angel identified stronger opponents among the weaker constructs and cast
  <Spell name="suggestion" src="phb" /> on one.
  - She ordered it to take its friends swimming.
  - The creature grappled an ally, rendered it unconscious, and dragged it toward the sea.
- Angel cast <Spell name="mass healing word" src="phb" /> on the wounded prisoners and apparently
  Brastok.
- Brastok survived a critical hit, defeated Gwyniviere's attacker, and threw Brana at another enemy

- Angel gave Gwyniviere Bardic Inspiration and used <Spell name="vicious mockery" src="phb" /> to

- More stitched sailors appeared aboard the ship.
  - One attacked Angel with needle-studded bamboo fans.
  - Another scattered needles around the cages, injuring several unconscious prisoners but missing

- Rance broke a cage's padlock while a bodyguard protected his charge.
- Angel cast <Spell name="slow" src="phb" /> on six shipboard enemies, slowing all six.
- The group removed Uther after checking him for seams and considering whether he
  might be a shapeshifter.

  hold.
  - She found many boxes, a differently designed submarine, and about fifteen chained prisoners of

  - The spell detected him farther away, lost him briefly, and reacquired him much closer.
- A prisoner carrying a strange thread fatally injured himself, and his body burst apart to reveal a
  tall man.
  - The timing matched the locating spell's interruption, but the man was not explicitly confirmed
    as Mallow at that moment.
- The man threatened Angel's captive companions and family, forcing her to accompany him into the
  submarine.
  - The apparent corpse operated a lever that dropped the submarine into the water.
  - An injured figure, possibly Brastok, tried to swim after it but could not catch it.
- Beneath Maiden's Bay, the captor described himself as a master who controlled puppets through
  strings.
  - He threatened an attack on the castle and Angel's mother.
  - He also claimed the Maidenfelds possessed a concealed key.
- Angel attempted to reach the person beneath the captor's cruelty.
  - She repeatedly reopened her own needle injury, allowed him to taste her blood, and silently
    prayed for freedom from his curses and bargains.
  - Golden scales had appeared when he healed her.
  - Angel's blood broke the fey deals and curses on Mallow. As soon as those bonds broke, the
    people under his control passed out, before his transformation into a child.
- The captor underwent a failed transformation as scales and incipient wings formed and fell away.
- Auraxx, the gold dragon, emerged as a benevolent presence and thanked Angel for freeing his soul.
  - Her celestial blood and prayer brought about his release; the exact metaphysical mechanism
    and source of celestial blood's magical power remained unexplored.
  - Auraxx offered one boon without demanding an exchange.
  - Angel rejected simple escape and asked to undo the captor's horrors.
  - Auraxx warned that reversing everything would risk another break in reality.
- Angel asked Auraxx to return the captor to mortal childhood so she could raise him under better
  influences.
  - Auraxx warned that his nature would remain damaged.
  - An infant and a ten-year-old child were considered before the final choice was made.
  - Angel rejected total memory erasure.
