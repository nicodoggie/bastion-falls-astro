# Disappeared Children

Children aged 5 and below have been disappearing from their homes in the last
few years. There seems to be no pattern to the disappearances, and no evidence
to suggest that the children were abducted.

## Hooks

- Various people in town will be randomly gossiping about the disappearances,
  and with a passive perception of 10, the party will overhear these
  conversations.
- Bulletin boards in town squares or taverns would have flyers offering small
  bounties (2d10 + 5 sp) for the return of some children of minor nobility.
- Drunk gravediggers talking about how some babies' graves started missing
  bodies, after another drunk talks about spotting some kind of toddler with
  chicken legs stealing yarrow from their garden.
