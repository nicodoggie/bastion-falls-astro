## Summary

### Voyage toward Port Mibram

- The party continued through Maiden’s Bay aboard the *Illegally Bear* toward Port Mibram,
  travelling under Marchioness Maria Rannek’s authority amid the wider emergency involving Marquess
  Uther Rannek, Lance, and Christian Marlion.
  - The weather cleared after departure from Port-Sera-by-Malterdale, though the upper Malter Valley
    remained clouded.
  - Sapphire kept watch from the bow. A ship remained astern but showed no clear sign of pursuing
    the *Illegally Bear*; seagulls following the vessel soon dispersed.
- Crewman Yannick offered Sapphire a free pouch of crushed mushrooms and herbs, described as a
  tea-based party substance that could last through a night. Sapphire declined while on watch and
  kept the unidentified, highly suspicious substance for later.
- Andrew and Sapphire remain wary of the pirate crew, while Andrew also fears that the crisis
  affecting Eastern Naval Command may spread to the west.
- Maria asked after Angel. Sapphire believes Angel is likely with Sir Hilis Brastok and is not
  presently alarmed.

### Feywild discussions and personal matters

- The group discussed Fey mating bonds as sacred, rare connections rather than ordinary romance.
  - Lover’s Lake remains a possible place to seek a boon, though it is not a usual destination and
    its rules are unclear.
- Fey bargains, including bargains with the Hags of Hama, were treated as literal and dangerous:
  apparent clarity does not prevent ruinous loopholes.
  - The group speculated that Rebecca’s severe forgetfulness could be connected to a hag bargain
    involving painful memories. John continues to care for her.
- Sapphire described her family home, Hell’s Kitchen, a brothel in Maple Bridge near a peaceful
  lake about a day from Pumpkin Town. Her mother raised Sapphire and her sisters to prioritize
  themselves, persevere, and remain kind to others. Pumpkin Town, in the Autumn Court, is especially
  active in autumn; its feared Pumpkin King is said to consume nightmares.
- The group discussed difficult family relationships, including divorce under Divine Masochist
  social pressure, but took no action.

### Magical disturbance

- The sky darkened only over the party as Arcana identified an imminent magical discharge.
  - A *counterspell* stopped a forming lightning bolt.
  - Motes of light like pieces of St. Elmo’s fire appeared overhead. Sapphire traced the spell to
    the elderly court wizard casting from a submarine in the open bay.

### The Malthrek submarine

- The party spotted an elderly spellcaster casting from a submarine in Maiden’s Bay.
  - His legs had been physically switched by Crispin Mallow. The resulting backward-foot appearance
    resembled the earlier opposite-foot sign associated with Oren Bell; visible stitching may
    describe the procedure, but the legs were canonically swapped.
  - Andrew recognized him as a Malthrek court wizard who had once delivered documents to John during
    training.
- Sapphire manifested a green-and-gold longbow and critically wounded the wizard in the neck.
  Unseen hands pulled him through the hatch before the submarine submerged; Andrew’s later shot
  struck only its hull.
- Oren Bell revealed that he had already placed a tracking spell on the fleeing submarine or its
  target. The vessel dove deeper, and Sapphire’s later Arcana check indicated that its occupants had
  dispelled Oren’s tracking effect.
- Sapphire used a summoned shark and *beast sense* to follow the submarine through Maiden’s Bay.
  - The submarine was limited by dredged channels and sought deeper water.
  - It directed a burst from its magical water-propulsion effect at the shark. The effect primarily
    moves the submarine but can be used defensively; the shark evaded it.
  - Near a reef and seaweed cover, Sapphire hid the shark among nearby sharks. The submarine
    initially believed it had lost its pursuer, after which Sapphire resumed following from farther
    away without drawing another attack.
- Sapphire also bargained with a seagull to report where the submarine later surfaced or docked,
  paying it with a huge tuna caught using her Feywild fishing rod.
  - The gull reported that the submarine’s occupants had harmed other seagulls with indescribable
    lightning-based magic that seemed to stimulate clouds and call down bolts. Its description of
    being “cooked” meant being cartoonishly scorched by lightning, not transformed into something.

### Cover aboard the *Illegally Bear*

- The group continued posing as guards transporting captive “merchandise” aboard the *Illegally
  Bear*. Sapphire identified herself as a guard when Yannick initially mistook her for merchandise.
  - Yannick said that he had seen Angel and Sir Hilis Brastok having sex. Given their apparent roles
    as guard and “merchandise,” he may have understood the encounter as exploitation or abuse, but
    those roles were only a cover and the encounter was not actually abuse.
  - Hellion later told Captain Illegal E. Bear that the supposed merchandise were actually nobles
    rescued from the Reformist Church.
- Andrew brought Trajan into the conversation; Trajan’s altered eye and hair colours served as a
  disguise.
- Captain Illegal E. Bear explained that submarines have been striking ships with lightning-like
  attacks that damage or slow their masts while allowing them to continue to port.
- Bear is transporting nobles rescued from the Reformist Church, giving the submarines a possible
  motive to pursue the *Illegally Bear*.
  - Hellion warned Bear that Reformists target yellow-eyed people, including children, and
    transplant their livers into Reformists as part of a ritual. Hellion knows of the practice
    through Hedwig and Minfilia’s experiences and actual events in Raibon. Bear wanted no
    involvement with the church.
- The party agreed to protect the *Illegally Bear* in exchange for an additional 100 gp upon
  docking and cooperation with navigation.
  - They insisted on using the official docks at Port Mibram rather than a secluded cove.
  - Bear agreed not to direct cruel or bigoted remarks at the party.
- Bear demonstrated a concealed gear-driven mechanism allegedly connected to Racos Palma and his
  former ship, *Nightshade*. Its capabilities remain unclear.
- The vessel remains the *Illegally Bear*. Bear also circulated a forbidden copy of *Legally Bare*.

## Open Hooks

- Determine why the elderly Malthrek court wizard attacked the party from the submarine and whom he
  intended to target.
- Find the submarine, identify its crew and destination, and learn how its occupants detected and
  dispelled Oren’s tracking magic.
- Learn what spell the elderly court wizard attempted, why Mallow switched his legs, and whether he
  survived Sapphire’s shot.
- Understand the submarine’s magical water-propulsion system, its possible defensive uses, and
  whether its occupants realized that Sapphire’s shark resumed following them.
- Determine whether the submarine attacks target commerce generally, the Reformist escapees aboard
  the *Illegally Bear*, or the party and Ranneks specifically.
- Find Stacy Cartwright and the missing Malthrek court wizards, determine whether they have been
  compromised or repurposed, and establish their individual assignments. The party recalls that
  Stacy may have been sent to meet Count Ranlis, but does not know that pairing for certain.
- Determine how Mallow’s alteration of the elderly court wizard connects to the submarine operation
  and the other missing court wizards.
- Identify Yannick’s mushroom-and-herb pouch and determine its actual effects and safety; his claim
  that it was a tea-based party substance remains unverified and highly suspicious.

### Boundaries

- Treat Fey bargains as literal magical agreements whose apparent plain meaning may conceal
  consequential loopholes.
- Treat Fey mating bonds as rare and sacred connections; their mechanics, permanence, and the
  consequences of a broken bond remain unsettled.
- The visible ship astern did not show clear signs of pursuit during Sapphire’s watch; do not treat
  it as confirmed hostile.
- The *Illegally Bear* is the vessel’s name. Bear’s alleged mechanism and its connection to
  *Nightshade* do not establish that the ship can fly.
- Bear agreed only to refrain from cruel or bigoted remarks toward the party, not to renounce such
  behaviour generally.
