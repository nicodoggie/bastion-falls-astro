# What Happened So Far

We first focus on Lilya Bkornblume, a lieutenant in the Royal Seneran Navy, and
part of its Ground Command. Under the command of
[Lord Captain Boyle Eastonton](/world/characters/lord-captain-boyle-eastonton/),
her squad's mandate is to patrol the area in and around
[Port Surrey-on-the-Brams](/world/locations/port-surrey-on-the-brams/) as a
peace-keeping force.

In addition to their regular duties, their captain allows them to complete
bounties issued by the Royal Navy's Commissioning Office. Lilya and her squad
want to earn enough money to commission a ship for themselves, and transition to
Sea Command. Lilya has an ulterior motive for that however, is to be able to
finally see dolphins in their natural habitat.

The members of her squad are:

- Corporal [Jalester Silvermane](/world/characters/jalester-silvermane/)
- Private [Kaelin Thralson](/world/characters/kaelin-thralson/)
- Private [Tormor Stavner](/world/characters/tormor-stavner/)
- Private [Elmor Havelman](/world/characters/elmor-havelman/)
- Private [Erik Bekker](/world/characters/erik-bekker/)

One morning, after receiving a list of bounties from the Commissioning Office,
specifically for one Murky Marbrams (150 gp for any information leading to his
arrest), Lilya was approached by
[Kurris Wudward](/world/characters/kurris-wudward/). Kurris explained to Lilya
that her adoptive brother, Henry Surrata-Spellvig, heard that the Lord Malthren
was dying, but had no heirs, so he decided to convince the aging lord to name
him as the next Lord Malthren. In order to do so, he needed to go to
[the Narrows](/world/locations/the-narrows/), which was infamously home to many
of the island's most dangerous criminals. Kurris was hoping that Lilya could
escort Henry to the Narrows, and safely bring him back to Port
Surrey-on-the-Brams. She told Kurris that she would think about it, but she
actually already decided to do it. Mabrams, after all, was last seen in
[Tidemark](/world/locations/tidemark/), which they would have to pass through to
get to the Narrows.

Meanwhile, in Tidemark, we met
[Philippa Vertin](/world/characters/phillipa-vertin/) was preparing for the
annual mission to the Narrows when she was called on by
[Sister Agatha Thralvig](/world/characters/agatha-thralvig/). The Bishop of
Surrey had requested that one of his most trusted paladins,
[Sir Aigral Eastonton](/world/characters/aigral-eastonton/), join and lead the
mission. Philippa did not mind, of course, and graciously deferred to Sir
Eastonton. Most of the preparations were done at this point, the supplies just
needed to be loaded onto the carriages that would take them to the Narrows.

---

Lilya didn't tell Kurris what she decided, instead she went to Henry's quarters,
asked her squad to hogtie him, and carry him to the stables. She only explained
that they were taking him to the Narrows after they were a distance away from
Port Surrey's southern gate. They rode straight through Admiralty Way, stopping
only at the [Crosswise Tavern](/world/locations/crosswise-tavern/) to exchange
horses and to replenish supplies.

At the tavern, they attempt to get information on Mabrams. They overheard that
Mabrams maybe in the Narrows, but no one actually dares to claim the bounty for
themselves. People also mentioned that the road to the Narrows was more
dangerous these days than it already was, mentioning that there were raiders
that had been attacking caravans and travelers.
