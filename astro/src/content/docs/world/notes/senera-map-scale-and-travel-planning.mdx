This article records out-of-world cartographic conventions and travel estimates used when planning
Senera. The grid references, pixel measurements, and route calculations are editorial tools, not
in-world terminology or surveyed canonical distances.

## Controlling Scale

The current Map of Senera is provisionally treated as a scale map. The controlling ruler is the
[Gulf of Inespell](../locations/gulf-of-inespell.mdx), which extends approximately 125 miles inland
and is approximately 63 miles wide at its mouth. Measuring the gulf on the map gives a working scale
of approximately **0.135 mile per pixel**, or approximately 7.4 pixels per mile.

The resulting working calibration is:

- Map canvas: 4,500 by 4,500 pixels
- Full map width: approximately 608 miles
- Main planning-grid cell: 300 pixels, or approximately 40.5 miles per side
- Main-cell diagonal: approximately 57 miles
- Main-cell area: approximately 1,640 square miles
- Three-by-three subcell: 100 pixels, or approximately 13.5 miles per side
- Subcell diagonal: approximately 19 miles

Conflicting dimensions in older regional descriptions should be reconsidered against this ruler
rather than used as independent calibrations. The convention can be revised if the map itself is
later redesigned.

## Nested Planning Grid

The planning map uses main cells labelled with an uppercase letter and a number. Each main cell can
be divided into a three-by-three lowercase grid. Parentheses distinguish a nested subcell from a
main cell: `B4(c3)` means subcell c3 within main cell B4, not main-grid C3.

A range uses the same convention. `B3(b1-c2)` denotes the rectangular group comprising b1, c1, b2,
and c2 within main cell B3.

- [Maiden's Field](../locations/rannek-marches.mdx#maidens-field) occupies `B3(b1-c2)`,
  approximately 27 miles east to west and 27 miles north to south, or about 729 square miles.
- [Maiden's Port](../locations/maidens-port.mdx) lies on the coast east of `B3(c1)`.
- Maylert lies within `B4(c3)`. Use the subcell centre only as a provisional measuring point until
  its exact location and road alignment are drawn.
- [Rannekford](../locations/rannekford.mdx) is provisionally measured from the settlement dot at
  approximately `(1185, 2135)` on the source map.
- [Port Mibram](../locations/port-mibram.mdx) is provisionally measured at approximately
  `(1250, 2010)`.

The Rannekford and Port Mibram points are approximately 19 miles apart in a straight line, before
allowing for the road's actual course.

The uncertain handwritten reading previously discussed as “Wylfen Fields” should not be equated with
Maiden's Field. The `D4-E5` region appears to be the Western Foothills.

## Route Decisions

- Andrew and Sapphire take the eastern road from Rannekford through Port Mibram toward Maiden's
  Field. Their loaded brewery carriage and settlement-by-settlement investigation make travel days
  and elapsed mission days different quantities.
- Hellion and his group take the western road. Maylert is a possible stopping place on that
  approach. Its placement bends a route through Maylert southward, so estimates that treated the
  western road as a direct Rannekford-to-Field line do not apply if Maylert is a required waypoint.

## Travel Assumptions

Use these as sustainable daily rates rather than brief maximum speeds:

- Loaded merchant or brewery carriage: approximately 15–20 effective road miles per travel day
- Light passenger carriage or mounted group: approximately 18–24 effective road miles per travel day
- General slow, normal, and fast overland planning bands: approximately 18, 24, and 30 miles per day
- Sustained high-speed travel requires organized relay horses; one team cannot maintain relay speeds

Raised embankments and well-maintained commercial roads can support the upper part of a band. Mud,
flooding, a failed weir, a washed-out bridge, a closed ferry, military activity, or investigative
stops can add detours or whole non-travel days.

## Provisional Route Estimates

These ranges combine straight-line geometry with allowances for plausible road curvature and delta
crossings. Exact road polylines have not yet been drawn.

### Eastern Road Through Port Mibram

- Rannekford to Port Mibram: approximately 22–27 road miles
- Rannekford through Port Mibram to the nearest part of Maiden's Field: approximately 45–55 road
  miles, or roughly 2–4 loaded-carriage travel days
- To a representative interior destination: approximately 65–80 road miles, or roughly 3–6
  loaded-carriage travel days
- A farther western or northwestern destination may be approximately 85–100 road miles away, or
  roughly 4–7 loaded-carriage travel days

Investigation and assistance in settlements should be counted separately from those travel days.

### Western Road and Maylert

Using the centre of `B4(c3)` only as a provisional point:

- Rannekford to Maylert: approximately 39–48 road miles
- Maylert to the nearest part of Maiden's Field: approximately 54–66 road miles
- Maylert to a representative interior destination: approximately 70–86 road miles
- Rannekford via Maylert to the nearest part of the Field: approximately 94–114 road miles, or
  roughly 4–7 days for a light carriage or mounted group
- Rannekford via Maylert to a representative interior destination: approximately 110–134 road miles,
  or roughly 5–8 days for a light carriage or mounted group

These totals supersede the earlier short western-route estimate if Maylert is on the travelled
route. If Maylert is only an optional detour, the direct western road requires its own traced
alignment before receiving a durable mileage.
