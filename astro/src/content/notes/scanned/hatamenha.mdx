Hatamenha (the Medoriyana)

Council for the Reconstruction of the Great Ahurian Empire (CRGAE)

Location: Tahusa Valley, Sorha

Race: Human

Skin Color: Dark Brown

Age: 89

Hair Color: Grey

Eye Color: Dark brown

Background:

Hatamenha was once the magistate of the first region of Sorha and second son of
Sorha XXVII, he was definitely in the running to be one of the princes of
Ahuria. But because his mother had been finally displaced into the Great
Monastary, his position was similarly rescinded and he had the choice of
following his mother into the Grand Monastary or live in exile as a Harsi. He
chose the latter believing that monastary life was stagnation and was a
disgrace.
