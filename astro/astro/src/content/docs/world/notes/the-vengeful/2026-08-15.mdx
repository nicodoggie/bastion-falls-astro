## Summary

### Road to Maiden’s Field

- Hellion Blanchimontt, Sir Erik Malmann, Oren Bell, and Klaus continued east by carriage, resting overnight near hills that may have been called the Shield.
- The party later found a recently overturned produce carriage after one of its draft horses fled past them.
  - Hellion and Oren freed the injured mare Lacey from her yoke and restored her with healing magic.
  - The carriage contained unmarked boxes of fresh produce but no occupants. The party gathered the goods with magic before they spoiled.
  - Blood, torn clothing, and tracks indicated that several people had attacked the carriage.
  - Four sets of footprints left the road. Someone tried to conceal the trail, after which only two sets remained.
- Klaus tracked the missing travelers through bramble-covered hills and into a ravine too narrow for the carriage.
  - A deliberately small plume of smoke revealed a concealed camp.
  - Sir Malmann brought the carriage as far as practical while Oren and Klaus approached on foot.

### The Concealed Camp

- Four armed captors guarded two bound and gagged human traders in a wooden cage.
  - The prisoners appeared to be ordinary vegetable traders and lacked the yellow eyes associated with magical ability.
  - Their captors nevertheless believed moonlight had given the prisoners a blue cast, marking them as “chosen by the Lord.”
- Oren’s eyes turned bright yellow as glowing purplish-green, tentacle-like markings spread across his hands and upper body.
  - The display appeared strongly eldritch rather than necromantic.
  - Believing Oren was the Reformist acolyte they had been expecting, the captors lowered their weapons and knelt.
- Oren convinced the four captors to abandon the Reformists and release the traders.
  - The cage had apparently already been unlocked by an unknown person.
  - The injured traders received water and treatment before departing for their village.
  - One trader had lost several teeth but expected to recover.
  - The traders privately mentioned changelings but refused to explain what they knew.
  - They thanked someone called Phillip before leaving.

### Displaced Reformist Recruits

- The captors were displaced villagers who said Hicklanders had burned their settlement about a month earlier because they refused to join the rebellion.
- The Reformists had sheltered their families afterward, winning their trust through food, lodging, and promises of new homes.
  - The families were reportedly living near Maiden’s Field, possibly at a homestead called Goat’s Head.
  - The recruits feared punishment for abandoning the Divine Masochist Church, although they disagreed over whether apostates were hanged or pilloried.
- Their apparent leader, Dean, was especially concerned for his daughter Carissa, who had gone away to “become a sister.”
  - Dean described his formerly reckless daughter as unnaturally obedient following Reformist instruction.
  - Oren ordered the recruits to help rescue Carissa rather than continue serving the Reformists.
- The recruits agreed to accompany the party toward Maiden’s Field, leaving their camp and cage behind.
  - The party explained that they had been hired to investigate and remove Reformist activity in Senera.
  - They could not promise an immediate rescue of the recruits’ families but offered to find and fund assistance, possibly by hiring mercenaries.
  - Sir Malmann silently prayed for Carissa and the other endangered relatives after the recruits asked him not to preach to them.

### Reformist Initiation and Abuse

- A captured Reformist operative claimed that bandits, rather than Hicklanders, had destroyed the recruits’ village and that the Reformists had merely exploited the attack.
- The operative described the Reformists as the “pure” form of the Divine Masochist Church, stripped of its aristocratic trappings despite the continued involvement of nobles.
- One recruit described a Reformist “purity ritual” in which women, including his wife and possibly his daughters, entered a chapel unclothed and were whipped before a priest and witnesses.
- The operative said Carissa had not been replaced by a changeling or Face-Stealer and had not yet received a parasite.
  - He claimed she was being inducted through revelry similar to that used on youths in Kurtis.
  - His description presented Carissa’s sexual exploitation as proof of her obedience.
- Oren argued that the Reformists preyed upon desperation, restrictive customs, and suppressed desires, concealing abuse behind charity and teachings about transforming pain into pleasure.

### Parasites and the Biomancers

- Oren produced a flask containing a parasite and accused the Reformists of implanting eggs that grew into mind-altering worms.
  - The operative called the parasites “seeds” intended to advance humanity’s ascension.
  - He recoiled violently whenever the flask approached him.
  - The party considered whether a *Ring of Mind Shielding* might explain his reaction.
- Oren found paralytic poison darts, a bone saw, a butcher’s knife, and another unidentified object on the operative.
- The party suspected that the operative served Crispin Mallow, a hunted senior Reformist and biomancer said to keep victims alive and aware while harvesting their bodies for patchwork creations.

### The Gardener in Maiden’s Field

- Oren interrogated the bound operative after removing a poison-filled suicide tooth, briefly placing a *Bag of Holding* over his head.
- In exchange for a promised death, the operative revealed that an unnamed male superior was in Maiden’s Field, “playing the gardener again.”
  - The operative begged to die before his cooperation was discovered.
  - He feared being transformed into something he called “that patchwork.”
- A party member cut the operative’s throat beside a hollow dead willow and placed his body inside.
  - The recruits, who had surrendered without violence, recoiled from the execution.

### Word from Castle Rannek

- During an apparent *Sending* exchange, Sister Pira told Hellion that her group had safely reached Castle Rannek.
  - Stacy remained unconscious but was breathing better, which Pira attributed to exhaustion.
  - Gwyniviere was shocked and sleep-deprived after seeing the limbs and other material removed from Stacy.
  - Lord Branrum was speaking with Gwyniviere and had offered to pose nude for her anatomical studies.
- Hellion warned Pira that an exact duplicate of him might reach the castle.
  - Trajan had already alerted Pira, the Marchioness, Lady Angel, and others nearby.
- Hellion asked Pira to cast *Locate Object* as often as her spell slots allowed, using his piece of the Urdefolcin heart.
  - Her previous casting continued to indicate the same direction.
  - Hellion requested an immediate warning if the target’s distance changed suddenly or substantially.
- Pira asked Hellion to tell Sir Malmann that she missed him.

### Sapphire and Andrew

- Around dawn in Kurtis, the inn filled with the familiar smell of fried meat or bacon.
- Andrew remained asleep beside Sapphire, who returned to sleep before their planned departure later that morning.

### The Missing

- Of the forty-eight people known to be missing, one was confirmed dead and forty-seven remained unaccounted for.
  - The missing included the party’s friends and the court wizards.
  - Those involved were being summoned to consolidate what they knew about the disappearances.
- The group believed an unidentified man was exploiting their suffering.
  - They agreed that he had to be stopped, although they did not yet understand his identity, motives, or knowledge of their circumstances.

## Open Hooks

- Find the Reformist superior in Maiden’s Field and determine what “playing the gardener again” means.
- Identify the acolyte whom the camp’s recruits had been expecting.
- Find Carissa and the recruits’ other relatives, determine how the Reformists secured Carissa’s obedience, and learn whether any of them have received parasites.
- Determine who destroyed the recruits’ settlement and whether the Reformists were involved in the attack or only exploited its survivors.
- Learn who opened the traders’ cage, why the traders mentioned changelings, and whether their reported blue coloration had any significance.
- Determine what the Reformist “seeds” do, why the operative feared them, and whether a *Ring of Mind Shielding* affected his reaction.
- Protect Castle Rannek from Hellion’s duplicate and continue monitoring the Urdefolcin heart.
- Find the forty-seven missing people and identify the adversary exploiting the group’s suffering.

### Confirmations Needed

- Confirm the identity of Phillip and whether the name was genuine or a cover.
- Confirm whether the recruits’ families are at a place called Goat’s Head.
- Reconcile what happened to both carriage horses and who performed each act of healing.
- Identify the companion who disappeared from Oren’s sight during the approach to the camp.
- Establish the referents of “the clothes,” “the catch,” and the unidentified prince who supposedly allowed only dogs inside.
- Confirm whether Hellion relayed Sister Pira’s message to Sir Malmann.

### Boundaries

- Preserve the competing accounts of the recruits’ displacement: the villagers blamed Hicklanders, while the captured operative blamed bandits and said the Reformists merely exploited the attack. Neither account has been verified.
- Do not treat Carissa as a confirmed changeling, Face-Stealer, or parasite host. The cause of her altered behavior remains unresolved.
- Treat claims about Carissa’s exploitation and the Reformists’ initiation practices as testimony from the operative and recruits, not independently established fact.
- The Reformists present themselves as a purified form of the Divine Masochist Church, but they are not interchangeable with every member or institution of that church.
- Crispin Mallow is only a suspected referent for the superior in Maiden’s Field; the operative did not name him or explain the phrase “playing the gardener again.”
