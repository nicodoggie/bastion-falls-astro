## Summary

### Leaving Kurtis Settlement

- Sapphire woke after only a few hours of rest but felt recovered from the previous night’s injuries. Andrew was less fortunate and began the morning with a headache.
- The party remained concerned that an unidentified force might be advancing toward both Maiden’s Field and the Ranneks.
  - Scouts found no reinforcements searching for Pablito Escobar, Torkin, or the other captured Reformist operatives.
  - With the captives’ parasites removed, their allies may believe them dead, although a later investigation or retaliation remains possible.
- The Band of the Ladle and local mercenaries were briefed and remained available to protect the settlement.
  - Arika was told to seek aid from Rannekford if trouble returned.
  - Her mother still intended to present the evidence against the mayor to the town council.
- Andrew and Sapphire adopted a Taminok Brewery cover.
  - Andrew wore his brewery uniform.
  - Sapphire received a fitted golden-yellow dress bearing the family seal, apparently sent by Satoria.
  - The dress did not fully conceal the outline of Sapphire’s severed wings, so she kept her cloak over it.
- Sapphire introduced herself to Arika by her true name and apologized for previously relying on aliases.
  - She explained that attempting to lie causes an unexplained internal jolt.
- Arika’s family provided breakfast and waived payment for the party’s stay in gratitude for her rescue.
- The party found a box of provisions in its carriage, including preserves, jerky, marmalade, jams, pickled plums, pickled eggs, and sunset-beet soup.
- Andrew invited Arika to a Taminok Brewery festival featuring wrestling and a possibly unlimited supply of beer.
- The party continued directly toward Maiden’s Field rather than detouring to investigate an unverified Reformist preacher at a nearby homestead.
  - The abandoned homestead lead could be passed to other authorities.
  - The travelers hoped to reach shelter before an approaching storm.

### Families, trade, and Feywild goods

- Sapphire recalled learning knots and shipboard language from her former crew.
  - Dom was patient with her, while Snow was much less so.
  - She also spoke of Aza, Caedus, Mai, and Wilhelm Köhler.
  - Mai willingly left with Wilhelm, who reportedly became a better person because of her.
- Sapphire once worked as a barmaid at her mother’s brothel, Hell’s Kitchen.
  - Her mother’s daughters could propose improvements while retaining the right not to participate.
- Inspired by Feywild media sprites, Sapphire suggested hiring bards or skalds to advertise Taminok Brewery.
  - These sprites carry commercial songs between towns using domestic magic popularized by the Autumn Court.
- Andrew remained uneasy about his influential merchant family.
  - They possess wealth and connections but no noble title.
  - They once tried unsuccessfully to remove him from military service after he had reached adulthood.
- The travelers discussed several Feywild delicacies.
  - Sunset beets and berries grow in lands of perpetual sunset.
  - Sunrise beets also exist but are tougher.
  - Pumpkin Town produces Golden Nectar from golden pumpkins.
- A magical quartermaster’s notebook automatically tracks supplies as they are removed.
  - Its owner spent a week cataloguing Feywild goods and jokingly called the notebook his romance book.

### Magic, scholarship, and the Feywild courts

- The party challenged General Strandiz’s influential argument that divination is little more than unreliable guesswork.
  - Fairy weather magic and *Druidcraft* were offered as practical counterexamples.
  - Strandiz’s thesis was only recently approved in Senera.
  - Wartime Apgarian manuscripts have also begun entering the country, sometimes illegally through its ports.
  - Democratic writings are reportedly still burned by the military.
- Necromancy remains prohibited in Senera and condemned by the Church.
  - A theory associated with the University of Thrantorbury claims the Church suppresses necromantic scholarship to preserve control over healing and resurrection.
  - The discussion considered *Revivify*, *Spare the Dying*, *Resurrection*, and *True Resurrection* as part of the disputed boundary between healing and necromancy.
- Minfilia reportedly practices necromancy and possesses a specialized book from Scholomance in Murkendraw concerning lichdom.
  - She reportedly cannot use the book’s process because she is a cleric.
- Creating a lich requires rare materials, a soul-separating potion, the removal of part of the subject’s soul, and a sufficiently powerful vessel.
  - A phylactery often possesses legendary or sentimental importance, but neither quality is an absolute requirement.
  - Destroying a phylactery makes recovery of the separated soul fragment difficult.
- The party discussed several Feywild magical institutions.
  - Megima, in the Summer Court, teaches Evocation.
  - Golaroxy teaches Transmutation.
  - Ionguale, located in a desert, may teach Abjuration.
  - Avium is a general academy rather than a dedicated Divination school.
- Focused institutional study can unlock greater capabilities within a practitioner’s magical vocation.
- Secrets can serve as currency or create binding relationships in the Feywild.
  - Named archfey included the Keeper of Secrets, the Cobbler, and Queen Arcadia of the Ashes and the Scorched Forest.
- The principal courts were described as Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter, Day, Night, and Dawn.
  - The former Dusk Court was overthrown and became Midnight in the Feydark.
  - Lesser courts include Coral, Crimson, Iron, Crystal, Time, Star, and Dream.
- Captain Hedwig resembles Queen Arcadia, while Minfilia strongly resembles Lady Verenestra.
  - Similar resemblances among Hedwig’s crew prompted speculation about their Feywild connections.

### Stacy and Crispin Mallow’s victims

- Stacy survived Crispin Mallow’s alterations and continued recovering under Sister Pira’s care.
  - Her organs had been rearranged and unnaturally connected.
  - Parts incorporated into her body appear to have come from a Face-Stealer.
- Mallow appears to leave his victims conscious of their alterations so that survivors and witnesses will be horrified.
- A distressed painter attempted another portrait of Branrum, but memories of Stacy intruded upon the work.
  - The resulting painting combined Branrum with unnatural anatomy and Stacy-like golden scales.
  - The painter took the portrait into the garden to reflect and seek another emotional outlet.
- Stacy’s recovery offered hope that other victims transformed by Mallow might also be restored.

### Castle Rannek’s security crisis

- Angel’s mother assumed duties normally handled by Uther while Castle Rannek responded to the disappearance of dozens of personnel.
- A guard involved in the previous day’s fire was confirmed as a genuine castle guard rather than an impersonator.
  - His legs had been switched before he set himself and a building ablaze.
  - Angel’s mother believed the perpetrator was weaponizing missing personnel to terrorize their families and burden survivors with guilt.
- Castle personnel adopted more invasive identity examinations in response to the Face-Stealer threat.
- Trajan remained at the castle while wearing Hellion’s face.
  - Hellion had asked him to keep Angel at the castle, but Trajan accepted that she would leave after learning Uther was alive.
  - He remained to protect Maria and Gwyniviere because Hellion suspected the enemy was trying to divide their defenders.
- Gwyniviere stayed behind to support her mother and confront the distress caused by what she had witnessed.
  - She and Angel embraced before parting.
  - Gwyniviere told Angel she was proud of her and hoped they could paint together again after the rescue.
- Hellion advised Angel to support Gwyniviere through quiet presence rather than attempting to resolve her inner struggle for her.

### The Lord’s Judgment

- Marchioness Maria Rannek sent Angel to investigate the Lord’s Judgment, a registered mercenary guild operating from a weapon-decorated tavern.
- Sir Hilis Brastok, Trajan, Castle Rannek soldiers, and others accompanied her.
- Rumors of Angel’s capture had already spread through Maiden’s Field, and patrons recognized her when she entered.
- Bertram Dorand, the guild’s leader and a notary, verified the party’s authority and granted access to the premises.
- Angel ordered that nobody leave while soldiers secured the exits and counted the occupants.
  - The patrons were unusually subdued, but *Detect Magic* found no enchantment influencing them.
- Magic led the investigators to a locked basement filled with supplies, trophies, gifts, and snow globes.
  - Brastok opened the door using Bertram’s key and retained the key.
  - Angel found a black-purple Orb of Enhancement hidden among the stored objects.
  - Opening the basement released magic resembling that associated with the earlier parasites or “worms.”

### Lotan’s capture

- Angel used *Locate Creature* to pursue whoever had placed the orb.
- Angel, Brastok, and several soldiers chased the moving target onto the roof.
  - *Hold Person* stopped him before he could jump to a neighboring building.
- The captive wore Leland’s face and spoke with his voice, but his legs were visibly mismatched and a stitch beneath his face revealed extensive alteration.
- Telepathic examination showed that he possessed neither Leland’s memories nor Leland’s affection for Angel.
  - His actual name was Lotan.
  - His constrained eye movements suggested that he was coerced or unable to answer freely.
- *Detect Magic* revealed evocation magic in Lotan’s chest.
  - Angel dispelled it and identified the fading symbol as a *Glyph of Warding*.
- A further magical search led Angel, Brastok, Lotan, and the guards across the river.
  - They confronted a lone spellcaster who recognized Leland’s face and defended himself with *Shield*.
  - The party used an augmented *Command*—“Drop”—against him.

### Lotan’s memories

- Hearing Crispin Mallow’s name triggered memories of Mallow’s abuse.
  - Mallow forced Lotan into his Face-Stealer form.
  - He made Lotan consume another person’s facial skin.
  - He drove Lotan through more transformations than Lotan could safely withstand.
- Lotan remembered Mallow as a concealed-faced man with bright golden wings.
- Through Lotan’s memories, Angel saw Uther Rannek, the real Kethbjorn, Sandor, and several court wizards imprisoned in separate cages.
  - Uther was badly injured and extremely weak.
  - Sandor had been severely beaten.
  - Their captor had not yet overcome their minds.
- Mallow’s exposed flesh appeared partly golden and draconic, apparently because of his grafts.
- Angel threatened Mallow through her connection with Lotan, invoking Minfilia, Oscar, Hellion, and Sapphire and promising to deliver Mallow to Minfilia alive.
  - She ended the connection before learning whether Mallow could answer through Lotan.
- Lotan’s memories also revealed Face-Stealers being tortured and conditioned from childhood.
  - They were stripped of personal identity, trained to adopt other faces, and taught to serve the Divine Masochist.
- Angel told Lotan that his body and identity belonged to him and offered to introduce him to Trajan.
- Hilis gave Lotan a sleeping pill so he could be transported safely.
  - Trajan accepted responsibility for him and planned to seek treatment from Sister Pira and Castle Rannek’s healing pool.

### Lord’s Judgment joins Angel

- Angel returned to the guild and reported that the hidden orb had led her to evidence that Mallow held her father.
- She hired all twenty members of Lord’s Judgment for 1,000 gp under a two-month contract.
  - She paid 100 gp upfront.
  - The guild accepted on the condition that it would not participate in persecution.
  - Bertram sealed the contract and placed the company under Hilis’s operational command.
- Bertram revealed that he is an Eldritch Knight and one of three spellcasters in the otherwise martial company.
- For the coming operation, Angel requested Bertram and his fighters while leaving the guild’s spellcasters behind.

### Searching for Lance

- Angel searched Lance’s room at Castle Rannek.
  - She found a key marked “Rannekford Storage Company.”
  - She also took one of Lance’s early wooden giraffe carvings.
- Using the carving, Angel cast *Locate Creature*.
  - The spell initially pointed toward Maiden’s Field and then abruptly lost Lance.
- Angel asked Hellion to continue searching near Lance’s last detected position while she focused on Uther.
- Hellion believed Lance was still alive, but acknowledged that this was intuition rather than proof.

### The Malthreks arrive

- Duke and Duchess Malthrek, Katalin Malthrek, and two guards arrived through Castle Rannek’s private *Teleportation Circle*.
- Tyrion subjected the unexpected arrivals to the castle’s Face-Stealer protocol.
  - Both the duke and duchess passed.
  - The duke successfully recalled recently restored security codes but warned that the Reformists probably possessed them as well.
- Katalin was formally introduced as the Malthreks’ daughter-in-law.
- The duke and duchess apologized for the harm they had caused while compromised and promised restitution to the Ranneks.
  - They intended to share their recovered memories and help repair the damage.
- Repeated uses of *Encode Thoughts* and *Greater Restoration* were gradually restoring the couple’s memories.
  - The duke recalled additional locations in the western Malthreks.
  - His parasite had already been extracted and killed.
- The Malthreks revealed that Tyrion’s superior had been replaced by an infiltrator.
  - The impersonation may have continued for more than a year.
  - An earlier infiltrator may have been replaced after making a mistake.
- They also described attempted replacements of Rannekford magistrates and successful substitutions involving Gwyniviere, Mark Malthrek, and another family member.
  - A golem duplicate of Mark remains imprisoned in the military dungeons.
  - Mark lost roughly a year of memories after prolonged parasite exposure.
  - Gwyniviere released him from their engagement rather than hold him to a relationship he could no longer remember.
- Emily Hazeldine is expected to marry Mark and help lead the Malthrek court wizards.
- The duke and duchess announced their intention to abdicate after helping repair the damage caused during their rule.

### The war-room council

- Maria received the unexpected Malthrek delegation and moved the gathering to the situation room at the duke’s request.
- Angel reported the Lord’s Judgment investigation, Lotan’s rescue, the imprisoned Ranneks and court wizards, and the search for Lance.
- Katalin identified Crispin Mallow as the current Reformist Candlebearer.
  - Mallow may be an ascended Dark Archon answerable directly to Sovereign Rothschild, although this remained a theory.
  - Rothschild was described as an archlich and the Reformist patriarch.
  - The intelligence reportedly originated with the celestial Deathengel.
- Mallow was credited with the Faltir operation that transformed salt into black powder and with constructing exceptionally precise golems.
- Katalin described Mallow’s magical needle, which can use ordinary thread to control people through his fleshcrafting.
  - His experiments employ rare magical materials, including dragon bones, scales, and an Urdefolcin’s heart.
  - Sister Pira now possesses the heart.
- Mallow may be exploiting the Hicklander rebellion to pursue a vendetta against the Maidenfelds.
- The council concluded that the Face-Stealers serving Mallow should be treated as captives and victims.
  - The rescue force intends to disable and restrain them rather than kill them.
- Angel may herself be one of Mallow’s targets.
  - The council considered whether he intends to use *Planar Binding* on her.
  - Directly teleporting to Uther was rejected as too dangerous because Mallow may have prepared wards.
- Katalin refused to leave the duke and duchess despite the urgency of the rescue.
- Antoinette offered healing, *Greater Restoration*, and teleportation support.

### Auraxx and the Gold Canary

- The council identified Auraxx as the gold dragon imprisoned within the Gold Canary.
  - Auraxx protected Feywild elves and was cursed into canary form by an archfey.
  - He is not allied with the Reformist Church.
- Gwyniviere and Hilis had both seen Mallow manifest golden wings and dragon-like scales.
- Angel produced a dragon scale and passed it to another member of the council.
- Mallow’s access to pieces of Auraxx may explain some of his golden, draconic alterations.

### Regional infiltration and forbidden magic

- The Reformists appear to have infiltrated the Admiralty, Malthrek forces, Eastonton’s Paladinate, and several Hicklander movements.
- The Malthreks reported that their northern lands contain natural mines used to manufacture Reformist orbs.
- Lord Garward may have helped introduce Reformist influence to Eastonton.
  - Lord Boyle Eastonton discovered that Garward had not been the same since the war.
- Deathengel’s intelligence identifies Isana Walburton as a simulacrum.
  - Tanduaria publicly executes unaffiliated arcane casters while its Church secretly preserves restricted magical knowledge, including *Simulacrum*.
- The council compared different regional systems for controlling spellcasters.
  - Tanduaria channels them through the Church.
  - Poasah places them under the Thirteen and the Crown.
  - Apgar distrusts them and reportedly relies on forced drafts.
  - Senera remains comparatively accepting despite its own prohibitions.
- Duke Malthrek connected regional fear of magic to events at the Bastion and the rise of magically empowered warlords in nearby Burtz.
- Katalin warned of another place called Suma where suspected spellcasters, including children, are allegedly killed without inquiry.

### Kurtis intelligence reaches the council

- Airur reported that Pablito Escobar and Torkin remained detained at Kurtis Settlement.
- The mayor was imprisoned and facing trial for corruption, graft, and coercive misconduct involving minors.
- The Band of the Ladle held evidence against him, and a protective squad was being dispatched.
- Reformist recruiters had used parties, alcohol, other substances, and an inhibiting orb to target young residents.
  - Authorities suspected the operatives planned to spread parasites through the wider population.
- Andrew, Sapphire, Thilas, Airur, Tormil, and James remained safe and continued toward Maiden’s Field to gather intelligence.

### Port Mibram and the Admiralty

- Angel changed her route to visit Vice Marshal Tyrion Donmar at Port Mibram before proceeding to Maiden’s Field.
- A newly established security perimeter around the Admiralty required Angel and Hilis to pass private identity examinations based on questions prepared by Maria.
- Tyrion reported that Maiden’s Field had issued a 1,000 gp dead-or-alive bounty for Master Kelthyr Kulok.
- Maria later promoted Tyrion to Marshal.
  - She praised him for taking command of the Admiralty’s Helm, opposing a compromised superior, and auditing and protecting its remaining personnel.
- Tyrion reported that he was now the Western Admiralty’s only remaining marshal.
  - The Marshal of the Fleet and the marshal responsible for the Malter River were missing.
  - Reformist infiltration may have obstructed a coordinated Royal Navy response for roughly eighteen months.

### Activating the Port Mibram circle

- Port Mibram possessed the largest *Teleportation Circle* Angel had seen.
  - Hellion’s magical orbs had already been installed around it, but the military casters did not know how to use them.
- Angel altered one corner of the sigil into a triangle.
- Although she could not cast *Teleportation Circle* herself, Angel channeled energy from the orbs through herself and activated the installation without using a spell slot.
- She taught a military caster to direct the spell through the orbs rather than the circle itself.
  - Angel had learned the technique by watching Minfilia in the Travertines.
- The orbs should recharge naturally if left in place.
- Angel ordered the circle guarded against unauthorized use.
  - Its sigil would be shared with allies, including Hedwig and Safar.
  - The circle could move reinforcements, supplies, refugees, and rescued captives whenever a capable caster was available.

### Allies and preparations for Maiden’s Field

- Hellion’s group remained about half a day from Maiden’s Field.
  - He withheld part of his plan to protect it from possible mind readers.

- Hellion intended to ask Oren why the Reformist Church feared Maiden’s Field.

  - The field party would include Angel, Gwyniviere, Hilis, Bertram, and members of Lord’s Judgment.

  - She feared that allowing her eleven-year-old daughter to join it made her a poor mother.
  - The council argued that Angel had knowingly chosen to help Mallow’s victims, possessed extraordinary abilities, and would remain under Hilis’s protection.
- The Ranneks pledged to help reclaim the Northern Malthreks once their own people and territory were secure.
- Before departure, a Divine Masochist led the company in a prayer of comfort at a religious complex containing several angelic chapter houses, including the Society of the Angel of Delight.

### Boundaries

- Trajan and Hellion are distinct people. Trajan was deliberately wearing Hellion’s face and using his name during these events.
- Leland is not established as a Face-Stealer. Lotan is a separate victim who was forced to wear Leland’s face and voice.
- Auraxx is the gold dragon imprisoned in the Gold Canary. Crispin Mallow, not Auraxx, is the Reformist Candlebearer.
- Angel’s image of Oscar as a revenant was used to intimidate Mallow and does not establish that Oscar is a revenant.
- The comparison between baelnorns and “archliches” was a simplified explanation, not a settled equivalence.
- *Locate Creature* established only Lance’s last detected direction before the spell failed; it did not establish whether he died, hid underground, entered an extradimensional space, or became magically concealed.
- Healing magic is dangerous to Face-Stealers transformed into monstrosities, as Stacy was. Hilis explicitly distinguished them from ordinary Face-Stealers, who are not harmed by healing magic.
- Face-Stealers coerced by Mallow are to be treated as victims and rescued where possible, even when they are encountered as hostile operatives.
- Mallow’s supposed vendetta against the Maidenfelds, Dark Archon status, and intention to bind Angel remain theories or hostile reports rather than confirmed motives.
- Katalin and Gwyniviere’s council-chamber encounter was consensual, but the transcript does not reliably assign most individual actions or dialogue.
