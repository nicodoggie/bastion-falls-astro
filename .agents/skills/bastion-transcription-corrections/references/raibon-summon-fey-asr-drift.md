# Raibon Summon Fey ASR Drift

Use when cleaning Vengeful notes/transcripts where a Summon Fey explanation contains `Rubana`, `Rebun`, or another name-shaped fragment near fey-spirit summoning.

## Pattern

In the 2026-06-21 Vengeful transcript, the corrected transcript heard:

- `Rubana` around 00:33:18-00:33:55.
- The raw transcript had `Rebun` in a nearby short line and `Rubana` in the merged line.

Nico later confirmed this was not a new character or place. It was the speaker explaining a difference between summoning a fey spirit in **Raibon** versus in **Senera**.

## Canonical interpretation

- Use **Raibon** for this context.
- The point is that fey spirits behave differently in Raibon because Raibon's fey are trapped there and isolated from fey crossings back to their homeland.
- Do not invent a `Rubana` character, place, fey pool, schoolmate, or spell variant.
- Do not flatten the passage into only "not a real name"; the Raibon-vs-Senera trapped-fey distinction is the useful lore.

## Note cleanup guidance

When patching the active note:

- Add the Raibon/Senera distinction near the Summon Fey mechanics/context.
- Keep the appearance distinction: an Arcadia-like or Luana-like summoned spirit is not the real Arcadia/Luana.
- Avoid saying the spirit is "not a trapped local spirit" if the confirmed point is that Raibon's fey spirits are trapped/local-isolated in a broader metaphysical sense.

## Correction rule guidance

A shared rule can live under the Summon Fey / spell-shaping correction, with aliases such as:

- `Rubana`
- `Rebun`
- `Rubana summons`
- `Raibon summons`

Instruction should say that `Rubana`/`Rebun` are ASR drift for Raibon in this transcript context, not new entities.
