## Core premise

A comic-serious rivalry exists between cabbage/Brassican ferment mystics and the Bovine Collective over the term **lactomancy**.

- Cabbage claim: lactomancy means magic/divination through **lactic acid fermentation**.
- Bovine claim: lactomancy belongs to milk, udders, cream, curds, whey, and herd-memory.
- Neutral terms floated: galactomancy for milk/star-milk magic, zymomancy for fermentation generally, brinomancy for salt-brine divination. Both sides dislike compromises.

## Cabbage / Brassican side

Cabbage civilizations archive memory in sealed fermentation crocks: **brine-relics**. These can hold ancestral memories, hidden lore, anti-corruption magical techniques, treaties, maps, recipes, and warnings. Access modes include brine-reading, taste communion, spell extraction, and druidic/plant communion.

Possible failed civilizations:

- **Crock-Cities of Veyr-Saal** — terraced salt gardens; anti-aberration brines; fell after spring poisoning with sweetwater.
- **Brassican Concord of Nine Pressings** — clan confederation by fermentation houses/generational pressings.
- **Sour Crown Dynasty** — rulers chosen by prophetic fermentation of shed leaves.
- **Pale Jar Monasteries** — brine-mystic lesson crocks preserving techniques and warding recipes.

Cabbage cosmology centers **salt** as the first principle: boundary, balance, preservation, memory, separation, and proper measure. The stars are **The Scattered Salt** across the **Black Brine**; the Milky Way may be derided as **The White Lie**.

Important cabbage theological line:

> Salt does not prevent change. Salt prevents meaningless change.

Salt stands between the burns:

- Acid: honest but hungry transformation; zeal that devours softness.
- Base: deceptive, slick dissolution that unmakes boundaries; false mercy.
- Salt: balanced sting that reveals, measures, preserves, and allows meaningful fermentation.

## Bovine Collective side

The Bovine Collective sees the Milky Way as proof that milk/lactomancy extends beyond pasture into the stars. **Galactomancy** is lactomancy at cosmic scale.

Possible bovine cosmological names:

- **The Great Udder Above**
- **The White Track**
- **The Spilled Blessing**
- **The Churn Beyond Pasture**

Bovine magic themes: nourishment, prophecy, abundance, maternal protection, dream-sharing, herd-memory, cream rising, moon milk, circular time through rumination.

Galactomancer practices:

- Cream-rising divination in pans left beneath stars.
- Star-whey separation under moonlight.
- Churns turned according to constellations.
- Tools: silver pails, moon-cooled cream, bells tuned to planetary intervals, horn lenses, butter lamps, casein star maps.

Possible bovine factions:

- Pastoral lactomancers — village/traditional milk magic.
- Galactomancers — astronomer-priests and cosmic dairy mystics.
- Butter adepts — practical magical engineers.
- Whey ascetics — reject cream as worldly attachment.

## Rivalry texture

Cabbage insults for bovines: udder literalists, cream chauvinists, pasture-bound etymologists, sweetness cultists with astronomy privileges.

Bovine insults for cabbages: pickle oracles, sour-leaf pretenders, barrel witches, salt-sogged revisionists, vegetal acid mystics, coleslaw necromancers.

Representative exchange:

> Bovine: "Your people ferment in crocks. Ours are named in the heavens."
>
> Cabbage: "A mere linguistic accident! You bovines see the stars and see milk; we see truth. The cosmos was and always will be formed from the salt."

## Joint heresy / adventure material

A forbidden syncretic rite: **The Rite of Sour Milk Under Stars**. Milk inoculated with an ancient brine-relic culture and left beneath the Milky Way creates **Star-Curd**, a living oracle revealing futures-that-almost-happened, ancestral dreams of extinct civilizations, erased maps, or the taste of broken oaths.

A radical theology: milk is the first mercy; salt is the first law. Creation was sacred curdling: nourishment accepting boundary without ceasing to be nourishment.
